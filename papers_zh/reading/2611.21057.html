WiTac：结合腕部视觉的触觉表征
面向接触密集型操作

摘要

手、物体与目标的相对位置和姿态不同，即使触觉读数相近，也可能需要向不同方向修正动作。先融合触觉和腕部画面，再交给策略，可以利用接触发生时的视觉环境，更准确地描述接触。预训练编码器 WiTac 在生成动作之前学习这种触觉表征。WiTac 使用近 300 小时的 SharpaDex 数据预训练，把触觉、手部运动和腕部画面融合成统一的接触表征。将 WiTac 作为下游 VLA 策略的条件输入后， GR00T N1.7 和 π0.5\pi_{0.5} 在四项接触密集型操作任务中的成功率均明显高于各自的纯视觉基线，平均增幅分别为 +28.8% 和 +15.0% 。相较于不使用腕部视角的触觉编码器，两者的增幅分别为 18.8% 和 7.5% 。离线分析还表明，加入腕部视觉能更准确地描述接触、更好地区分任务与接触状态的组合，并提高触觉有噪声时的特征稳定性。即使观测到相近的接触，也更容易从表征中判断应朝哪个方向采取动作。项目页面见 https://wrist-informed-tactile-representations.github.io/ 。

Similar tactile contacts can call for different corrections depending on the configuration of the hand, object and target. Rather than supplying touch and wrist vision separately, fusing them before policy injection better grounds tactile features in visual context, yielding more accurate contact representations. We introduce Wrist-Informed Tactile Representations (WiTac), a pretrained encoder that learns such representations before action generation. Pretrained on nearly 300 hours of SharpaDex data, WiTac deeply fuses tactile signals with hand motion and wrist views into a comprehensive contact representation. Conditioning downstream VLA policies (GR00T N1.7 and π 0.5 ) on WiTac substantially improves task success over vision-only base policies across four contact-rich manipulation tasks (+28.8% and +15.0% on average), and further outperforms a wrist-free tactile encoder by 18.8% and 7.5%, respectively. Offline analyses further show that incorporating wrist vision yields more accurate contact descriptors, stronger joint task–contact discrimination, greater feature stability under tactile noise, and clearer action-direction readout from similar contacts. Project page: https://wrist-informed-tactile-representations.github.io/ .

1 引言

富含接触的操作需要能够同时响应局部触碰与周围任务几何结构的动作。在将电池插入插槽时，不同的电池与插槽位置会导致类似的手指动作，因此需要不同的修正动作。仅依靠局部触觉变形无法明确这种空间结构，而近距离手腕摄像头能够观察手部、被抓握的物体以及附近目标的结构，从而提供在操作过程中识别接触关系所需的空间参考信息。

Contact-rich manipulation requires actions that respond jointly to local touch and surrounding task geometry. When inserting a battery into a slot, similar finger loading can accompany different battery–slot alignments and call for distinct corrective motions. Local tactile deformation alone leaves this spatial configuration ambiguous, whereas close-range wrist cameras observe the hand, grasped object, and nearby target geometry, providing the spatial reference needed to interpret contact during manipulation.

多用途机器人 vision-language-action ( VLA ) 策略如 GR00T [17] 以及 π0.5\pi_{0.5} [18] 它们已在视觉-语言流中接收到相机图像，但缺乏与触觉信号的前融合，以支撑抓取与接触相关状态。最新的视觉-触觉设计通过将独立触觉特征与视觉流结合，将触觉纳入策略之中。 [9, 25] 通过触觉信号调节视觉特征 [15] 或对视觉预测触感产生的离散残差进行编码 [30] 然而，独立的触觉输入使得局部触摸在周围的视觉几何环境中无法被有效支撑，而视觉调节与残余编码则无法直接形成连续的接触表征，从而无法在手腕的视觉语境中保留精细的触摸细节。

Generalist vision-language-action (VLA) policies such as GR00T [ 17 ] and π 0.5 [ 18 ] already receive camera images in their vision-language streams, yet they lack pre-fusion with tactile signals to ground grasp- and contact-related states. Recent visuotactile designs incorporate touch into policies by supplying standalone tactile features alongside visual streams [ 9 , 25 ] , modulating visual features with tactile signals [ 15 ] , or encoding discrete residuals from visually predicted touch [ 30 ] . However, separate tactile inputs leave local touch ungrounded in the surrounding visual geometry, while visual modulation and residual coding do not directly form continuous contact representations that preserve detailed touch within its wrist visual context.

To address this limitation, we introduce Wrist-Informed Tactile Representations (WiTac), a pretrained encoder that fuses touch with wrist vision before policy injection to produce grounded visuotactile contact tokens ( figure 1 ). Pretrained on nearly 300 hours of synchronized dexterous manipulation data from SharpaDex v1.0 [ 19 ] , WiTac summarizes each finger’s tactile history with robot state and wrist-relative fingertip kinematics, then queries wrist patch features through cross-attention to form finger-level contact tokens. Pretraining jointly supervises contact-state and history reconstruction, masked wrist-feature recovery, and action-conditioned wrench-response prediction, encouraging the tokens to retain physical contact detail as they incorporate visual context. During policy learning, the frozen WiTac encoder conditions VLA policies through a lightweight gated cross-attention interface alongside their native visual, language and state inputs. Across four real-world contact-rich tasks, WiTac consistently boosts closed-loop success on both GR00T N1.7 and π 0.5 over vision-only base policies (+28.8% and +15.0% on average) and its wrist-free variant WiTac-NW (+18.8% and +7.5%), while offline analyses confirm improved contact reconstruction, joint task–contact discrimination, noise stability, and action-direction readout.

2 相关研究

触觉与视觉触觉表征。

预训练的触觉编码器为接触感知与操作提供了可复用特征。Sparsh [9] 通过针对触觉图像的自我监督目标来学习触摸表征，而 T3 [32] 则将传感器特定的编码器与跨传感器与任务共享的 Transformer 相结合。AnyTouch [4] 通过掩码建模与跨传感器对齐技术，实现了多视觉触觉传感器之间静态触觉图像与动态触觉视频的统一处理。Sparsh -X [10] 将表示学习扩展至来自多感官触觉传感器的图像、音频、运动及压力信号。物理监督提供了另一条路径：UniVTAC [2] 能够重建触觉外观，并从模拟触觉观测数据中预测变形、标记位置及物体相对姿态。 外部视觉与语言也能影响触觉表征：早期研究利用自监督技术来学习富含接触信息的紧凑型视觉-触觉特征，用于策略学习 [12]；VITaL [6] 通过时间对比预训练实现视觉与触觉嵌入的对齐；OmniVTLA [3] 在双编码器架构中将触觉与视觉及语言概念进行对齐；而 VT-MUSE [22] 则通过跨模态对齐与掩蔽感知重建技术对序列化视觉-触觉潜在表示进行预训练。

Pretrained tactile encoders provide reusable features for contact perception and manipulation. Sparsh [ 9 ] learns touch representations through self-supervised objectives on tactile images, while T3 [ 32 ] combines sensor-specific encoders with a shared Transformer across sensors and tasks. AnyTouch [ 4 ] unifies static tactile images and dynamic tactile videos across multiple visuo-tactile sensors through masked modeling and cross-sensor alignment, and Sparsh-X [ 10 ] extends representation learning to image, audio, motion, and pressure signals from a multisensory tactile sensor. Physical supervision offers another route: UniVTAC [ 2 ] reconstructs tactile appearance and predicts deformation, marker locations, and relative object pose from simulated tactile observations. External vision and language can also shape tactile representations: early work uses self-supervision to learn compact visuotactile features for contact-rich policy learning [ 12 ] , VITaL [ 6 ] aligns visual and tactile embeddings through temporal contrastive pretraining, OmniVTLA [ 3 ] aligns touch with visual and linguistic concepts in a dual-encoder architecture, and VT-MUSE [ 22 ] pretrains sequential visuotactile latents through cross-modal alignment and masked sensory reconstruction.

用于平行颌部夹爪的视觉触觉策略。

Vision-language-action ( VLA ) 等模型，如 GR00T [17] 和 π0.5\pi_{0.5} [18]，提供了通用的视觉、语言和运动能力，而最新的夹持器系统通过策略调节或预测世界建模技术，借助触摸交互来扩展这些能力。在反应式控制中，RDP [24] 将缓慢的潜在扩散策略与高频闭环触觉反馈相结合。在 VLA 策略中，SimpleTouch [25] 将 tokens 从冻结的预训练触觉编码器传输至通过动作监督与未来潜在预测训练的触觉专家 TacFiLM [15]，该模型将预训练的触觉特征映射为特征级缩放与偏移参数，以调节视觉编码器；而 ResTacVLA [30] 则通过不确定性门控机制，对手腕预测与观测触觉之间的量化残差进行编码。 为了预测接触状态的演变，DreamTacVLA [26] 通过将多尺度视图与触摸信号对齐，并利用世界模型预测的未来触觉状态来优化动作规划；而 OmniVTA [33] 则将双流视觉-触觉世界模型与注重接触感知的融合策略与 60 Hz 反射式控制器相结合。World-action models 进一步统一了动态预测与控制：VTAM [29] 将光学触觉图像视为经过流衍生力代理处理的预训练视频主干模型的额外视图；Dream-Tac [14] 通过注重接触感知的注意力偏置机制，联合预测未来的视觉观测、触觉动态及动作；FAWAM [7] 和 ForceWAM [23] 则同时预测未来的扭矩与动作。

Vision-language-action (VLA) models such as GR00T [ 17 ] and π 0.5 [ 18 ] provide generalist visual, language, and motor capabilities that recent gripper-based systems extend with touch through policy conditioning or predictive world modeling. For reactive control, RDP [ 24 ] pairs a slow latent diffusion policy with high-frequency closed-loop tactile feedback. Within VLA policies, SimpleTouch [ 25 ] passes tokens from a frozen pretrained tactile encoder to a tactile expert trained with action supervision and future-latent prediction, TacFiLM [ 15 ] maps pretrained tactile features to feature-wise scale and shift parameters that modulate the visual encoder, and ResTacVLA [ 30 ] encodes the quantized residual between wrist-predicted and observed touch with uncertainty gating. To anticipate contact evolution, DreamTacVLA [ 26 ] aligns multi-scale views with touch and refines draft actions using future tactile states predicted by a world model, while OmniVTA [ 33 ] couples a two-stream visuo-tactile world model and contact-aware fusion policy with a 60 Hz reflexive controller. World-action models further unify dynamics prediction and control: VTAM [ 29 ] treats optical tactile images as an additional view of a pretrained video backbone with a flow-derived force proxy, Dream-Tac [ 14 ] jointly predicts future visual observations, tactile dynamics, and actions using a contact-aware attention bias, and FAWAM [ 7 ] and ForceWAM [ 23 ] predict future wrenches alongside actions.

Moving from parallel-jaw grippers to multi-fingered dexterous hands introduces distributed contacts across fingers, higher-dimensional kinematics, and heavier visual self-occlusion. To bridge embodiments, FTP-1 [ 27 ] maps heterogeneous tactile signals into morphology-aware tokens processed by a shared tactile Transformer expert, and ME-Dex 1.0 [ 31 ] aligns gripper and dexterous-hand tactile inputs onto a canonical hand template within a three-expert world-action model. For multi-fingered hands specifically, SaTA [ 11 ] anchors tactile features to wrist-frame fingertip poses, ViTacFormer [ 8 ] fuses vision and touch via cross-attention with an autoregressive tactile-prediction head, and STAR [ 13 ] jointly predicts masked wrist-image and tactile patches during pretraining before passing separate tactile tokens to a VLA policy. At the policy and world-model level, T-Rex [ 16 ] combines a temporal tactile VQ-VAE with a variable-rate Mixture-of-Transformers to exploit high-frequency tactile feedback, DeCAL [ 5 ] pairs contact-gated fusion with latent visuo-tactile co-imagination, and DexTacWAM [ 28 ] compresses per-finger touch into a video diffusion world model to jointly predict visual and contact dynamics. Rather than training a generative video-tactile world model during policy learning or supplying deployment tactile tokens encoded without wrist views, WiTac pretrains a frozen wrist-informed contact encoder that fuses ten-finger tactile histories, kinematics, and wrist imagery before conditioning VLA policies through lightweight gated cross-attention.

联合编码也优于独立注入。

联合编码与独立注入两种方式都将触觉与手腕视觉特征输入到策略中，区别在于这两种模式是通过独立注意力分支进入策略，还是在注入策略之前被融合为统一的接触本体 tokens。在注入策略之前进行融合可带来 10 – 25% 更高的成功率，使四项任务的平均表现从 50.0% 提升至 66.3%（table 5）。这证实了在注入策略之前将触觉与手腕视觉信息结合起来，比将跨模态融合工作留给动作策略更为有效。

Both joint encoding and separate injection supply tactile and wrist visual features to the policy, differing in whether the two modalities enter the policy through independent attention branches or are fused beforehand into unified contact tokens. Fusing them before policy injection yields 10–25% higher success across all four tasks, raising the four-task average from 50.0% to 66.3% ( table 5 ). This confirms that grounding touch in wrist visual context prior to policy injection is substantially more effective than leaving cross-modal fusion to the action policy.

5 结论

我们提出了 WiTac，这是一种预训练编码器，它将触觉历史、手部运动学以及手腕视觉信息整合到指级接触层面。 tokens 动作生成前。在四项接触密集任务中，WiTac 其无腕部版本的 WiTac-NW 的平均成功率有所提升 18.8% 以及 7.5% 使用 GR00T N1。 7 以及 π0.5\pi_{0.5} 分别地。离线分析进一步表明，WiTac能够更精确地重构接触描述符，更清晰地区分关节任务与接触状态，在合成触觉噪声下特征漂移更小，且与所展示的调整方向匹配度也更高，优于WiTac-NW。这些发现共同支持了将手腕上下文纳入触觉表示以实现灵巧操作的方法。

We presented WiTac, a pretrained encoder that integrates tactile histories, hand kinematics, and wrist vision into finger-level contact tokens before action generation. Across four contact-rich tasks, WiTac improves average success over its wrist-free variant WiTac-NW by 18.8% and 7.5% with GR00T N1.7 and π 0.5 , respectively. Offline analyses further show that WiTac reconstructs contact descriptors more accurately, better distinguishes joint task–contact states, exhibits lower feature drift under synthetic tactile noise, and better aligns with demonstrated adjustment directions than WiTac-NW . Together, these findings support incorporating wrist context into tactile representations for dexterous manipulation.

局限性。

WiTac 采用特征级交叉注意力机制，在共享的 3 D 空间中实现视觉与触觉的对齐，而无需基于深度的对齐处理，尽管它包含了指尖运动信息。该算法仅将触觉历史与当前手腕图像结合，并未明确建模其关节时间演化过程。我们的评估也仅局限于一个机器人平台上的四项任务；不同手型、触觉传感器及相机配置下的迁移效果仍需验证。

WiTac uses feature-level cross-attention without depth-based alignment of vision and touch in a shared 3D space, despite incorporating fingertip kinematics. It combines tactile histories with only current wrist images, without explicitly modeling their joint temporal evolution. Our evaluation is also limited to four tasks on one robotic platform; transfer across hand morphologies, tactile sensors, and camera configurations remains to be validated.
