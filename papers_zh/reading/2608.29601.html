𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-Foundation
迈向触觉智能时代

NeoteAI Team & Fudan TEAI Team
摘要

我们提出 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-Foundation，这是一套利用触觉实现具身操作的范式，涵盖触觉感知硬件、大规模多模态数据、触觉表征学习和标准化评测。首先，我们开发了支撑规模化采集和实际部署的触觉基础设施，包括视觉式触觉传感器、触觉通用操作接口（𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-TacUMI），以及一套同步视触觉采集系统。该系统支持多种机器人本体，也支持使用 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-TacUMI 进行示教。依托这套设施，我们构建了 NeoData，其中包含超过 30,00030{,}000 小时的同步视觉和触觉示教，覆盖六种本体和 450450 项任务。数据包含数十亿对配对的 RGB 和触觉帧，采自真机遥操作与 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-TacUMI 示教。 为支持开放研究，我们进一步发布 OpenNeoData，这是一个包含 5,0005{,}000 小时数据的开源子集，源自 NeoData，覆盖六种本体、超过 250250 项任务及超过 200200 种技能。该数据集弥补了现有操作语料的一项重要不足。接触状态、局部作用力以及滑移初始征兆仅靠视觉很难观测，却对柔性物体操作、精确装配、精细力控和持续表面接触至关重要。依托 NeoData 中大规模且异构的触觉观测，我们提出视触觉表征学习模型 NeoForce。模型以稠密三轴力场作为统一且不依赖具体硬件的监督，从不同传感器设计中学习可迁移的触觉表征，供下游具身策略使用。 为系统评估利用这些基础设施、数据集与触觉表征构建的具身模型，我们进一步提出综合基准，包含真机任务集 NeoReal 和仿真任务集 NeoSim，用于标准化评测富接触操作。两套基准上的实验表明，策略收益来自触觉信号反映的物理接触状态，设备特有的信号外观本身无法解释这种收益。结果表明，规模化触觉数据与监督可用于学习通用触觉表征，进而让具身策略完成精细的富接触操作。我们发布数据集、表征模型和评测基准，支持后续触觉具身操作研究。

We present 𝒩 0 -Foundation, a paradigm for tactile-enabled embodied manipulation, which integrates tactile sensing hardware, large-scale multimodal data, tactile representation learning, and standardized evaluation. First, we engineer the underlying tactile infrastructure for scalable data collection and practical deployment, including a vision-based tactile sensor, a Tactile Universal Manipulation Interface ( 𝒩 0 -TacUMI), and a synchronized visual–tactile data collection system supporting both multiple robot embodiments and 𝒩 0 -TacUMI-based demonstrations. Leveraging this infrastructure, we construct NeoData, which contains more than 30,000 hours of synchronized visual and tactile demonstrations, spanning six embodiments, 450 tasks, and billions of paired RGB and tactile frames collected through a mixture of real-robot teleoperation and 𝒩 0 -TacUMI demonstrations. To facilitate open research, we further release OpenNeoData, a 5,000 -hour open-source subset of NeoData that spans six embodiments, more than 250 tasks, and over 200 skills. The dataset addresses a central limitation of existing manipulation corpora: contact states, local forces, and incipient slip are rarely observable from vision alone, yet they are critical for deformable-object manipulation, precise assembly, delicate force control, and sustained surface interaction. Capitalizing on the large-scale, heterogeneous tactile measurements within NeoData, we propose NeoForce, a visuo-tactile representation learning model that uses dense three-axis force fields as unified, hardware-agnostic supervision to learn transferable tactile representations across different sensor designs for downstream embodied policies. To enable systematic evaluation of tactile embodied models built upon our infrastructure, datasets and tactile representations, we further propose a comprehensive benchmark, which combines the real-world NeoReal suite and the simulated NeoSim suite for standardized evaluation of contact-rich manipulation. Experiments across both suites show that policies benefit from the physical contact state rather than from the device-specific appearance of the tactile signal. These results indicate that large-scale tactile data and supervision make a universal tactile representation possible, which in turn enables embodied policies to perform fine-grained contact-rich manipulation. We release the dataset, the representation, and the benchmark, aiming at supporting future work on tactile-enabled embodied manipulation.

1 引言

从大规模示教数据中学习可泛化的操作策略，已经成为具身智能的主要研究方向。近期 vision-language-action（VLA）模型的进展表明，扩大多样化的多模态示教规模，能够明显提高视觉运动策略的泛化性和鲁棒性 (Brohan et al., 2022; O’Neill et al., 2024; Physical Intelligence et al., 2025)。世界–动作模型为直接学习策略提供了另一条途径。它根据机器人采取的动作预测环境后续如何变化，在不同真实任务和环境中展现出泛化潜力 (Wu et al., 2024; Li et al., 2025b; Ye et al., 2026c)。这两条路线的进展越来越依赖操作数据的规模和多样性。真机数据既包括单一本体的遥操作记录 (Walke et al., 2023; Mandlekar et al., 2021)，也包括在多个机构、不同机器人上采集的跨本体数据 (O’Neill et al., 2024; Khazatsky et al., 2024)。 以 UMI (Chi et al., 2024) 为代表的便携式采集系统，让操作者可以离开固定工作站，在真实环境里规模化采集示教，也催生了多种不依赖特定本体的采集接口 (Liu et al., 2024; Xu et al., 2025a; Ha et al., 2024; Zeng et al., 2025)。与此同时，大规模第一人称视频记录了丰富的手与物体交互，为具身学习提供互补的行为和语义先验 (Damen et al., 2018; Hoque et al., 2026; Punamiya et al., 2026)。

Learning generalizable manipulation policies from large-scale demonstration data has become a central paradigm in embodied intelligence. Recent progress in vision-language-action (VLA) models has demonstrated that scaling diverse multimodal demonstrations substantially improves the generalization and robustness of visuomotor policies ( Brohan et al., 2022 ; O’Neill et al., 2024 ; Physical Intelligence et al., 2025 ) . Complementing direct policy learning, World Action Models learn action-conditioned environment dynamics by modeling how the future world evolves under robot actions, showing promising generalization across real-world tasks and environments ( Wu et al., 2024 ; Li et al., 2025b ; Ye et al., 2026c ) . Progress in both paradigms is increasingly driven by the scale and diversity of available manipulation data. Real-robot datasets range from teleoperated single-embodiment corpora ( Walke et al., 2023 ; Mandlekar et al., 2021 ) to cross-embodiment collections gathered across multiple robots and institutions ( O’Neill et al., 2024 ; Khazatsky et al., 2024 ) . Portable collection systems such as the UMI ( Chi et al., 2024 ) further enable scalable in-the-wild demonstration collection beyond fixed robot workcells and have inspired a growing family of embodiment-agnostic interfaces ( Liu et al., 2024 ; Xu et al., 2025a ; Ha et al., 2024 ; Zeng et al., 2025 ) . Meanwhile, large-scale egocentric videos provide abundant observations of human–object interactions, offering complementary behavioral and semantic priors for embodied learning ( Damen et al., 2018 ; Hoque et al., 2026 ; Punamiya et al., 2026 ) .

现有进展仍有两方面的局限。首先，大多数大规模具身数据集以视觉观测为主。接触密集任务的成功取决于局部作用力、摩擦、初始滑移和接触状态变化，外部或腕部相机很难观察到这些信息，仅凭视觉往往不足以完成任务。这会影响形变物体操作、精密插接与装配、精细力控制、持续表面接触和双手协作等多类任务。其次，触觉数据的格式各自为政。相机式视触觉传感器 (Yuan et al., 2017; Lambeta et al., 2020)、电容阵列 (Schmitz et al., 2011; Maiolino et al., 2013) 和纤维式传感器 (Xing et al., 2025) 各自输出与硬件相关的信号。 这种异构性使一种设备采集的触觉示教难以直接用于另一种设备，也使汇集不同设备数据训练策略更加困难，触觉数据集和模型因而难以从扩大规模中获益。

Despite this progress, two limitations remain. First, most large-scale embodied datasets are dominated by visual observations. However, vision alone is often insufficient for contact-rich manipulation, where success depends on local force, friction, incipient slip, and contact transitions that are weakly observable from external or wrist-mounted cameras. These limitations affect a broad range of task families, including deformable-object manipulation, precise insertion and assembly, delicate force control, sustained surface interaction, and bimanual coordination. Second, the tactile modality remains fragmented. Camera-based visuo-tactile sensors ( Yuan et al., 2017 ; Lambeta et al., 2020 ) , capacitive arrays ( Schmitz et al., 2011 ; Maiolino et al., 2013 ) , and fiber-based sensors ( Xing et al., 2025 ) produce signals in different hardware-specific formats. This heterogeneity prevents tactile demonstrations collected on one device from being reused directly on another, which complicates policy training over pooled tactile data and ultimately prevents tactile datasets and models from benefiting from scale.

为解决上述问题，我们建立了以触觉为核心的具身操作体系 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-Foundation，读作 Neo-Foundation。如图 1 所示，𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-Foundation 将可扩展的触觉采集设施、大规模多模态数据采集、统一表征学习和标准化策略评估纳入同一研究框架。𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-Foundation 包括四个部分。触觉基础设施、大规模数据集 NeoData、借助 NeoForce 学习的触觉表征，以及综合评估协议。我们先构建可支持规模化采集和实际部署的触觉基础设施。 这些设施包括耐用的光学触觉传感器、𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-TacUMI，以及同步采集视觉和触觉的数据系统，可用于多个本体的真机示教和便携的 UMI 示教。基于这些设施，NeoData 收录了超过 30,00030{,}000 小时的同步视触觉示教，覆盖六种本体和 450+450+ 项任务，共有数十亿帧配对的 RGB 与触觉数据。数据集兼有真机遥操作与 UMI 示教，既提供真实机器人动作，又支持规模化的人类操作采集。通过同步记录画面和触觉，NeoData 保留了仅看画面难以推断的接触状态、局部力和初始滑移信号。 为支持开放研究，我们进一步发布开源子集 OpenNeoData。该子集包含 5,0005{,}000 小时数据，覆盖六种本体、超过 250250 项任务与超过 200200 种技能。基于 NeoData 规模较大且异构的触觉数据，我们以稠密三轴力场为不同触觉传感器提供共同的物理监督，形成与硬件无关的统一触觉表征学习方法。在此基础上，我们开发了视触觉表征模型 NeoForce，为下游具身策略学习可迁移且保留时间结构的触觉表征。我们还结合真机基准 NeoReal 与仿真基准 NeoSim，为接触密集操作提供标准化评估。

To address these limitations, 𝒩 0 -Foundation, pronounced as Neo-Foundation, establishes a tactile-centric foundation for embodied manipulation. As summarized in Fig. 1 , 𝒩 0 -Foundation establishes a scalable paradigm for tactile-enabled embodied learning that connects scalable tactile data infrastructure, large-scale multimodal data acquisition, unified representation learning, and standardized policy evaluation. 𝒩 0 -Foundation is organized around four technical pillars: tactile infrastructure, a large-scale dataset named NeoData, tactile representation learning with NeoForce, and a comprehensive evaluation protocol. First, we build the underlying tactile infrastructure required for scalable collection and practical deployment. It includes durable vision-based tactile sensors, 𝒩 0 -TacUMI, and a synchronized visual–tactile data collection system supporting both real-robot demonstrations across multiple embodiments and portable UMI-based demonstrations. Built on this infrastructure, NeoData contains more than 30,000 hours of synchronized visual and tactile demonstrations, spanning six embodiments, 450 + tasks, and billions of paired RGB and tactile frames. The corpus combines real-robot teleoperation with UMI demonstrations, jointly providing physically grounded robot executions and scalable human-operated trajectories. By recording tactile signals alongside visual observations, NeoData captures contact states, local forces, and incipient slip that are difficult to infer from vision alone. To support open research, we further release OpenNeoData, a 5,000 -hour open-source subset spanning six embodiments, more than 250 tasks, and over 200 skills. Based on the large-scale and heterogeneous tactile data in NeoData, we introduce a unified, hardware-agnostic tactile representation learning formulation that uses dense three-axis force fields as a common physical supervision space across different tactile sensor designs. Under this formulation, we develop NeoForce, a visuo-tactile representation model that learns transferable and temporally structured tactile representations for downstream embodied policies. Finally, we provide standardized evaluation for contact-rich manipulation by combining the real-world NeoReal suite with the simulated NeoSim suite.

2 相关研究

2.1 本体硬件与数据收集

具身操控系统将机器人身体、感知模块与演示接口相结合。传统遥操作直接记录可执行的机器人动作，但将采集任务绑定在特定机械臂和夹爪上。UMI (Chi et al., 2024) 则采用手持夹爪及相关末端执行器指令，将演示采集与物理机器人分离；后续系统进一步将这一原理应用于移动、灵巧且独立于硬件平台的平台 (Ha et al., 2024; Xu et al., 2025a; Liu et al., 2024)。便携式视觉触觉接口（如 Touch in the Wild (Zhu et al., 2026)、FreeTacMan (Wu et al., 2025a)、ViTaMIn-B (Li et al., 2025a)、TacUMI (Cheng et al., 2026) 和 exUMI (Xu et al., 2025b)）进一步将接触传感器连接到手持或可穿戴采集器上。TAMEn (Wu et al., 2026a) 则结合跨形态可穿戴接口、MoCap/VR 跟踪技术、在线可行性检查机制以及触觉恢复遥操作，构成一种闭环采集系统。 然而，这些系统通常仅针对少数机器人平台，并保留特定设备的触觉信息流。NeoData 将无机器人采集与多机器人遥操作相结合，并将六种本体形式整合为统一的动作与观察模式。

Embodied manipulation systems couple the robot body, sensing stack, and demonstration interface. Conventional teleoperation records executable robot actions directly but binds collection to particular arms and grippers. UMI ( Chi et al., 2024 ) instead uses a handheld gripper and relative end-effector commands to decouple demonstration collection from physical robots, and subsequent systems extend this principle to mobile, dexterous, and hardware-independent platforms ( Ha et al., 2024 ; Xu et al., 2025a ; Liu et al., 2024 ) . Portable visuo-tactile interfaces (Touch in the Wild ( Zhu et al., 2026 ) , FreeTacMan ( Wu et al., 2025a ) , ViTaMIn-B ( Li et al., 2025a ) , TacUMI ( Cheng et al., 2026 ) , and exUMI ( Xu et al., 2025b ) ) further attach contact sensors to handheld or wearable collectors. TAMEn ( Wu et al., 2026a ) additionally combines a cross-morphology wearable interface, MoCap/VR tracking, online feasibility checking, and tactile recovery teleoperation in a closed-loop collection engine. Yet these systems generally target a few robot platforms and retain device-specific tactile streams. NeoData combines robot-free collection with multi-robot teleoperation and maps six embodiments to a common action and observation schema.

2.2 本体化操作数据集

2.3 表征与操作中的机器人学习

机器人学习日益将可扩展策略学习与可复用感知相结合。RT- 1 (Brohan et al., 2022)与扩散策略(Chi et al., 2023)能够实现有效的动作建模，用于现实世界的操作任务；而VLA模型，如π0.5\pi_{0.5}，则将这一方法扩展为通用策略(Physical Intelligence et al., 2025)。近期出现的具身化基础模型进一步验证了这种扩展理论。 Xiaomi-Robotics- 0 将流匹配动作生成与异步推理相结合，用于实时双手控制 (Cai et al., 2026b)；Xiaomi-Robotics- 1 利用手持 UMI 设备收集的超过 100 小时的现实轨迹数据进行预训练 (Xiaomi Robotics Team et al., 2026)；LingBot- VLA 2.0 整合了约 60,000 小时涵盖机器人与以自我为中心的人类数据的资料，涵盖 20 种机器人配置及全身动作空间 (Wu et al., 2026b)；HY-Embodied 系列则构建了支持下游 VLA 训练的混合Transformer（MoT）与混合专家（MoE）视觉语言模型 (Tencent Robotics X et al., 2026; Wang et al., 2026)，而并行系统则统一了理解、生成与动作功能，或简化了 VLA 技术栈 (Cai et al., 2026a; Ye et al., 2026b)。另一条研究路线则采用视频与环境建模进行控制。 LingBot-Vision 通过遮蔽边界建模技术进行密集空间表示的预训练 (Fu et al., 2026) , LingBot-Video 利用机器人导向视频素材进行 MoE 视频预训练 (Ma et al., 2026) ，如 LingBot-VA 这样的因果视频动作模型 2.0 与动作协同预测场景演化 (Li et al., 2026a; Zhang et al., 2026a) , world-action models 将世界预测整合至策略学习中 (Niu et al., 2026; Yuan et al., 2026a; Lou et al., 2026) ，以及交互式或 3 D世界模型充当模拟器与具身化数据引擎 (Gao et al., 2026b; Team HY-World et al., 2026) ，其中小米机器人-U0显著提升了分布外成功率 π0.5\pi_{0.5} 通过合成本体数据 (Li et al., 2026b) . 然而，在这些基础模型研究中，观察结果与预训练语料仍属于视觉、语言及本体感知范畴；而接触现象既未被感知也未被标准化。此外，TouchGuide 的触觉接触模型在推理阶段能够引导预训练的视觉运动策略，这仍是一个罕见的例外案例 (Zhang et al., 2026b)。

Robot learning increasingly combines scalable policy learning with reusable perception. RT-1 ( Brohan et al., 2022 ) and Diffusion Policy ( Chi et al., 2023 ) demonstrate effective action modeling for real-world manipulation, and VLA models such as π 0.5 scale this recipe into generalist policies ( Physical Intelligence et al., 2025 ) . Recent embodied foundation models extend this scaling thesis. Xiaomi-Robotics-0 couples flow-matching action generation with asynchronous inference for real-time bimanual control ( Cai et al., 2026b ) , Xiaomi-Robotics-1 pre-trains on more than 100k hours of real-world trajectories collected with handheld UMI devices ( Xiaomi Robotics Team et al., 2026 ) , LingBot-VLA 2.0 curates roughly 60,000 hours of robot and egocentric human data spanning 20 robot configurations and whole-body action spaces ( Wu et al., 2026b ) , and the HY-Embodied series builds Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT) and Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) vision language models that support downstream VLA training ( Tencent Robotics X et al., 2026 ; Wang et al., 2026 ) , while concurrent systems unify understanding, generation, and action or simplify the VLA stack ( Cai et al., 2026a ; Ye et al., 2026b ) . A complementary line uses video and world modeling for control. LingBot-Vision pretrains dense spatial representations via masked boundary modeling ( Fu et al., 2026 ) , LingBot-Video scales MoE video pretraining on robot-oriented footage ( Ma et al., 2026 ) , causal video-action models such as LingBot-VA 2.0 predict scene evolution jointly with actions ( Li et al., 2026a ; Zhang et al., 2026a ) , world-action models integrate world prediction into policy learning ( Niu et al., 2026 ; Yuan et al., 2026a ; Lou et al., 2026 ) , and interactive or 3D world models serve as simulators and embodied data engines ( Gao et al., 2026b ; Team HY-World et al., 2026 ) , with Xiaomi-Robotics-U0 substantially improving the out-of-distribution success rate of π 0.5 through synthesized embodied data ( Li et al., 2026b ) . Across these foundation-model efforts, however, observations and pretraining corpora remain visual, linguistic, and proprioceptive, while contact is neither sensed nor standardized, and TouchGuide’s tactile contact model, which steers pretrained visuomotor policies at inference time, remains a rare exception ( Zhang et al., 2026b ) .

在感知方面，掩模重建、联合嵌入预测以及自监督视觉核心网络提供了可迁移的表示学习目标 (Assran et al., 2023; Oquab et al., 2023)。触觉观测数据则更难以复用，因为来自传感器的原始图像 GelSight (Yuan et al., 2017) 和 DIGIT (Lambeta et al., 2020) 与传感器特定的光学结构、几何形状及弹性体外观存在关联。先前的研究通过基于典型力的预训练 (Wu et al., 2025b)、传感器不变的接触几何结构 (Gupta et al., 2025)、动作感知的触觉潜变量 (Xu et al., 2025b; Xu et al., 2026)，或连续变形表示 (Zheng et al., 2026) 来减少这种依赖性。NeoData 则采用经过校准的 xx、-yy 和 zz 轴力作为数据集层面的交换格式，NeoForce 在此共享物理接口上进行学习，从而让不同的触觉演示能够支持统一的操作策略。

On the perception side, masked reconstruction, joint-embedding prediction, and self-supervised visual backbones provide transferable representation-learning objectives ( Assran et al., 2023 ; Oquab et al., 2023 ) . Tactile observations are harder to reuse, because raw images from sensors such as GelSight ( Yuan et al., 2017 ) and DIGIT ( Lambeta et al., 2020 ) entangle contact with sensor-specific optics, geometry, and elastomer appearance. Prior work reduces this dependence through canonical force-based pretraining ( Wu et al., 2025b ) , sensor-invariant contact geometry ( Gupta et al., 2025 ) , action-aware tactile latents ( Xu et al., 2025b ; Xu et al., 2026 ) , or continuous deformation representations ( Zheng et al., 2026 ) . NeoData instead uses a calibrated field of x -, y -, and z -axis forces as the dataset-level interchange format, and NeoForce learns over this shared physical interface so that heterogeneous tactile demonstrations can support a common manipulation policy.

2.4 本体操控基准测试

诸如 RLBench (James et al., 2020)、Meta-World (Yu et al., 2019)、CALVIN (Mees et al., 2022)、ManiSkill2 (Gu et al., 2023) 和 LIBERO (Liu et al., 2023) 等仿真套件能够标准化多任务处理、语言相关任务以及终身操作能力训练，而家庭规模的相关系统则进一步扩大了任务与场景的多样性 (Nasiriany et al., 2024; Nasiriany et al., 2026; Li et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2025)。然而，扰动研究表明，此类套件中接近饱和的得分可能反映出对训练期间所见的特定任务配置的过度拟合，而非对任务本身的真正理解 (Zhou et al., 2025; Fei et al., 2025)，这促使人们开始对通用策略的能力水平进行诊断 (Gao et al., 2026a)。因此，评估工作不得不依赖于物理硬件条件。 FurnitureBench (Heo et al., 2025) 实现了长距离现实场景组装的标准化处理；SIMPLER (Li et al., 2024) 则构建了能够预测实际性能的模拟双胞胎模型；而 RoboDojo (Chen et al., 2026b) 与 DuoBench (Jülg et al., 2026) 则依据统一协议将模拟任务与真实任务进行组合，这正是我们所采用的配对模拟与真实测试方法。然而，所有这些基准测试仅关注视觉与本体感知，因此接触质量既未被感知也未被评估。

Simulation suites such as RLBench ( James et al., 2020 ) , Meta-World ( Yu et al., 2019 ) , CALVIN ( Mees et al., 2022 ) , ManiSkill2 ( Gu et al., 2023 ) , and LIBERO ( Liu et al., 2023 ) standardize multi-task, language-conditioned, and lifelong manipulation, and household-scale successors further extend task and scene diversity ( Nasiriany et al., 2024 ; Nasiriany et al., 2026 ; Li et al., 2022 ; Zhang et al., 2025 ) . Perturbation studies show, however, that near-saturated scores on such suites can reflect overfitting to the specific task configurations seen during training rather than task understanding ( Zhou et al., 2025 ; Fei et al., 2025 ) , motivating capability-level diagnosis of generalist policies ( Gao et al., 2026a ) . Evaluation is therefore anchoring itself to physical hardware. FurnitureBench ( Heo et al., 2025 ) standardizes long-horizon real-world assembly, SIMPLER ( Li et al., 2024 ) builds simulated twins whose scores predict real performance, and RoboDojo ( Chen et al., 2026b ) and DuoBench ( Jülg et al., 2026 ) couple simulated and real task suites under shared protocols, which is the paired sim-and-real methodology that we adopt. All of these benchmarks, however, observe only vision and proprioception, so contact quality is neither sensed nor scored.

触觉操控基准测试仍然十分匮乏，因为仿真系统必须同时再现接触力学与传感器变形现象。Taxim (Si and Yuan, 2022)与TacSL (Akinola et al., 2025)分别模拟了特定视觉式触觉传感器的表现；UniVTAC与(Chen et al., 2026a)则通过八项任务基准测试实现了多传感器触觉仿真的统一；ContactWorld (Zhang et al., 2026c)在十二项仿真任务及一项真实任务上对视觉-触觉世界模型进行了评估；SoftVTBench (Jing et al., 2026)发现，某些策略在违反变形限制的情况下仍能取得成功，而整合触觉感知则会大幅降低其效果。这些研究虽显著扩展了触觉评估的范围，但其数据仍局限于特定传感器或仿真环境，因此所得到的协议无法在不同触觉硬件或实际部署场景中应用。 在我们的研究中，NeoReal 与 NeoSim 被配对用于策略评估，从而可以可重复地比较触觉反馈与表征选择，而无需将评估与任何特定传感器的外观相关联。

Tactile manipulation benchmarks remain scarce because simulation must reproduce both contact mechanics and sensor deformation. Taxim ( Si and Yuan, 2022 ) and TacSL ( Akinola et al., 2025 ) simulate the appearance of specific vision-based tactile sensors, UniVTAC ( Chen et al., 2026a ) unifies multi-sensor tactile simulation with an eight-task benchmark, ContactWorld ( Zhang et al., 2026c ) evaluates visuo-tactile world models on twelve simulated tasks and one real task, and SoftVTBench ( Jing et al., 2026 ) finds that policies often succeed while violating deformation limits, a failure that integrating tactile sensing substantially reduces. These efforts substantially expand tactile evaluation within their own scope, but their data remains tied to a particular sensor or confined to simulation, so the resulting protocols do not transfer across tactile hardware or to physical deployment. In our work, both NeoReal and NeoSim are paired for policy evaluation, so that tactile feedback and representation choices can be compared reproducibly without tying evaluation to the appearance of any particular sensor.

Fusing real-robot and 𝒩 0 -TacUMI demonstrations requires effectively integrating cross-embodiment data and unifying it into a consistent format. This motivates a multi-step cleaning pipeline that turns heterogeneous raw recordings into a complete, high-quality corpus suitable for model training. As illustrated in Fig. 5 , we summarize this process into a staged quality selection pipeline of five complementary checks.

数据完整性。

首次检查旨在确认每个片段均包含必要的视觉、触觉、动作及元数据流。缺失相应相机流、触觉流、动作流或元数据流的片段将被剔除，从而确保每个保留的序列都能形成完整的多模态记录。

The first check verifies that each episode contains the required visual, tactile, action, and metadata streams. Episodes that are missing a required camera, tactile, action, or metadata stream are removed so that each retained sequence provides a complete multimodal record.

运动质量。

第二个检查环节会识别可能破坏策略训练的运动伪影。我们会对每个episode的轨迹平滑度进行评估，并标记出诸如突然的跟踪脉冲和跟踪丢失片段等低质量区域，随后会移除这些伪影占主导的episode。

第四次检查用于验证记录下的视频流，并修复那些仍可修复的缺陷。我们逐一检查每个视频片段中的损坏内容、严重模糊、帧丢失以及异常照明情况。此外，我们将异步记录的多种模态数据对齐到统一的时间线中，确保视频、触觉及动作帧之间存在严格的一对一对应关系。对于诸如相机映射错误、左右臂标签错误以及末端执行器姿态异常等记录错误，在可能的情况下进行修复，例如通过重新映射相机或利用正向运动学技术恢复姿态，否则则将其舍弃。最后，我们删减那些几乎相同的静态片段，因为这些片段对应的动作不一致，会导致模型在推理过程中不必要的暂停。

The fourth check validates the recorded streams and repairs the defects that remain recoverable. We inspect every view for corrupted content, severe blur, dropped frames, and abnormal illumination. In addition, we align the asynchronously recorded modalities to a unified timeline with a strict one to one correspondence between video, tactile and action frames. Recording errors such as swapped camera mappings, mislabeled left and right arms, and abnormal end-effector poses are repaired when possible, for example by remapping the cameras or recovering the pose through forward kinematics, and are otherwise discarded. We finally trim near-static segments, whose nearly identical observations correspond to inconsistent actions and cause the model to pause unnecessarily at inference time.

转移可行性。

NeoData 中的大部分数据是在无机器人环境下采集的，因此所记录的动作无法保证在目标机器人上实际实现。因此，最终筛选阶段会对每条轨迹进行逆向运动学计算，并剔除无法解决或超出预期本体感知的关节及工作空间限制的姿态，从而保持 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TacUMI 数据集的可扩展性，同时确保所有保留的演示在迁移后仍能正常运行。通过所有五项检查的episode即为已发布的语料库，这些episode具有完整的模态、良好的训练条件、视觉有效性，并且可在目标本体上执行。

A large fraction of NeoData is collected robot-free, so the recorded motions are not guaranteed to be physically realizable on the target robots. The final check therefore runs inverse kinematics on each trajectory and rejects poses that fail to solve or that exceed the joint and workspace limits of the intended embodiment, which preserves the scalability of 𝒩 0 -TacUMI collection while keeping every retained demonstration executable after transfer. Episodes that pass all five checks constitute the released corpus, complete in modality, well conditioned for training, visually valid, and executable on the target embodiment.

4.5 数据标注

我们引入了一种自动标注流程，为每个操作序列分配四级层次结构。这些层级分别为任务（L3）、子任务（L2）、动作（L1）和原子片段（L0），如图 6 所示。该层次结构将语义结构与时间定位分离开来。模板定义了任务中应发生的内容，而基于信号的分割则决定了特定序列中各事件的发生时间。

We introduce an automatic annotation pipeline that assigns every manipulation episode a four-level hierarchy. The levels are task (L3), subtask (L2), action (L1), and atomic segment (L0), as shown in Fig. 6 . The hierarchy separates semantic structure from temporal localization. The template defines what should occur in a task, while signal-based segmentation determines when each event occurs in a specific episode.

该流程包含两个阶段。在第一阶段中，视觉语言模型将代表性场景汇总为与时间无关的任务模板，该模板记录了任务目标、有序的语义阶段以及动作级子步骤。在将其应用于完整任务类别之前，人类专家会验证该模板，以消除语义歧义。在第二阶段中，通过融合基于动作与视觉事件的边界，对每个场景进行分割。随后，模型根据验证后的模板对生成的固定区间进行标注，而确定性代码则用于构建 L0、L1、L2 和 L3 时间范围。这种设计使时间端点基于测量信号，同时利用语言模型进行语义标注，从而减少了长操作视频中的时间漂移现象。 通过将每个场景划分为有标签的子任务，该流程提供了训练长期视野、多阶段及多任务本体策略所需的时间结构。

The pipeline has two stages. In the first stage, a vision-language model (VLM) summarizes representative episodes into a time-independent task template, which records the task objective, ordered semantic phases, and action-level substeps. A human expert verifies the template to remove semantic ambiguity before it is applied to the full task category. In the second stage, each episode is segmented by fusing action-based and visual event boundaries. The model then labels the resulting fixed intervals according to the verified template, and deterministic code assembles the L0, L1, L2, and L3 temporal extents. This design keeps temporal endpoints grounded in measured signals while using language models for semantic labeling, which reduces temporal drift on long manipulation videos. By segmenting each episode into labeled subtasks, this pipeline supplies the temporal structure needed for training long-horizon, multi-stage, and multi-task embodied policies.

5 触觉表征

本节提出了一种触觉表征方法，该方法使触觉信息能够被纳入本体操控策略中，act。该方法将每一次触觉观测视为感知表面上的密集三轴力场，从而将接触现象表示为一个物理量，而非特定设备的外观特征。我们首先阐述该表征方法的动机与设计原则，随后定义该表征方法，描述生成力场的数据流程，最后介绍 NeoForce——该模型用于从这些力场中学习具有时间结构的触觉特征。

This section proposes a tactile representation that allows tactile information to act on embodied manipulation policies, describing every tactile observation as a dense three-axis force field over the sensing surface and thereby expressing contact as a physical quantity rather than as the appearance of a particular device. We first present the motivation and the design principles behind the representation, then define it, describe the data pipeline that produces force fields, and present NeoForce, the model used to learn temporally structured tactile features from these force fields.

5.1 动机

与基于电子像素形成稳定图像空间的视觉硬件不同，触觉硬件以信号形式呈现为多种不同的形态。光学凝胶传感器 (Yuan et al., 2017; Lambeta et al., 2020)、电容式阵列 (Schmitz et al., 2011; Maiolino et al., 2013) 以及压阻式皮肤 (Stassi et al., 2014) 在单元、分辨率和感知几何结构方面均存在差异，因此基于任意单一信号构建的下游表示与模型都受限于其产生设备，无法被移植到其他地方。因此，要实现触觉操作的扩展，必须将这些异构信号转换为触觉领域能够共享的通用形式。为了提供可转移的接口，通用触觉表示应满足三个原则。

Unlike visual hardware, which records a consistent image space based on electronic pixels, tactile hardware is fragmented in the form of the signal it emits. Optical gel sensors ( Yuan et al., 2017 ; Lambeta et al., 2020 ) , capacitive arrays ( Schmitz et al., 2011 ; Maiolino et al., 2013 ) , and piezoresistive skins ( Stassi et al., 2014 ) differ in units, resolution, and sensing geometry, so downstream representations and models built on any single signal form are bound to the device that produced it and cannot be carried elsewhere. Scaling tactile manipulation therefore requires converting these heterogeneous signals into one universal form that the tactile community can share. To provide a transferable interface, a universal tactile representation should satisfy three principles.

物理基础构建。

该表征应基于物理接触所传递的信息，而非基于外观层面的代理。在多种操作场景中，末端执行器对物体的传递效果完全由接触界面处的物理效应决定，因此其行为是通过内在属性来体现的，而非通过硬件测量到的接触方式来体现。

The representation should be anchored to what touch physically delivers rather than to an appearance-level proxy. Across various manipulation scenarios, what an end-effector delivers to an object is completely described by its physical effect at the contact interface, so its behavior is expressed by an intrinsic attribute rather than by how the contact is measured through the hardware.

跨传感器统一。

该表示应成为一个通用的空间，使不同的触觉设备能够利用该空间。通过适当的校准程序，来自任何传感器的信号都应能映射到同一目标空间，从而让不同的触觉数据能够直接比较并共同进行训练。

The representation should be a common space that different tactile devices can utilize. Signals from any sensor with an appropriate calibration procedure should be mappable into the same target space, which makes heterogeneous tactile data directly comparable and jointly trainable.

丰富的空间语义。

复杂的接触具有空间结构特征。接触斑的位置、形状及范围，以及其内部的强度变化情况，能够区分完整的抓取与边缘接触，也区分稳定握持与初始滑移，因此该表征必须保留这种分布特征，而非对其进行概括。

Complex contact is spatially structured. The location, shape, and extent of the contact patch, together with how intensity varies inside it, distinguish a full grasp from an edge contact and a stable hold from incipient slip, so the representation must preserve this distribution rather than summarize it.

5.2 定义

遵循上述三条原则，我们采用力作为触觉表征所编码的物理属性，因为它能够独立描述接触效果，而不依赖于测量传感器。在感测表面的每个点处，我们记录为两个垂直于触觉平面的平面分量，这些分量反映了推动滑动和抓取作用的剪切力，同时还有一个法向分量，用于反映按压过程中的压力。通过在高分辨率晶格上对这种三维力进行采样，可以得到一个密集的力场，该力场能够表达在感测表面发生的复杂接触情况，并保留接触强度的空间分布。具体来说，对于每一个触觉传感器在 tt 时刻，力场会呈现出来。

Following the three principles above, we adopt force as the physical attribute that the tactile representation encodes, since it describes the effect of contact independently of the sensor that measured it. At every point of the sensing surface we record the local force as two orthogonal in-plane components tangent to the tactile plane, which capture the shear that drives sliding and grasping, together with one normal component, which captures the pressure exerted during pressing. 5.5 触觉表征评估

我们评估 NeoForce，以回答两个问题。基于力的表征能否编码接触强度在空间和时间上的变化？换成不同操作任务时，模型学习的力场分布能否保持一致？首先，我们比较加入潜在表征预测和仅做重建两种训练目标。在留出的接触样本上，用平均绝对误差（MAE）和均方根误差（RMSE）评估重建的力，用平均交并比（mIoU）评估预测的接触区，分别判断力大小和接触定位的误差。随后按技能类别对重建结果分组，并与第 5.3 节流程产生的参考力场比较。

We evaluate NeoForce to figure out two essential problems, namely the effectiveness of the force-based representation on encoding contact intensity over space and time, and whether the distribution of the force space it learns stays consistent once the manipulation task varies. We first ablate the latent prediction objective against a reconstruction-only baseline and score held-out contacts with mean absolute error (MAE) and root mean squared error (RMSE) on the reconstructed force, together with mean intersection over union (mIoU) on the predicted contact region, so that errors in force magnitude and errors in contact localization are read separately. We then group the reconstructions by skill type and compare them with the ground truth force fields produced by the pipeline of Section 5.3 .

这些结果支持第 5.1 节的设计。基于力的表征编码了各类触觉信号共有的力学属性，又能用于多种具身操作，因此可以稳定承载触觉交互信息。我们希望其泛化能力还能进一步提升，让触觉信号提供与硬件无关、跨传感器一致且能由下游操作策略直接使用的信息。

Taken together, these results support the design of Section 5.1 . The force-based representation encodes the universal force attribute of touch and generalizes over a wide range of embodied manipulation behaviors, which makes it a stable carrier of tactile interaction. We expect this representation to generalize further, so that a tactile signal carries hardware-agnostic information that remains consistent across sensors and that downstream manipulation policies can consume directly.

In short, a policy benefits from the physical contact representation rather than from the device-specific appearance, and a sensor-agnostic force field provides a universal interface that delivers such a representation.

6.3 NeoSim

NeoSim 在仿真环境中构建，为大规模触觉感知评估提供了可重复的实验平台。继 UniVTAC 和 (Chen et al., 2026a) 之后，NeoSim 在仿真中执行高精度触觉数据收集，并以与第 5 节中统一表示方式相同的 xx、yy 和 zz 格式呈现每次接触力场。这种设计使得基于力输入的策略能够在仿真中通过与真实触觉数据相同的表示方式进行评估。

NeoSim is constructed in the simulation environment and provides a reproducible arena for tactile-aware evaluation at scale. Following UniVTAC ( Chen et al., 2026a ) , NeoSim performs high-precision tactile data collection in simulation and renders per-contact force fields in the same x , y , and z format as the unified representation in Section 5 . This design allows policies that consume force-based tactile inputs to be evaluated in simulation with the same representation used for real tactile data.

该套系统包含 1212 接触丰富的任务，包括 44 单臂任务 88 双臂任务。单臂任务包括倒球、拔掉插头并重新插上、插入USB接口以及抓取芯片，这些任务需要应对倒液、插入和精细抓取过程中的力量调节。双臂任务则包括插入螺丝、放置齿轮、拆开碗状容器、杯具传递、堆叠杯子、堆叠盘子、堆叠碗状容器以及拆开杯具，这些任务进一步需要双手协调、稳定的接触保持、杯具传递以及嵌套操作。这些任务涉及刚性物体、薄壁容器、易碎部件、可变形接触面以及狭窄间隙的组装。

The suite contains 12 contact-rich tasks, including 4 single-arm tasks and 8 dual-arm tasks. The single-arm tasks are Pour Ball, Unplug and Plug Charger, Plug USB, and Grasp Chip, which stress force regulation during pouring, insertion, and delicate grasping. The dual-arm tasks are Insert Screw, Place Gears, Unstack Bowl, Cup Handover, Stack Cups, Stack Plates, Stack Bowls, and Unstack Cup, which further require bimanual coordination, stable contact maintenance, handover, and nesting. These tasks span rigid objects, thin-walled containers, fragile parts, deformable contact, and tight-clearance assembly.

NeoSim 旨在补充 NeoReal，通过提供可控、可重复且大规模的评价实验，而此类实验在物理硬件上难以实现。它使得不同策略、触觉输入选择及表示方式在大规模接触动态下能够进行可控比较。NeoSim 的详细任务描述，以及 RGB 观测结果和触觉力场的示例，均载于附录 F 中。

NeoSim is intended to complement NeoReal by providing controlled, reproducible, and large-scale evaluation that is difficult to obtain on physical hardware. It enables controlled comparisons across policy families, tactile input choices, and representation choices under large-scale contact dynamics. A detailed per-task description of NeoSim, together with rendered examples of RGB observations and tactile force fields, is provided in Appendix F .

We evaluate on NeoSim how current policy families handle contact-rich manipulation in a reproducible simulated setting. Using the same policy families and NeoData pretraining as above, NeoSim reports success rate over 100 randomized rollouts per task. Tab. 4 reports the NeoSim results. π 0.5 leads with a mean success rate of 45.8 % , followed by LingBot-VA at 32.1 % , while Xiaomi-Robotics-0 and StarVLA- α form a middle group near 23 % and the remaining baselines stay below 11 % . The dual-arm tasks are consistently more difficult than the single-arm tasks. Averaged over the four single-arm tasks, every policy scores higher than it does over the eight dual-arm tasks, and the drop is severe for most of them, from 49.3 % to 10.1 % for StarVLA- α , from 42.0 % to 14.1 % for Xiaomi-Robotics-0, and from 23.8 % to 1.0 % for InternVLA-A1. Only π 0.5 and LingBot-VA retain substantial bimanual competence, at 34.3 % and 30.0 % , respectively, and they do so almost entirely on Plate Stack and Bowl Stack, where the two arms can act in a loosely coupled manner. The tasks that demand sustained mutual contact remain nearly unsolved for every policy, with the best score reaching only 18 % on Place Gears and 25 % on Cup Handover, and on each of these two tasks at most two of the seven policies succeed at all. Fast-WAM fails on all twelve tasks despite being one of the three strongest policies on NeoReal, which indicates that its competence does not transfer to the simulated contact dynamics. These results expose contact-rich bimanual manipulation as the main bottleneck for current policy families that rely primarily on visual observations, which reveal little about whether a grasp is secure and whether contact is stably maintained. In short, tactile signals make grasp security and contact stability directly observable to the policy, which is exactly the information that contact-rich bimanual manipulation currently fails on. This in turn strengthens the necessity of equipping robots with tactile hardware and of policies that consume a tactile representation.

7 结论

我们提出了 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -Foundation，这是一个集成了触觉功能的本体操控综合资源，涵盖了硬件、数据、表征及评估方面。该报告还提供了 NeoData 数据集，这是一个大规模多模态数据集，包含六种本体形式下的超过 30,00030{,}000 小时同步视觉与触觉演示数据，以及 450+450+ 任务数据，这些数据是通过真实机器人遥操作与 UMI 演示相结合的方式收集的。此外，还提供了 NeoForce 工具，用于将传感器信号转换为触觉表征，以便用于下游本体模型。最后，我们提出了一种标准化评估方法，结合真实世界的 NeoReal 套件与模拟的 NeoSim 套件，以进行富含接触操作的标准化评估。 实验表明，触觉反馈能够提升富含接触感的操控能力；潜在监督机制能够促进时间维度的触觉表征学习；而 NeoForce 力表征则提供了一种简洁的物理替代方案，以取代原始触觉图像。

We presented 𝒩 0 -Foundation, an integrated resource for tactile-enabled embodied manipulation that spans hardware, data, representation, and evaluation. The report contributes NeoData, a large-scale multimodal dataset with more than 30,000 hours of synchronized visual and tactile demonstrations across six embodiments and 450 + tasks, collected through a mixture of real-robot teleoperation and UMI demonstrations. It also contributes NeoForce, which convert sensor signals into tactile representation for downstream embodied models. Finally, we propose a standardized evaluation that combines the real-world NeoReal suite and the simulated NeoSim suite for standardized evaluation of contact-rich manipulation. The experiments indicate that tactile feedback improves contact-rich manipulation, that latent supervision improves temporal tactile representation learning, and that the NeoForce force representation provides a compact physical alternative to raw tactile images.

仍有多个研究方向有待探索。当前统一的表征方法主要针对平行下颌视觉触觉手指，将其推广到灵巧手及非相机式换能器上，将能进一步拓宽其应用范围。将策略学习扩展至完整的多模态语料库，并加强 NeoSim 与实际部署之间的关联，是自然的后续步骤。NeoData 及其表征与基准测试，为开发更强大且可迁移的触觉感知操控策略提供了基础。

Several directions remain open. The unified representation currently targets parallel-jaw visuo-tactile fingers, and extending it to dexterous hands and non-camera-based transducers would broaden its applicability. Scaling policy learning to the full multimodal corpus and tightening the connection between NeoSim and real deployment are natural next steps. NeoData, together with its representation and benchmark, provides a foundation for more capable and transferable tactile-aware manipulation policies.

Every episode is a synchronized, temporally aligned stream of observations and actions. 附录 B 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TacUMI 细节

收集装置。

在 NeoData 中不含机器人的部分，与第 3.3 节所介绍的 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TacUMI设备相结合。该设备是一种手持式平行夹持器，配备了两个触觉传感器、一个用于六自由度姿态的红外跟踪器，以及一个用于检测手指间距的磁编码器。所有数据流均按照统一时钟同步记录于 3030 fps，且相同的传感器和架构也被用于遥操作机器人，从而使两种运行模式能够直接互操作。

The robot-free portion of NeoData is gathered with the 𝒩 0 -TacUMI device introduced in Section 3.3 , a handheld parallel gripper carrying two tactile sensors, an infrared tracker for six-degree-of-freedom pose, and a magnetic encoder for finger separation. All streams are recorded synchronously at 30 fps against a common clock, and the same sensors and schema are reused on the teleoperated robots so that the two regimes are directly interoperable.

动作。

在每个时间步中，跟踪器报告夹持器姿态 Pt∈SE⁡(3)P_{t}\in\mathrm{SE}(3)，而磁编码器则报告夹持器宽度 gtg_{t}。我们并非将绝对姿态作为目标进行存储，而是记录相对于前一步帧的变换 kk，再定义为 Δ​Pt=Pt−1​Pt+k\Delta P_{t}=P_{t}^{-1}P_{t+k}，并将该动作定义为 at=(Δ​Pt,gt+k)a_{t}=(\Delta P_{t},g_{t+k})，从而将相对末端执行器运动与指令的夹持器宽度进行关联。这种相对姿态约定对世界坐标系具有不变性，能够支持不同本体之间的转换。

At each timestep the tracker reports the gripper pose P t ∈ SE ⁡ ( 3 ) and the magnetic encoder reports the gripper width g t . Rather than storing absolute poses as targets, we record the relative transform to a frame k steps ahead, Δ ​ P t = P t − 1 ​ P t + k , and define the action as a t = ( Δ ​ P t , g t + k ) , pairing the relative end-effector motion with the commanded gripper width. This relative pose convention is invariant to the world frame and supports transfer across embodiments.

附录 C 标注流程细节

我们构建了一个半自动分层标注流程，该流程通过四层时间嵌套结构对每帧机器人操作视频进行标注：任务层（L3）、子任务层（L2）、动作层（L1）以及原子段（L0）。该流程分为两个阶段运行：任务模板生成阶段与每集视频标注阶段。

We build a semi-automatic hierarchical annotation pipeline that labels every robot manipulation video with a four-level, temporally nested structure of task (L3), subtask (L2), action (L1), and atomic segment (L0). The pipeline runs in two stages, a task template generation stage and a per-episode annotation stage.

任务模板构建

对于每个任务类别，我们首先利用 VLM Gemini- 3.5 -Flash 的视频总结能力，从几集代表性视频中生成一个与时间无关的任务模板。该模板明确了任务所共享的 L3 总体目标、有序的 L2 语义阶段集合以及每个阶段的固定 L1 子步骤。随后由人类专家对模板进行审核并确认。这一审核是整个流程中唯一的手动质量控制步骤，能够消除因同一任务的不同视频版本而产生的语义表述差异。

For each task category, we first use the video summarization capability of a VLM, Gemini-3.5-Flash, to generate a time-independent task template from a few representative episodes. The template specifies the L3 overall objective shared by the task, an ordered set of L2 semantic phases, and the fixed L1 substeps under each phase. A human expert then reviews and confirms this template. This review is the only manual quality control step in the entire pipeline, and it removes the semantic wording drift that would otherwise arise across different episodes of the same task.

片段分割

在模板确认后，我们按“边界优先、标签其次”的方式对每个场景进行标注。最低级别的L0原子区间首先由两个并行分支界定，这两个分支基于两种互补的模式进行运作。动作信号分支根据末端执行器和夹持器的运动信号以及我们自收集的触觉信号，确定一系列物理上可识别的原子动作边界。这些边界包括抓取边界——其判断标准是夹持器从开启状态转变为闭合状态，同时触觉信号急剧上升，表明物体已被握住；其余边界则采用类似的判断标准；此外还有释放边界、运动开始与停止边界、轴切换边界、垂直反转边界以及接触与分离边界。每个边界都精确对应机械状态发生变化的时刻。 视觉边界分支应用了无监督通用事件边界检测（GEBD）技术 Shou et al. (2021) ，一种能够从视觉流中识别事件边界的方法。我们对视频进行子采样处理 44 fps , 提取 ResNet- 50 He et al. (2016) 在 ImageNet 上预训练的特征 Deng et al. (2009) ，并在每个时间步长中，通过余弦距离来衡量，比较各特征的平均值之间的可预测性 55 前文及后续内容 55 采用以下框架，以语义突变点处的局部最大值作为边界。该分支能够捕捉控制信号无法编码的感知动作转换，例如从折叠状态转换为擦拭状态的过程，这种转换并不具有明显的机械性间断。 这两个边界集合随后被融合并去重，总段数被限制在 2424 以内，同时保留了细粒度特性，最终得到一个时间固定且划分更精确的 L0 原子区间集合。

After the template is confirmed, we annotate each episode in a boundary-first, label-second order. The lowest level L0 atomic intervals are first delimited by two parallel branches that operate on two complementary modalities. The action signal branch locates a set of physically grounded atomic action boundaries from the kinematic signals of the end effector and gripper together with our self-collected tactile signals. These include the grasp boundary, whose criterion is that the gripper transitions from open to closed while the tactile signal rises sharply, indicating that an object is now held, with the remaining boundaries using analogous criteria, as well as the release boundary, the motion start and stop boundaries, the axis switch boundary, the vertical reversal boundary, and the contact and separation boundaries. Each boundary is aligned precisely to the moment at which the mechanical state changes. The visual boundary branch applies unsupervised Generic Event Boundary Detection (GEBD) Shou et al. (2021) , a method that perceives event boundaries from the visual stream. We subsample the video at 4 fps, extract ResNet-50 He et al. (2016) features pretrained on ImageNet Deng et al. (2009) , and at each timestep compare the predictability, measured by cosine distance, between the average features of the 5 preceding and the 5 following frames, taking local maxima at points of abrupt semantic change as boundaries. This branch captures perceptual action transitions that the control signals cannot encode, such as the transition from folding to wiping that carries no clear mechanical discontinuity. The two boundary sets are then fused and deduplicated, and the total number of segments is capped at 24 while fine granularity is preserved, yielding a temporally fixed and more accurately partitioned set of L0 atomic intervals.

分层标注

最后，我们让同一款 VLM——Gemini- 3.5 -Flash——仅对这些预分割区间进行标注。它将每个 L0 段映射到模板中的相应 L1 和 L2 层级，并生成相关描述；随后，确定性代码从下往上组装出 L1、L2 和 L3 层级的时间范围。所有层级的时间端点均来自信号计算，而非 VLM 推理，因此这种设计能够确保各层级时间区间的精确性，并从根本上避免 VLM 在长视频处理中的时间漂移问题。

Finally, we let the same VLM, Gemini-3.5-Flash, label only these pre-segmented intervals. It assigns each L0 segment to the corresponding L1 and L2 level in the template and generates the associated description, after which deterministic code assembles the temporal extents of the L1, L2, and L3 levels from the bottom up. The temporal endpoints at all levels come from signal computation rather than VLM inference, so this design guarantees the accuracy of the temporal intervals at every level and fundamentally avoids the temporal drift of VLMs on long videos.

D. 1 真实力校准数据

We collect real tactile data and calibrate the applied force to build the training data for the tactile model. The calibration corpus is produced by a controlled annotation and curation pipeline rather than by uncalibrated in the wild demonstrations. As the acquisition source, a six-degree-of-freedom robot arm presses an indenter against the tactile surface while a six axis force and torque sensor records the reference load under a quasi-static protocol, in which the indenter is held stationary once it reaches a target depth so that the tactile image and the measured force are captured in a stable state. Under automated control, the indenter traverses the entire sensing plane at penetration depths from 1 to 6 ​ mm in 1 ​ mm increments, and shear supervision is enriched by tilting the indenter through rotation angles from 1 ∘ to 20 ∘ to cover diverse horizontal loads. Each curated sample takes the form of an optical flow image paired with a pixel aligned multi dimensional force field, where the two in-plane components are combined into a resultant shear stress and the normal component records pressure. The pipeline yields approximately 4,300 optical flow and multi dimensional force pairs, which are randomly shuffled and split into training, validation, and test partitions with an 80 % , 10 % , and 10 % ratio, with the test partition held out exclusively for final evaluation.

The tactile image to force field converter adopts GoogLeNet ( Szegedy et al., 2015 ) as the backbone, whose inception modules aggregate multiscale features at a controlled computational cost, and modifies the output layer to regress the three-axis per-pixel force field that aligns with the shear and pressure targets. The converter is trained on a mixture of the real calibration corpus and the simulation augmented data described above, so that it benefits from both physically measured contact and the broader shape diversity of simulation. Following the reference training protocol, the model is implemented in PyTorch and optimized with AdamW using a batch size of 25 , an initial learning rate of 0.001 decayed by a factor of 10 every 10 epochs, for a total of 50 epochs. The objective combines an L 1 per-pixel force regression loss with an L 2 contact segmentation loss, each normalized by the number of valid contact pixels so that the loss reflects the average per-pixel error and is robust to the varying size of the contact region. As reported in Fig. 13 , the converter regresses force accurately in the real setting, reaching R 2 = 0.950 with RMSE = 0.310 for pressure and R 2 = 0.957 with RMSE = 0.091 for shear, and it remains reliable on the simulated data, reaching R 2 = 0.884 with RMSE = 0.696 for pressure and R 2 = 0.910 with RMSE = 0.163 for shear. The consistently high linearity and low error across both domains and both force components confirm that the converter performs effective force regression and can serve as the annotation model that labels the large-scale tactile corpus with physically meaningful force fields.

We train NeoForce on 20,000 real-robot and 𝒩 0 -TacUMI demonstrations spanning 30 tasks and evaluate it on 2,500 held-out episodes, which endows the model with broad contact-rich manipulation priors. Each training sample is a chunk of synchronized RGB observations and tactile force fields, and the chunk length is set to 4 in our experiments. The visual and tactile streams are patchified independently and then jointly processed by a shared transformer backbone, for which we adopt a ViT-B initialized from DINOv2 pretrained weights, on top of which sit two prediction heads. The binary success criterion in Appendix E scores a trial as 1 only when the full task goal is reached, which discards all information about how far a policy has progressed before failing. To provide a finer view of partial competence, we additionally define a task progressive score for every NeoReal task. Each task is decomposed into ordered sub-goals that follow its manipulation description, and a fixed number of points is awarded at each key milestone, so that the points accumulate monotonically as the task advances and sum to 100 at full completion. Tab. 5 lists the decomposition and the points assigned to each milestone, one row per task. Unless otherwise noted, a milestone is credited only if all earlier milestones in the same row have been achieved, and the reported progressive score of a trial is the total points of the last milestone reached.

根据表 5，每次部署的得分取决于其达到的最远里程碑。我们依次遍历任务的各个里程碑并累计分数，直到执行不再进展或触发第 E.4 节中的任一失败条件为止，此时该部署的累计得分即为总积分。完成任务的部署将通过所有里程碑，因此获得全部 100100 分；而中途失败的部署则仅获得其已完成的里程碑分数。图 10 中报告的每项任务累计得分，即为 2020 评估试验中各项部署得分的平均值。 成功的试验总能达成所有里程碑，并贡献最多的 100100 点；而部分完成的试验仍会贡献正点。因此，任务的逐步得分始终至少与其二元成功率相当，从而揭示出仅通过成功率无法体现的部分能力。

Following Tab. 5 , every rollout is scored by the furthest milestone it reaches. We walk through the ordered milestones of the task and accumulate their points until the execution stops making progress or triggers one of the failure conditions in Section E.4 , and the progressive score of that rollout is the total accumulated points. A successful trial always attains all milestones and contributes the maximum 100 points, while partially completed trials still contribute positive points, so the progressive score of a task is always at least as large as its binary success rate and exposes the partial competence that the success rate alone hides.

E. 3 NeoReal 训练设置

NeoReal 策略遵循预训练后训练的流程。在预训练阶段，每个策略都在大量数据上进行预训练 400,000400{,}000 来自episode NeoData 获取广泛且富含接触信息的操控先验。在训练后阶段，预训练的策略会被专门化至每个任务。 NeoReal 任务执行 300300 为该任务专门收集的演示数据。

NeoReal policies follow a pretrain then post train recipe. In the pretraining stage, each policy is pretrained on over 400,000 episodes from NeoData to acquire broad contact-rich manipulation priors. In the post-training stage, the pretrained policy is specialized to each NeoReal task using 300 demonstrations that are collected specifically for that task.

所有策略与基线模型均在 8×8\times NVIDIA A100 GPU 上训练。第三人称外部视图与手腕视图均作为视觉输入，所有任务中的动作均以末端执行器姿态进行参数化。单臂任务在 Flexiv Rizon 4 机械臂上执行，而双臂任务则使用 Piper 或 ARX X5 机械臂执行。

All policies and baselines are trained on 8 × NVIDIA A100 GPUs. Both the third-person external view and the wrist view are provided as visual inputs, and actions are parameterized as end-effector poses for all tasks. Single-arm tasks are executed on a Flexiv Rizon 4s arm, while dual-arm tasks are executed on Piper or ARX X5 arms.

E. 4 NeoReal 评估设置

每个任务均通过 2020 次试验进行评估。在每次试验开始时，我们将机器人臂重置为在限定范围内选取的初始姿态，从而确保评估过程具有可控的随机性，并测试其在初始配置下的泛化能力。被操控物体的位置同样在其自身范围内随机化，从而增强物体布局的空间泛化性。

Each task is evaluated over 20 trials. At the start of every trial we reset the robot arm to an initial pose sampled within a bounded range, so that the evaluation carries a controlled degree of randomization and tests generalization over the starting configuration. The positions of the manipulated assets are likewise randomized within their own ranges, adding spatial generalization to the object layout.

在试验中，如果机械臂达到关节极限、移出工作空间边界、进入自锁状态或与环境发生碰撞，我们将该运行视为执行失败。如果机械臂重复相同动作超过 55 次仍未进展，则视为任务推进失败。如果任务在 55 分钟内未完成，则视为超时失败。只有当机械臂在附录 E.2 中的任务进度得分规定的所有里程碑均完成，且未触发上述任何失败情况时，试验才被视为成功。我们报告了 2020 次试验的平均成功率。

During a trial, we count the run as an execution failure if the arm reaches a joint limit, moves out of the workspace boundary, enters a self-locking configuration, or becomes stuck against or collides with the environment. If the arm repeats the same action more than 5 times without progress, we count it as a task progression failure. If the task is not completed within 5 minutes, we count it as a timeout failure. A trial is scored as a success only when the arm completes all milestones of the task defined in the task progressive score of Appendix E.2 without triggering any of the above failures. We report the mean success rate across the 20 trials.

F. 1 仿真平台

NeoSim 建立在 UniVTAC 和 (Chen et al., 2026a) 的基础之上，后者是一个基于 Isaac Sim 和 Isaac Lab 的视觉-触觉仿真平台；(Mittal et al., 2023) 的触觉层由 TacEx (Nguyen et al., 2024) 提供。传感器凝胶被模拟为具有增量势接触的有限元软体，并输出触觉 RGB 图像、标记运动数据以及凝胶深度图。在此平台之上，我们还模拟了无标记粒子凝胶表面，其中密集的斑点涂层通过有限元表面位移进行传播，其切向分量来自标记运动数据，法向分量来自凝胶深度图。我们还会从接触求解器中得出每个顶点的接触力，并将其插值到密集力场中，该力场作为 NeoSim 中的触觉真实值。

NeoSim is built on UniVTAC ( Chen et al., 2026a ) , a visuo-tactile simulation platform based on Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab ( Mittal et al., 2023 ) whose tactile layer is provided by TacEx ( Nguyen et al., 2024 ) . The sensor gel is simulated as a finite element soft body with incremental potential contact and outputs tactile RGB images, marker motion, and gel depth maps. On top of this platform, we additionally simulate a markerless particle gel surface, where a dense speckle coating is advected by the finite element surface displacement, whose tangential component is taken from marker motion and whose normal component is taken from the gel depth map. We also derive per-vertex contact forces from the contact solver and interpolate them into the dense force field that serves as the tactile ground truth in NeoSim.

每个任务均从模块化的配置文件中实例化，该文件规定了任务资源、初始物体布局、随机化范围以及记录下的观测数据流。每个episode中的初始物体姿态会在任务特定的范围内进行随机化。成功与否则依据任务特定的几何标准来判定。例如，在插入任务中，只有当轴向对齐、径向偏移和插入深度均达到阈值时，才视为成功。

Each task is instantiated from a modular configuration file that specifies the task assets, the initial object layout, the randomization ranges, and the recorded observation streams. Initial object poses are randomized per episode within task-specific ranges. Success is judged by task-specific geometric criteria. For insertion tasks, for example, a trial counts as successful only when the axial alignment, the radial offset, and the insertion depth all reach their thresholds.

F. 2 单臂任务

这四个单臂任务均通过单臂执行，并注重力调节、插入精度以及无损伤抓取能力。

The four single-arm tasks are executed with a single arm and stress force regulation, insertion precision, and damage-free grasping.

针对球状物体。机械臂抓取一个装有小球的杯子，并将小球倾倒在目标盘上。杯子具有弹性，小球在运动过程中会移动，因此策略必须保持稳定的抓取力，并调节倾倒角度。粒子凝胶渲染技术揭示了杯中物质如何作用于手指，这是仅凭视觉无法观察到的。

Pour Ball. The arm grasps a cup filled with small balls and pours them onto a target plate. The cup is compliant and the balls shift during motion, so the policy must maintain a stable grasp force and modulate the pouring angle, and the particle gel rendering reveals how the contents load the fingers, which vision alone cannot observe.

断开与重新连接充电器。机械臂将充电器连接器从插座中脱离，然后重新插入。无论是释放还是插入动作，都依赖于对接触力及连接瞬间的微小电阻的感知，因此这本质上是一种由力引导的对接任务。

Unplug and Plug Charger. The arm disconnects a charger connector from a socket and inserts it again. Both the release and the insertion depend on sensing the seating force and the small resistance at the moment of connection, making this a canonical force-guided mating task.

插入 USB 线。这是一个高精度插入任务，其中机械臂需对准并将 USB 连接器插入其接口中。由于间隙极小，成功的关键在于在连接器卡住之前通过触觉感知到边缘接触及微小的偏移。

Plug USB. A precision peg-in-hole task in which the arm aligns and inserts a USB connector into its port. The tight-clearance means that success hinges on tactile detection of edge contact and small misalignment before the connector jams.

抓取芯片。机械臂抓取一片薄而脆弱的芯片，并将其提起，同时避免其碎裂或掉落。这一任务定义了一个狭窄的力窗口：力太小会导致芯片滑落，而力过大则会损坏芯片，因此直接涉及精细的力限制机制。

Grasp Chip. The arm grasps a thin, fragile chip and lifts it without crushing or dropping it. This task defines a narrow force window, since too little force lets the chip slip while too much force damages it, so it directly probes fine-grained force limiting.

F. 3 双臂任务

这八项双臂任务需要使用两只手臂，并需测试在交接、插入和堆叠过程中的双手协调能力及稳定接触。

The eight dual-arm tasks require two arms and additionally test bimanual coordination and stable contact during handover, insertion, and stacking.

插入螺丝。一只手臂固定套筒，另一只手臂则将其插入并旋入螺丝。需要两只手臂的协同力感知，以保持部件对齐，并检测旋入时的正确定位。

Insert Screw. One arm stabilizes a sleeve while the other inserts and threads a screw into it. Coordinated force sensing on both arms is needed to keep the parts aligned and to detect proper seating during threading.

安装齿轮。机械臂抓取齿轮，并将其套在固定销上。触觉反馈可确认每个齿轮都紧密地固定在销上，而非靠在边缘，这种状态在视觉上具有模糊性。

Place Gears. The arms pick up gears and thread them onto holder pegs. Tactile feedback confirms that each gear is seated flush against the peg rather than resting on an edge, a state that is visually ambiguous.

解压碗结构。一只手臂抓住上层碗的边缘，将其堆叠起来，同时另一只手则支撑起剩余的堆叠物。通过触摸感知薄边至关重要，因为接触面较小，容易被视觉忽略。

Unstack Bowl. One arm grips the rim of the top bowl in a nested stack and lifts it clear while the other stabilizes the remaining stack. Detecting the thin rim through touch is decisive, since the contact surface is small and easily missed by vision.

杯状物传递。一只手臂握住杯子，并将其递给另一只手臂，后者再将其放置。在传递过程中，两只手臂需感知到共享接触点，以确保杯子在传递过程中既不会掉落，也不会被压碎。

Cup Handover. One arm grasps a cup and hands it to the second arm, which then places it. The handover moment requires both arms to sense the shared contact so that the cup is neither dropped nor crushed during the transfer.

堆叠杯子。两个独立的杯子被组合在一起，并按中心对齐的方式堆叠。策略需确保杯子能够正确排列，并在一个杯子进入另一个杯子时感知到接触点。

Stack Cups. Two separated cups are brought together and nested into a centered stack. The policy must align the cups and sense the seating contact as one cup enters the other.

堆叠板。两个独立的板被组合成处于中心位置的堆叠结构。其薄而平坦的几何形状使得确认其处于中心且紧密接触的状态对于稳定的堆叠至关重要。

Stack Plates. Two separated plates are combined into a centered stack. The thin, flat geometry makes tactile confirmation of centered, flush contact important for a stable stack.

堆叠碗。将两个独立的碗组合在一起以实现居中嵌套。与盘子类似，通过触觉对齐确保碗能够同心地放置，而非偏置。

Stack Bowls. Two separated bowls are brought together for centered nesting. As with plates, tactile alignment ensures the bowls seat concentrically rather than off-center.

解套杯结构。一只手臂操控嵌套杯对中的顶部杯子，另一只手臂则负责支撑底部。嵌套杯的紧密配合增加了策略必须感知并克服的分离力，这与较小规模的解套碗结构类似。

Unstack Cup. One arm manipulates the top cup of a nested cup pair while the other stabilizes the base. The tight fit of nested cups increases the separation force the policy must sense and overcome, mirroring Unstack Bowl at a smaller scale.

F. 4 NeoSim 训练设置

这些演示数据由基于 cuRobo 运动规划器 (Sundaralingam et al., 2023) 编写的策略自动收集。只有当规划器成功完成任务且最终状态通过几何有效性检查时，该片段才会被保留，收集过程会持续进行，直至每个任务获得 100100 级别的成功案例。所有评估过的策略均在同一 100100 演示数据上进行训练。每个原始案例都保存了同步的外部与手腕 RGB 视图、双指触觉信号以及关节与末端执行器状态，从而能够将演示数据转换为各策略的原生输入格式——例如某些基线模型的关节空间动作，或另一些模型的末端执行器姿态与关节状态的组合形式。每个任务与策略组合均在 4×4\times NVIDIA A100 GPU 上完成训练。

Demonstrations are collected automatically by scripted expert policies built on the cuRobo motion planner ( Sundaralingam et al., 2023 ) . An episode is kept only when the planner succeeds and the final state passes the geometric success check, and collection continues until 100 successful episodes are obtained per task. All evaluated policies are trained on the same 100 demonstrations for each task. Every raw episode stores synchronized external and wrist RGB views, the two finger tactile streams, and both joint and end-effector states, so the demonstrations can be converted into the native input format of each policy, such as joint space actions for some baselines and end-effector poses combined with joint states for others. Each task-and-policy combination is trained on 4 × NVIDIA A100 GPUs.

F. 5 NeoSim 评估设置

每个任务均通过由 100100 固定随机种子生成的 100100 测试实例进行评估，这些种子与用于演示集生成的种子互不重复，因此所有策略面对相同的初始配置。当任务成功、提前停止条件被触发或步数限制被达到时，测试实例即终止，而步数限制根据任务难度进行个性化设置。所报告的指标为 100100 测试实例的平均成功率。策略推理在 NVIDIA RTX 4090 GPU 上运行，并通过远程推理接口与模拟器进行通信，该接口将策略服务与模拟过程分离，使得评估可以在相同环境配置下在多台机器上部署。

Each task is evaluated over 100 rollouts generated from 100 fixed random seeds that are disjoint from the seeds used for demonstration collection, so all policies face the same set of initial configurations. A rollout terminates when the task succeeds, when an early stop condition is triggered, or when the step limit is reached, and this limit is set per-task according to its difficulty. The reported metric is the mean success rate over the 100 rollouts. Policy inference runs on NVIDIA RTX 4090 GPUs and communicates with the simulator through a remote inference interface, which decouples policy serving from simulation and allows evaluations to be deployed across multiple machines under an identical environment configuration.
