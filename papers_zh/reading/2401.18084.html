将触觉与一切相连
统一多模态触觉表征学习

摘要

将触觉与其他模态建立联系，对人类和计算系统都意义重大。触觉数据的采集成本高，传感器输出也缺乏统一标准，使得包含触觉的多模态学习仍然困难。我们提出 UniTouch，这是一个面向光学触觉传感器的统一模型，可将触觉与视觉、语言和声音等模态关联。具体做法是将 UniTouch 的嵌入与预训练图像嵌入对齐，后者已经与多种其他模态建立关联。我们还引入按传感器分别学习的 tokens，使模型能同时利用多种异构触觉传感器的数据。UniTouch 可以在 zero-shot 条件下完成多种触觉任务，包括预测机器人抓取结果和回答关于触觉图像的问题。 据我们所知，UniTouch 是首个展现这些能力的模型。项目主页为 https://cfeng16.github.io/UniTouch/。

The ability to associate touch with other modalities has huge implications for humans and computational systems. However, multimodal learning with touch remains challenging due to the expensive data collection process and non-standardized sensor outputs. We introduce UniTouch, a unified tactile model for vision-based touch sensors connected to multiple modalities, including vision, language, and sound. We achieve this by aligning our UniTouch embeddings to pretrained image embeddings already associated with a variety of other modalities. We further propose learnable sensor-specific tokens, allowing the model to learn from a set of heterogeneous tactile sensors, all at the same time. UniTouch is capable of conducting various touch sensing tasks in the zero-shot setting, from robot grasping prediction to touch image question answering. To the best of our knowledge, UniTouch is the first to demonstrate such capabilities. Project Page: https://cfeng16.github.io/UniTouch/ .

1 引言

在我们的五种主要感官中，触觉感知对人类生存而言或许最为关键，因为它在感知物理接触方面发挥着重要作用，其重要性甚至可与视觉相媲美。 [46, 79, 73] 我们形成触觉与其他感官之间跨模态关联的能力 [91] 因此，它构成了我们诸多身体能力的基础。例如，在触摸之前，我们通过视觉预测表面触感；在撞击之前，我们通过触觉预测物体的声音。这些跨模态关联也是计算系统的重要组成部分，例如机器人操作系统。 [6, 68, 85, 107, 114, 75, 65, 83, 116, 8, 84] , 材料与几何形状估计 [111, 119, 38, 10] , 辅助技术 [42] ，以及纹理识别 [118, 78, 50] .

Amongst our five main senses, touch sensing is perhaps the most crucial to human survival, due to its role in perceiving physical contact — rivaling even vision in its overall importance [ 46 , 79 , 73 ] . Our ability to form cross-modal associations between touch and our other senses [ 91 ] thus underlies a great deal of our physical capabilities. For example, we predict from vision how a surface will feel before we touch it, and we predict from touch how an object will sound before we strike it. These cross-modal associations are also a key component of computational systems, such as for robotic manipulation [ 6 , 68 , 85 , 107 , 114 , 75 , 65 , 83 , 116 , 8 , 84 ] , material and geometry estimation [ 111 , 119 , 38 , 10 ] , assistive technology [ 42 ] , and texture recognition [ 118 , 78 , 50 ] .

尽管具有重要性，与触觉与其他模式之间的跨模态关联，相比视觉、语言和声音等其他模式，在多模态研究领域受到的关注却要少得多。触觉的获取成本很高 [111, 30, 32] 由于需要通过触摸传感器主动探测物体，这限制了用于训练触觉“基础”模型的数据收集规模。此外，触摸传感器尚未完全标准化，因此不同传感器的输出存在较大差异 [31, 121] . 即便在常用的视觉传感器中，机械设计与弹性材料上的差异也会导致不同的伪影现象，从而限制其泛化能力 Fig. 2 ). 因此，现有的触觉表征通常受限于单一传感器。

Despite their importance, cross-modal associations between touch and other modalities have received considerably less attention from the multimodal research community than those of other modalities, such as vision, language, and sound. Touch is expensive to acquire [ 111 , 30 , 32 ] as it requires actively probing objects with touch sensors, limiting the scale of data collected for training tactile “foundation” models. Moreover, touch sensors are not fully standardized, and thus there are large differences between outputs of different sensors [ 31 , 121 ] . Even amongst the commonly used vision-based sensors, the difference in mechanical design and elastomeric material will lead to divergent artifacts, limiting generalization ( Fig. 2 ). As a result, existing tactile representations are typically constrained to a single sensor.

一项新兴研究方向致力于解决从声音、点云和深度等低资源模态中学习的问题，其方法是通过将示例与预训练的视觉-语言嵌入 [64, 35, 109] 进行对齐来实现。在本文中，我们表明这种方法可应用于触觉感知。我们将触觉信号与视觉信号进行对齐，从而将触觉与其他多种模态（如语言和声音）联系起来。随后，我们可以利用其他模态训练出的现成模型中的表示式（例如 CLIP [87] ），以解决各种触觉感知任务。为了应对不同触觉传感器之间的巨大差异，我们同时用多个触觉信号训练一个单一模型，并引入可学习的 tokens 来模拟传感器特定的特性，例如触觉信号中的校准参数和强度分布。

An emerging line of work has addressed the challenges of learning from other low-resource modalities, like sound, point clouds, and depth, by aligning examples with pretrained vision-language embeddings [ 64 , 35 , 109 ] . In this paper, we show that this approach can be adapted to tactile sensing. We align tactile signals to visual signals, thereby linking touch to a variety of other modalities, such as language and sound. Then we can use the representations within off-the-shelf models trained on other modalities ( e.g . CLIP [ 87 ] ), to solve a variety of tactile sensing tasks. To deal with the large variations in different touch sensors, we train a single model with multiple tactile signals at once, and introduce learnable tokens to model sensor-specific properties, such as the calibration and intensity profiles in the touch signal.

我们训练的模型，称为 UniTouch，是一个适用于多种基于视觉的触觉传感器的通用接口。 我们的模型统一了众多先前研究的触觉感知任务“零样本”问题，并大幅扩展了触觉感知可应用于的任务范围，具体表现为：(i) 将其应用于 zero-shot 触觉理解任务，如材料识别与机器人抓取稳定性预测。(ii) 通过将触觉与其他模态在共享的潜在空间中对齐，我们在跨模态检索方面取得了优异表现。(iii) 学习到的表示还可以支持图像生成任务，包括在现成的文本到图像扩散模型中使用进行触觉到图像生成 [71, 112] 以及触觉驱动的图像风格化 [111, 112]。(iv) 我们将触觉与大型语言模型（LLM）结合，使得我们能够在多种触觉领域执行任务，如触觉问答，包括接触定位、抓取稳定性预测等。(v) 最后，我们实现了“X到触觉”功能。 从视觉、文本和音频数据中生成触觉图像。我们的实验表明，我们的 zero-shot 模型在多种任务上所表现出的性能，优于以往提出的方法，甚至更为出色。

Our trained model, which we call UniTouch , is a general-purpose interface for multiple vision-based tactile sensors. Our model unifies many previously studied tactile sensing tasks “zero shot” and greatly expands the range of tasks that touch sensing can be applied, as shown in : (i) We apply it to zero-shot touch understanding tasks like material recognition and robotic grasp stability prediction. (ii) We obtain strong performance in cross-modal retrieval with touch by aligning touch with other modalities in a shared latent space. (iii) The learned representation can also support image synthesis tasks, including touch-to-image generation [ 71 , 112 ] and tactile-driven image stylization [ 111 , 112 ] , by using it within off-the-shelf text-to-image diffusion models. (iv) We combine touch with large language models (LLM), allowing us to perform tasks such as tactile question answering in a variety of tactile domains, including contact localization, grasping stability prediction, and etc . (v) Finally, we perform “X-to-touch” generation, producing touch images from vision, text, and audio. Our experiments suggest our zero-shot model achieves competitive (or even better) performance than previously proposed approaches on multiple tasks.

2 相关研究

触觉感知。

早期的触觉传感器主要被设计用于检测基本、低维度的感官输出，如力、压力、振动和温度等。 [61, 62, 19, 56] 最近，基于视觉的触觉传感器受到了越来越多的关注。 GelSight [117, 54] 作为代表性传感器之一，该传感器采用带有嵌入式摄像头与照明系统的弹性凝胶结构。当与物体接触时，凝胶会发生变形，从而生成高分辨率高度图。 [55] ，提供有关触摸形态与物理特性的详细信息 [97, 66] . 一种变体 DIGIT [59] , 采用一种特殊设计的基于硅胶的弹性体凝胶，其表面更硬，且配备不同的照明系统。另一种变体为 GelSlim [97] 包含一种具有弹性、松散编织结构的织物凝胶表面。近期研究还涉及触觉传感器的模拟技术。 [90, 101, 1, 36, 17, 53] . 税收机器人 [90] 模拟了光学响应 GelSight 传感器与 TACTO [101] 计算局部接触几何形状及相应的渲染结果。我们重点关注这些基于视觉的传感器，因为它们在视觉触觉数据集中非常常见。 [30, 32, 33, 118, 94, 117, 111, 100, 108] 这些技术被广泛应用于各种应用中 [68, 34, 95, 12, 60, 127, 45, 67, 41, 115, 9, 51, 11] ，所有系统均采用图像作为输出格式。尽管这些基于视觉的触觉传感器与模拟器具有相似的成像模式，但设计与校准上的差异导致显著的领域差距，如所展示的那样 Fig. 2 因此，研究人员通常分别研究每个传感器。在我们的研究中，我们提出了一种新颖的方法，通过统一的触摸编码器来理解多个传感器。

Early tactile sensors were chiefly engineered to register fundamental, low-dimensional sensory outputs such as force, pressure, vibration, and temperature [ 61 , 62 , 19 , 56 ] . Lately, there has been a growing focus on vision-based tactile sensors. GelSight [ 117 , 54 ] as one of the representative sensors, features an elastomeric gel with an embedded camera and illumination system. The gel deforms upon contact with an object and creates a high-resolution height map using photometric stereo [ 55 ] , which provides detailed information about the shape and physical properties of touch [ 97 , 66 ] . One variant, DIGIT [ 59 ] , has a specially designed silicone-based elastomer gel with a harder surface and a different illumination system. Another variant GelSlim [ 97 ] contains a stretchy, loose-weave fabric gel surface. Recent work also turns into the simulation of tactile sensors [ 90 , 101 , 1 , 36 , 17 , 53 ] . Taxim [ 90 ] simulates the optical response of a GelSight sensor and TACTO [ 101 ] calculates the local contact geometry and the corresponding rendering. We focus on these vision-based sensors as they are widely available in visuo-tactile datasets [ 30 , 32 , 33 , 118 , 94 , 117 , 111 , 100 , 108 ] , are commonly used in various applications [ 68 , 34 , 95 , 12 , 60 , 127 , 45 , 67 , 41 , 115 , 9 , 51 , 11 ] , and all adopt image as the output format. While these vision-based tactile sensors and simulators share similar imaging patterns, the difference in design and calibration results in a significant domain gap, as shown in Fig. 2 . Hence, researchers typically study each sensor separately. In our work, we introduce a novel approach to understanding multiple sensors through our unified touch encoder.

基于触感的表示学习。

最初的尝试旨在学习用于特定任务的触觉表征 [29, 96, 63, 118, 72] . Lee等人 [63] 开展卷积神经网络的协作训练 CNN ) 用于一个 RGB 用于促进接触丰富的操作任务的相机与力传感器。同样，Yuan等人。 [118] 采用类似的方法论，利用该方法在视觉与触觉模式之间建立共享的潜在空间 Gelsight 用于精确织物分类的触摸传感器。近期，研究人员通过自监督技术掌握了触摸的通用表征。杨等人 [111] 用于学习的触觉表征 Gelsight 具有视觉-触觉对比多视图编码的传感器 [98] 以及 Kerr 等 [57] 提出了一种用于该任务的对比式预训练方法 DIGIT 传感器。 其他研究采用 BYOL 框架 [39] 或对比预测编码 [120] 方法来学习非视觉式触觉传感器（如 BioTac）的表征。某些研究 [52] 则使用掩蔽自编码器从触觉输入直接学习触觉表征。与那些仅专注于单一传感器视觉-触觉学习的方法不同，我们的方法旨在学习可应用于多种传感器且能与多种模态相互连接的触觉表征。

The initial efforts learn tactile representations for specific tasks [ 29 , 96 , 63 , 118 , 72 ] . Lee et al . [ 63 ] undertook a collaborative training of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) for an RGB camera and a force sensor to facilitate contact-rich manipulation tasks. Similarly, Yuan et al . [ 118 ] employed a comparable methodology to establish a shared latent space between visual and tactile modalities using the Gelsight touch sensor, aimed at precise fabric classification. Recently, researchers have learned general representations of touch through self-supervision. Yang et al . [ 111 ] learned tactile representations for Gelsight sensors with visuo-tactile contrastive multiview coding [ 98 ] and Kerr et al . [ 57 ] proposed a contrastive pretraining method for the DIGIT sensor. Other works adopted BYOL framework [ 39 ] or contrastive predictive coding [ 120 ] to learn representations for non vision-based tactile sensors like BioTac. Some work [ 52 ] applies masked autoencoders to learn tactile representations directly from tactile inputs. Unlike methods concentrated solely on visuo-tactile learning for a single sensor, our approach aims to learn touch representations that can be applied across various sensors and interconnected with multiple modalities.

多模态表示学习。

视觉-语言预训练策略 [23, 88, 106, 77, 86] 的成功表明了其能够弥合视觉内容（如图像或视频）与文本描述之间的差距 [48, 49, 69]。此外，一些研究人员将 CLIP 的应用拓展至 3 D 领域 [122, 128, 123, 37]。某些研究通过利用与视频的自然关联来学习共享的视听表示 [82, 2, 80, 44, 13, 105, 25, 93, 27]。还有一些研究则致力于研究共享的音声-语言表示 [40, 103, 26]。Bender 等研究者通过结合图像和文本注释，构建了葡萄酒风味的嵌入空间 [4]。Chen 等研究者从双耳声音和视觉中学习共享的空间信息 [15]。还有一些研究则学习了视觉与元数据之间的关联 [126, 102, 14]。 Imagebind [35] 提出仅通过图像对齐的方式学习六种不同模态的联合嵌入，并实现了 zero-shot 跨模态能力。在我们的研究中，我们将这一概念扩展至触觉感知，并通过将触觉数据与图像进行对齐，将其与文本和音频等其他模态相结合，从而在无需配对数据的情况下，促进对跨模态触觉交互的更全面理解。

The success of vision-language pretraining [ 23 , 88 , 106 , 77 , 86 ] has demonstrated the ability to bridge the gap between visual content, such as images or videos, and textual descriptions [ 48 , 49 , 69 ] . Furthermore, some researchers have extended the application of CLIP into the 3D domain [ 122 , 128 , 123 , 37 ] . Some works learn shared audio-visual representation [ 82 , 2 , 80 , 44 , 13 , 105 , 25 , 93 , 27 ] by leveraging natural correspondence with videos. Some works also study shared audio-language representation [ 40 , 103 , 26 ] . Bender et al . [ 4 ] crafted an embedding space for the flavors of wines by leveraging both image and text annotations. Chen et al . [ 15 ] learned shared spatial information from binaural sound and vision. Some works learned the association between vision and metadata [ 126 , 102 , 14 ] . Imagebind [ 35 ] proposed to learn a joint embedding for six diverse modalities solely through image alignment and emerge zero-shot cross-modal capabilities. In our work, we extend this concept to the sense of touch and bind it to other modalities including text and audio by aligning tactile data with images, encouraging a more comprehensive understanding of cross-modal touch interactions without paired data.

3 方法

我们的目标是为不同的触觉传感器学习一个统一的触觉表征，以捕捉触觉与视觉、文本、音频等不同模态之间的关系。首先，我们提出了基于 [35] 的对比式视觉-触觉预训练方法，该方法能够揭示触觉与其他模态之间的关联。随后，我们介绍了我们的触觉编码器设计及数据采样策略，该策略可同时应用于不同的触觉传感器。最后，我们展示了所学习的表征如何被应用于各种下游任务。

We aim to learn a unified tactile representation for different touch sensors that captures relationships between touch and different modalities, e.g . vision, text, and audio. First, we present our contrastive visuo-tactile pretraining, inspired by [ 35 ] , that can emerge interconnections of touch and other modalities. We then introduce our touch encoder design and data sampling strategy that can be used for different tactile sensors at once. Finally, we show how our learned representation can be applied to various downstream tasks.

3.1 图像与绑定触摸

我们仅通过触觉与视觉信息，无需配对文本和音频数据，就能学习出多模态触觉表征。我们通过采用对比学习技术，将触觉嵌入与预训练图像嵌入进行对齐，如 Fig. 3 所示；其中，图像嵌入已与语言和音频训练等模态进行对齐，这些训练数据来自大规模图像配对数据集 [35]。

在推理过程中，为了将我们学习到的表示式推广到未见过的传感器类型，我们从已学习的传感器集合 {𝐬k}k=1N\{{\mathbf{s}}_{k}\}_{k=1}^{N} 中检索与特定传感器最邻近的 tokens 本体。具体而言，我们首先为每种传感器计算一个原型，即一个 1 D 向量，该向量平均了该传感器所收集的触觉图像中的所有原始像素，并在训练后保存这些原型。然后，在推理阶段，我们计算输入触觉图像与所有传感器原型之间的 L1 距离，并检索出距离最小的传感器。

To generalize our learned representation to unseen types of sensors during the inference, we retrieve the nearest neighbor sensor-specific tokens from the learned sensor set { 𝐬 k } k = 1 N . Specifically, we first compute a prototype for each sensor, a 1D vector that averages all the raw pixels belonging to the tactile images collected by this sensor, and store these prototypes after training. Then, during the inference stage, we compute the L1 distance of between an input tactile image and all the sensor prototypes and retrieve the sensor with minimum distance.

3.3 应用

通过将我们的触觉嵌入数据与联合潜在空间进行对齐，我们建立了触觉与其他模态之间的关联。这些对齐使得我们能够在无需额外训练的情况下，实施各种 zero-shot 及跨模态应用。

By aligning our touch embedding to the joint latent space, we establish a link between touch and other modalities. These alignments allow us to perform various zero-shot and cross-modal applications without any further training.

Zero-shot 触觉理解。

触觉与文本的协同对齐实现了 zero-shot 触觉理解，例如材料分类与抓取稳定性预测。遵循 CLIP [88] 原则，我们通过模板与类别名称对触觉图像与文本提示进行编码。我们计算其相似度得分并对其进行排序，从而实现 zero-shot 分类。

Emergent alignment of touch and text enables zero-shot touch understanding, e.g . , material classification and grasp stability prediction. Following CLIP [ 88 ] , we encode the touch images and text prompts with templates and class names. We compute their similarity score and rank them to achieve the zero-shot classification.

触摸型 LLM。

利用现有的视觉-语言模型 [124, 28] 以及与之对应的图像嵌入 [35]，我们将触摸嵌入与之对齐后，通过切换至触摸编码器即可构建触摸-语言模型。基于触摸图像和语言输入，我们可以通过问答方式获得更全面的理解。

Using an existing vision-language model [ 124 , 28 ] with the image embedding [ 35 ] that we align our touch embedding with, we can create our touch-language model by switching to our touch encoder. Given the touch image and language inputs, we can obtain a more comprehensive understanding via question-answering.

通过触摸实现图像合成。

通过文本与触觉的绑定，还可以拓展触觉图像合成的更多潜在能力。我们利用了预训练的文本到图像扩散模型。 [89] 并利用我们的触摸功能来调控去噪过程，从而实现 zero-shot 触屏到图像生成 [71, 112] 以及由触觉驱动的图像风格化。

Binding touch with text also opens up more potential abilities for image synthesis with touch. We leverage the pretrained text-to-image diffusion model [ 89 ] and use our touch features to condition the denoising process, achieving zero-shot touch-to-image generation [ 71 , 112 ] and tactile-driven image stylization.

X到触摸生成。

我们还利用扩散模型将其他模态与触觉交互联系起来，从而实现“x到触觉”的生成，即通过观看、描述或聆听来模拟触觉体验。我们使用预训练的联合图像嵌入来训练图像到触觉的扩散模型 [112]，随后便能够从文本和音频中生成触觉效果。

We also connect other modalities to touch using the diffusion model so that we can achieve x-to-touch generation, where we imagine the touch by seeing, describing, or listening. We train an image-to-touch diffusion model [ 112 ] using the pretrained joint image embedding and then we can generate touch from text and audio as well.

5 讨论

我们提出了 UniTouch，这是一种用于基于视觉的触觉传感器的统一多模态触觉表示方法。为了实现这一点，我们通过对比学习将触觉嵌入映射到共享的多模态嵌入空间。此外，我们还引入了传感器特定的 tokens，从而能够同时从不同传感器中学习数据。UniTouch 则统一了众多现有的触觉感知任务，显著扩展了触觉感知的任务范围。不过，多模态（基础）模型领域显然仍处于发展初期。像我们这样的智能体利用多种感官观测的互补优势，在日常任务中整合所有五种感官。基于这一目标，我们认为我们的研究是朝着该方向迈出的具体一步，为超越视觉和触觉的多模态触觉体验开辟新途径，并将触觉感知整合到多模态基础模型中。

We introduced UniTouch , a unified multimodal tactile representation for vision-based tactile sensors. To achieve this, we align our touch embedding to a shared multimodal embedding space using contrastive learning. We further introduce sensor-specific tokens that enables learning from different sensors all at once. UniTouch unifies many existing tactile sensing tasks and significantly expands the range of tasks for touch sensing. Nonetheless, the field of multimodal (foundational) model is admittedly still young. Agents, like ourselves, leverage complementary strengths of multi-sensory observations, incorporating all five senses in everyday tasks. With that goal in mind, we see our work as a concrete step towards that direction, opening new avenues for multimodal touch experience beyond vision and touch and integrating tactile sensing into multimodal foundation models.

局限性。

由于各种触觉传感器的输出格式各不相同（例如图像、气压信号、力值等），我们将研究范围限定在基于视觉的触觉传感器上。扩大训练策略规模是未来进一步整合新型触觉传感器的关键。此外，与其他多模态基础模型一样，我们的表征属于“黑盒”形式，在可解释性方面存在局限性，而可解释性在相关领域却具有重要价值。

As the full range of tactile sensors exhibit differing output formats (e.g. image, barometric signals, force), we limit our scope to vision-based tactile sensors. Scaling up our training strategy is key to further integrate emerging tactile sensors in the future. In addition, like other multimodal foundational models, our representation is “black-box”, which does not easily for interpretability in the space, where one may benefit from explainability.

附录 A. 1 数据集与指标

我们提供了本文所用数据集的更多细节，所有数据均可公开获取。

We provide more details of datasets used in our paper, all of which are publicly available.

接触即结束 [111]。

Touch and Go 数据集是一个最新的真实场景视觉-触觉数据集，记录了人类在室内外环境中使用 GelSight 触觉传感器与各种物体进行交互的情况。该数据集包含约 4,000 个不同的物体实例和 20 种材料类型的 13,900 次触摸实例。由于它是唯一一个真实场景的未处理数据集，我们将其应用于多种任务，包括材料分类、基于触摸的图像生成、Touch LLM 以及 X-to-touch 生成。我们使用 [111] 的官方训练/测试数据划分，该数据划分是根据触摸事件而非帧数进行的，以此避免训练集与测试集之间存在类似的触摸图像。对于 Touch-LLM 和 X-to-touch 应用，我们通过对用户要求他们提供描述对象、触摸感受及表面纹理的标题来对 400 视觉图像进行标注。

The Touch and Go dataset is a recent, real-world visuo-tactile dataset featuring human interactions with various objects in both indoor and outdoor environments using a GelSight tactile sensor. It comprises 13,900 instances of touch across approximately 4,000 distinct object instances and 20 types of materials. Since it is the only real-world in-the-wild dataset, we apply it to multiple tasks including material classification, image synthesis with touch, Touch LLM, and X-to-touch generation. We use the official train/test split of [ 111 ] where the dataset is split by touches, not by frames to avoid similar touch images between the train and test set. For Touch-LLM and X-to-touch applications, we label 400 visual images by asking turkers to provide their captioning to describe the object, touch feeling, and texture from it.

成功的感受 [6]。

“成功感”是一组由机器人收集的视觉-触觉数据集，用于研究机器人在桌面上的物体抓取行为。这些触觉图像均通过 GelSight 触觉传感器采集，其中包含 9.3 对视觉与触觉图像。我们将该数据集用于机器人抓取稳定性预测研究。由于没有明确的训练集/验证集/测试集划分标准，按照 [111, 33] 的规则，我们以 8 : 1 : 1 的比例对数据集中的物体进行划分。

The Feeling of Success is a robot-collected visuo-tactile dataset of robots grasping objects on a tabletop. The tactile images are all captured by GelSight tactile sensors. It contains 9.3k paired vision and touch images. We apply this dataset to robotic grasping stability predictions. As there is no official split of train/val/test, following [ 111 , 33 ] , we split the dataset by objects in the ratio of 8:1:1.

YCB-Slide [94] .

YCB-Slide数据集包含YCB物体上的DIGIT级滑动交互数据。该数据集采用视频格式，我们在实验中选取了所有180组帧。该数据集包含10种YCB物体，包括糖盒、番茄汤罐、芥末瓶、漂白剂清洁剂、杯子、电钻、剪刀、调节扳手、锤子以及棒球。虽然触觉图像是通过滑动交互收集的，但视觉输入则是通过YCB物体的模拟生成。在我们的实验中，我们将每个物体视为一个独立的材料，我们的目标是对10类进行分类。我们将该数据集用于材料分类研究。

The YCB-Slide dataset comprises DIGIT sliding interactions on YCB objects. The dataset is in the video format where we take all 180k frames for our experiments. The dataset contains 10 YCB objects including a sugar box, a tomato soup can, a mustard bottle, a bleach cleanser, a mug, a power drill, scissors, an adjustable wrench, a hammer, and a baseball. While the tactile images are collected via sliding interaction, the visual input is generated by simulation of the YCB objects. In our experiment, we treat each of the objects as an individual material and our goal is to classify 10 classes. We apply this dataset to material classification.

对象文件夹 1.0 [30] .

ObjectFolder 1.0 数据集是一个模拟数据集，包含来自在线存储库的 100 对象的 3 D 模型。触摸图像由 TACTO 模拟器生成。由于原始数据集是一个具有无限点的 3 D 模型，我们对每个对象随机采样 200 个点。我们将该数据集用于材料分类和抓取稳定性预测实验。值得注意的是，在抓取稳定性预测实验中，我们选择了符合特定条件的 6 对象进行抓取，从而实现了相对均衡的抓取成功与失败情况。根据 [30] 的数据，所有材料均可被归类为 7 材料类别，包括木材、钢铁、聚碳酸酯、塑料、铁、陶瓷和玻璃。这些类别也适用于 ObjectFolder 2.0 和 ObjectFolder Real 数据集。

The ObjectFolder 1.0 dataset is a simulation dataset containing 3D models of 100 objects from online repositories. The touch images are simulated by TACTO simulators. As the raw dataset is a 3D model with infinite points, we randomly sample 200 points for each object. We apply this dataset to material classification and grasping stability prediction experiments. It is worth noting that for grasping stability prediction experiments, we select 6 objects suitable for grasping following their setting and achieve relatively balanced successful and failure outcomes for grasping. Following [ 30 ] , all materials can be categorized into 7 material categories including wood, steel, polycarbonate, plastic, iron, ceramic, and glass. These categories are also applied to ObjectFolder 2.0 and ObjectFolder Real datasets.

对象文件夹 2.0 [32] .

ObjectFolder 2.0 数据集将 [30] 扩展至 1000 对象，并优化声学与触觉仿真流程，以生成更真实的多感官数据。在触觉仿真方面，该数据集使用 Taxim 仿真器而非 TACTO。与 ObjectFolder 1.0 的预处理方式类似，我们对每个对象抽取 200 点样本。为了避免与 [30] 产生重叠，我们仅选取 101 至 1000 范围内的对象。该数据集被应用于材料分类、跨模态检索、机器人抓取稳定性预测以及 Touch-LLM 模型。在跨模态检索和 Touch-LLM 任务中，我们会根据对象的视觉输入，对描述其接触点的文本进行标注，例如“木制桌子的角落”。

The ObjectFolder 2.0 dataset extends [ 30 ] to 1000 objects and improves the acoustic and tactile simulation pipelines to render more realistic multisensory data. For the tactile simulation, it utilizes the Taxim simulator instead of TACTO. Similar to the preprocessing of ObjectFolder 1.0, we sample 200 points for each object. To avoid overlapping with [ 30 ] , we only take the 101-1000 objects. We apply this dataset to material classification, cross-modal retrieval, robot grasping stability prediction, and Touch-LLM. For cross-modal retrieval and Touch-LLM tasks, we annotate text descriptions that depict the contact point of the object from its visual input, e.g . “ The corner of a wooden table. ”

ObjectFolder 真实 [33] 。

X到触摸生成

我们基于稳定扩散技术构建了 X-to-touch 生成模型。虽然现有的多模态触觉数据集通常仅包含视觉与触觉信息，但我们首先训练了图像到触觉的扩散模型，随后通过替换图像条件信息来实现文本到触觉及音频到触觉的零样本生成，因为这两种输入已进行对齐处理。我们使用 Adam 优化器，基础学习率为 1 e- 6，并以上述学习率策略进行 30 次训练。我们在 4 RTX A40 GPU 上以 48 的批量大小进行训练。由于需要使用已对齐的条件嵌入，条件模型在训练过程中被冻结。通过交叉注意力机制，这些条件嵌入被整合到模型中。我们采用冻结的预训练 VQGAN 模型来获取潜变量表示，其空间维度为 64× 64。 在推理过程中，我们进行了 200 步的去噪处理，并设定了引导尺度 s = 7.5。

We conduct our X-to-touch generation model based on stable diffusion. While most existing multimodal tactile datasets only contain vision and touch, we first train an image-to-touch diffusion model and we are able to conduct text-to-touch and audio-to-touch zero shot by replacing the image conditioning as they are already aligned. We use the Adam optimizer with a base learning rate of 1e-6. Models are all trained with 30 iterations using the above learning rate policy. We train our model with a batch size of 48 on 4 RTX A40 GPUs. Since we want to use the aligned condition embeddings, the conditional model is frozen during training. The condition embeddings are integrated into the model using cross-attention. We use the frozen, pretrained VQGAN to obtain our latent representation, with a spatial dimension of 64×64. During the inference, we conducted the denoising process for 200 steps and set the guidance scale s = 7.5.

附录 A. 3 评估细节

触屏到图像生成

遵循 [112] 规则，我们采用三种评估指标：Frechet Inception Distance (FID)、对比视觉-触觉预训练指标 (CVTP) 以及材料分类一致性指标。FID是图像生成领域的标准评估指标，通过训练有素的网络来比较真实图像与生成图像激活的分布。CVTP [112] 是一种与 CLIP 类似的指标，用于衡量生成图像所学习的视觉与触觉嵌入向量与条件化触觉信号之间的余弦相似度，该指标使用现成的网络实现。材料分类一致性 [112] 则利用材料分类器对预测图像与真实图像进行分类，并统计两者一致率，其中我们通过输入“[CLS]材料的种类”这一提示来使用 CLIP 作为 zero-shot 材料分类器。

Following [ 112 ] , we use three evaluation metrics of Frechet Inception Distance (FID), Contrastive Visuo-Tactile Pre-Training (CVTP), and Material Classification Consistency. FID is a standard evaluation metric in image synthesis that compares the distribution of real and generated image activations using a trained network. CVTP [ 112 ] is a metric similar to CLIP but measures the cosine similarity between the visual and tactile embeddings learned for the generated images and conditioned tactile signals, which used an off-the-shelf network. Material classification consistency [ 112 ] uses a material classifier to categorize the predicted and ground truth images and measure the rate at which they agree, where we use CLIP as the zero-shot material classifier by feeding the prompt of "material of [CLS]" .

批量内采样混合率选择。

我们评估了不同的选择 σ\sigma 对于批量内采样，其中 σ\sigma 表示来自同一数据集的数据占比，其余来自其他数据集。我们设定了 σ\sigma to {0,0.5,0.75,1.0}\{0,0.5,0.75,1.0\} 并评估它们 zero-shot 所有六个数据集上的材料分类性能表现，如所示 Fig. 6 . 我们观察到，如果我们进行选择 σ=0\sigma=0 , 区分传感器内部样本的能力显著减弱，从而导致性能下降。 σ\sigma 随着增加，模型能够更精准地区分传感器内部样本。在极端情况下 σ=1.0\sigma=1.0 当所有样本均来自同一数据集时，模型将不会接触到不同类别之间的负样本。我们观察到，在这种情况下，模型性能实际上在下降。这证明了设计在平衡不同传感器与内部传感器负样本之间的有效性。 我们通过实验发现，选择 σ=0.75\sigma=0.75 能够在这些因素之间实现良好的权衡。

We evaluate different choices of σ for in-batch sampling, where σ denotes the percentage of the data that comes from the same dataset while the rest from others. We set σ to { 0 , 0.5 , 0.75 , 1.0 } and evaluate their zero-shot material classification performance on all six datasets, as shown in Fig. 6 . We observe that if we select σ = 0 , the ability to distinguish between intra-sensor samples is significantly undermined thus leading to inferior performance. As the σ is increasing, the model is able to better distinguish between intra-sensor samples. In the extreme case when σ = 1.0 where all samples come from the same dataset, the model will have no exposure to the inter-class negatives. We observe that the performance in this case is actually decreasing. This demonstrates the effectiveness of design to balance between inter-sensor and intra-sensor negatives. We empirically found that selecting σ = 0.75 obtains a good trade-off between these factors.
