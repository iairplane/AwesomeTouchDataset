OpenViTac：在统一的仿真与真机框架中学习和评测视触觉策略

摘要

触觉反馈为具身智能体补充视觉无法直接提供的物理信息，让它与真实环境的交互更可靠。近年来 vision-tactile-language-action （VTLA）策略进展很快，但仍缺少能同时评测仿真和真机触觉操作的统一基准。我们为此提出 OpenViTac ，用于评测仿真和真实环境中的机器人视触觉策略。OpenViTac 将接触密集型操作划分为四个与触觉相关的能力维度，并提供相互对应的仿真和真机设置，用同一框架评估 VLA、WAM 和 VTLA 策略。在这个基准上，我们研究不同触觉表征和接入方法如何影响预训练 VLA 模型的表现。 根据实验中表现最佳的触觉表征和接入策略，我们提出触觉增强框架 OpenVTLA 。借助仿真与真机成对设置，我们进一步研究联合训练，并分析影响跨域策略学习的因素。OpenViTac 为评测和改进机器人视触觉操作提供了统一的平台。

Tactile feedback provides embodied agents with physical information beyond visual observations, enabling more reliable interaction with the real world. However, despite the rapid progress of vision-tactile-language-action (VTLA) policies, there remains a lack of unified benchmarks for evaluating tactile-enabled robot manipulation across simulation and the real world. To address this gap, we introduce OpenViTac , a visuo-tactile manipulation benchmark for evaluating robot policies across simulation and the real world. OpenViTac organizes contact-rich manipulation into four tactile-relevant capability dimensions and provides paired simulation–real-world settings for consistent evaluation of VLA, WAM, and VTLA policies. Building upon this benchmark, we investigate how different tactile representations and integration strategies affect the performance of pretrained VLA models. Correspondingly, we introduce OpenVTLA , a tactile augmentation framework that combines the best-performing representation and integration strategy. Furthermore, we leverage the paired benchmark setting to study sim–real co-training and analyze factors affecting cross-domain policy learning. Together, OpenViTac provides a unified platform for evaluating and advancing visuo-tactile robot manipulation.

1 引言

“视觉与触觉的概念使得这两个物种完全不同且具有多样性。”
— 乔治·伯克利

“The ideas of sight and touch make two species entirely distinct and heterogeneous.” — George Berkeley

机器人领域的最新进展 Vision-Language-Action ( VLA ) 模型 [2, 16] 使机器人能够利用大规模的视觉与语言知识，以实现更普遍的可泛化操作能力 [12] 这些基础策略在多种操控场景中均展现了出色的性能 [20, 15] 然而，其核心仍基于视觉观察。不过，许多现实世界中的交互需要理解那些无法仅凭视觉直接观察到的物理状态，例如物体属性、接触动力学以及交互力。 [9] 通过提供来自物理交互的直接反馈，实现触觉感知 [8, 31] 通过补充物理信息来增强视觉感知，使本体化智能体能够更清晰地理解并与物理世界进行交互，例如接触几何结构、力信号以及材料相关的响应。 这促使了从纯粹基于视觉的策略向 Vision-Tactile-Language-Action（VTLA）模型及 [11, 43, 28] 的转变，这些模型将视觉所感知的全局场景理解与触摸带来的局部交互反馈相结合。

Recent advances in Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models [ 2 , 16 ] have enabled robots to leverage large-scale visual and linguistic knowledge for increasingly generalizable manipulation [ 12 ] . These foundation policies have demonstrated strong performance across diverse manipulation scenarios [ 20 , 15 ] , yet remain primarily grounded in visual observations. However, many real-world interactions require understanding physical states that are not directly observable from vision alone, such as object properties, contact dynamics, and interaction forces [ 9 ] . By providing direct feedback from physical interaction, tactile sensing [ 8 , 31 ] augments visual perception with complementary physical information, enabling embodied agents to better understand and interact with the physical world, such as contact geometry, force cues, and material-dependent responses. This has motivated a shift from purely vision-based policies toward Vision-Tactile-Language-Action (VTLA) models [ 11 , 43 , 28 ] , which combine global scene understanding from vision with local interaction feedback from touch.

受触觉反馈前景的驱动，近期 VTLA 研究迅速扩大了具备触觉功能的机器人策略的设计空间，探索了多种不同的触觉表征方式 [46, 10, 9] , 融合策略 [26, 39] ，以及策略架构 [43, 28] . 然而，这些方法通常在不同的传感器、任务、机器人本体及实验协议下进行评估，这使得系统性比较变得困难，且触觉感知的贡献也未能得到充分理解。现有的操作基准测试，例如 LIBERO [20] , RoboTwin [5] ，以及 RoboDojo [4] 主要专注于基于视觉的策略，不支持触觉感知。最近的视觉-触觉基准测试，例如 Tabero [37] 以及 UniVTAC [3] , 已开始引入触觉观察与接触丰富的任务用于评估。然而，如表格所示 1，现有的基准测试仍缺乏对触觉相关能力、多种硬件配置以及虚拟与真实场景对应性的全面覆盖。

Driven by the promise of tactile feedback, recent VTLA studies have rapidly expanded the design space of tactile-enabled robot policies, exploring different tactile representations [ 46 , 10 , 9 ] , fusion strategies [ 26 , 39 ] , and policy architectures [ 43 , 28 ] . Nevertheless, these approaches are typically evaluated under different sensors, tasks, robot embodiments, and experimental protocols, making systematic comparison challenging and leaving the contribution of tactile sensing insufficiently understood. Existing manipulation benchmarks, such as LIBERO [ 20 ] , RoboTwin [ 5 ] , and RoboDojo [ 4 ] , primarily focus on vision-based policies and do not support tactile sensing. Recent visuo-tactile benchmarks, such as Tabero [ 37 ] and UniVTAC [ 3 ] , have started to introduce tactile observations and contact-rich tasks for evaluation. However, as shown in Tab. 1 , existing benchmarks still lack comprehensive coverage of tactile-relevant capabilities, diverse hardware configurations, and sim–real correspondence.

2.1 视觉-触觉机器人学习

近期机器人基础策略主要围绕 vision-language-action（VLA）、world-action models（WAMs）和 [16, 12] 模型发展。触觉反馈能够提供补充的接触及物理属性信息，从而提升对接触敏感的操控能力，因此催生了诸如 VTLA 模型、[43, 33, 28, 36] 及触觉感知型 world-action models [21, 32] 等触觉增强策略。然而，现有方法在触觉信息的融入方式上存在显著差异，包括特征调制 [26]、动作级调节 [33]、专用触觉专家系统 [43] 以及后期动作优化 [28, 36]。由于目前尚未就触觉信息应如何融入预训练视觉语言模型达成共识，本文在共享 π0.5\pi_{0.5} 框架下，系统研究了各类典型的触觉整合策略。

Recent robot foundation policies have largely developed around vision-language-action (VLA) models and world-action models (WAMs) [ 16 , 12 ] . Tactile feedback provides complementary contact and physical-property information that can improve contact-sensitive manipulation, motivating tactile-augmented policies such as VTLA models [ 43 , 33 , 28 , 36 ] and tactile-aware world-action models [ 21 , 32 ] . Existing approaches, however, differ substantially in how touch is incorporated, including feature modulation [ 26 ] , action-level conditioning [ 33 ] , dedicated tactile experts [ 43 ] , and late-stage action refinement [ 28 , 36 ] . Given the lack of consensus on how tactile information should be incorporated into pretrained VLAs, in this paper we systematically study representative tactile integration strategies under a shared π 0.5 backbone.

2.2 视觉-触觉数据集与基准测试

诸如 LIBERO [20]、RoboTwin 和 [5] 等通用操控基准测试，以及 RoboDojo [4]，能够在多种模拟任务中提供标准化的评估，但主要侧重于基于视觉的操控。针对表示学习，已有大量触觉数据集被构建出来 [42, 14, 10, 9]；而具备触觉功能的真实机器人操控数据，才刚刚开始在任务、传感器及本体类型方面实现规模化应用 [43, 31, 21]。在策略层面评估方面，ManiFeel [22] 和 TacO [48] 研究了下游操控中的触觉感知问题，而 UniVTAC、[3] 和 NeoSim、[31] 则进一步在模拟环境中进行了系统化的评估。然而，现有的模拟基准测试与真实环境仍存在显著差异。OpenViTac 提供了一个以触觉为中心的统一基准测试，包含一致的模拟与真实任务组合，并包含来自多种触觉传感器的真实数据。

General manipulation benchmarks such as LIBERO [ 20 ] , RoboTwin [ 5 ] , and RoboDojo [ 4 ] provide standardized evaluation across diverse simulated tasks, but primarily focus on vision-based manipulation. A broad range of tactile datasets has been developed for representation learning [ 42 , 14 , 10 , 9 ] , while tactile-enabled real-robot manipulation data have only recently begun to scale across tasks, sensors, and embodiments [ 43 , 31 , 21 ] . For policy-level evaluation, ManiFeel [ 22 ] and TacO [ 48 ] study tactile sensing in downstream manipulation, while UniVTAC [ 3 ] and NeoSim [ 31 ] further provide systematic evaluation in simulation. However, existing simulation benchmarks still exhibit substantial discrepancies from real-world settings. OpenViTac provides a unified tactile-centric benchmark with aligned sim–real tasks and additional real-world data across multiple tactile sensors.

3 OpenViTac

我们引入了 OpenViTac，这是一个统一的基准测试，用于在模拟环境与真实世界中评估视觉-触觉机器人操作能力。OpenViTac将具备触觉功能的操作能力划分为四个维度，并提供了 11 模拟任务与 8 真实世界任务组合，以系统评估 VLA、WAM 和 VTLA 策略。基于 OpenViTac，我们通过触觉表征与整合策略研究了预训练 VLA 模型的触觉适配问题，最终得到 OpenVTLA 成果，并进一步探讨了由这种配对基准设置所实现的模拟与真实环境协同训练机制。

We introduce OpenViTac , a unified benchmark for evaluating visuo-tactile robot manipulation across simulation and the real world. OpenViTac organizes tactile-enabled manipulation into four capability dimensions and provides 11 simulation tasks with 8 paired real-world tasks for systematic evaluation of VLA, WAM, and VTLA policies. Building upon OpenViTac, we study tactile adaptation for pretrained VLA models through tactile representation and integration strategies, leading to OpenVTLA , and further investigate sim–real co-training enabled by the paired benchmark setting.

3.1 基准测试

基准测试设计。OpenViTac用于评估策略是否能够利用触觉反馈，当视觉观察提供的关于物理交互的信息不完整时。如图 2 所示，我们围绕四种视觉-触觉能力构建该基准测试：属性感知、对脆弱性的感知、丰富的接触感知以及精准感知。更多细节请参见附录 B。

Benchmark design. OpenViTac evaluates whether policies can exploit tactile feedback when visual observations provide incomplete information about physical interaction. As illustrated in Fig. 2 , we organize the benchmark around four visuo-tactile capabilities: property perception , fragility-aware , contact-rich , and precision . Further details are provided in Appendix B .

任务套件。我们将这些任务划分为四种互补的视觉-触觉能力：( 1 ) 属性感知（PP）用于评估触觉反馈是否能够揭示视觉上模糊的物理属性，并支持基于属性的决策。该任务包括基于属性的放置操作所需的重量分类（WC）、硬度分类（HC）和粗糙度分类（RC）；基于粗糙度的抓取重构（RR）用于基于属性进行重构操作；以及空罐选择（ECS）用于通过物理交互区分空罐与装满的罐子。（ 2 ）脆弱感知（FA）用于评估对脆弱物体的力敏感操作。抓取芯片（GC）任务要求在不损坏芯片的情况下进行抓取和放置。（ 3 ）接触丰富（CR）任务评估需要持续且有效物理接触的操作。 它包括齿轮组件（GA），该组件在组装过程中需确保齿轮的咬合状态；以及拉出抽屉（PD），该组件在驱动可关节式抽屉运行时需保持稳定的抓取状态。（4）精准操控（PR）旨在在严格的几何公差条件下实现基于接触引导的操控操作。该方案包含插入式 USB 接口（In-USB）与版本一插入块（In-B v1），用于实现精确的对齐与插入操作；此外还有版本二插入块（In-B v2），该版本还要求在插入前选择指定的物体。

Task suite. We organize the tasks into four complementary visuo-tactile capabilities: (1) Property Perception (PP) evaluates whether tactile feedback can reveal visually ambiguous physical properties and support property-conditioned decisions. It includes weight classification (WC), hardness classification (HC), and roughness classification (RC) for property-conditioned placement; roughness-guided regrasp (RR) for property-aware regrasping; and empty-can selection (ECS) for distinguishing empty from filled cans through physical interaction. (2) Fragility-Aware (FA) evaluates force-sensitive manipulation of delicate objects. Grasp chip (GC) requires grasping and placing a fragile chip without damaging it. (3) Contact-Rich (CR) evaluates manipulation that requires sustained and effective physical contact. It includes gear assembly (GA), which requires maintaining gear engagement during assembly, and pull drawer (PD), which requires maintaining a stable grasp while actuating an articulated drawer. (4) Precision Manipulation (PR) evaluates contact-guided manipulation under tight geometric tolerances. It includes insert USB (In-USB) and insert block v1 (In-B v1) for precise alignment and insertion, and insert block v2 (In-B v2), which additionally requires selecting the instructed object before insertion.

3.2 调整预训练的 VLAS 以适配触觉任务

大规模视觉-触觉机器人数据在传感器、本体及操作任务层面仍存在局限性且异质化，因此难以在现代虚拟机器人系统的规模下训练基于触觉感知的策略。 [43, 33] 因此，我们研究了一个实际的后训练场景，在该场景中所有变体均从相同的预训练状态开始 π0.5\pi_{0.5} [12] 主干网络在触觉观测的表示方式及其融入策略的方式上有所不同。如图所示。 3 我们依据两个维度来研究这些选择：触觉表征与触觉整合。

Large-scale visuo-tactile robot data remain limited and heterogeneous across sensors, embodiments, and manipulation tasks, making it difficult to train tactile-native policies at the scale of modern VLAs [ 43 , 33 ] . We therefore study a practical post-training setting in which all variants start from the same pretrained π 0.5 [ 12 ] backbone, while varying how tactile observations are represented and incorporated into the policy. As shown in Fig. 3 , we investigate these choices along two dimensions: tactile representation and tactile integration .

触觉表征。第一个设计选择在于，对于预训练的 VLA，应如何表示触觉观测数据。最直接的选择是复用 SigLIP [45]，即 π0.5\pi_{0.5} 的原生视觉编码器，该编码器保留了现有的表征接口，但是在自然图像而非触觉观测数据上进行的预训练。因此，我们进一步考虑了最新的触觉特定表征，包括 T3、[46]、Sparsh、[10] 以及 AnyTouch2、[9]。T3 处理单个触觉帧，而 Sparsh 和 AnyTouch2 则结合短暂的时间历史以捕捉不断变化的接触信息。AnyTouch2 进一步采用紧凑型触觉表征，这与触觉观测所携带的更局部化、更具体的接触信息相一致。我们在第节中在相同的 token 级串联接口下对这些表征进行了比较。 4.3；更多背景信息见附录 A.1。

Tactile Representation. The first design choice is how tactile observations should be represented for a pretrained VLA. The most direct option is to reuse SigLIP [ 45 ] , the native visual encoder of π 0.5 , which preserves the existing representation interface but is pretrained on natural images rather than tactile observations. We therefore further consider recent tactile-specific representations, including T3 [ 46 ] , Sparsh [ 10 ] , and AnyTouch2 [ 9 ] . T3 processes individual tactile frames, while Sparsh and AnyTouch2 incorporate short temporal histories to capture evolving contact information. AnyTouch2 further adopts a compact tactile representation, consistent with the more localized and contact-specific information carried by tactile observations. We compare these representations under the same token-level concatenation interface in Sec. 4.3 ; additional background is provided in Appendix A.1 .

触觉整合。在获得触觉表征之后，第二个设计选择在于触觉信息如何以及何时进入预训练的 VLA。如图 3 所示，我们考虑了五种典型的策略。

Tactile Integration. Given a tactile representation, the second design choice is where and how tactile information enters the pretrained VLA. As illustrated in Fig. 3 , we consider five representative strategies:

Diverse simulated data improves real-world performance. As shown in Tab. 5 , adding simulated demonstrations improves both policies without increasing the real-data budget. With 500 simulated demonstrations, π 0.5 improves from 15 % to 40 % on In-USB and from 35 % to 50 % on GA. OpenVTLA improves from 20 % to 40 % and from 50 % to 60 % , respectively. Averaged over the two tasks, these correspond to gains of 20 percentage points for π 0.5 and 15 points for OpenVTLA. The smaller gain for OpenVTLA may partly reflect residual differences in contact-induced tactile signals across domains, motivating consistent sensor calibration and improved tactile simulation.

协同训练与接触式训练都能在照明干扰下提升性能。在受干扰的照明条件下，仅基于真实数据的 π0.5\pi_{0.5} 在 In-USB 上降至 0%0\%，在 GA 上降至 10%10\%。通过添加 500500 模拟演示，这些结果分别提升至 20%20\% 和 40%40\%，表明更广泛的模拟训练能够支持在视觉干扰下的更好性能，尽管相对于标准照明条件而言性能仍有所下降。OpenVTLA 对干扰的敏感度较低：其仅基于真实数据的策略在 In-USB 上达到 10%10\%，在 GA 上仍保持 50%50\%，而通过协同训练则得到 30%30\% 和 60%60\%。特别是，其在 GA 下的成功率在两种训练设置下均不受照明干扰影响。这一现象与触觉反馈提供对环境照明敏感度较低的接触信息相符合，从而进一步增强了来自多样化模拟体验的鲁棒性。

Co-training and touch improve performance under lighting perturbations. Under perturbed lighting, real-only π 0.5 falls to 0 % on In-USB and 10 % on GA. Adding 500 simulated demonstrations raises these results to 20 % and 40 % , showing that broader simulated training supports better performance under visual disturbance, although degradation relative to standard lighting remains. OpenVTLA is less sensitive to the perturbation: its real-only policy achieves 10 % on In-USB and retains 50 % on GA, while co-training yields 30 % and 60 % . In particular, its GA success rate is unchanged by lighting perturbations under both training settings. This pattern is consistent with tactile feedback providing contact information that is less sensitive to ambient lighting, complementing the robustness gained from diverse simulated experience.

5 结论、局限性及未来工作

结论。我们引入了 OpenViTac，这是一个视觉-触觉操控基准测试，用于系统评估在模拟环境及现实世界中的触觉支持型机器人策略。OpenViTac将操控行为划分为四个与触觉相关的能力维度，并提供了一个统一的测试平台，用于比较 VLA、WAM 和 VTLA 策略。基于该基准测试，我们研究了预训练的 VLA 模型的触觉表征与整合策略，并引入了 OpenVTLA，它在模拟与现实环境评估中均表现出优异性能。我们进一步探讨了模拟与现实环境的协同训练机制，证明额外的模拟经验能够提升现实世界中的操控性能。

Conclusion. We introduced OpenViTac , a visuo-tactile manipulation benchmark for systematically evaluating tactile-enabled robot policies across simulation and the real world. OpenViTac organizes manipulation into four tactile-relevant capability dimensions and provides a unified testbed for comparing VLA, WAM, and VTLA policies. Building on this benchmark, we studied tactile representation and integration strategies for pretrained VLA models and introduced OpenVTLA , which achieves strong performance in both simulation and real-world evaluations. We further explored sim–real co-training, showing that additional simulated experience can improve real-world manipulation performance.

限制与未来工作。由于触觉渲染、传感器建模及多模态校准方面的不足，OpenViTac 在触觉观测方面仍存在模拟与真实之间的差距。未来的研究将改进传感器与相机校准技术，并扩展测试基准，以涵盖更丰富的以触觉为中心的交互场景，包括可变形物体操作、表面交互以及长距离家庭任务。我们还计划支持更多种类的机器人本体，包括灵巧手，从而在更复杂的接触动力学条件下更广泛地评估视觉-触觉操作能力。

Limitations and future work. OpenViTac still faces a sim–real gap in tactile observations due to limitations in tactile rendering, sensor modeling, and multimodal calibration. Future work will improve sensor and camera calibration and extend the benchmark toward richer tactile-centric interactions, including deformable-object manipulation, surface interaction, and long-horizon household tasks. We also plan to support more diverse robot embodiments, including dexterous hands, to broaden the evaluation of visuo-tactile manipulation under more complex contact dynamics.

A. 1 触觉表征学习

早期触觉表征学习通常利用视觉与触觉的成对观测数据来学习跨模态特征。Touch and Go [42] 支持基于人类收集的视觉与触觉交互的自监督学习，而 SSVTP [14] 与 Visuo-Tactile Transformers [6] 则进一步探索用于感知与操控的跨模态表征。后续研究则转向在不同传感器和任务之间可转移的触觉表征。UniTouch 与 [41] 将异构触觉观测数据与预训练的视觉嵌入进行对齐，Sparsh 与 [10] 通过自监督预训练学习通用触觉特征，UniT [40] 采用生成式重建方法，而 T3 与 [46] 则致力于实现不同异构触觉传感器及下游任务之间的迁移。 近期的方法越来越多地利用触觉的时间结构：AnyTouch2与[9]模型在短暂的观察历史中处理动态且受力量影响的触觉信息，而基于运动的方法则进一步利用触觉变形动态来描述不断变化的接触状态 [39]。这些进展促使我们在统一的策略接口下对通用视觉、特定触觉以及时间感知表示进行比较。

Early tactile representation learning often exploits paired visual–tactile observations to learn cross-modal features. Touch and Go [ 42 ] supports self-supervised learning from human-collected visual–tactile interactions, while SSVTP [ 14 ] and Visuo-Tactile Transformers [ 6 ] further explore cross-modal representations for perception and manipulation. Subsequent work shifts toward more transferable tactile representations across sensors and tasks. UniTouch [ 41 ] aligns heterogeneous tactile observations with pretrained visual embeddings, Sparsh [ 10 ] learns general-purpose tactile features through self-supervised pretraining, UniT [ 40 ] adopts generative reconstruction, and T3 [ 46 ] targets transfer across heterogeneous tactile sensors and downstream tasks. More recent methods increasingly exploit the temporal structure of touch: AnyTouch2 [ 9 ] models dynamic and force-aware tactile information over short observation histories, while motion-based approaches further leverage tactile deformation dynamics to characterize evolving contact states [ 39 ] . These developments motivate our comparison of generic visual, tactile-specific, and temporally-aware representations under a common policy interface.

A. 2 仿真与真实世界机器人学习

仿真提供了一种可扩展的方法，用于生成交互数据并评估机器人策略。最近的触觉平台与基准测试，包括 Tabero [37]、SoftVTBench [13]、UniVTAC、[3] 以及 NeoSim 和 [31]，进一步将仿真技术拓展至触觉操作领域。然而，这些仿真任务通常并非真实世界环境的直接对应物，因此存在物体、场景布局、任务配置及初始条件方面的差异。另一项相关研究则通过真实环境与仿真环境的对齐来减少这种差异：SIMPLER [19] 对仿真评估结果进行与真实世界策略行为的校准；RialTo [35] 用于构建用于策略优化的数字孪生；SimFoundry [30] 则自动化地创建与任务相关的数字模型。 这些研究强调了保持模拟环境与真实世界之间与任务相关的对应关系的重要性，而非完全复制物理场景。近期研究进一步表明，适当对齐的模拟数据可以通过模拟与真实场景的协同训练来补充真实演示效果。 [24, 17] 基于这些发现，我们构建了对齐的模拟与物理触觉操作任务，并研究模拟演示能否提升现实世界的视觉-触觉策略学习。

Simulation provides a scalable way to generate interaction data and evaluate robot policies. Recent tactile platforms and benchmarks, including Tabero [ 37 ] , SoftVTBench [ 13 ] , UniVTAC [ 3 ] , and NeoSim [ 31 ] , further extend simulation to tactile manipulation. However, their simulated tasks are generally not constructed as direct counterparts of real-world settings, leaving differences in objects, scene layouts, task configurations, and initial conditions. A complementary line of work reduces such discrepancies through real-to-sim alignment: SIMPLER [ 19 ] calibrates simulated evaluation against real-world policy behavior, RialTo [ 35 ] reconstructs digital twins for policy improvement, and SimFoundry [ 30 ] automates the construction of task-relevant digital cousins. These studies highlight the importance of preserving task-relevant correspondence between simulation and the real world rather than reproducing physical scenes exactly. Recent studies further show that appropriately aligned simulated data can complement real demonstrations through sim–real co-training [ 24 , 17 ] . Motivated by these findings, we construct aligned simulated and physical tactile-manipulation tasks and study whether simulated demonstrations can improve real-world visuo-tactile policy learning.

B. 2 仿真与真实环境

OpenViTac 构建可扩展的视觉-触觉仿真环境，并配套相应的真实世界任务实例，以实现一致的策略评估。这些仿真环境由 NVIDIA 开发。 Isaac Sim 5.1.0 以及 Isaac Lab 2.3.0 ，通过基于 TacEx 的触觉模拟生成触觉观测数据 [27] 该平台支持三种典型的触觉传感器，包括 GelSight , Xense 以及 NeoteAI，还结合了两种广泛使用的夹爪配置：Franka手与Robotiq夹爪。

OpenViTac builds scalable visuo-tactile simulation environments with corresponding real-world task instantiations for consistent policy evaluation. The simulation environments are developed upon NVIDIA Isaac Sim 5.1.0 and Isaac Lab 2.3.0, with tactile observations generated using TacEx-based tactile simulation [ 27 ] . The platform supports three representative tactile sensors, including GelSight, Xense, and NeoteAI, together with two widely used gripper configurations: the Franka Hand and Robotiq gripper.

为了生成真实的视觉-触觉观测结果，OpenViTac 将视觉资源构建与触觉模拟分离开来。虽然 TacEx 通过接触交互实现基于物理的触觉渲染，但它并未直接模拟物体的完整视觉外观，例如各种纹理和颜色。因此，我们独立构建视觉上真实的物体资源，并利用共享物体几何结构将其与 TacEx 兼容的触觉表示关联起来。这种设计使得同一模拟对象能够产生一致的 RGB 和触觉观测结果。

To generate realistic visuo-tactile observations, OpenViTac separates visual asset construction from tactile simulation. While TacEx provides physics-based tactile rendering through contact interaction, it does not directly model the full visual appearance of objects, such as diverse textures and colors. Therefore, we construct visually realistic object assets independently and associate them with TacEx-compatible tactile representations using shared object geometry. This design enables consistent RGB and tactile observations from the same simulated objects.

为了进行实际场景评估，我们采用与模拟阶段相同的机器人本体、夹持器配置、触觉传感器及任务目标来执行相应的任务。这种一致性确保了模拟与现实之间的交互语义一致，从而能够对不同策略进行有意义的比较。

For real-world evaluation, we instantiate the corresponding tasks using the same robot embodiments, gripper configurations, tactile sensors, and task objectives as in simulation. Such correspondence preserves consistent interaction semantics between simulation and reality, enabling meaningful comparison of policies across both environments.

为了促进不同机器人学习框架下的策略部署与比较，OpenViTac 提供了标准化的机器人状态与动作接口。该接口支持末端执行器空间与关节空间两种表示方式，包含多种旋转参数化形式，如四元数、旋转矩阵和旋转向量等。这些表示方式在模拟环境与真实环境中均保持一致性，从而能够与采用不同动作与状态模型的预训练操作策略相兼容。

To facilitate policy deployment and comparison across different robot learning frameworks, OpenViTac provides standardized robot state and action interfaces. Both end-effector-space and joint-space representations are supported, including multiple rotation parameterizations such as quaternions, rotation matrices, and rotation vectors. These representations are consistently provided in both simulation and real-world environments, enabling compatibility with pretrained manipulation policies that adopt different action and state formulations.

附录 D 额外实验结果

我们进一步探讨了可能有助于触觉表征有效性的两个因素：与触觉相关的预训练以及时间背景。所有变体均保持相同的 OpenVTLA 架构和 token 级集成接口，仅改变所研究的因素。

We further examine two factors that may contribute to the effectiveness of tactile representations: tactile-specific pretraining and temporal context. All variants retain the same OpenVTLA architecture and token-level integration interface, changing only the factor under study.

触觉预训练的影响。表 S5 将预训练的 AnyTouch2 编码器与从零开始初始化的结构相同的编码器进行了比较。去除触觉预训练后，整体性能从 68.7%68.7\% 降至 56.5%56.5\%，且所有四个能力组均出现一致的性能下降。最显著的下降出现在“脆弱性感知”（FA）任务上，而在“属性感知”（PP）和“接触密集”（CR）任务上也观察到了显著的性能下降。这些结果表明，针对触觉特性的预训练能够产生有效的表征，这些表征能够有效应用于后续的 VLA 适应过程。

Overall, these results indicate that tactile dynamics provide useful information beyond static contact appearance, particularly for tasks that depend on physical-property and contact-state estimation.
