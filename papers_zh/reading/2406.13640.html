摘要

本文提出 T3，即可迁移触觉 Transformer，一个能扩展到多种传感器与任务的触觉表征学习框架。相机式触觉感知的异构性较强，传感器形态各异，现有数据集也面向不同任务。T3 针对这一问题设计。T3 以传感器专用编码器、共享 Transformer 主干和任务专用解码器，提取不同传感器与任务组合中的共通潜在信息。预训练 T3 使用新建的基础触觉数据集 FoTa。该数据集汇集多个开源数据集，包含逾 3 百万个数据点，来自 13 种传感器，覆盖 11 项任务。 FoTa 是当时触觉感知领域规模最大、种类最丰富的数据集，并以统一格式公开。跨传感器和跨任务实验表明，在 FoTa 上预训练的 T3 能在部分传感器与任务组合中实现 zero-shot 迁移，也可以用少量特定领域数据继续微调。网络规模扩大时，性能随之提升。T3 也可充当长时程接触密集操作的触觉编码器。亚毫米级多针电子元件插装实验表明，采用 T3 的策略，其成功率较从头训练触觉编码器的策略高 25%，较无触觉感知的策略高 53%。数据、代码和模型检查点已在 https://t3.alanz.info 公开。

This paper presents T3 : Transferable Tactile Transformers, a framework for tactile representation learning that scales across multi-sensors and multi-tasks. T3 is designed to overcome the contemporary issue that camera-based tactile sensing is extremely heterogeneous , i.e. sensors are built into different form factors, and existing datasets were collected for disparate tasks. T3 captures the shared latent information across different sensor-task pairings by constructing a shared trunk transformer with sensor-specific encoders and task-specific decoders. The pre-training of T3 utilizes a novel Foundation Tactile ( FoTa ) dataset, which is aggregated from several open-sourced datasets and it contains over 3 million data points gathered from 13 sensors and 11 tasks. FoTa is the largest and most diverse dataset in tactile sensing to date and it is made publicly available in a unified format. Across various sensors and tasks, experiments show that T3 pre-trained with FoTa achieved zero-shot transferability in certain sensor-task pairings, can be further fine-tuned with small amounts of domain-specific data, and its performance scales with bigger network sizes. T3 is also effective as a tactile encoder for long horizon contact-rich manipulation. Results from sub-millimeter multi-pin electronics insertion tasks show that T3 achieved a task success rate 25% higher than that of policies trained with tactile encoders trained from scratch, or 53% higher than without tactile sensing. Data, code, and model checkpoints are open-sourced at https://t3.alanz.info .

1 引言

触觉感知方式在机器人学领域越来越受到重视，它能够提供重要的精细接触信息，有助于实现灵活且接触丰富的操作任务，例如机器人抓取任务 [1, 2] 和织物操作任务 [3, 4]。基于摄像头的触觉感知方式 [5] 是一种通过将摄像头嵌入软弹性体下方来捕捉与环境之间的精细交互的技术，因其高分辨率与低成本而成为最流行的触觉感知方法之一。然而，基于摄像头的触觉传感器具有极大的异质性，目前尚未出现被机器人学界广泛采用的统一传感器设计。不同的触觉传感器在摄像头形状、类型与数量、安装位置以及照明颜色等方面存在显著差异。 这种固有的异质性使得机器人学家难以构建一种通用型触觉编码器，使其能够在不同的传感器和下游任务中通用应用。

The tactile sensing modality has gained increasing popularity within the robotics community, by providing important fine-grained contact information for dexterous and contact-rich manipulation tasks, such as robot grasping [ 1 , 2 ] and fabric manipulation [ 3 , 4 ] . Camera-based tactile sensing [ 5 ] , a sensing method that operates by embedding a camera beneath a soft elastomer to capture the fine-grained interactions with the environment, is among the most popular methods of tactile sensing for its higher resolution and lower cost. However, camera-based tactile sensors are extremely heterogeneous , and there has not been a converged sensor design widely adopted by the robotics community. Different tactile sensors can differ significantly in shapes, types and numbers of cameras, placement and colors of illumination, etc. Such inherent heterogeneity hinders roboticists from building a general-purpose tactile encoder that is transferable across different sensors and downstream tasks.

现有的学习架构与数据集都专注于特定的传感器-任务组合，当遇到新出现的传感器或任务时，往往需要从头开始进行数据收集与编码器训练。在长期视角的触觉操作任务中，这一问题对学习效率的负面影响更为显著，因为触觉编码器的训练所依赖的奖励信号更为稀疏。从直观角度来看，尽管不同的触觉传感器产生的触觉图像差异巨大，且不同任务从触觉输入中获取的信息也各不相同，但由于传感器与任务之间的触觉感知具有内在相似性，因此应该存在可共享的潜在信息。因此，设计一种能够提取此类共享潜在表示并将其跨不同传感器-任务组合之间传递的架构，在技术上可行，且在实践上也是非常必要的。

Existing learning architectures and datasets focus on one specific sensor-task pairing, and when it comes to a newly emerged sensor or task, data recollection and training an encoder from scratch are often required. This issue harms learning efficiency in a more significant way in longer horizon tactile manipulation tasks, where the training of the tactile encoder is guided by a more sparse reward. Intuitively, although different tactile sensors produce vastly different tactile images and various tasks extract different information from a tactile input, there should be shareable latent information due to the inherent similarities in tactile sensing across sensors and tasks. Therefore, it is both technically viable and practically desirable to design an architecture capable of extracting such shared latent representations and transferring them across different sensor-task pairings.

Learning from heterogeneous tactile inputs shares many similarities with multi-modal representation learning. Recent works in multi-modal foundation models have demonstrated the potential capability to bridge the gap between different modalities and learning a common latent representation, such as in the perception domain [ 6 , 7 , 8 ] as well as the robotic manipulation domain [ 9 , 10 ] . However, learning such foundation models often requires temporally aligned multi-modal inputs or a distance function that describes the resemblance between inputs from different modalities. In heterogeneous tactile learning , the hardware-level disparity often makes it ill-defined to align images collected from different embodiments.

2 相关研究

基于摄像头的触觉传感器

基于摄像头的触觉传感器通常采用柔软弹性体作为接触介质，并包含反射层、照明光源以及一个或多个摄像头，用于捕捉弹性体的形变，从而能够获得接触表面的详细图像。基于摄像头的触觉传感器在设计上具有极大的多样性，例如单色 [11] 与彩色 [12, 13, 14] 的照明方式，有无标记的 [15, 16, 17] 等不同的形态，以及平面 [12, 13]、圆顶形 [18, 14]、手指形 [19, 20]、柔顺型 [21, 22]、多连接型 [23] 甚至手掌形 [24] 等不同的结构形式。这种多样性和差异性使得研究人员能够在多种应用中使用这些传感器，例如缺陷检测 [25] 和机器人工具使用 [9] 等场景。 然而，它们也构成了利用机器学习对传感器所提供信息进行编码的重大障碍。独特的传感器-任务组合通常需要重新收集数据并重新训练神经网络。

Camera-based tactile sensors are often constructed with a soft elastomer as the contact medium, a reflective layer, a light source to provide illumination, and one or more than one camera to capture the deformation of the elastomer, which therefore provides a detailed view of the contact surface. Specific design choices in camera-based tactile sensors are highly diverse, such as monochrome [ 11 ] v.s. colored [ 12 , 13 , 14 ] in illumination, with v.s. without markers [ 15 , 16 , 17 ] , and different form factors such as flat [ 12 , 13 ] , dome-shaped [ 18 , 14 ] , finger-shaped [ 19 , 20 ] , compliant [ 21 , 22 ] , multi-linked [ 23 ] or even palm-shaped [ 24 ] . Such diversity and disparity have enabled researchers to use those sensors across a spectrum of applications such as in defect inspection [ 25 ] and robotic tool-using [ 9 ] . Nevertheless, they also add a substantial barrier in leveraging machine learning to encode the information coming from those sensors. A unique sensor-task pairing often requires data re-collection and neural network re-training.

触觉操作数据集

近年来，多项大规模触觉感知数据集已被发布，这些数据集致力于解决视觉-触觉跨模态推理 [26, 27, 28]、物体或材料分类 [28, 29, 4]、如机器人抓取稳定性分析 [2] 或形状重建 [30] 等复杂任务。这些数据集还涵盖了与多种物体的交互，包括家庭用品和玩具 [2, 26, 27, 28, 30]、织物和衣物 [4]，以及木材和碎石等自然物体 [29]。从数据量来看，其中一些数据集包含超过一百万张图像 [26, 30]，使得可用的触觉图像总数与 ImageNet- 1 K [31] 相当。

Several large-scale tactile sensing datasets have been released in recent years tackling visual-tactile cross-modality reasoning [ 26 , 27 , 28 ] , object or material classification [ 28 , 29 , 4 ] , longer horizon tasks such as robotic grasping stability analysis [ 2 ] , or shape reconstruction [ 30 ] . Those datasets also cover interactions with a wide variety of objects, including household objects and toys [ 2 , 26 , 27 , 28 , 30 ] , fabrics and clothes [ 4 ] , natural objects such as wood and gravel [ 29 ] . Quantity-wise, several of those datasets contain well over a million images [ 26 , 30 ] , making the total number of available tactile images on par with ImageNet-1K [ 31 ] .

在使用现有的开源触觉数据集时，数据量通常并非问题，但一个显著缺陷是上述所有数据集在传感器与任务方面均缺乏多样性。绝大多数触觉数据集都是仅通过一种特定的触觉传感器收集而成的。受此限制，即使能够从某个数据集中训练出性能良好的触觉编码器，该预训练的编码器也无法应用于其他传感器。为了像 [32, 33] 在自然图像领域那样学习通用的触觉感知表示，拥有大量且多样化的触觉数据集以及多种传感器与任务组合是必不可少的。

While quantity is often not an issue when using existing open-sourced tactile datasets, one significant drawback is that all the aforementioned datasets lack diversity in terms of sensors and tasks. The vast majority of tactile datasets were collected entirely with only one specific tactile sensor. With this constraint, even if a sufficiently well-performing tactile encoder can be trained from one dataset, such a pre-trained encoder will not be able to transfer to another sensor. In order to learn a general-purpose representation for tactile sensing like [ 32 , 33 ] does in the natural image domain, a large and diverse tactile dataset with a wide variety of sensor-task pairings is a prerequisite.

基于异构数据的表示学习

从不同的数据领域中学习，例如将多感官反馈作为输入，或针对不同任务预测输出，一直是机器人学习领域的研究热点。许多现有研究将触觉、视觉以及偶尔的其他模态（如机器人状态和声音）结合起来，通过每种模态经过特定模态编码器处理，然后将其串联起来，再通过解码器网络进行处理，以预测最终输出 [34, 15, 35, 9, 36]。除了基于学习的神经网络外，使用分析工具（如因子图或凸优化）来整合多个领域的预测结果也是常见的做法 [37]。

Learning from heterogeneous data domains, such as taking multi-sensory feedback as inputs or predicting outputs for different tasks, has been an active area of research, especially in the robot learning domain. Many existing works combine tactile, vision, and occasionally other modalities such as robot states and sound, by passing each modality through one modality-specific encoder, then concatenating them and passing them through a decoder network to predict the final output [ 34 , 15 , 35 , 9 , 36 ] . Apart from learning-driven neural networks, it is also common to use analytical tools, such as factor graphs or convex optimization, to combine predictions from multiple domains [ 37 ] .

另一类多模态机器人学习分支能够主动分析不同领域之间的关系，并将这些领域相互连接，从而学习出可共享的潜在表示。大量研究探索了使用对比学习来整合时间上对齐的多模态数据 [6, 10, 38]。为了实现视觉与触觉的融合，Chen等人设计了多模态自注意力机制 [39] 以学习共享表示；而Li等人则提出了跨模态生成对抗网络，能够从视觉图像预测触觉图像，反之亦然 [26]。Bachmann等人将掩蔽自编码技术应用于对齐的 RGB 图像、深度图及语义分割数据，从而在三个领域之间学习出共享表示 [7]。 然而，尽管跨模态表征学习与异构触觉表征学习具有许多共同点，但一个重要区别在于，异构触觉表征学习缺乏针对不同任务由不同传感器收集的对齐数据。从不同硬件设备采集到的两种触觉图像之间的相似性往往难以明确界定，而这正是许多自监督学习技术的先决条件。因此，设计一种能够从非对齐触觉数据中学习并生成可跨不同触觉传感器及不同任务转移的表征的新架构显得十分必要。

Another branch of multi-modal robot learning actively reasons the relationship among heterogeneous domains and cross-links them to learn a shareable latent representation. Extensive research has explored using contrastive learning to join temporally aligned modalities [ 6 , 10 , 38 ] . To connect vision and touch, Chen et al. designed a multi-modal self-attention mechanism [ 39 ] to learn a shared representation, while Li et al. proposed a cross-modal Generative Adversarial Network and were able to predict tactile images from visual images and vice versa [ 26 ] . Bachmann et al. applied masked auto-encoding (MAE) on aligned RGB images, depth maps, and semantic segmentation and learned a shared representation across three domains [ 7 ] . However, although cross-modality representation learning shares a lot of commonalities with heterogeneous tactile representation learning, one significant difference is that heterogeneous tactile representation learning lacks aligned data collected with different sensors for different tasks. It is often ill-defined to infer the resemblance of two tactile images collected from different hardware, which is a prerequisite for many self-supervised learning techniques. It is therefore desirable to devise a new architecture that is able to learn from unaligned tactile data and produce a representation that is transferable across different tactile sensors and different tasks.

4 具有异质触觉的学习 T3

我们提出了可迁移触觉变换器（T3）作为骨干网络，以从多种触觉传感器中学习，并为多种下游任务生成输出。

We propose Transferable Tactile Transformers ( T3 ) as the backbone network to learn from diverse tactile sensors and produce outputs for diverse downstream tasks.

网络架构

训练数据集包含来自不同传感器 NsN_{s} 的输入数据以及 NtN_{t} 不同的任务。T3 由 NsN_{s} 编码器 {E​n​c1,…,E​n​cNs}\{Enc_{1},...,Enc_{N_{s}}\} 构成，每个触觉传感器对应一个编码器；同时还有 NtN_{t} 解码器 {D​e​c1,…,D​e​cNt}\{Dec_{1},...,Dec_{N_{t}}\}，每个解码器负责一个下游任务；此外还有一个共享的主干网络，记为 T​r​u​n​kTrunk。在训练与推理过程中，我们确保每一批数据都来自相同的传感器-任务组合。对于从传感器 ii 收集到的用于任务 jj 的数据点 (Xi,Yj)(X_{i},Y_{j})，相应的编码器 E​n​ciEnc_{i} 与解码器 D​e​cjDec_{j} 会连接到主干网络。T3 中的各个网络组件可以采用不同的架构。实际上，我们使用 ViT [41] 构建主干网络及编码器，因为它们在自然图像领域具有出色的学习能力与可扩展性。 对于解码器，我们采用 ViT 用于生成任务（MAE 重建任务），ResNet+ MLP 用于姿态估计任务，而 MLP 则用于分类任务。关于网络配置的更多细节，请参见附录 A.5。

A training dataset contains inputs from N s different sensors and N t distinguished tasks. T3 is constructed with N s encoders { E ​ n ​ c 1 , … , E ​ n ​ c N s } , one for each individual tactile sensor; N t decoders { D ​ e ​ c 1 , … , D ​ e ​ c N t } , each responsible for one downstream task; and one shared trunk, denoted as T ​ r ​ u ​ n ​ k . During both training and inference, we ensure that one batch of data always comes from the same sensor-task pairing. For a given pair of data point ( X i , Y j ) that is collected from sensor i for task j , the corresponding encoder E ​ n ​ c i and decoder D ​ e ​ c j are attached to the trunk. The constituent network components of T3 can take different architectures. In practice, we use ViT [ 41 ] in the trunk and encoders for its proven learning capacity and scalability in the natural image domain. For decoders, we use ViT for generative tasks (reconstruction for MAE), ResNet+MLP for pose estimation tasks, and MLP for classification tasks. More details about network configurations are discussed in Appendix A.5 .

T3的训练过程分为两个预训练阶段和一个可选的微调阶段。预训练被分为两个阶段，旨在提高数据利用率，并实现局部细粒度理解与全局语义理解。1阶段侧重于使用自监督学习进行更精细的像素级理解，能够充分利用 FoTa 数据集中的所有数据。2阶段则侧重于语义理解，需要特定的任务标签，而此类标签仅存在于 FoTa 数据集的某一部分中。

The training process of T3 is split into two pre-training phases and one optional fine-tuning phase. Pre-training is divided into two phases to improve data utilization and to attain both local fine-grained understanding and global semantic understanding. Phase 1 focuses on more pixel-level understanding using self-supervised learning, and it is able to utilize all data from the FoTa dataset. Phase 2 focuses on semantic understanding and it requires suitable task labels that only a subset of the FoTa dataset possesses.

预训练 I：基于遮蔽自动编码的自监督学习 (MAE)

MAE 最初被提出作为一种用于自然图像的自监督预训练技术 [42]，如今在音频处理 [8]、点云处理 [43] 以及多模态表示学习 [7] 等领域也获得了广泛应用。其运作机制为：首先随机遮蔽原始输入中的某一部分，然后构建解码器以重建缺失部分。使用 MAE 进行预训练的神经网络在多种领域中的表现均优于未使用 MAE 训练的神经网络。此预训练阶段能够充分利用 FoTa 中的所有数据，包括未标记数据（例如机器人抓取过程中沉淀期收集的中间触觉图像 [2]），或难以定义损失函数的有语义标签的数据（例如场景的自然语言描述 [27]）。

MAE was first proposed as a self-supervised pre-training technique for natural images [ 42 ] and it has gained increased popularity in other domains such as audio processing [ 8 ] , point-cloud processing [ 43 ] , and multi-modal representation learning [ 7 ] . It operates by first randomly masking out a certain portion of the original inputs, then it constructs a decoder to reconstruct the missing portion. Neural networks that were pre-trained with MAE have been shown to be able to outperform ones that were trained without MAE in various domains. This pre-training stage is able to utilize all data in FoTa , including unlabeled data (e.g. intermediate tactile images collected during the settling period of a robot grasp in [ 2 ] ), or data with semantic labels for which loss functions are hard to define (e.g. natural language description of a scene in [ 27 ] ).

5.2 Zero-shot 预训练 T3 的可迁移性至新传感器或新任务

我们测试了预训练后的 T3 在有无微调的情况下能否被迁移到新的传感器或下游任务中。我们选择了两个新的传感器（GelSight Svelte、DensetTact2. 0）以及两个已见过的数据集任务（物体分类、两个重叠触觉图像之间的姿态估计）。这两个已见过的数据集任务是通过 GelSight Wedge、GelSight Finray 和 GelSight Mini 的数据进行预训练的。在实验中，我们使用 GelSight Wedge 的预训练编码器作为 GelSight Svelte 的编码器，并用 GelSight Mini 的编码器替代 DensetTact2. 0 的编码器。4 组合的相关结果分别展示在图 3.c 和图 3.d 中。Zero-shot 的迁移仅在物体分类任务中带来了微小的改进，但相较于数据集平均值，在姿态估计任务中则表现出显著的提升。 我们进一步使用少量 2,000 数据点对网络进行微调。经过微调后，分类准确率立即提升到了 17% 水平，而 DenseTact2 的姿态估计均方根误差（RMSE）则降低了 5.5mm 和 0。在 GelSight Svelte 传感器上，微调前后的姿态估计误差几乎相同，且接近最优状态。这些结果表明，在某些传感器与任务配对中可以实现零迁移性，并且通过最小的微调就能获得更好的效果。

We tested to what extent a pre-trained T3 can be transferred to a new sensor or downstream task with and without fine-tuning. We picked two novel sensors (GelSight Svelte, DensetTact2.0) and two seen tasks (object classification, pose estimation between two overlapped tactile images). The two seen tasks were pre-trained with data from GelSight Wedge, GelSight Finray, and GelSight Mini. During this experiment, we use the encoder pre-trained for GelSight Wedge as the encoder for GelSight Svelte, and GelSight Mini’s encoder in place of DenseTact2.0’s encoder. Results on the 4 combinations are shown in Fig. 3 .c and Fig. 3 .d. Zero-shot transfer yielded only minor improvements over random guesses in the object classification task; however, it demonstrated significant improvement over dataset averages in the pose estimation task. We further fine-tuned the networks with a small amount of 2,000 data points. The classification accuracy immediately bounced up to 17% with fine-tuning, and the pose estimation root mean squared error (RMSE) was reduced by 5.5mm for DenseTact2.0. On the GelSight Svelte sensor, the pose estimation errors before and after fine-tuning were nearly identical and close to optimal. These results indicate that zero-transferability can be achieved in certain sensor-task pairings, and that better outcomes can be attained with minimal fine-tuning.

5.3 T3 在长视距操控任务中

在接触丰富的操作任务中，触觉感知特别具有帮助作用。大量人类研究表明，触觉对于人类完成精细操作任务至关重要，例如手术 [46, 47]。然而，由于缺乏预训练的触觉编码器，目前最先进的触觉操作研究通常都采用从零开始训练触觉编码器，并使用特定传感器与任务组合收集的新数据。在长距离多模态操作任务中，从零开始训练往往效率低下，因为轨迹奖励较少，且需要训练的组件数量较多（例如不同模态编码器与策略网络），尤其是在许多强化学习和行为克隆应用中，如 [48, 9]。

Tactile sensing can be especially helpful in contact-rich manipulation tasks. Numerous human studies have shown that the sense of touch is important for humans to perform delicate manipulation tasks such as surgery [ 46 , 47 ] . However, most state-of-the-art tactile manipulation research opted to train a tactile encoder from scratch, with fresh data collected with the specific sensor-task pairing, due to the lack of a pre-trained tactile encoder. In the case of long-horizon multi-modal manipulation tasks, training from scratch is often inefficient, due to the sparseness of trajectory rewards and the number of components that need to be trained (e.g. modality encoders and policy networks) especially in many reinforcement learning and behavior cloning applications such as [ 48 , 9 ]

为了探究预训练的 T3 是否有助于填补这一空白，我们设计了一项基于行为克隆的机器人精密插入任务。该任务的目标是将 3 电子元件插入 PCB 中：包括 3 引脚切换开关、12 引脚双叠式 USB 接口以及 17 引脚 VGA 连接器，这些元件需插入具有对应安装孔位的 PCB 中。此任务需要极高的精度，因为 PCB 上的孔与元件引脚之间的间隙仅为 0.4​m​m0.4mm。实现这种精度需要通过触觉反馈下的主动探索。在实际应用中，由于遮挡现象严重，仅依赖视觉识别往往不足。

To investigate whether a pre-trained T3 helps to bridge this gap, we designed a robotic precision insertion task with behavior cloning. The goal of this task is to insert 3 electronics parts: a 3-pin toggle switch, a 12-pin double-stack USB port, and a 17-pin VGA connector, onto a PCB with corresponding mounting holes to each component. This task requires high precision, where the clearance between the holes on the PCB and the pins on the parts is only 0.4 ​ m ​ m . Achieving this precision requires active exploration with tactile feedback. In real-world applications, relying on vision alone is often insufficient due to heavy occlusion.

6 局限性与未来研究工作

当前训练流程的一个局限性是 FoTa 数据集存在不平衡问题。收集的数据为 2 最流行的传感器数量超过 50% 占整个数据集的比例。因此，训练出的策略也可能倾向于那些更受欢迎的传感器。另一个限制是，当前的架构存在…… T3 专注于单张图像编码 T3 在预训练 II 和微调阶段，通过明确的标签进行训练。使用稀疏或隐式标签对触觉图像序列进行表征学习，可能成为本研究的未来方向。此外， T3 目前仅限于学习基于摄像头的触觉传感器的表征。扩展 FoTa 数据集及... T3 非相机式传感器的框架设计是未来的另一研究方向。

One limitation of the current training pipeline is that the FoTa dataset is unbalanced. Data collected with the 2 most popular sensors constitutes over 50% of the entire dataset. Therefore, the trained policy could also be biased towards those more popular sensors. Another limitation is that the current architecture of T3 focuses on per-image encoding and T3 is trained with explicit labels during pre-training II and fine-tuning. Representation learning on tactile image sequences with sparse or implicit labels can be a future direction of this work. Furthermore, T3 is currently limited to learning representations for camera-based tactile sensors. Extending the FoTa dataset and the T3 framwork to non-camera-based sensors is another future line of work.

7 结论

本文提出了 T3，这是一个基于摄像头的触觉感知框架，能够跨多种传感器与任务进行迁移；同时还有 FoTa 触觉数据集，该数据集在数据量及传感器与下游任务多样性方面均处于最高水平。实验表明，使用 FoTa 预训练的 T3 在多种任务中的表现得到了提升，包括长视距操作任务，例如亚毫米级电子元件插入操作；而 T3 在无需大量微调的情况下即可成功迁移到新的传感器-任务组合中。

This paper presents T3 , a large camera-based tactile sensing framework that transfers across multiple sensors and tasks; and FoTa , a tactile dataset that is by far the largest in quantity and most diverse in sensors and downstream tasks. Experiments show that T3 pre-trained with FoTa improves performance for various tasks, including longer-horizon manipulation tasks such as sub-millimeter electronics insertion, and that T3 transfers to a new sensor-task pairing with little fine-tuning.

A. 4.1 7 - DoF 机器人平台

We built a robotic data collection platform with a 7-DoF Franka Emika robotic arm with a custom-made parallel jaw gripper attached at the end-effector.
