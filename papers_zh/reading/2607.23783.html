摘要

我们提出 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-TWAM，这是一种面向富接触操作的原生触觉世界—动作模型（world-action model），能够预测未来画面和接触状态。据我们所知，它是首个接受大规模训练的触觉世界—动作模型（world-action model），在富接触任务中表现出较强能力。我们用包含丰富触觉信息的示教对 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-TWAM 进行大规模视触觉联合预训练，数据涵盖六种本体和 450450 项任务。我们使用基于力的统一触觉表征 NeoForce，使反映物理接触的信号成为生成动作的条件。为改善长程、多阶段操作，我们用触觉接触事件划分任务阶段，并在执行期间根据事件推进任务。 为提高实时性，我们在 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-TWAM 中采用不对称的 Transformer 混合架构。全宽专家处理多样的视频，宽度较小的动作与触觉专家处理相应的下游任务。真机和仿真基准表明，𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-TWAM 能够完成多种富接触任务，增加数据规模有助于更精确地预测触觉与动作。总体而言，𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-TWAM 为世界—动作模型（world-action model）增加联合预测视觉、触觉与动作的能力，为开放的富接触任务中的精细操作提供基础。我们将公开 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-TWAM 的代码和模型检查点，以推动触觉机器人操作研究。

We present 𝒩 0 -TWAM , a tactile-native world-action model for contact-rich manipulation that predicts both future vision and contact. To our knowledge, it is the first tactile world-action model trained at large scale, and it demonstrates strong capability on contact-rich tasks. We pre-train 𝒩 0 -TWAM at large scale with visuo-tactile joint training over tactile-rich demonstrations, spanning six embodiments and 450 tasks. We use NeoForce, a unified force-based tactile representation to form a physically grounded contact signal as condition for action generation. To boost long-horizon and multi-stage manipulation, we introduce tactile contact events for task staging and advance through them during execution. For real-time efficiency, we adopt an asymmetric Mixture-of-Transformers architecture on 𝒩 0 -TWAM , which pairs a full-width expert for versatile videos and slim architecture for downstream action and tactile experts. Evaluations on both real and simulated benchmarks justify the powerful capabilities of 𝒩 0 -TWAM in various contact-rich tasks, as well as demonstrate the benefit of data scaling on precise tactile and action prediction. In summary, 𝒩 0 -TWAM endows a world-action model with predictive capabilities to foresee vision, tactile and action, building a solid foundation for fine-grained manipulation on open contact-rich tasks. The codebase and model checkpoints of 𝒩 0 -TWAM will be made publicly available to foster further research and development in tactile-enabled robotic manipulation.

1 引言

在接触密集的场景中，很难学到通用操作策略。把螺丝拧到位、从一摞杯子中分离出一个，或者用夹爪抓住柔软物体，成败主要取决于指尖正在发生什么。相机往往看不清这些细节，力或触觉传感器可以直接读到。要在这类场景中做出合适的动作（act），策略需要两种纯视觉运动回归难以自然具备的能力：感知触觉，以及预判接下来一小段时间内交互会怎样发展。

Learning a general manipulation policy is hard in contact-rich settings. Seating a screw, peeling one cup off a stack, or closing a gripper on a soft object are decided less by the global layout of the scene than by what is happening at the fingertips, a distinction that a camera can rarely resolve but that a force or tactile sensor reports directly. A policy that is to act well in these regimes therefore needs two things that pure visuomotor regression does not naturally provide: access to touch, and the ability to anticipate how the immediate future of an interaction will unfold rather than reacting only to the present frame.

近期两条研究路线分别提供了其中一种能力。Vision-language-action（VLA）模型给大型预训练视觉语言模型加装动作头，把观测映射为动作。它继承了广泛的语义先验，却把丰富的当前视觉信息压缩为低维动作，没有显式建模接下来会发生什么 [Black et al., 2025b] 。视频世界模型则学习观测未来会怎样变化；越来越多的世界–动作模型把视频预测器和动作生成结合起来，让控制建立在所预测的未来之上 [Li et al., 2026c, Ye et al., 2026c] 。如果模型能预见下一时刻的后果，就有机会避开不理想的动作，这对接触密集型控制有吸引力。不过这类模型所预测的未来几乎都是视觉的。

Two recent lines of work each supply one of these ingredients. Vision-language-action (VLA) models attach an action head to a large pretrained vision-language model and map observations to actions, inheriting broad semantic priors but compressing a rich visual present into a low-dimensional action with no explicit model of what happens next [ Black et al., 2025b ] . Video world models, on the other hand, learn to roll the future of an observation forward in time, and a growing body of world-action models couples such a video predictor with action so that control is grounded in a predicted future [ Li et al., 2026c , Ye et al., 2026c ] . The latter framing is attractive for contact-rich control (if the model can predict the next moment, it can decide against it), yet the predicted future of these models is almost always purely visual.

已有三类方法开始引入触觉。第一类触觉策略只把触觉作为输入，从不预测触觉 [Huang et al., 2025b] 。第二类给策略外挂一个独立、通常冻结的触觉预测器，在执行动作的网络之外完成预测 [Ye et al., 2025, Zang et al., 2026] 。同期的感知触觉的 world-action models 将触觉纳入模型，却用门控或掩码限制触觉 tokens ，以保护视觉流 [Lou et al., 2026, Wu et al., 2026a, Zheng et al., 2026] 。这几条路线都没有把触觉作为主模型联合预测的一部分。缺少的是一个在同一训练目标、同一因果步骤中联合预测视觉和触觉的模型，以及一个以联合预测结果为依据生成动作的策略。

Touch is beginning to enter this picture, but so far along three partial paths. Tactile policies consume touch as an input channel and never predict it [ Huang et al., 2025b ] . A second family forecasts touch with a separate (often frozen) tactile predictor bolted onto the policy, so prediction occurs happens outside the network that acts [ Ye et al., 2025 , Zang et al., 2026 ] . And concurrent touch-aware world-action models admit touch into the model but keep it at arm’s length, gating or masking tactile tokens to protect the visual stream [ Lou et al., 2026 , Wu et al., 2026a , Zheng et al., 2026 ] . On every path, touch is not part of what the model predicts. What is missing is a model that predicts touch together with vision, under the same objective and at the same causal step, and a policy that reads its actions from that jointly predicted future.

为解决这一问题，我们提出 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-TWAM（触觉 World-Action Model ）。据我们所知，这是首个经过大规模训练的触觉 world-action model 。𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-TWAM 以视频扩散 Transformer 为骨干，保留联合建模未来观测与动作的做法，把触觉纳入未来预测本身。骨干改为混合 Transformer（MoT）[Liang et al., 2025] ：视频、动作、触觉三个专家各有独立参数和模型容量，每层只通过一处共享自注意力交换信息。同期一些触觉模型限制注意力可读取的触觉信息，以保护视觉流；𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-TWAM 则通过各自独立的权重划分容量，保留视觉与触觉之间的充分注意。 由帧编号确定的因果次序，让共享注意力在一次前向计算内按「先预测、再执行（act）」的顺序工作。每个动作块里，视频和触觉专家共同生成未来场景与未来接触，动作专家再以刚预测出的信息为条件生成动作。未来接触来自触觉预测；动作专家还会通过交叉注意力读取此刻的触觉观测。策略因此能够结合当前实际感受到的接触，依据预期的触觉来决定动作。

We close this gap with 𝒩 0 -TWAM (Tactile World-Action Model), a world-model policy and, to our knowledge, the first tactile world-action model trained at large scale. 𝒩 0 -TWAM is built on a video-diffusion transformer backbone and keeps the world-action recipe of jointly modeling future observations and action, but it predicts tactile as part of the predicted future rather than treating it as a side input. The backbone is reorganized as a Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT) [ Liang et al., 2025 ] : three per-modality experts (video, action and tactile) each keeps their own weights and capacity and interact only through a single shared self-attention at every layer. Where concurrent touch-aware models protect their visual stream by restricting touch in attention , 𝒩 0 -TWAM isolates capacity in weights , so vision and touch stay fully attentive to one another. A frame-id causal schedule turns this shared attention into a “predict-then-act” cascade inside one forward pass: within a chunk the video and tactile experts co-generate the future scene and the future contact, and the action expert is conditioned on those just-predicted quantities. That future contact is the predicted tactile; as it acts, the action expert also reads the observed tactile, cross-attended in from the current reading. The policy therefore acts on touch it has already predicted, grounded in the touch it feels now.

如果把三种模态都做成全宽专家，最大组件的成本会增加到原来的三倍，而动作流和触觉流现有的大规模预训练不足以支撑这一成本。𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-TWAM 采用非对称设计：视频专家保留原始全宽，动作和触觉专家更窄，从头训练，两者只通过共享注意力间接利用视觉先验。这使参数量约为三个专家都取全宽时的一半，推理也因此省算力。每个块的未来视频和触觉预测完成后，模型缓存对应的注意力键和值，后续每一步动作去噪只需重跑轻量动作专家。

Treating three modalities as full experts would, however, triple the cost of the largest component, and the action and tactile streams have no large-scale pretraining to justify that cost. 𝒩 0 -TWAM instead uses an asymmetric design: the video expert keeps its full, original width, while the action and tactile experts are slim and trained from scratch, benefiting from the visual prior only indirectly through the shared attention. This halves the parameter count relative to an all-full-width design, and the same asymmetry keeps inference cheap: once a chunk’s video and tactile are predicted, their attention keys and values are cached, so each action-denoising step re-runs only the lightweight action expert rather than the full-width video expert.

Across simulated and real contact-rich benchmarks, 𝒩 0 -TWAM is the strongest method overall: it reaches 84.5 % on UniVTAC and 49.4 % on NeoSim in simulation, and averages 46.3 % across our eight-task real-robot suite, against 30.0 % for the strongest vision-language-action baseline and 21.9 % and 14.4 % for the vision-only world-action baselines. Ablations attribute the gains to both tactile roles (removing either the predicted foresight target or the observed conditioning degrades success), and under distribution shift the policy degrades more gracefully than vision-only world-action baselines. 2 模型

𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 是一种 world-action model：它并非根据观察结果回溯行为，而是学习一个生成模型，该模型能够将机器人未来将看到和感受到的事物按时间顺序推演，并据此预测出该行为。 三种选择使其与视频截然不同 world-action model . (i) 触觉与视觉被联合建模：未来的触觉信号在与未来视频相同的流匹配目标下，于相同的因果步骤中生成，因此模型直接预测触觉，而非从像素中推导出来。(ii) 容量由权重独立控制，而非注意力机制：每种模态拥有各自的专家模型，且这些专家共享单一的自注意力机制，因此视觉与触觉保持各自的权重独立，同时完全相互关注；而并发的触觉感知模型则通过门控或掩蔽机制来限制触觉信息。 tokens 用于保护视觉流。 (iii) 触摸具有双重作用：它既被提前预测为预测目标，又被当前观察为动作所呈现的观测结果。我们构建了该架构 (§ 2.1 ), 发展两种触觉角色（§） 2.2 ），并以触觉感知执行作为结尾（§） 2.3 ).

𝒩 0 -TWAM is a world-action model: rather than regress actions from observations, it learns one generative model that rolls the future the robot will see and feel forward in time and reads the action out of that predicted future. Three choices set it apart from a video world-action model. (i) Touch is modeled jointly with vision : the future tactile signal is generated under the same flow-matching objective as the future video, at the same causal step, so the model predicts touch directly rather than deriving it from pixels. (ii) Capacity is isolated in weights, not attention : each modality gets its own expert, and the experts share a single self-attention, so vision and touch keep private weights yet stay fully attentive to one another; concurrent touch-aware models instead gate or mask tactile tokens to protect the visual stream. (iii) Touch plays two roles : it is both predicted ahead of time, as a foresight target, and observed now, as an observation the action reads. We set up the architecture (§ 2.1 ), develop the two tactile roles (§ 2.2 ), and close with tactile-aware execution (§ 2.3 ).

扩散强制级联。

共享注意力由因果掩码 ℳ\mathcal{M} 控制，该掩码在单次前向传播过程中实现方程 ( 1 ) 的分解。两个索引按 token 的顺序排列：一个潜在帧位置和一个噪声状态。在一段中，其两个潜在帧占据连续位置；来自同一帧的视觉与触觉 tokens 共享一个位置，而动作则遵循其条件化的预测帧。在状态中，每个 token 继承其潜在帧组的噪声水平，在生成过程中具有噪声特性，一旦成为历史记录则变得纯净。在训练时，这些纯净的历史 tokens 即为真实历史：模型根据真实历史进行教师强制学习，仅对当前片段进行去噪，遵循扩散强制机制 [Chen et al., 2024]，在该机制下独立时间组噪声水平将教师强制行为泛化至连续 tokens。 作为鲁棒性度量，干净视频与触觉历史数据会以概率 0.50.5 被轻微重新加噪，从而使模型学会容忍在测试时不完美的承诺历史，而非真实值；动作历史则保持完全干净。该掩码遵循以下三条规则：(i) 在自身位置之前，干净 token 会关注干净 tokens；(ii) 带噪 token 会严格在自身位置之前关注干净历史；(iii) 带噪 token 会在自身位置关注带噪 tokens，即协同生成。

The shared attention is governed by a causal mask ℳ that realizes the factorization of Eq. ( 1 ) inside one forward pass. Two indices order every token: a latent-frame position and a noise state. Within a chunk, its two latent frames take consecutive positions; vision and touch tokens from the same frame share one position, and the action follows the predicted frames on which it conditions. In state, each token inherits the noise level of its latent-frame group and is noisy while it is being generated and clean once it has become history. At training time these clean history tokens are the ground-truth past: the model is teacher-forced on the true history and only denoises the current chunk, following the diffusion-forcing regime [ Chen et al., 2024 ] in which independent temporal-group noise levels generalize teacher forcing to continuous tokens. As a robustness measure, the clean video and tactile history is lightly re-noised with probability 0.5 , so the model learns to tolerate a committed history that at test time is imperfect rather than ground truth; the action history stays exactly clean. The mask then follows three rules: (i) a clean token attends to clean tokens at or before its position; (ii) a noisy token attends to the clean history strictly before its position; and (iii) a noisy token attends to noisy tokens at its own position, i.e. co-generation.

图 3 展示了该掩码对两个数据块的作用效果。方程 ( 1 ) 中的“预测-然后- act”级联结构完全由该掩码产生，注意力操作本身并未发生任何变化。由于每个时间组独立地获取噪声水平，训练过程涵盖了所有干净与噪声数据组的组合；推理时使用的两种模式（即具有噪声当前数据块的干净历史记录，以及具有仍为噪声的动作的干净预测帧）仅仅是这些组合中的两种而已。

2.2 触觉建模

我们使用的触觉传感器基于视觉技术：触觉传感器本质上就像一台小型相机，能够拍摄到在接触时发生变形的软凝胶，因此触觉感知本身便是一种 RGB 视频流。因此，𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 将场景视频与触觉感知视为同等重要的信息模态：相同的 VAE 编码了这两种信息，相同的生成目标也用于预测它们。

The tactile sensors we use are vision-based : a tactile sensor is essentially a small camera imaging a soft gel that deforms on contact, so a tactile observation is itself an RGB video stream. 𝒩 0 -TWAM therefore treats scene video and touch as equally important modalities: the same VAE encodes them and the same generative objective predicts them.

诸如安全抓取、初期滑脱或过度力等接触状态对后续动作具有决定性影响，却往往无法被外部场景摄像头捕捉到。因此，𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 赋予了触觉两种角色：一是模型所预测的未来状态（§ 2.2.1），二是策略在执行过程中所呈现的当前状态（§ 2.2.2）。

Contact states such as a secure grasp, incipient slip, or excessive force are decisive for the next action yet often invisible to the external scene cameras. 𝒩 0 -TWAM therefore gives touch two roles: a future the model predicts (§ 2.2.1 ) and a present the policy reads as it acts (§ 2.2.2 ).

2.2.1 预测未来接触行为

每条触觉流 VAE 编码 [Wan Team et al., 2025] 并以相同的方式进行补丁处理 (1,2,2)(1,2,2) 将补丁作为视频潜在表示，因此触摸作为普通潜在表示进入主干网络 tokens ; 一种学习到的传感器ID嵌入（最多四个传感器）使得一位专家能够保持单臂和双手数据的不变性。与视频共享潜在空间正是共享注意力具有意义的关键，因为视觉与触觉相互关联 tokens 直接实现可比性。不过，触觉仍然保留着私密性：触觉统计数据稀疏且由事件驱动，同时具备触觉感知能力的世界模型表明，注入此类数据可 tokens 转化为视觉动态模型后，视频与动作预测性能会下降 [Wu et al., 2026a] 在重量层面隔离触觉，而非限制其注意力，使得视觉与触觉能够保持相互关注，同时维持各自的运作能力。触觉专家也不会对文本产生交叉关注。

Each tactile stream is VAE-encoded [ Wan Team et al., 2025 ] and patchified with the same ( 1 , 2 , 2 ) patch as the video latent, so touch enters the backbone as ordinary latent tokens; a learned sensor-id embedding (up to four sensors) lets one expert serve single-arm and bimanual data unchanged. Sharing the latent space with video is what makes the shared attention meaningful, since vision and touch tokens become directly comparable. Touch nonetheless keeps private weights : tactile statistics are sparse and event-driven, and concurrent touch-aware world models report that injecting such tokens into a visual dynamics model degrades video and action prediction [ Wu et al., 2026a ] . Isolating touch at the weight level, rather than restricting it in attention , lets vision and touch stay mutually attentive while keeping separate capacity. The tactile expert also does not cross-attend to text.

分阶段训练。

我们在两个训练阶段中分别处理这两种角色（§ 4.1）。预训练仅使用预测结果，忽略实际观测数据：其目的在于大规模学习生成式世界模型，而预测的触觉潜变量则作为符合该目标的生成目标，迫使模型结合未来场景预测未来接触情况，从而在大规模语料库中学习接触动态。实际观测数据则不符合该目标，它仅作为条件输入而非生成目标，依赖于预训练的 NeoForce 编码器以及对应的当前触觉读数；最重要的是，在预训练过程中为策略提供真实的当前接触信息，使策略能够依赖该信号，从而补充其所需的预测能力。 训练结束后，会激活方程 ( 7 ) 所描述的路径，将其叠加在已学会预测未来接触行为的模型之上；§ 4.5 中的消融实验则分别考察了这两种贡献。

We separate the two roles across the two training stages (§ 4.1 ). Pre-training uses only the predicted stream, with the observed pathway off: its job is to learn a generative world model at scale, and the predicted tactile latent is a generation target that fits that objective, forcing the model to predict future contact jointly with the future scene and so learn contact dynamics across the large-scale corpus. The observed stream does not fit that objective. It is a conditioning input rather than a generation target, it depends on the pretrained NeoForce encoder and on paired current-tactile readings, and, most importantly, giving the policy the true current contact during pre-training would let it rely on that signal and under-train the foresight it is meant to complement. Post-training then switches on the observed pathway of Eq. ( 7 ), adding it on top of a model that has already learned to predict future contact; the ablations of § 4.5 separate the two contributions.

The encoder of the observed pathway is NeoForce , the tactile representation model of 𝒩 0 -Foundation [ NeoteAI Team and Fudan TEAI Team, 2026 ] , summarized here only as far as 𝒩 0 -TWAM needs it.

NeoForce 接收同步的 RGB 观测数据及触觉力映射，并在力空间中生成触觉表征。其触觉输入并非原始凝胶图像，而是由学习式估计器 gθg_{\theta} 生成的密集三轴表面力映射 F^=gθ​(T)∈ℝH×W×3\hat{F}=g_{\theta}(T)\in\mathbb{R}^{H\times W\times 3}。面内分量 [fx,fy][f_{x},f_{y}] 承载驱动滑动与抓取所需的剪切力，法向分量 fzf_{z} 承载按压压力，而二进制接触掩码则标记出表面被触碰的位置，为平行夹持器提供六个通道。由于这些单元是物理性的，而非与特定凝胶外观相关，因此一个编码器可同时服务于多个传感器 [Yuan et al., 2026a]。 视觉与触觉信号被独立地分割为多个片段，并通过由 DINOv2 权重初始化而成的共享 ViT 框架进行融合，从而在统一空间中处理视觉与触觉信息，该表示方式能够捕捉到不同区域间的接触变化过程。重建头模块则解析出力图与接触掩码，从而确保该表示与物理量保持关联。

NeoForce takes a synchronized chunk of RGB observations and tactile force maps and returns a tactile representation in force space . Its tactile input is not a raw gel image but a dense three-axis surface force map F ^ = g θ ​ ( T ) ∈ ℝ H × W × 3 produced by a learned estimator g θ . The in-plane components [ f x , f y ] carry the shear that drives sliding and grasping, the normal component f z carries the pressure of pressing, and a binary contact mask marks where the surface is touched, giving six channels for a parallel gripper. Because these units are physical rather than tied to the appearance of a particular gel, one encoder can serve different sensors [ Yuan et al., 2026a ] . The visual and tactile streams are patchified independently and fused by a shared ViT backbone initialized from DINOv2 weights, so vision and touch are read in a common space and the representation captures how contact evolves across the chunk. A reconstruction head decodes the force map and contact mask back out, which keeps the representation tied to a physical quantity.

𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 同时处理两个阶段，并采用不同的处理方式。估计器 gθg_{\theta} 是一个经过校准的传感器到物理模型的转换器，保持固定状态，因此梯度仅在力映射层面停止。NeoForce 编码器在方程 ( 7 ) 中启动 EobsE_{\mathrm{obs}} 过程，并通过动作损失进行微调，这使得策略能够将大规模学习到的接触先验适配到所学习的行为中。其重建头则负责提供上述可选的力空间锚点。

𝒩 0 -TWAM imports both stages and treats them differently. The estimator g θ is a calibrated sensor-to-physics converter and stays frozen , so gradients stop at the force map. The NeoForce encoder warm-starts E obs in Eq. ( 7 ) and is fine-tuned with the action loss, which lets the policy specialize contact priors learned at scale to the behaviors it is learning. Its reconstruction head comes along to supply the optional force-space anchor noted above.

2.3 触觉感知式执行

在测试阶段 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} TWAM 按块进行自回归运行：在每个块内，遵循图中所展示的级联过程。 3 对未来视频和触觉潜像进行去噪处理，然后根据这些刚刚预测出的量来制定动作策略，随后在滑动之前进行解码执行。自回归展开机制、其滚动键值缓存以及异步预测管道，都是视频处理中的标准技术 world-action models . 有何独特之处 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 是指推理具备触觉感知能力：触摸在三个时间尺度上同时进入闭环，即传感器速率下的反射行为（如方程中的路径描述） 7 )), 以块率进行的预测（与视频同步去噪的触觉预视），以及以事件率进行的任务时钟（§中的触觉间断调度器） 2.3.3 ).

At test time 𝒩 0 -TWAM runs autoregressively over chunks: within each chunk it follows the cascade of Fig. 3 , denoising the future video and tactile latents, then the action conditioned on those just-predicted quantities, then decoding and executing before sliding on. The autoregressive rollout, its rolling key/value cache, and the asynchronous prediction pipeline are standard machinery for video world-action models. What is specific to 𝒩 0 -TWAM is that inference is tactile-aware : touch enters the closed loop on three time scales at once, a reflex at sensor rate (the observed pathway of Eq. ( 7 )), a prediction at chunk rate (the tactile foresight denoised alongside video), and a task clock at event rate (the tactile-punctuated scheduler of § 2.3.3 ).

2.3.1 流传输与部署

按照训练中的 frame-id 因果顺序，滚动缓存提交操作进行，且注意力窗口与训练窗口保持一致。通过基于观察的视频流流式处理机制，我们将这种机制扩展至更深层：当真实帧图像及真实触觉数据到来时，它们会替换相应的预测值，从而使滚动上下文始终与机器人实际看到和感知到的内容保持一致。方程 ( 7 ) 所描述的触觉路径位于去噪循环之外，因此其条件化信息可在块级重新预测期间以传感器速率进行更新。第一个块没有已提交的历史记录，因此其第一帧被限制为观察到的初始帧，而对应的动作则等于块起始锚点的零变化量；或者，第一帧也可以保持自由状态，像其他帧一样从噪声中恢复。

The rolling cache commits in the frame-id causal order of training, with an attention window aligned to the training window. Following observation-grounded streaming of the video stream, we extend the same grounding to touch : as real frames and real tactile readings arrive, they replace the corresponding predicted entries, so the rolling context stays anchored to what the robot actually saw and felt on one timeline. The observed tactile pathway of Eq. ( 7 ) sits outside the denoising loop, so its conditioning can be refreshed at sensor rate between chunk-level re-predictions. The first chunk has no committed history, so its first frame is clamped to the observed initial frame, and the paired action is the zero delta of the chunk-start anchor; alternatively, the first frame can be left free and denoised from noise like any other frame.

2.3.2 实时推理

𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM的三种特性使其继承的滚动生成、缓存机制及异步管道能够在77 B参数规模下实时运行：（1）。该级联机制在每个action chunk内部形成单向依赖关系：视频查询与触觉查询均不会关注当前块中的动作。因此，一旦预测阶段完成对未来视频和触觉信号的去噪工作，其各层的键与值在整个后续动作循环过程中将保持不变。 非对称 MoT 架构使得该策略在计算上具有高效性：我们缓存这些键与值，仅在每次动作去噪步骤中重新运行轻量级动作专家。 2 ) 预测的视频与触觉潜像采用相同的去噪步骤安排，在预测循环的每个步骤中均遵循相同的标量时间步长，而动作分支则在其独立循环中采用独立的步骤安排。因此，动作分支可以采用更短的去噪步骤安排，而无需承担视频专家所需的每步计算成本。 3 ) 在连续的块之间，滚动历史缓存会复用之前已提交的块，而异步流水线则会在机器人执行当前块时预测下一个块。只要下一个块的计算在当前块的执行窗口内，推理延迟就可以被隐藏在机器人执行过程中。 作为可选的部署时间配置，模型主干也可以以较低的精度进行运行。每个专家中的线性层在Blackwell类硬件上采用FP8或NVFP4格式，而数值敏感操作（包括注意力QKV投影和softmax、归一化层以及输出头）则保持为bf16格式。该策略减少了主要视频专家的内存占用和矩阵乘法成本，对动作输出的影响微乎其微。触觉模式带来的额外计算成本也微不足道。预测到的接触信号是在进行场景预测的同一次前向过程中生成的，而方程 ( 7 ) 中的观测分支仅包含一个轻量级交叉注意力模块，该模块在去噪循环之外以传感器速率进行更新。

Three properties of 𝒩 0 -TWAM enable its inherited rolling generation, caching mechanism, and asynchronous pipeline to operate in real time even at the 7 B-parameter scale: (1) The cascade imposes a one-way dependency within each action chunk: neither the video queries nor the tactile queries attend to the actions in the current chunk. Consequently, once the prediction phase has completed denoising the future video and tactile signals, their per-layer keys and values remain unchanged throughout the subsequent action loop. The asymmetric MoT architecture makes this strategy computationally effective: we cache these keys and values and only re-run the lightweight action expert at each action denoising step. (2) The predicted video and tactile latents share one denoising-step schedule, advancing under the same scalar timestep at every step of the prediction loop, while the action uses an independent schedule in its own loop. The action branch can therefore adopt a shorter denoising schedule without incurring the per-step computational cost of the video expert. (3) Across successive chunks, a rolling history cache reuses previously committed chunks, while the asynchronous pipeline predicts the next chunk as the robot executes the current one. As long as the computation for the next chunk fits within the execution window of the current chunk, the inference latency can be hidden behind robot execution. As an optional deployment-time configuration, the model backbone can also be served at reduced precision. The linear layers in each expert operate in FP8, or NVFP4 on Blackwell-class hardware, while numerically sensitive operations (including the attention QKV projections and softmax, normalization layers, and output heads) remain in bf16. This strategy reduces the memory footprint and matrix-multiplication cost of the dominant video expert while having a negligible effect on the action output. The additional computational cost introduced by the tactile modality is negligible. The predicted contact signal is generated in the same forward pass that already performs scene prediction, while the observed branch in Eq. ( 7 ) consists only of a lightweight cross-attention module that is refreshed at sensor rate outside the denoising loop.

2.3.3 通过触觉标记实现长视距执行

在长周期任务中，单一的 episode 级指令无法告知模型机器人当前处于任务的哪个阶段。任务中的观察结果往往具有阶段模糊性（例如“制作一杯茶”过程中一半的表格与倒茶前的表格非常相似），注意力窗口并不覆盖整个 episode，且固定的提示也不提供关于当前阶段的信息，因此文本路径在任务中未被使用，策略陷入停滞，重复某一步骤或直接跳过。缺失的结构其实存在于触觉信息流中：子任务由接触事件界定（抓取从接触开始到释放结束；倒茶则从容器被装载到放置结束），在这两个阶段之间，触觉是稳定的，而在其间则会发生剧烈的变化。 触觉流具有自然的间断点，在 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 模型中，触觉已然成为一种模型可观察并预测的流，因此我们在训练的双方都利用相同的信号结构来完成任务（图 5）。

In long-horizon tasks, a single episode-level instruction does not tell the model which stage of the task the robot is currently in. Mid-task observations are often stage-ambiguous (a table halfway through “make a cup of tea” looks much like the table before pouring), the attention window does not span the episode, and a constant prompt carries no information about the current stage, so the text pathway goes unused within a task and the policy stalls, repeats a step, or skips ahead. The missing structure already lives in the tactile stream: sub-tasks are delimited by contact events (a grasp begins at contact onset and ends at release; a pour begins when the vessel is loaded and ends when it is set down), between which touch is steady and at which it changes sharply. The tactile stream is naturally punctuated , and in 𝒩 0 -TWAM touch is already a stream the model observes and predicts, so we let the same signal structure the task on both sides of training (Fig. 5 ).

分割演示。

我们利用触觉“标记”对长距离演示进行分割，主要通过检测触觉信号变化中的接触开始与释放事件来实现，必要时则使用夹持器开口作为替代方法。因此，整个演示被划分为多个子任务片段。这种方法将基于关键帧的模仿学习 [Shridhar et al., 2022] 扩展至接触事件层面。由于抓取行为只有在夹持器真正与物体接触后才会导致触觉信号增强，相同的触觉信号分析也能区分成功的抓取与无物体时的闭合动作（图 5 a）。因此，单一信号既足以用于分割演示，也足以验证任务是否成功。我们使用 Gemini 3.5 Flash [DeepMind, 2025] 为每个片段生成简短的子任务指令，并通过人工抽查进行验证，且在训练后阶段，每个片段都会与其对应的子任务指令进行关联。

We segment long-horizon demonstrations using tactile “punctuation,” primarily detecting contact-onset and release events from changes in tactile signals, with gripper aperture used as a fallback when necessary. The full demonstration is thereby divided into multiple sub-task clips. This approach extends keyframe-based imitation learning [ Shridhar et al., 2022 ] to contact events . Because a grasp produces a rise in tactile signals only after the gripper has actually made contact with an object, the same tactile reading can also distinguish a successful grasp from a gripper that closes on nothing (Fig. 5 a). Thus, a single signal is sufficient both to segment the demonstration and to verify task success. We use Gemini 3.5 Flash [ DeepMind, 2025 ] to generate a short sub-task instruction for each clip, with human spot checks, and during post-training each clip is conditioned on its own corresponding sub-task instruction.

在推理过程中推进子任务。

触摸行为不仅被模型观测与预测，还与推理时间规划器相连。在推理过程中，轻量级调度器会维护子任务队列，并在当前子任务完成时推进到下一个提示（见图）。 5 b). 完成过程结合了模型的两种触觉功能：预测的未来触觉信号会提前触发一步动作，而观测到的触觉流中的相应释放或接触起始事件则会在切换完成前确认该动作。子任务序列可以在可重复任务中预先设定，或在开放环境中由视觉-语言规划器生成。 [Ahn et al., 2022, Liang et al., 2022] ; 在任何情况下，触觉事件检测器都会决定何时从实际执行阶段推进，每次推进都会通过下一个提示重新初始化流上下文，以匹配训练过程中使用的每片段条件。

Touch is not only observed and predicted by the model, but also connected to the inference-time planner. At inference, a lightweight scheduler maintains a queue of sub-tasks and advances to the next prompt when the current sub-task completes (Fig. 5 b). Completion combines the model’s two tactile roles: the predicted future tactile signal triggers the advance one step early, and the corresponding release or contact-onset event in the observed tactile stream then confirms it before the switch is committed. The sub-task sequence can be fixed in advance for repeatable tasks or generated by a vision–language planner in open-ended settings [ Ahn et al., 2022 , Liang et al., 2022 ] ; in either case, the tactile-event detector decides when to advance from the actual execution, and each advance re-seeds the streaming context with the next prompt, matching the per-clip conditioning used in training.

3.1 数据集

NeoData 是一个庞大的多机器人操作语料库，时长超过 30,00030{,}000小时，涵盖六种本体及 450450种高接触任务。其中大部分案例包含同步的单指触觉信息以及多视角 RGB。这种大规模的触觉覆盖特性使其与仅基于视觉的预训练语料不同，也使得 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 能够学会预测接触情况，而不仅仅是识别外观。关于收集系统、传感器、数据筛选及完整统计数据，请参阅 NeoData 报告中的 [NeoteAI Team and Fudan TEAI Team, 2026]。真实机器人的预训练案例则使用 § 4.2 中描述的 InTac S1 视觉触觉传感器。

NeoData is a large multi-robot manipulation corpus of over 30,000 hours, spanning six embodiments and 450 contact-rich tasks, in which a large fraction of the episodes carry synchronized per-finger tactile alongside multi-view RGB. Tactile coverage at this scale is what separates it from vision-only pre-training corpora, and is what lets 𝒩 0 -TWAM learn to predict contact rather than only appearance. We refer to the NeoData report [ NeoteAI Team and Fudan TEAI Team, 2026 ] for the collection system, sensors, curation, and full statistics; the real-robot post-training episodes use the InTac S1 visuotactile sensors described in § 4.2 .

3.2 数据处理

潜在编码。

为了在 10910^{9} 帧规模下高效训练，我们使用一个固定的因果视频 VAE [Wan Team et al., 2025] 来编码相机视图与触觉流，将每个触觉流视为一个小型视频，从而使触觉信息能够共享视觉的潜在空间；同时，我们使用固定的 umT5 文本编码器来编码语言指令。所有这些输入在准备阶段均被转换为潜在表示并缓存到磁盘上，因此编码器在训练正向过程中不会运行。

To train efficiently at the scale of 10 9 frames, we encode both the camera views and the tactile streams with a single frozen causal video VAE [ Wan Team et al., 2025 ] , treating each tactile stream as a small video so that touch shares the latent space of vision, and we encode the language instruction with a frozen umT5 text encoder. All of these inputs are converted to latents once during preparation and cached to disk, so the encoders never run in the training forward pass.

分块处理。

每个片段被划分为固定的 3333 潜在帧窗口，这些窗口是 § 2.1 的级联处理所依据的单位。一个窗口包含 387387 原始帧，在 3030 到 fps 之间被下采样至 129129 帧，然后在 1010 到 fps 之间被压缩为 3333 潜在帧，最终通过 VAE 的 4×4\times 时间下采样过程完成。连续的窗口以 2424 潜在帧为步长前进，同时保留 ∼27%\sim\!27\% 的重叠区域以覆盖片段边界，而尾部窗口则被对齐到整个片段的末尾。一次性的验证过程会记录哪些窗口在所有所需模态上均已完成，只有完整的窗口才被用于训练。

Each episode is segmented into fixed windows of 33 latent frames, the unit over which the chunked cascade of § 2.1 operates. A window spans 387 raw frames at 30 fps, subsampled to 129 frames at 10 fps and compressed to 33 latent frames by the VAE’s 4 × temporal downsampling. Consecutive windows advance by a stride of 24 latent frames, leaving a ∼ 27 % overlap that keeps segment junctions covered, and tail windows are right-aligned to the episode end. A one-time validation pass records which windows are complete across all required modalities, and only complete windows are used for training.

统一动作空间。

为了在异构机器人上训练策略，每个本体对应的动作都会被规范化为一个单一的 2020 维末端执行器空间，每个臂对应 1010 维空间。每个本体都会记录其末端执行器的姿态；位置和 6 D-旋转目标则被定义为 § 2.1 中基于块锚点的差值，同时夹持器保持绝对状态。动作按机器人进行归一化处理，而非全局统一，且重尾的夹持器通道采用分位数裁剪技术。

To train one policy across heterogeneous robots, every embodiment’s action is canonicalized into a single 20 -dimensional end-effector space, 10 per arm. Every embodiment is recorded with end-effector pose; the position and 6D-rotation targets are formed as the chunk-anchored deltas of § 2.1 while the gripper is kept absolute, and actions are normalized per robot rather than globally, with quantile clipping on the heavy-tailed gripper channel.

接触事件阶段分割。

对于 § 2.3.3 的长期流程中，我们也在演示阶段设定阶段边界，以触觉信号为主要指示：在接触丰富的任务中，大多数过渡情况（首次接触、接触丧失、滑移、插入定位）都会表现为触觉感知上的明显变化。当触觉信号较弱或模糊时，我们则依赖夹持器开口作为辅助信号，该信号能够可靠地标识抓取与释放动作，而最终的人工检查则用于修正剩余的边界。这样就能确保 § 2.3.3 的子任务标注及推理时间调度以有意义的接触事件为基准。

For the long-horizon pipeline of § 2.3.3 we also mark stage boundaries within a demonstration, with the tactile stream as the primary signal: most transitions in contact-rich tasks (first contact, loss of contact, slip, an insertion seating) show up as a sharp change in the tactile reading. Where the tactile cue is weak or ambiguous, we fall back to the gripper aperture, which reliably marks grasps and releases, and a final human check corrects the remaining boundaries. This keeps the sub-task labeling and inference-time scheduling of § 2.3.3 keyed on meaningful contact events.

We pre-train the world-action model on tens of thousands hours of manipulation, from the multi-robot corpus of § 3 : real-robot demonstrations across six embodiments (ARX5, UR, Flexiv, Franka, PiPER and hand-collected UMI) data. Each clip provides multi-view RGB pre-encoded with the Wan2.2 VAE at 4 × temporal and 16 × spatial compression, a synchronized predicted tactile stream, and the 20 -D bimanual delta end-effector action. The training objective is the multi-modal flow-matching loss of Eq. ( 3 ): video, touch, and action are trained with equal 1 : 1 : 1 modality weights; every latent-frame group is noised independently, with one noise level shared across a frame’s spatial tokens for video and tactile, and across the action steps aligned with that frame for action. At this stage tactile enters only as the predicted foresight target (§ 2.2 ), while the observed tactile pathway is disabled, so pre-training concentrates capacity on predicting future vision and contact at scale. We follow a two-stage curriculum: the model is first pre-trained on the real-robot data to convergence, then continued from that checkpoint with UMI data mixed in (about 60 % of the batch). The video expert is a ∼ 5 B video-diffusion transformer of the Wan2.2-TI2V-5B family [ Wan Team et al., 2025 ] , warm-started from LingBot-VA [ Li et al., 2026c ] , while the action and tactile experts are trained from scratch. We optimize with AdamW at a learning rate of 10 − 4 and weight decay 0.1 for 30,000 steps, about 2.2 epochs, at an effective batch of 512 . Training runs on 128 NVIDIA H800 GPUs under FSDP2 with bf16 mixed precision, and the optimizer updates only the MoT backbone since the tokenizer and text encoder are frozen. We hold out 3 % of each task’s data as a validation split and score a random subset of it at each validation pass.

The observed tactile pathway (§ 2.2.2 ) uses a ViT-B backbone initialized from DINOv2 [ Oquab et al., 2023 ] and shared across the patchified visual and tactile streams, and each input is a chunk of 4 synchronized RGB frames and tactile force maps. We first pre-train NeoForce on 20,000 real-robot and Neo TacUMI demonstrations spanning 30 tasks [ NeoteAI Team and Fudan TEAI Team, 2026 ] , for 100,000 steps on 8 NVIDIA A100 GPUs at a learning rate of 2 × 10 − 5 . 𝒩 0 -TWAM then loads that checkpoint and fine-tunes the encoder with the action loss during post-training.

训练后阶段。

对于每种下游设置，我们会对预训练的 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 检查点进行适配，以适应特定任务的需求，同时保留多模态流匹配级联结构。每个演示被划分为同步的多视图 RGB、语言、动作和触觉数据窗口，其中 RGB 和语言数据被预编码为 Wan- VAE 潜在向量和文本嵌入，从而让优化过程专注于变换器及触觉条件模块。动作数据按 2424 步长的周期进行分组，每个潜在帧对应 1212 个分组。观测到的触觉编码器因应用场景不同而有所差异：在真实机器人中，它属于上述的 NeoForce 力空间编码器；而在仿真环境中，由于信号并非由真实传感器产生，则采用从零开始训练的轻量级编码器。

For each downstream setting, we adapt the pre-trained 𝒩 0 -TWAM checkpoint to task-specific demonstrations while preserving the multi-modal flow-matching cascade. Each demonstration is segmented into synchronized windows of multi-view RGB, language, action, and tactile, with RGB and language pre-encoded as Wan-VAE latents and text embeddings so that optimization focuses on the transformer and tactile-conditioning modules. The action is chunked at a horizon of 24 steps, 12 per latent frame. The observed-tactile encoder differs by domain: on real robots it is the NeoForce force-space encoder above, while in simulation, where the signal is not produced by a real sensor, it is a lightweight encoder trained from scratch.

观察到的触觉读数用于动作预测（§ 2.2）：当前帧的触觉潜在信息通过零初始化的观测路径被嵌入，并在块因果掩模下与动作专家进行交叉叠加，因此这些纯净的 tokens 信息仅对动作查询可见，不会进入视频流，且本身也不是去噪目标；§ 2.2 中预训练的力空间头部则接入同一端口。与此同时，预测得到的触觉潜在信息在级联过程中仍属于去噪目标，因此动作专家会结合预测的未来接触信息与观测到的当前接触信息。

Observed tactile readings condition action prediction (§ 2.2 ): the current-frame tactile latent is embedded by the zero-initialized observed pathway and cross-attended into the action expert under a chunk-causal mask, so these clean condition tokens are visible to action queries, never enter the video stream, and are not themselves a denoising target; the pretrained force-space head of § 2.2 plugs into the same port. In parallel, predicted tactile latents remain a denoising target under the cascade, so the action expert draws on both predicted future contact and observed current contact.

条件丢失。

在训练过程中，我们通过两种不同的机制，以概率 0.10.1 随机放弃语言与触觉条件。放弃语言的方式遵循标准无分类器方法，即用学习到的零嵌入替代文本嵌入。而放弃触觉则采用“缺失”而非“归零”的方式：在被放弃的样本中，完全不传递任何触觉张量，因此触觉相关的 tokens 不会出现在注意力序列中，触觉模块也得不到梯度更新，从而训练出纯视频-动作模型。这种“缺失”设计具有三个后果。首先，它将有意的丢弃与真正无触觉数据统一起来：没有触觉传感器的本体场景同样可以通过相同的接口进行训练。 其次，该模型同时学习了边缘式仅视觉策略与触觉条件策略，因此当传感器失效或缺失时，策略会退化为仅视觉模式，而不会对异常输入作出反应。第三，在推理过程中引入了无分类器引导旋钮：触觉条件预测与无条件动作预测度量之间的差距，能够反映策略对触觉信息的重视程度，且该旋钮的强度可通过调整来增强。在所有报道的评估中，该旋钮均保持为 w=1w{=}1 状态（不启用触觉 CFG 增强）；其所支持的仅视觉 fallback 机制将在 § 4.4 中探讨。

During training we randomly drop the language and tactile conditions, each with probability 0.1 , by two different mechanisms. Dropping language follows the standard classifier-free recipe, replacing the text embedding with a learned null embedding. Dropping tactile instead uses absence rather than zeroing: on a dropped sample no tactile tensor is passed at all, so the tactile tokens do not appear in the attention sequence, the tactile modules receive no gradient, and that step trains the pure video–action model. This absence design has three consequences. First, it unifies deliberate dropout with genuinely tactile-less data: episodes from embodiments without tactile sensors train through exactly the same interface. Second, the model learns the marginal, vision-only policy alongside the tactile-conditional one, so if a sensor fails or is absent at deployment the policy degrades to its vision-only self instead of reacting to an out-of-distribution zero input. Third, it exposes a classifier-free-guidance knob at inference: the gap between the tactile-conditional and unconditional action predictions measures, and can amplify, how much the policy listens to touch. In all reported evaluations we leave this knob at w = 1 (no tactile CFG amplification); the vision-only fallback it enables is probed in § 4.4 .

优化。

训练后数据采用 LeRobot 数据集格式，真实机器人配置则使用三台 RGB 摄像头及最多四条触觉数据流。目标函数以相等的 1:1:11{:}1{:}1 权重组合视频潜在表示、预测触觉数据及掩码动作去噪损失。我们使用 AdamW 优化器对所有Transformer参数进行微调，学习率为 10−410^{-4}，权重衰减为 0.10.1，采用 bf16 混合精度，并在 2.02.0 下实施梯度裁剪；特定任务训练时，除非另有说明，每颗 GPU 使用一个样本，采用四步梯度累积，并执行 10001000 次优化器迭代。

Post-training data use the LeRobot dataset format, and the real-robot configuration uses three RGB cameras and up to four tactile streams. The objective sums the video-latent, predicted-tactile, and masked-action denoising losses at equal 1 : 1 : 1 weights. We fine-tune all transformer parameters, including the observed tactile encoder, with AdamW at a learning rate of 10 − 4 and weight decay 0.1 , bf16 mixed precision, and gradient clipping at 2.0 ; task-specific runs use one sample per GPU, four-step gradient accumulation, and 1000 optimizer steps unless noted.

All methods are evaluated closed-loop under the asynchronous receding-horizon protocol of § 2.3 . At each control cycle the policy consumes the current multi-view RGB together with the rolling history cache and the current observed-tactile latent, runs the cascade once to predict the future scene and contact, and denoises a single action chunk— 16 steps of the unified 20 -D bimanual delta end-effector action—with the slim action expert on its own short denoising schedule. Chunks are executed asynchronously, the robot running the current chunk while the next is predicted, and the observed-tactile condition is refreshed at sensor rate between chunk-level re-predictions; a trial terminates on task success or when it reaches a per-task step budget of 1.5 × the length of that task’s training demonstrations.

为什么 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 具有领先优势？

𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 保留了强烈的视频-动作先验信息，但通过两种互补的方式对控制回路进行补充。预测的触觉专家会协同去噪未来的接触情况与未来视频数据，而因果级联机制则使动作专家能够在生成下一次动作之前，先了解这一确定的触觉未来状态（§ 2.2）。这种预测性路径有助于策略避免那些视觉结果看似合理但预测到的接触方式与稳定抓取或插入行为不符的动作。同时，观测路径将当前的力-空间触觉表征直接输入到动作专家中（§ 2.2.2），为滑移、剪切、不对称接触及过度压力提供短延迟的修正信号。因此，这两种路径分别针对不同的故障模式：触觉预知能在接触发生前减少错误，而观测到的触觉反馈则有助于在接触开始后进行恢复。

𝒩 0 -TWAM retains the strong video–action prior, but adds touch to the control loop in two complementary forms. The predicted tactile expert co-denoises future contact with future video, and the causal cascade lets the action expert read this committed tactile future before producing the next action (§ 2.2 ). This anticipatory path helps the policy avoid actions whose visual outcome appears plausible but whose predicted contact is inconsistent with a stable grasp or insertion. In parallel, the observed pathway injects the current force-space tactile representation directly into the action expert (§ 2.2.2 ), providing a short-latency correction signal for slip, shear, asymmetric contact, and excessive pressure. The two paths therefore address different failure modes: tactile foresight reduces errors before contact is committed, whereas observed touch supports recovery after contact begins.

该实现在保留预训练策略的同时添加了这些信号。触觉模块使用私有专家权重，仅通过共享注意力机制与视觉与动作信息进行交互，从而防止稀疏、事件驱动的触觉统计信息覆盖视觉表征。分阶段训练方法首先在大规模层面学习预测的触觉预知能力，仅在任务适应过程中激活所观察到的触觉反射，避免反应性信号成为替代预测接触建模的捷径。所观察到的交叉注意力输出以零初始化，因此训练后仍从预训练行为开始，而非突然改变策略。最后，触觉条件丢弃机制在有无触觉 tokens 的情况下训练相同的动作专家，从而限制在自由空间运动过程中对触觉的过度依赖，使策略能够适应暂时缺失接触观测的情况。 这些设计选择使得训练后能够增加接触敏感度，同时不牺牲从基础 world-action model 继承来的场景理解能力。

The implementation preserves the pretrained policy while adding these signals. Touch uses private expert weights and exchanges information with vision and action only through shared attention, preventing sparse, event-driven tactile statistics from overwriting the visual representation. The staged recipe first learns predicted tactile foresight at scale and activates the observed tactile reflex only during task adaptation, preventing the reactive signal from becoming a shortcut that replaces predictive contact modeling. The observed cross-attention output is zero-initialized, so post-training starts from the pretrained behavior rather than an abruptly perturbed policy. Finally, tactile-condition dropout trains the same action expert both with and without tactile tokens, which limits over-reliance on touch during free-space motion and makes the policy robust to temporarily missing contact observations. These design choices let post-training add contact sensitivity without sacrificing the scene understanding inherited from the base world-action model.

任务级收益。

相同的机制解释了优势最显著的领域。在果实采集和瓶子站立任务中，触觉反馈平衡了安全抓取与物体变形、滑落以及质量中心偏移的风险。擦除木板则涉及持续负载的传递与支撑转移。插座插入和河内塔任务对这两种路径都提出了极高要求：接触边缘或插针后的受力方向提供了修正信号，而触觉预知则有助于区分可恢复的对齐状态与即将发生的卡住或不稳定状态。在长期任务中（如制作柠檬茶和河内塔任务），这些接触事件也为§ 2.3.3中的触觉驱动执行机制提供了可靠的边界，减少了某个失败过渡导致后续动作序列受损的可能性。 在触摸贡献最小的两项任务中，即杯堆叠和袋包装任务，其结果也由视觉引导放置决定，因此具有强视觉能力的 π0.5\pi_{0.5} 在这两项任务中能够保持或超越其他模型。

The same mechanism explains where the advantage is largest. For Fruit Collection and Bottle Standing, tactile feedback balances secure acquisition against object deformation, slip, and a shifting center of mass. Board Wiping exposes sustained load and support transfer against the surface. Socket Plugging and Hanoi Tower place the strongest demand on both pathways: observed force direction supplies a correction signal after rim or peg contact, while tactile foresight helps distinguish a recoverable alignment from an impending jam or unstable seating. In the long-horizon tasks (Making Lemon Tea and Hanoi Tower), these contact events also provide reliable boundaries for the tactile-punctuated execution mechanism of § 2.3.3 , reducing the chance that one failed transition corrupts the remaining action sequence. The two tasks where touch adds least, Cup Stacking and Bag Packing, are also the two whose outcome is set by visually guided placement, which is why the vision-strong π 0.5 stays on par or ahead there.

基线分析。

π0.5\pi_{0.5} 是最强的基线模型。作为强大的 vision-language-action 策略，它具备广泛的语义先验，并能提供可靠的视觉引导定位，因此在以定位为主的任务中与 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 接近，在杯堆叠和袋装打包任务中则处于领先位置；然而，该模型直接从当前帧中生成动作，缺乏对下一时刻的建模，也无需接触感知，因此既无法预测，也无法感知到接触事件。在各类世界动作模型中，LingBot-VA 的表现优于 FastWAM：它拥有强大的视觉基础，并保留明确的未来视频生成能力，从而能够实现可靠的场景级规划；而 FastWAM 以效率为导向的推理方式将视频分支简化为单次编码过程，未能保留迭代式的未来扩展机制 [Yuan et al., 2026c]。这三种基线模型均缺乏触觉路径，因此视觉上相似的接触状态（“对齐但未插入”与“完全就座”）难以区分。 这种排序方式体现了两项累积优势：明确的迭代预测机制使得 LingBot-VA 区别于 FastWAM；而在预测-行动循环中同时纳入预测结果与观测数据，正是 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 能够超越所有基线模型，包括视觉能力更强的 π0.5\pi_{0.5} 的原因。图 9 中的组件消融实验则分别检验了这种特性与整体基线比较的关联。

π 0.5 is the strongest baseline. As a large vision-language-action policy it brings broad semantic priors and dependable visually guided placement, which keeps it close to 𝒩 0 -TWAM on placement-dominated tasks and ahead on Cup Stacking and Bag Packing; but it regresses actions directly from the current frame, with no model of the next moment and no touch, so it can neither anticipate nor feel a contact event. Among the world-action models, LingBot-VA ranks above FastWAM: it shares the strong visual foundation and retains explicit future-video generation for reliable scene-level planning, whereas FastWAM’s efficiency-oriented inference collapses the video branch into a single-pass encoder rather than preserving the iterative future rollout [ Yuan et al., 2026c ] . None of the three baselines has a tactile pathway, so visually similar contact states (“aligned but not inserted” versus “fully seated”) stay hard to tell apart. The ordering thus points to two cumulative benefits: explicit iterative prediction separates LingBot-VA from FastWAM, and adding both predicted and observed touch inside that prediction–action loop is what carries 𝒩 0 -TWAM past every baseline, including the vision-strong π 0.5 . 4.4 泛化

我们沿三个维度测试真机的泛化能力，分别在最适合观察该变化的任务上单独控制该变量（表 4 ）。未见物体：立瓶和装袋中的任务目标和工作空间保持不变，换成训练时未见过的物品，包括形状、大小或材质不同的瓶子，以及不同的待装物品。成功要求把接触技能迁到新的几何和物理性质上。未见位置：叠杯使用原来的杯子，但初始位置和偏移超出训练分布，检验能否迁移到新的摆放位置。视觉扰动：水果收集保持任务和物理条件不变，只在测试时改变光照或背景，考察视觉通路受干扰的表现。 触觉能直接报告接触状态，因此视觉输入改变时，𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-TWAM 有望依靠触觉，比纯视觉 world-action models 更能保持性能。

We probe generalization on our real-robot suite along three axes, each isolated on the task where it matters most (Table 4 ). Unseen object : on Bottle Standing and Bag Packing we keep the task and workspace fixed but replace the manipulated objects with instances never seen in training, bottles of new shape, size, and material, and new items to pack, so success requires transferring the contact skill to novel geometry and physical properties rather than memorizing specific instances. Unseen position : on Cup Stacking we use the same cups but initialize them at placements and offsets outside the training distribution, testing whether the stacking behavior generalizes spatially rather than replaying a fixed trajectory. Visual perturbation : on Fruit Collection we leave the task and physics unchanged but perturb the visual scene at test time (lighting and background changes), stressing the visual pathway. Because tactile reports contact directly, 𝒩 0 -TWAM should lean on touch and degrade more gracefully than vision-only world-action models as the visual input shifts.

4.5 性能缩减研究

我们对 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-TWAM 的三个设计进行消融：预训练数据规模、作为前瞻目标的预测触觉，以及作为动作专家条件的实测触觉。20%20\% 数据版本使用和完整模型相同的架构、任务示教与后训练安排，从只用完整语料的 20%20\% 做过预训练的检查点初始化，用来观察下游收益如何随预训练数据量变化。两种触觉消融均从保留双通路的模型出发，各关闭一条。实测通路是一条侧支，通过交叉注意力把当前触觉传给动作流，关闭时不再建立这条侧支。 预测通路既是骨干自注意力序列的一部分，也是触觉专家的生成目标。我们保留通路，只在加噪前把预测触觉潜变量置零。序列布局、注意力掩码和扩散损失在各 token 位置上都与完整模型的对应 token 一致，改变的只有通路所承载的信息。各消融版本使用对应的预训练检查点；触觉消融沿用同一检查点，后训练的数据、超参数和步数相同。我们在 UniVTAC 和 NeoSim 上测试这些版本，其中 20%20\% 预训练版本只在 UniVTAC 上测试。图 9 给出各基准的任务平均成功率和两者的均值，附录 A 列出逐任务结果。在 UniVTAC 上，预训练规模是影响最大的单项因素，从 20%20\% 语料的检查点初始化，成功率由 84.584.5 降为 65.465.4 。 两条触觉通路同样重要。去掉前瞻预测时，UniVTAC 成功率降至 71.871.8；去掉实测触觉条件时，降至 70.570.5 。NeoSim 的排序相同，变化更明显，成功率从 49.449.4 分别降至 41.141.1 和 29.629.6 。这表明预测触觉和实测触觉各自都有贡献。

We ablate the main design choices of 𝒩 0 -TWAM : the scale of the pre-training data, the predicted tactile foresight target, and the observed tactile conditioning of the action expert. 4.6 分析

Delta 与绝对末端执行器动作。

我们采用 § 2.1 中提出的基于块锚点的末端执行器参数化方法，该方法具有平移不变性，且能保持目标点位置较小且居中，因此可在不同本体及工作空间布局中实现清晰传递。但在实际部署中，它并非总是最佳选择。对于对对齐要求严格的任务，绝对末端执行器姿态可能优于位移目标（表 5）：位移目标是以移动块起始锚点为基准测量的，因此每块的小误差会累积，而目标点始终不绑定于固定位置；而绝对姿态则直接将每个目标点定位在世界坐标系中。因此，这两种参数化方法在泛化性与精确度之间进行了权衡：位移参数适用于平移不变的传递，绝对参数则适用于达到特定姿态的场景。我们在统一多本体策略中保留位移参数，而在特定任务设置中采用绝对参数，因为亚厘米级的定位精度对任务成功至关重要。

We default to the chunk-anchored delta end-effector parameterization of § 2.1 , which is translation-invariant and keeps the targets small and centered, so it transfers cleanly across embodiments and workspace placements. It is not always the best choice at deployment. On alignment-sensitive tasks the absolute end-effector pose can outperform the delta target (Table 5 ): a delta is measured from the moving chunk-start anchor, so small per-chunk errors accumulate and the target is never tied to a fixed location, whereas an absolute pose grounds each target directly in the world frame. The two parameterizations therefore trade generalization against precision: delta for translation-invariant transfer, absolute for reaching a specific pose. We keep delta for the unified multi-embodiment policy and adopt absolute on the task-specific settings where sub-centimeter placement dominates success.

模拟触觉图像的真实性。

本研究中的两种触觉图像均源自相同的接触模拟过程，仅在于变形凝胶的成像方式不同。上述的 UniVTAC 数值采用了模拟器的纯净输出结果，而基于校准的 GelSight 渲染（Taxim [Si and Yuan, 2022]）则通过逐像素照明模型将模拟的接触高度场转化为平滑、确定性的 RGB 图像，从而使接触区域呈现为清晰的颜色与强度分布。真实的视觉传感器则完全并非如此，它输出的图像呈现为银色、有斑点且阴影柔和的海绵状凝胶状态，并伴随传感器噪声（图 10）。 因此，我们在同一信号基础上生成更真实的凝胶渲染图像，无需重新模拟：我们从光学图像的亮度中重建出明亮的银色凝胶基底，然后叠加一层密集且无标记的彩色斑点，再通过每个顶点表面的切向位移（驱动标记运动的相同机制）以及深度压痕梯度（在凝胶围绕压头膨胀时将斑点向外推挤）来引导该斑点分布。轻微的深度阴影和弹性颗粒则进一步增强了整体视觉效果。此时，接触状态以变形纹理表面呈现，而非颜色代码。 通过此渲染进行了训练与评估 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 应保持在清洁区域附近，而非急剧下降： 82.4%82.4\% 针对 84.5%84.5\% 在第八个 UniVTAC 任务，以及 58.5%58.5\% 针对 63.8%63.8\% （单臂）及 39.3%39.3\% 针对 42.3%42.3\% （双臂）式 NeoSim (表) 6 ; 任务级分解数据位于附录中 B ). 因此，这种真实外观在领域内精度较低，尽管它取代了干净的合成图像，并带有传感器噪声、颗粒以及变形纹理表面。由于它共享真实视觉传感器相同的视觉域，这种凝胶渲染图像也构成了一个预训练的力编码器能够识别的触觉信号，我们将在下文中对此进行探讨。

Both tactile images in this study come from the same contact simulation and differ only in how the deformed gel is imaged. The UniVTAC numbers above use the simulator’s clean output, a calibration-based GelSight render (Taxim [ Si and Yuan, 2022 ] ) that maps the simulated contact-height field to a smooth, deterministic RGB image through a per-pixel illumination model, so contact appears as a clean color-and-intensity blob. A real vision-based sensor looks nothing like this; it returns a silvery, speckled, softly shaded gel image with sensor noise (Fig. 10 ). We therefore render a more realistic gel-rendered image on top of the same signal, without re-simulating: we rebuild a bright silver gel base from the optical image’s luminance, overlay a dense, markerless field of fine colored speckles, and advect that field with the contact, tangentially by the per-vertex surface displacement (the same field that drives the marker motion) and normally by the depth-indentation gradient, which pushes the speckles outward as the gel bulges around the indenter; a faint depth shade and elastomer grain complete the look. Contact then reads out of a deforming textured surface rather than a color code. Trained and evaluated on this rendering, 𝒩 0 -TWAM stays close to the clean field rather than dropping sharply: 82.4 % against 84.5 % on the eight UniVTAC tasks, and 58.5 % against 63.8 % (single-arm) and 39.3 % against 42.3 % (dual-arm) on NeoSim (Table 6 ; the per-task breakdown is in Appendix B ). The realistic appearance therefore costs little accuracy in-domain, even though it replaces the clean synthetic blob with sensor noise, grain, and a deforming textured surface. Because it shares the visual domain of a real vision-based sensor, the gel-rendered image is also a tactile stream a pretrained force encoder can read, which we examine below.

在凝胶渲染触觉系统中预训练的力编码器。

NeoForce is pretrained on real vision-based tactile images, the gel-particle appearance our gel-rendered render imitates. Because the two share this domain, reading the gel-rendered tactile through NeoForce, instead of the observed-tactile encoder trained from scratch, raises success from 82.4 % to 88.1 % on the eight UniVTAC tasks and from 58.5 % to 64.8 % on the four single-arm NeoSim tasks (Table 6 ; per-task numbers in Appendix B ). The gains concentrate on tasks whose success turns on a weight-bearing or shear cue that a force representation reads well: Put Bottle in Shelf ( → 96 ), Insert HDMI ( → 75 ), and Pull-out Key ( → 83 ) on UniVTAC, and Grasp Chip ( → 92 ) and Pour Ball ( → 68 ) on NeoSim, while the sharp-contact insertion tasks are already near ceiling and change little. We still report the clean field in the main results, where every method uses the same simulated tactile; the gel-rendered + NeoForce number is a sim-to-real signal, showing that once the simulated touch looks real, a force encoder pretrained on real touch transfers into it.

触觉预知能力。

对模型是否真正能够预测触摸进行直接验证的方法，就是将其预测的未来触觉图像与真实数据进行对比（图 11）。在柠檬茶抓取实验中，预测的触觉图像及其接触流图与真实传感器数据在双手指方面高度一致：模型能够预判接触发生的位置与方式，而不仅仅是未来场景。如果我们所期望的正是通过预测触摸而非仅预测外观来推动下游性能提升，那么这就是一个明确的定性信号。

A direct test of whether the model truly predicts touch is to compare its predicted future tactile against the ground truth (Fig. 11 ). On the lemon-tea grasp, the predicted tactile images and their contact-flow maps track the ground-truth sensor closely for both fingers: the model anticipates where and how contact forms, not only the future scene. This is the qualitative signal we expect if predicting touch , rather than appearance alone, is what drives the downstream gains.

5 相关研究

Vision-language-action 模型。

Vision-language-action ( VLA )模型将操控视为基于观察与语言生成条件动作的过程。RT风格且开放通用的策略[Brohan et al., 2023, Zitkovich et al., 2023, Ghosh et al., 2024, Kim et al., 2025, Black et al., 2025b, Black et al., 2025a]表明，大规模视觉-语言预训练能够将语义知识转化为机器人控制能力。近期系统通过实时异步执行以及更大规模的跨本体数据集[Cai et al., 2026b, Xiaomi Robotics Team et al., 2026, Wu et al., 2026b]进一步扩展了这一方法，而具身式框架与统一策略则增加了以动作为中心的推理能力以及视觉或潜在预见能力[Tencent Robotics X et al., 2026, Wang et al., 2026b, Cai et al., 2026a, Ma et al., 2026a]。然而，这些基础级系统仍主要依赖视觉、语言与本体感知。它们通常依据VLABench [Zhang et al., 2024]、LIBERO-Plus [Fei et al., 2025]和RoboDojo [Chen et al., 2026b]等统一模拟与真实环境基准进行测试，这些基准涵盖泛化能力、记忆能力、精准度及长期视野技能，但缺乏接触密集、依赖触觉的任务𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM所要求的特性。 在接触丰富的操控中，决定因素可能表现为指尖压力、初始滑移或即将发生的碰撞；甚至某些预测型模型则模拟视觉或潜在的未来状态，而非动作专家所关注的明确触觉轨迹。𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM旨在填补这一空白。

Vision-language-action (VLA) models cast manipulation as conditional action generation from observations and language. RT-style and open generalist policies [ Brohan et al., 2023 , Zitkovich et al., 2023 , Ghosh et al., 2024 , Kim et al., 2025 , Black et al., 2025b , Black et al., 2025a ] show that large-scale vision-language pretraining transfers semantic knowledge into robot control. Recent systems extend this recipe through real-time asynchronous execution and much larger cross-embodiment corpora [ Cai et al., 2026b , Xiaomi Robotics Team et al., 2026 , Wu et al., 2026b ] , while embodied backbones and unified policies add action-centric reasoning and visual or latent foresight [ Tencent Robotics X et al., 2026 , Wang et al., 2026b , Cai et al., 2026a , Ma et al., 2026a ] . Yet these foundation-scale systems still center on vision, language, and proprioception. They are typically measured on unified sim-and-real benchmarks such as VLABench [ Zhang et al., 2024 ] , LIBERO-Plus [ Fei et al., 2025 ] , and RoboDojo [ Chen et al., 2026b ] that span generalization, memory, precision, and long-horizon skills, but not the contact-rich, tactile-dependent tasks 𝒩 0 -TWAM targets. In contact-rich manipulation, the deciding variable may instead be fingertip pressure, incipient slip, or imminent collision; even predictive variants model visual or latent futures rather than an explicit tactile trajectory consumed by the action expert. 𝒩 0 -TWAM targets this gap.

操作相关的触觉感知。

触觉感知提供了视觉无法捕捉的接触信号。具备触觉感知能力的系统 VLA 系统 [Zhang et al., 2026b, Huang et al., 2025b, Zhang et al., 2026a, Hao et al., 2025, Yu et al., 2025] 将触觉观察与语言及动作进行对齐 [Huang et al., 2025b, Hao et al., 2025] ，向动作专家注入力反馈 [Yu et al., 2025] , 专门用于插入及其他高接触任务 [Zhang et al., 2026a] 或者利用触摸产生的可行性信号来引导预训练的视觉运动策略在推理阶段运行 [Zhang et al., 2026b] . 感知力的策略融合 RGB 采用高速率力/扭矩流，学习任务依赖的柔顺性，或向系统中添加扭矩适配器 VLA 解码器 [He et al., 2024, Lee et al., 2025, Yang et al., 2023, Hou et al., 2024, Zhang et al., 2025] ，视觉-触觉策略表明，触觉能够提升精细操作能力，并在视觉模糊或被遮挡时依然保持可靠性 [Huang et al., 2025a, Zhao et al., 2025, Bi et al., 2025b, Cheng et al., 2025, Wu et al., 2024c, Xu et al., 2026b] . 在此直接策略家族中，交互行为主要具有反应性特征 [Niu et al., 2026] 它作为对后续动作的直接观察输入，而非模型在行动前所预测的接触轨迹。 一种较新的方法实现了触觉预测：DreamTacVLA 对触觉世界模型进行优化，使得动作基于实际与预测的触觉结果 [Ye et al., 2025]；预测性的视觉-触觉策略通过自回归方式生成未来的接触信息或力域动作轨迹 [Heng et al., 2025, Wu et al., 2024d]；视觉-触觉世界模型与触觉动作模型则致力于基于接触信息的预测，以用于规划与闭环控制 [Higuera et al., 2026, Zheng et al., 2026, Zang et al., 2026, Zhang et al., 2026c, Yuan et al., 2026b, Lou et al., 2026, Wu et al., 2026a, Tian et al., 2026]。与现有技术相比，TWAM 所解决的关键问题 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} 并非是预测性触觉是否有效，而是如何在大规模系统中将预测性触觉纳入动作循环，同时保留预训练的视觉世界模型。

Tactile perception supplies the contact signals vision misses. Tactile-aware VLA systems [ Zhang et al., 2026b , Huang et al., 2025b , Zhang et al., 2026a , Hao et al., 2025 , Yu et al., 2025 ] align tactile observations with language and action [ Huang et al., 2025b , Hao et al., 2025 ] , inject force feedback into the action expert [ Yu et al., 2025 ] , specialize for insertion and other contact-heavy tasks [ Zhang et al., 2026a ] , or steer a pretrained visuomotor policy at inference with a touch-derived feasibility signal [ Zhang et al., 2026b ] . Force-aware policies fuse RGB with high-rate force/torque streams, learn task-dependent compliance, or add torque adapters to a VLA decoder [ He et al., 2024 , Lee et al., 2025 , Yang et al., 2023 , Hou et al., 2024 , Zhang et al., 2025 ] , and visuo-tactile policies show that touch improves fine-grained manipulation and stays robust when vision is ambiguous or occluded [ Huang et al., 2025a , Zhao et al., 2025 , Bi et al., 2025b , Cheng et al., 2025 , Wu et al., 2024c , Xu et al., 2026b ] . In this direct-policy family touch is largely reactive [ Niu et al., 2026 ] : it enters as an immediate observation for the next action rather than a contact trajectory the model predicts before acting. A more recent line makes touch predictive : DreamTacVLA fine-tunes a tactile world model so actions condition on both real and predicted tactile consequences [ Ye et al., 2025 ] , predictive visuo-tactile policies autoregress future contact or generate force-domain action trajectories [ Heng et al., 2025 , Wu et al., 2024d ] , and visuo-tactile world models and tactile action models study contact-aware prediction for planning and closed-loop control [ Higuera et al., 2026 , Zheng et al., 2026 , Zang et al., 2026 , Zhang et al., 2026c , Yuan et al., 2026b , Lou et al., 2026 , Wu et al., 2026a , Tian et al., 2026 ] . Against this prior art the question 𝒩 0 -TWAM addresses is not whether predicted touch helps, but how to keep it in the action loop at scale while preserving a pretrained visual world model.

机器人学世界模型

世界模型会学习环境的预测模型，并根据文本信息推导出未来状态 [Wan Team et al., 2025] , 驱动指令 [Hu et al., 2023] ，或机器人动作 [Wu et al., 2024b] 在大型视频数据集上训练，世界基础模型 [NVIDIA et al., 2025] 生成高保真未来的观测结果，从未标注视频中恢复可操控动作的动态机制 [Bruce et al., 2024] 或通过在潜在空间中进行预测，而非在像素层面进行预测 [Assran et al., 2025] 与其直接将当前观测映射为动作，一种互补的方法首先预测未来状态，然后基于该预测推导出动作，这种基于模型的智能体会针对预测结果优化策略。 [Hafner et al., 2020, Hafner et al., 2021, Hafner et al., 2019, Ha and Schmidhuber, 2018, Alonso et al., 2024] 针对以视频为优先的策略，这些策略通过预测的视觉未来进行规划 [Du et al., 2023, Du et al., 2024, Ko et al., 2024, Liang et al., 2024] . World-action models (WAMs) 在单一模型中将两个阶段连接起来 [Wu et al., 2024a, Guo et al., 2024, Li et al., 2025b, Zhu et al., 2025, Cen et al., 2025, Li et al., 2026c, Ye et al., 2026c] 共享或重新利用视觉潜在空间以用于动作 [Cheang et al., 2024, Bi et al., 2025a, Wang et al., 2026a, Kim et al., 2026] 在多种时间抽象模型下 [Li et al., 2026d, Li et al., 2025a, Luo et al., 2026] . 在保持共享推理路径的同时，为每种模式分配独立能力 [Liang et al., 2025] 将各模态的前馈、注意力与归一化过程解耦，但保留共享的全局注意力机制 [Ma et al., 2026b] ; LingBot-VA，该模型 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} TWAM 基于并应用了 MoT WAM 视觉与动作层面 tokens [Li et al., 2026c] ，而因果视频训练则有助于长期部署 [Huang et al., 2025c, Xu et al., 2026a] . 最近的 WAM 在测试时进行未来预测的支付方式上有所不同：FastWAM 将视频分支压缩为单次编码器 [Yuan et al., 2026c] , Metis 允许动作专家绕过未来的视频生成过程 [Li et al., 2026b] ，以及高效策略 WAM 修剪并缩短视频发布流程 [Li et al., 2026a] ，并在其他领域存在相关的蒸馏与简化技术 [Yan et al., 2026, Akbari et al., 2026, Song et al., 2026] ; 在这一方面，效率通常是通过削弱或绕过未来预测来获得的，而预测的载体则是像素。 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 占据了这一空间的触觉版本：它将视频、触觉及动作专家按级联方式排列，使得预测到的触摸动作成为动作专家所处理的中间预测结果，而非辅助信号或注意力偏差。[Wu et al., 2026a, Tian et al., 2026] 通过不对称分配宽度，在视觉专家保持完整预训练能力的同时，使触觉和动作专家在训练过程中变得更精简。这种不对称性使得流式推理成本较低，同时不会移除所预测的未来信息。

World models learn a predictive model of the environment and roll out future states conditioned on text [ Wan Team et al., 2025 ] , driving commands [ Hu et al., 2023 ] , or robot actions [ Wu et al., 2024b ] ; trained on large video corpora, world foundation models [ NVIDIA et al., 2025 ] generate high-fidelity future observations, recover action-controllable dynamics from unlabelled video [ Bruce et al., 2024 ] , or predict in a latent space rather than pixels [ Assran et al., 2025 ] . Rather than map the current observation directly to an action, a complementary line first predicts a future and then derives the action from it, from model-based agents that optimize a policy against predicted rollouts [ Hafner et al., 2020 , Hafner et al., 2021 , Hafner et al., 2019 , Ha and Schmidhuber, 2018 , Alonso et al., 2024 ] to video-first policies that plan through predicted visual futures [ Du et al., 2023 , Du et al., 2024 , Ko et al., 2024 , Liang et al., 2024 ] . World-action models (WAMs) couple the two stages in a single model [ Wu et al., 2024a , Guo et al., 2024 , Li et al., 2025b , Zhu et al., 2025 , Cen et al., 2025 , Li et al., 2026c , Ye et al., 2026c ] , sharing or repurposing visual latent spaces for action [ Cheang et al., 2024 , Bi et al., 2025a , Wang et al., 2026a , Kim et al., 2026 ] under various temporal abstractions [ Li et al., 2026d , Li et al., 2025a , Luo et al., 2026 ] . To give each modality its own capacity while keeping a shared reasoning path, MoT [ Liang et al., 2025 ] decouples per-modality feed-forward, attention, and normalization but preserves a shared global attention [ Ma et al., 2026b ] ; LingBot-VA, which 𝒩 0 -TWAM builds on, applies an MoT WAM over vision and action tokens [ Li et al., 2026c ] , and causal video training improves long-horizon rollouts [ Huang et al., 2025c , Xu et al., 2026a ] . Recent WAMs differ chiefly in how they pay for future prediction at test time: FastWAM collapses the video branch into a single-pass encoder [ Yuan et al., 2026c ] , Metis lets the action expert bypass future video generation [ Li et al., 2026b ] , and Efficient-WAM prunes and shortens the video rollout [ Li et al., 2026a ] , with related distillation and short-cutting elsewhere [ Yan et al., 2026 , Akbari et al., 2026 , Song et al., 2026 ] ; across this line efficiency is usually bought by weakening or bypassing the predicted future, and the predicted modality is pixels. 𝒩 0 -TWAM occupies the tactile version of this space: it orders the video, tactile, and action experts into a cascade so predicted touch is an intermediate prediction the action expert consumes rather than a side signal or attention bias [ Wu et al., 2026a , Tian et al., 2026 ] , and it allocates width asymmetrically to keep full pretrained capacity in the visual expert while training slimmer tactile and action experts. The same asymmetry keeps streaming inference cheap without pruning the predicted future away.

6 结论

我们提出了 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 是一种触觉原生世界模型策略，它将触觉纳入视频的预测未来中 world-action model 通过将预训练的视频扩散主干网络划分为三个与单一共享自注意力机制相连接的模态专家，并以帧号因果级联方式对它们进行排序， 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 预测近未来场景及近未来交互，然后从二者中读取动作。触觉具有双重角色（去噪预测目标与观测读取），而非对称设计使模型保持 7.2 B 和流式推理成本较低，因为每个动作去噪步骤仅重新运行轻量级的动作专家。 重要的是，触觉并非通过少量任务来评估的附加功能：𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} -TWAM 在大规模自采集真实机器人数据上进行了预训练，该数据包含同步的触觉信息，因此与视觉信息一起共同学习出统一的触觉表征，而非针对每个任务单独适配。在具有丰富接触行为的基准测试中，它是整体表现最强大的方法——在仿真环境中（84.584.5 在 UniVTAC 上，49.449.4 在 NeoSim 上），以及在真实机器人上（46.3%46.3\% 平均表现优于所有 vision-language-action 及仅基于视觉的世界动作基线），且消融实验表明，预测得到的触觉路径与实际观测到的触觉路径均具有贡献。

We presented 𝒩 0 -TWAM , a tactile-native world-model policy that brings touch into the predicted future of a video world-action model. By organizing a pretrained video-diffusion backbone into three per-modality experts tied by a single shared self-attention, and ordering them with a frame-id causal cascade, 𝒩 0 -TWAM predicts the near-future scene and the near-future contact and then reads action out of both. Tactile is given a dual role (a denoised foresight target and an observed reading), while an asymmetric design keeps the model at 7.2B and streaming inference cheap, since each action-denoising step re-runs only the lightweight action expert. Crucially, touch is not an add-on evaluated on a handful of tasks: 𝒩 0 -TWAM is pre-trained at scale, on a large self-collected real-robot data with synchronized tactile, so that a shared tactile representation is learned jointly with vision rather than fitted per task. Across contact-rich benchmarks it is the strongest method overall, in simulation ( 84.5 on UniVTAC, 49.4 on NeoSim) and on real robots ( 46.3 % average, ahead of every vision-language-action and vision-only world-action baseline), with ablations confirming that both the predicted and the observed tactile pathways contribute.

未来研究方向。

我们观察到三个主要方向。首先，更快的推理速度：单向级联的流式解码可以进一步加速，从而使策略以更高的控制速率运行。其次，更长的预测范围：延长视觉与触觉预测窗口，使模型能够更提前地预测接触事件。第三，更广泛的传感器覆盖：对更多类型、更多种类的触觉传感器进行训练，将扩大所学习的触觉表征能够应用于的本体范围。

We see three main directions. First, faster inference : streaming decoding of the one-directional cascade can be accelerated further, so the policy runs at higher control rates. Second, a longer predicted horizon : extending the vision and tactile prediction window would let the model anticipate contact events further ahead. Third, broader sensor coverage : training on more types and varieties of tactile sensors would widen the range of embodiments the learned tactile representation transfers to.

我们考虑了两种预训练数据方案：一种仅基于真实机器人遥操作数据进行训练；另一种则同时结合手工收集的 UMI 数据，随后再继续使用纯真实机器人遥操作数据。图 12 展示了后一种方案在前 18 k 步中的表现（完整训练过程共 30,00030{,}000 步）。由于 MoT 针对每种模态分别保存了独立的专家模型，因此我们可以分别记录视频、动作和触觉流领域的流匹配损失。在 + UMI 标记点之前，这三者均会下降并趋于稳定。 当 UMI 数据被混合进其中（约 60%60\% 的批次中）时，不同模态的表现会有所不同：视频损失在过渡阶段上升，随后稳定在比之前更高的水平，因为 UMI 视频内容是一种新的视觉分布，视频专家现在也必须对其进行适配；触觉损失几乎没有变化，因为两种数据源中的触觉信号相似；动作损失则仅出现微小的变化。

We consider two pre-training data recipes: one that trains on real-robot teleoperation data only, and one that additionally mixes in the hand-collected UMI data and then continues on the pure real-robot teleoperation data. Figure 12 shows the latter over the first 18k steps (the full run is 30,000 steps). Because the MoT keeps a separate expert per modality, we can log the flow-matching loss separately for the video, action, and tactile streams. Before the +UMI marker all three fall and stabilize. When the UMI data is mixed in (about 60 % of the batch) the modalities respond differently: the video loss rises at the transition and settles at a somewhat higher value than before, since the UMI footage is a new visual distribution the video expert must now also fit; the tactile loss barely moves, because the tactile signal is similar across the two data sources; and the action loss shows only a small step.
