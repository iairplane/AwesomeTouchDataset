摘要

Vision–language–action 基础模型能学到通用操作知识，但只靠视觉很难判断物体质量和局部滑移。负载改变时，机器人需要根据接触情况调整抓握，固定条件下录制的示教不一定覆盖这些调整。我们提出 VTLA-RL，研究不同负载下的视触觉抓取及其强化学习后训练。主要方法对历史触觉图像和 RGB 输入使用同一个视觉编码器，在统一动作空间中同时生成并执行运动目标与双侧抓握力目标。训练先混合真实和仿真示教，学到初步的运动与接触知识；再通过两阶段多任务强化学习，只用仿真交互和任务成功回报优化策略。这一阶段无需继续用真实示教监督，训练完成后策略直接部署到真机。 我们在仿真中比较不同的触觉表征和强化学习后训练方案，并在真机上分别测试负载变化和摆放位置受扰动的情形。完整方法的抓取成功率高于初始监督策略。不同方案从强化学习后训练中得到的提升并不相同，为这类抓取任务选择表征和训练方法提供了实验依据。

Vision–language–action foundation models learn general manipulation priors, but object mass and local slip are difficult to infer from vision alone. Changing loads require contact-driven grasp adjustments that demonstrations collected under fixed conditions may not adequately cover. We present VTLA-RL to study visuotactile grasping policies and their reinforcement learning (RL) post-training under varying loads. Our main approach shares a visual encoder between tactile-image histories and RGB inputs to jointly generate and execute motion and bilateral force targets in a unified action space. Mixed real and simulated demonstrations first establish motion and contact priors. Two-stage multi-task RL then uses only simulated interaction to optimize the policy through task-success returns without continued real-demonstration supervision, and the trained policy deploys directly to real robots. We compare tactile representations and RL post-training routes in simulation and separately evaluate load variation and placement perturbations on hardware. The full method achieves higher grasping success than the supervised initialization, while RL post-training gains differ across approaches, providing empirical evidence for choosing representations and training approaches for this task.

1 引言

将饮料箱从桌子移动至目标区域，需要语义理解、几何定位以及接触控制能力。视觉系统能够识别目标并规划运动路径，但在接触前难以区分外观相同但内部质量不同的容器。负载变化会改变稳定抓取所需的接触条件，而初始抓取状态在搬运过程中也可能出现松动。为了在不同负载下完成搬运，策略必须根据当前接触状态调整抓取姿态、运动方式及握力。

Moving a beverage carton from a table to a target area requires semantic understanding, geometric localization, and contact control. Vision can identify the target and plan motion, but has difficulty distinguishing visually identical containers with different internal masses before contact. Load changes alter the contact conditions required for stable grasping, and an initial grasp may slip during lifting or transport. To complete transport under varying loads, the policy must adjust its grasp pose, motion, and grip force based on the current contact state.

Vision–language–action ( VLA ) 模型提供可迁移的动作先验；接触行为则揭示了接触变形、滑移趋势及力变化。添加接触信息并不能直接确保策略能够利用这些信息在变化载荷下调整抓取行为。模仿学习主要继承已展示的运动与力行为，而在固定条件下收集的示范可能无法涵盖新的接触状态。模拟交互则提供了一个可重置的训练环境，有助于扩展这种经验，并降低在硬件探索过程中发生掉落、碰撞及材料损坏的风险。

Vision–language–action (VLA) models provide transferable action priors; touch reveals contact deformation, slip tendencies, and force changes. Adding touch does not directly ensure that a policy can use this information to adjust grasps under changing loads. Imitation learning mainly inherits demonstrated motion and force habits, while demonstrations collected under fixed conditions may not cover new contact states. Simulated interaction offers a resettable training environment for expanding this experience and reduces the risks of drops, collisions, and material damage during hardware exploration.

We present VTLA-RL (Fig. 1 ) to study visuotactile grasp adaptation under varying loads and the effects of tactile representations and reinforcement learning (RL) post-training routes. Without additional tactile pretraining, our main approach shares a pretrained visual encoder between tactile-image histories and RGB inputs, and uses one action expert to jointly generate motion and bilateral force targets. Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on mixed real and simulated demonstrations establishes motion and contact priors. Two-stage multi-task RL in simulation then introduces placement and mass randomization in sequence to optimize the unified motion–force policy through task-success returns. The policy deploys directly to real robots through a hybrid force–position controller.

基于固定位置和质量的真实及模拟演示，模拟强化学习通过随机化位置与质量参数来扩展交互体验。我们在存在位置扰动和不同负载条件下的硬件上进行了抓取测试。对比实验与消融实验表明，这种方法比监督式初始化具有更高的成功率，且能提升位置泛化能力与负载适应能力。硬件实验结果还显示，当前触觉阵列方法在SFT初始化时表现更优，而基于图像的方法则能在训练后获得更大的性能提升。

Starting from real and simulated demonstrations at fixed placements and masses, simulation RL expands interaction experience through placement and mass randomization. We evaluate grasping on hardware under placement perturbations and varying loads. Comparisons and ablations show higher success than the supervised initialization, with improved position generalization and load adaptation. Hardware results also show that the current tactile-array approach performs better at SFT initialization, whereas the image-based approach achieves larger post-training gains.

2 相关工作

2.1 触觉表征与基于接触感知的策略

Sparsh 通过自我监督机制在多个传感器上学习触觉特征；[10] 与 AnyTouch 以及 AnyTouch 和 2 分别学习静态/动态及与力相关的观测数据的通用表征，[7, 8]。TaF- VLA 通过触觉-力对齐技术将触觉特征与物理力相结合，[12]。这些方法提升了数据覆盖度、传感器适应性及物理表征能力，但仅依靠表征质量并不能决定策略在成功操作中选择的抓力大小。

Sparsh learns tactile features across sensors through self-supervision [ 10 ] ; AnyTouch and AnyTouch 2 learn general representations of static/dynamic and force-related observations, respectively [ 7 , 8 ] . TaF-VLA grounds tactile features in physical forces through tactile–force alignment [ 12 ] . These approaches improve data coverage, sensor adaptation, and physical representation, but representation quality alone does not determine how much grip force a policy chooses in successful manipulation.

VTLA , 触觉- VLA ，并且 VLA 通过偏好学习、物理知识及双层级反馈，扩展视觉策略的本体。 [36, 11, 2] . OmniVTLA 处理异构触觉输入 [5] ; ForceVLA 与 ForceVLA2 强调力量反馈、动作生成及闭环控制 [34, 17] ; AT- VLA 研究触觉注射的时机与位置 [16] . DreamTacVLA 与 OmniVTA 将触觉预测与接触动作相连 [32, 42] . FTP- 1 推进多源传感器预训练技术进展 [35] ; N0N_{0} - VTLA 进一步利用大规模触觉数据与 ALTER 优势条件下的离线策略优化技术 [20] . ViTaL 将局部视觉触觉技能与残差强化学习相结合 [41] ; TouchGuide 利用触摸交互来引导推理过程中的操作 [38] ; ViTaR 选择并缩放冻结状态下有限残差 VLA [29] ; TouchWorld 将预测到的触觉目标与高频残差修正机制 [43] 相结合。HapticVLA 采用注重安全的奖励加权策略 flow matching，以惩罚过强的握力，并通过无触觉传感器的方式提取触觉信息用于部署 [9]。VTLA -RL 采用塔贝罗式混合力-位置接口 [30]，共同生成运动与双侧力目标，并通过仿真强化学习对它们进行优化。由于在部署阶段保留了触觉反馈，该算法专注于在不依赖物体质量标签的情况下，根据负载变化调整运动和力值。

VTLA, Tactile-VLA, and VLA-Touch extend visual policies through preference learning, physical knowledge, and dual-level feedback [ 36 , 11 , 2 ] . OmniVTLA handles heterogeneous tactile inputs [ 5 ] ; ForceVLA and ForceVLA2 emphasize force feedback, action generation, and closed-loop control [ 34 , 17 ] ; AT-VLA studies the timing and location of tactile injection [ 16 ] . DreamTacVLA and OmniVTA connect tactile prediction to contact actions [ 32 , 42 ] . FTP-1 advances multi-source sensor pretraining [ 35 ] ; N 0 -VTLA further uses large-scale tactile data and ALTER advantage-conditioned offline policy improvement [ 20 ] . ViTaL combines local visuotactile skills with residual reinforcement learning [ 41 ] ; TouchGuide uses touch to guide actions at inference [ 38 ] ; ViTaR selects and scales bounded residuals over a frozen VLA [ 29 ] ; TouchWorld combines predicted tactile goals with high-frequency residual correction [ 43 ] . HapticVLA uses safety-aware reward-weighted flow matching to penalize excessive grip force and distills tactile knowledge for deployment without tactile sensors [ 9 ] . VTLA-RL uses a Tabero-style hybrid force–position interface [ 30 ] , jointly generates motion and bilateral force targets, and optimizes them through simulation RL. With tactile feedback retained at deployment, it focuses on adapting motion and force to changing loads without object-mass labels.

2.2 触觉模拟与评估

TACTO、Taxim 和 FOTS 能够生成光学触觉观测数据 [28, 24, 40]；TacSL 将触觉图像与接触力分布整合到 GPU 学习过程中 [1]；TacEx 通过模块化框架将软体接触与光学模型结合在一起 [21]。DiffTactile 支持可微化的接触机制与参数校准 [25]，Taccel 利用 GPU 并行性实现高保真度接触模拟 [18]，TaCauchy 提供基于连续介质的应力驱动的触觉仿真 [39]，而 TactileGenesis 则研究多种触觉抽象形式及其对策略学习的影响 [6]。丰富的接触模型能够降低数据成本，但也会引入材料参数、数值求解以及传感器与统计数据的匹配问题。

TACTO, Taxim, and FOTS generate optical tactile observations [ 28 , 24 , 40 ] ; TacSL incorporates tactile images and contact-force distributions into GPU learning [ 1 ] ; TacEx integrates soft-body contact and optical models through a modular framework [ 21 ] . DiffTactile supports differentiable contact and parameter calibration [ 25 ] , Taccel targets high-fidelity contact with GPU parallelism [ 18 ] , TaCauchy provides continuum stress-based tactile simulation [ 39 ] , and TactileGenesis studies multiple tactile abstractions and their effects on policy learning [ 6 ] . Rich contact models reduce data cost, but introduce material parameters, numerical solving, and sensor-statistics matching.

UniVTAC 整合多传感器仿真、触觉表征与操作基准测试 [3] ; Tacmap 通过几何接触表示法缩小了领域差距 [26] . SoftVTBench 将任务完成与物体变形分离，并利用仅包含评估功能的有限元状态来构建考虑变形的成功指标，表明接触质量需要独立的评估。 [14] . VTLA -RL 将模拟与硬件中的视觉触觉观测结果以及运动-力控制接口进行对齐，随后通过抓握成功情况以及在不同负载和位置扰动下的适应性与迁移效果进行评估。

UniVTAC unifies multisensor simulation, tactile representation, and manipulation benchmarks [ 3 ] ; Tacmap reduces the domain gap through geometric contact representations [ 26 ] . SoftVTBench separates task completion from object deformation and uses evaluator-only finite-element states to construct deformation-aware success, showing that contact quality requires independent evaluation [ 14 ] . VTLA-RL aligns optical tactile observations and motion–force control interfaces across simulation and hardware, then evaluates adaptation and transfer through grasping success under varying loads and placement perturbations.

2.3 用于 VLA 策略的强化学习

PiRL ( πRL\pi_{\mathrm{RL}} ) [4] 采用近端策略优化（PPO）技术来微调基于流模型的 VLA 策略的动作专家。RL Token (RLT) [31] 学习一种紧凑的输出表示，然后在训练轻量级演员-评论家模型时冻结该表示与 VLA。SimpleVLA-RL [15] 通过强化学习优化动作 token VLA 策略，结合并行轨迹采样与探索策略以提升任务性能。DSRL 冻结生成策略，将初始噪声视为可学习的动作，并通过 RL [27] 优化噪声输入。VTLA -RL 的强化学习框架基于 PiRL 和 RLinf [33]，采用 Hebero [37] 方法实现成功率加权多任务联合优化。

PiRL ( π RL ) [ 4 ] uses proximal policy optimization (PPO) to fine-tune the action expert of flow-based VLA policies. RL Token (RLT) [ 31 ] learns a compact readout representation, then freezes the VLA and representation while training a lightweight actor–critic. SimpleVLA-RL [ 15 ] optimizes action-token VLA policies through RL, combining parallel trajectory sampling and exploration strategies to improve task performance. DSRL freezes the generative policy, treats the initial noise as a learnable action, and optimizes the noise input through RL [ 27 ] . VTLA-RL’s RL framework builds on PiRL and RLinf [ 33 ] and follows Hebero [ 37 ] for success-rate-weighted multi-task joint optimization.

TacCoRL [19] 通过专用编码器和接触门机制，将正常状态的 taxel 历史数据融入 VLA 上下文与动作专家中，并结合混合示范初始化与模拟 PPO 技术，以提升接触密集任务中的恢复能力与完成度。其主要的强化学习方法仍保留真实示范监督机制。VTLA -RL 专注于在变化负载下的抓取任务，主要利用具有共享 RGB 编码器的触觉-图像历史数据，并通过 Tabero 式混合力-位置接口 [30]，协同生成运动与双力目标。在混合示范初始化之后，强化学习训练后期仅使用模拟交互及任务成功反馈，不再持续依赖真实示范监督。我们针对不同负载与位置扰动条件下的抓取适应性进行了评估。

TacCoRL [ 19 ] injects normal-taxel histories into the VLA context and action expert through a dedicated encoder and contact gating, combining mixed-demonstration initialization with simulation PPO to improve recovery and completion in contact-rich tasks. Its main RL approach retains real-demonstration supervision. VTLA-RL focuses on grasping under changing loads, primarily uses tactile-image histories with the shared RGB encoder, and executes jointly generated motion and bilateral force targets through a Tabero-style hybrid force–position interface [ 30 ] . After mixed-demonstration initialization, RL post-training uses only simulated interaction and task-success returns, without continued real-demonstration supervision. We evaluate grasping adaptation under varying loads and placement perturbations.

We use a π 0.5 backbone [ 13 ] with two 224 × 224 RGB views. TacImage concatenates eight tactile frames per finger, including the current frame, crops and downsamples to the RGB resolution, and shares the pretrained RGB vision transformer (ViT) without a separate tactile encoder. TacField shares TacCoRL’s [ 19 ] idea of encoding tactile-array histories as tokens; our causal temporal convolutional network (TCN) maps a reference frame and eight history frames of 2D marker arrays to one prefix token.

每个有效动作均具有 13 尺寸：末端执行器位置 3 ), 旋转向量 3 ), 夹持器 1 ），以及接触力目标（ 3 每根手指：一个正常分量和两个切向分量，以牛顿为单位，位于其传感器参考系中。一个流动动作专家共同生成运动与力目标，这些目标未经归一化，由塔贝罗式混合力-位置控制器执行。 [30] 在初始化阶段，力目标会接受示范监督，并作为后续仿真强化学习优化后的动作变量。

Each effective action has 13 dimensions: end-effector position (3), rotation vector (3), gripper (1), and contact-force targets (3 per finger: one normal and two tangential components, in newtons in its sensor frame). One flow action expert jointly generates motion and force targets, unnormalized for execution by a Tabero-style hybrid force–position controller [ 30 ] . Force targets receive demonstration supervision during initialization and remain action variables optimized by subsequent simulation RL.

3.2 仿真与真实场景对齐

真实与模拟场景均使用相应的几何模型、摄像头及机器人。遥操作提供真实演示，而 Tabero 风格的重放则提供模拟数据。三维高斯斑点图（3 DGS）重建技术能够减少视觉差异。图 3 展示了抓取过程中的视觉与触觉一致性。

5 结论

我们提出了 VTLA -RL 方法，用于研究视觉触觉抓取策略以及在不同负载条件下的训练后表现。该方法结合了混合示范初始化、共享视觉编码器以及统一运动-力策略，并通过模拟交互与直接真实机器人部署进行强化学习训练后优化。在模拟环境与真实硬件上对触觉表征与强化学习方法的比较，以及组件消融实验表明，该策略能够在随机放置及不同物体质量条件下实现有效的抓取。

We presented VTLA-RL to study visuotactile grasping policies and their post-training under varying loads. The method combines mixed-demonstration initialization, a shared visual encoder, and a unified motion–force policy, followed by RL post-training through simulated interaction and direct real-robot deployment. Comparisons across tactile representations and RL methods in simulation and on real hardware, together with component ablations, demonstrate effective grasping under randomized placements and varying object masses.

局限

当前方法仅在小规模数据与测试上进行了评估，而硬件实验所提供的证据有限，难以区分微小的性能差异。未来的研究将扩大任务多样性与评估规模，降低对初始化的依赖，并提升反馈的及时性。

The current method is evaluated only on small-scale data and tests, and the hardware experiments provide limited evidence for distinguishing small performance differences. Future work will expand task diversity and evaluation scale, reduce dependence on initialization, and improve feedback timeliness.

附录 H 快速计时与消融定义

FAST 同时执行观测收集、推理与执行操作。触觉历史信息在 20 Hz 时刻进行更新。预测结果与观测时间戳保持一致，已发生的动作则被跳过。在基础目标之间的四个插值点会生成 100 Hz 硬件指令。力值修正在插值完成后应用，并从写入缓冲区的动作数据中移除，从而避免累积效应。

FAST runs observation collection, inference, and execution concurrently. Tactile history updates at 20 Hz. Predictions align to observation timestamps, and elapsed actions are skipped. Four interpolation points between base targets yield 100 Hz hardware commands. The force correction is applied after interpolation and removed from the action written back to the buffer, preventing accumulation.

重叠块按从旧到新的顺序进行融合。新块的权重在三个基础步骤中从 0.2 线性递增至 0.8，且对之前的结果具有互补的权重。翻译、夹持器与力的映射均采用线性插值方式；旋转则采用局部旋转矢量坐标进行插值。每次接纳新数据后，都会从原始块重新计算融合结果。

Overlapping chunks are fused from oldest to newest. A new chunk’s weight increases linearly from 0.2 to 0.8 over three base steps, with complementary weight on the previous result. Translation, gripper, and force interpolate linearly; rotation interpolates in local rotation-vector coordinates. Fusion is recomputed from original chunks after each admission.

异步仿真会保留所有 50 预测的动作（2.5 秒），并要求被接受的观察时间戳之间至少间隔 0.2 秒。硬件则保留前 10 个动作（0.5 秒），立即接受返回的预测结果，并允许在运行过程中进行一次推理。其首个处理块从到达时间开始。当等待目标时，仿真会暂停，并设定 30 秒的超时时间；硬件会保留最后的命令，暂停力修正及历史更新操作，并在 2 秒后停止。完全失效的硬件目标会立即停止，且不会被重新播放。

Asynchronous simulation retains all 50 predicted actions (2.5 s) and requires admitted observation timestamps to be at least 0.2 s apart. Hardware retains the first 10 actions (0.5 s), admits returned predictions immediately, and permits one inference in flight. Its first chunk starts at arrival time. When waiting for a target, simulation pauses, with a 30 s timeout; hardware holds its last command, suspends force correction and history updates, and stops after 2 s. Fully expired hardware targets cause immediate stopping and are not replayed.

在 400 轮次的 TacImage PiRL 中，40,594 模拟推理事件的平均/第95百分位数墙钟往返时间分别为 0.353 / 0.953 秒。在模拟时间中，接受的观测间隔的平均值为 0.213 秒，约相当于 4.7 Hz。这些共享服务指标能够区分墙钟推理时间与模拟时间刷新率。

For TacImage PiRL at round 400, 40,594 simulation inference events give median/P95 wall-clock round-trip times of 0.353/0.953 s. The mean admitted-observation interval is 0.213 s in simulation time, approximately 4.7 Hz. These shared-service measurements distinguish wall-clock inference time from simulation-time refresh rate.

w/o FAST控制采用同步推理机制，随后执行十次开环动作。在推理过程中进行仿真暂停，并每0.5秒更新策略；两种执行模式均使用相同的60 Hz力反馈控制器。硬件w/o FAST也采用同步的十动作前缀机制。

The w/o FAST control uses synchronous inference followed by ten open-loop actions. Simulation pauses during inference and refreshes the policy every 0.5 s; both execution modes retain the same 60 Hz force-feedback controller. Hardware w/o FAST also uses synchronous ten-action prefixes.
