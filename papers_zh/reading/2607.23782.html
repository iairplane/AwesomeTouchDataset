摘要

我们提出 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-VTLA，一种视觉—触觉—语言—动作（vision–tactile–language–action，VTLA）基础模型，具备两项能力。（1）利用触觉感知和触觉反馈控制完成精细的富接触操作。（2）利用已有部署数据离线改进策略。我们以现有视觉骨干为出发点，提出一套触觉集成训练方案，包括视触觉预训练、分阶段接入触觉分支，以及以优势为条件的离线策略改进。预训练时，策略从大规模机器人视触觉数据集 NeoData 学习一般性的接触先验。据我们所知，𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-VTLA 是首个在大规模触觉数据上预训练的 VTLA 模型。 后训练时，我们为策略增加触觉预测分支，将规模化学习得到的接触规律转化为下游触觉操作中的精细运动调整。为离线改进策略，我们提出 ALTER。这是一种以优势为条件的离线强化学习方法，将相对任务进展及轨迹事件比较转换为二值优势标签，并用固定的部署语料训练策略。该方法进一步改善了柔性物体操作等富接触技能的任务学习。在各项富接触基准上，𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-VTLA 大幅超过强基线。在 NeoReal 的全部九项真机任务中均优于对照。在包含二十项任务的仿真基准上，平均成功率为 63.8%63.8\%，最强基线为 44.0%44.0\%。 经 ALTER 训练的 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-VTLA 策略在三项长程真机任务中达到 7575–95%95\% 的成功率。这些结果为适用范围更广的触觉驱动操作策略奠定了基础。

We present 𝒩 0 -VTLA , a vision–tactile–language–action (VTLA) foundation model capable of (1) fine-grained contact-rich manipulation with tactile perception and tactile-feedback control, and (2) offline policy improvement from stored deployment data. Stepping towards current visual-based backbones, we propose an overall training recipe for tactile integration, consisting of visuo-tactile pre-training, staged tactile-pathway integration, and advantage-conditioned offline policy improvement. During pre-training, the policy learns broad contact priors from NeoData, our large-scale visuo-tactile robot dataset. To our knowledge, 𝒩 0 -VTLA is the first VTLA model pretrained on tactile data at scale. During post-training, we augment the policy with a predictive tactile pathway, distilling the contact patterns learned at scale into the fine motion adjustments in downstream tactile-centric manipulation. For offline policy improvement, we introduce ALTER , an advantage-conditioned offline Reinforcement Learning (RL) method that converts relative progress and trajectory-event comparisons into binary advantage labels for policy training on a fixed deployment corpus. This procedure further improves task-specific learning on contact-rich skills such as deformable object manipulation. Across contact-rich benchmarks, 𝒩 0 -VTLA outperforms strong baselines by wide margins: it wins all nine real-robot NeoReal tasks and reaches 63.8 % mean success on the twenty-task simulation suite against 44.0 % for the strongest baseline. 𝒩 0 -VTLA policies trained with ALTER reach 75 – 95 % success on three long-horizon real-robot tasks. Results lay a foundation for versatile tactile-driven manipulation policies.

1 引言

Vision–language–action（VLA）模型让操作策略具备了通用能力。这类模型从预训练的视觉–语言骨干微调而来，可以遵循指令，并跨任务、场景和本体迁移 (Kim et al., 2024; Black et al., 2025; Physical Intelligence et al., 2025b)。触觉在这一进程中很少得到应用，现有策略因此仍不擅长接触密集操作。此前引入触觉的尝试，所用数据最多只有几十小时，集中在少数技能。据我们所知，本报告提出了首个使用规模化触觉数据预训练的 VLA 策略。我们聚焦于光学触觉感知 (Yuan et al., 2017; Lambeta et al., 2020)，在夹爪的每个夹指上安装自研传感器，以图像的形式读取接触信号。 触觉帧和普通相机图像的性质迥异。它们含有噪声，未接触时几乎没有信号，主要在刚开始接触或接触即将变化的短时间内提供信息。

Vision–language–action (VLA) models have made manipulation policies general. Fine-tuned from pretrained vision–language backbones, they follow instructions and transfer across tasks, scenes, and embodiments ( Kim et al., 2024 ; Black et al., 2025 ; Physical Intelligence et al., 2025b ) . Touch, however, has remained largely absent from this progress, leaving current policies with a persistent weakness in contact-rich manipulation: the tactile extensions attempted so far train on task-scale collections, at most tens of hours gathered for a handful of skills. This report presents, to our knowledge, the first VLA policy pretrained on tactile data at scale. We scope the report to vision-based tactile sensing ( Yuan et al., 2017 ; Lambeta et al., 2020 ) , instrumenting each gripper finger with our self-developed sensor so that contact is read out as an image. This signal is nothing like a camera view: tactile frames are noisy, nearly empty away from contact, and informative almost only in the brief windows when contact forms or is about to change.

现有系统主要通过两种方式融合触觉。第一种把触觉 tokens 拼接到视觉–语言上下文中，将触觉流作为额外的相机视角 (Huang et al., 2025; Cheng et al., 2026)。不过，触觉信号稀疏，多数时间没有变化，将它接入为高信息密度图像设计的输入前缀，效果有限。第二种在动作通路中加入当前触觉读数，用来引导去噪 (Xue et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2026b)。当前触觉帧记录的是已经执行的动作引起的接触，很难据此判断下一步动作应当预料到怎样的接触。仅根据当前读数生成动作时，策略对接触变化的反应总慢一步。

Existing systems integrate touch along one of two paths. The first concatenates tactile tokens into the vision–language context and treats the tactile stream as one more camera ( Huang et al., 2025 ; Cheng et al., 2026 ) ; yet a signal that is sparse and mostly silent buys little in a prefix built for information-dense views. The second injects the current tactile reading into the action pathway to guide denoising ( Xue et al., 2025 ; Zhang et al., 2026b ) ; this placement mischaracterizes the role of touch, since a tactile frame records contact that actions already taken have produced and, by itself, says little about the contact the next actions must anticipate. Conditioned on it alone, the policy stays one step behind its own contact events.

𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-VTLA 读作 NeoVTLA，采用第三种方式。视觉–语言前缀保持原有输入，动作专家根据预测的触觉信息生成动作。一个小型预测器同时读取视觉–语言上下文和当前触觉 tokens，输出潜在触觉 tokens zz，估计接下来一个 action chunk 内触觉的净变化。这样，策略可以提前考虑接下来动作将引起的接触。触觉帧采用接触差分图，由冻结的预训练视觉编码器编码，再经过轻量的可训练投影。如图 1 所示，新增通路分三个阶段训练。除了监督式任务适配，我们还将从部署记录中学习建模为以优势为条件的离线强化学习。 ALTER 用干净的示教、触觉检测到的物体掉落事件和记录下来的人工纠正，训练成对进度模型。随后，它为离线策略学习分配相对于当前任务阶段的二值优势条件。

𝒩 0 -VTLA , which we read as NeoVTLA, takes a third path: it keeps tactile out of the vision–language prefix and conditions the action expert on a prediction of touch rather than the current reading. A small predictor reads the vision–language context together with the current tactile tokens and emits latent tactile tokens z that estimate the net tactile change over the coming action chunk, so the policy acts on the contact its own actions are about to cause. The tactile frames are contact-difference images encoded by a frozen pretrained visual encoder through a lightweight trainable projection, and a three-stage recipe brings this newly initialized pathway online, as Figure 1 shows. Beyond supervised task adaptation, we formulate learning from stored deployment experience as advantage-conditioned offline RL. ALTER trains a pairwise progress model from clean demonstrations, tactile-detected object-drop events, and logged human corrections, then assigns stage-relative binary advantage conditions for policy learning.

完整系统以 vision–language–action 骨干为基础，该骨干基于 PaliGemma (Beyer et al., 2024)。系统还采用统一的跨本体动作空间和覆盖单双臂平台的视触觉数据集 NeoData，数据详情见配套报告 (NeoteAI Team and Fudan TEAI Team, 2026)。评估完整系统前，我们先验证潜变量通路。第 1 阶段后，潜在 tokens 检索对应未来触觉目标的准确率为 92.3%92.3\%（Top‑1），随机猜测为 3.2%3.2\%。更严格的检验是，这种从触觉学习的表征能否提高任务表现。我们按相同协议，把 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-VTLA 与外部基线在真机基准 NeoReal 及接触密集仿真任务集中进行比较。 在含二十项任务的仿真任务集中，𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0}-VTLA 以较大优势超过最强基线，在 NeoReal 的九项真机任务中均取得最高成功率。

The full system rests on a vision–language–action backbone built on PaliGemma ( Beyer et al., 2024 ) , a canonical cross-embodiment action space, and NeoData, a multi-platform visuo-tactile corpus spanning single and dual-arm configurations, documented in a companion data report ( NeoteAI Team and Fudan TEAI Team, 2026 ) . Before evaluating the full system, we verify the latent pathway itself: after Stage 1, the latent tokens retrieve their matching future-tactile targets at 92.3 % top-1 accuracy, where chance sits at 3.2 % . Whether this grounded representation translates into better task performance is the more demanding test. We evaluate 𝒩 0 -VTLA against external baselines on the NeoReal real-robot benchmark and the simulated contact-rich suite under identical protocols. On the twenty-task simulation suite 𝒩 0 -VTLA leads the strongest baseline by a wide margin, and it wins all nine real-robot NeoReal tasks.

2 模型

𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} - VTLA 是一种高接触性操作策略。它读取相机视图、语言指令、机器人状态以及触摸信息，并生成一系列未来动作。该模型由基于 PaliGemma 和 (Beyer et al., 2024) 预训练的 vision–language–action 主干网络以及一个新增组件组成，后者是感知与动作之间的潜在触觉路径。主干网络将视图、指令和状态存储在视觉-语言前缀中，并通过流匹配动作专家生成 action chunk。在新增的路径中，一个冻结后的主干触觉编码器将每个手指的接触差异图像转换为 tokens，而一个小预测器则根据场景和指令将这些 tokens 提炼为潜在的触觉 tokens zz，从而估计未来 action chunk 期间预期的净接触变化。 动作专家直接依赖于 zz，触觉信息 never 进入视觉-语言前缀。因此，触觉作为预测目标被纳入策略中，而非作为额外的观测数据。图 2 – 4 将这一设计描述为三步流程，后续章节则围绕此展开。第 2.1 节完善基础策略，第 2.2 节则逐步阐述触觉路径。

𝒩 0 -VTLA is a policy for contact-rich manipulation. It reads camera views, a language instruction, robot state, and touch, and it generates a chunk of future actions. The model consists of a pretrained vision–language–action backbone built on PaliGemma ( Beyer et al., 2024 ) and one added component, a latent tactile pathway between perception and action. The backbone carries the views, instruction, and state in its vision–language prefix and generates the action chunk with a flow-matching action expert. In the added pathway, a frozen-backbone tactile encoder turns each finger’s contact-difference image into tokens, and a small predictor distills those tokens, in the context of the scene and instruction, into latent tactile tokens z that estimate the net contact change expected over the coming action chunk. The action expert is conditioned on z directly, and tactile never enters the vision–language prefix. Touch therefore enters the policy as a prediction target rather than as one more observation. Figures 2 – 4 lay out this design as a three-step recipe, and the section follows them. Section 2.1 fixes the base policy, and Section 2.2 walks through the tactile pathway step by step.

2.1 基础架构

基础策略将 PaliGemma 视觉-语言主干与 (Beyer et al., 2024) 流匹配动作专家 (Lipman et al., 2023) 进行配对。摄像头视图、指令以及机器人状态构成模型前缀，状态以离散形式而非独立连续输入的形式进入该前缀。基于该前缀，专家在 H=50H=50 步长的范围内对 action chunk 进行去噪处理，该过程在如 3232 维的规范 3 容器中进行，其宽度与槽位布局均保持与预训练动作头相同，从而其权重可直接加载。流匹配目标在所有 3232 维度上均被公开。每个平台对应其本体使用的维度，其余平台则没有模型需要学习的目标，因此单臂与双臂数据可在同一模型中共存，且遵循同一固定宽度的目标。 其他所有方面，包括架构、分词及训练流程，均保持与预训练骨干模型一致的不变状态。

The base policy pairs a PaliGemma vision–language backbone ( Beyer et al., 2024 ) with a flow-matching action expert ( Lipman et al., 2023 ) . Camera views, the instruction, and the robot state form the model prefix , state entering that prefix in discretized form rather than as a separate continuous input. Conditioned on the prefix, the expert denoises an action chunk over a horizon of H = 50 steps in the canonical 32 -dimensional container of Section 3 , whose width and slot layout we inherit unchanged from the pretrained action head so that its weights load directly. The flow-matching objective is unmasked over all 32 dimensions. Each platform populates the dimensions its embodiment uses, the rest carry zero targets the model learns to reproduce, and single- and dual-arm data therefore coexist in one model under a single fixed-width objective. All other aspects, including architecture, tokenization, and training procedure, are inherited unchanged from the pretrained backbone.

步骤 1：预测器及其未来触觉目标。

规范动作与状态模式。

所有本体形式均被整合为一个固定的 3232 维状态与动作容器，该容器继承自 π0.5\pi_{0.5} 和 (Physical Intelligence et al., 2025b)。该容器针对双臂设计，第一 2020 维被划分为每个臂一个 1010 维的槽位，剩余的 1212 则未被使用且始终为零。每个槽位内包含 1010 维的 33 维末端执行器位置、66 维的旋转角度 (Zhou et al., 2019) 以及 11 维的夹持通道。双臂场景会同时填充两个槽位，单臂场景则仅填充第一个槽位，第二个槽位同样填充零值。因此，单臂与双臂数据均存在于同一个固定宽度的容器中，策略对零值维度所做的处理方式由第 2.1 节的目标所决定。动作以绝对末端执行器姿态的形式存储。 在训练过程中，每个块都根据其首帧进行重新编码，因此模型所预测的运动是相对于该块起始姿态的。在部署阶段，通过该变换的逆变换将预测的块映射回绝对姿态，而逆运动学则将这些姿态转化为发送给机器人的关节指令。归一化统计信息是在块相对表示上计算的，针对机器人与动作模式各对分别进行。

All embodiments are unified into one fixed 32 -dimensional state and action container, inherited from π 0.5 ( Physical Intelligence et al., 2025b ) . The container is laid out for two arms, with the first 20 dimensions split into one 10 -dimensional slot per arm and the remaining 12 left unused and always zero. Within a slot, the 10 dimensions comprise a 3 -dimensional end-effector position, a 6 -dimensional rot6d rotation ( Zhou et al., 2019 ) , and a 1 -dimensional gripper channel. Dual-arm episodes populate both slots. Single-arm episodes populate the first slot only and leave the second zero-filled as well. Single- and dual-arm data therefore coexist in one fixed-width container, and what the policy does with the zero-filled dimensions is fixed by the objective of Section 2.1 . Actions are stored as absolute end-effector poses. At training time each chunk is rewritten relative to its own first frame, so the model predicts motion relative to the pose at which the chunk begins. At deployment the predicted chunk is mapped back to absolute poses through the inverse of that transform, and inverse kinematics resolves those poses into the joint commands sent to the robot. Normalization statistics are computed on the chunk-relative representation, separately for each pairing of robot and action schema.

4 训练

𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} - VTLA 通过三个核心阶段实现部署。随后，三阶段流程使潜在的触觉路径生效。监督式训练后，将该通用模型特化为特定任务模型。完成此核心流程后，可选的额外步骤会在独立部署数据上对任务策略进行进一步训练。在整个过程中，可训练表面仅在每个新接口稳定之后才会发展，从而让触觉路径生效，同时不会破坏预训练策略的稳定性。

𝒩 0 -VTLA reaches deployment through three core phases. A three-stage recipe then brings the latent tactile pathway online. 阶段 2：将潜在表示与动作专家进行对齐。

经过预训练的动作专家从未处理过潜在的触觉 token 信息，因此接下来我们教它使用界面。我们将触觉感知堆栈冻结在 1 检查点，仅对潜在信号到专家模型的映射以及动作专家进行训练，以符合基础动作目标。具体来说，在专家的注意力中，来自视觉-语言前缀的键和值被屏蔽，不再用于动作查询，仅保留潜在的 tokens 和 zz 以及噪声动作 tokens 作为专家可关注的唯一条件。屏蔽该前缀消除了仅从场景和指令直接预测动作的捷径，因此降低动作损失的唯一途径就是通过 zz。在联合训练放松其余策略之前，专家通过 zz 学会识别触觉信息，这与为语言-动作模型所采用的分阶段对齐策略精神一致，即 (Wang et al., 2026a)。

The pretrained action expert has never consumed a latent tactile token, so we next teach it the interface. We hold the tactile perception stack frozen at its Stage 1 checkpoint and train only the latent-to-expert projection and the action expert, under the base action objective. Concretely, in the expert’s attention the keys and values from the vision–language prefix are masked out for the action queries, leaving the latent tokens z and the noised action tokens as the only conditioning the expert can attend to. Masking the prefix removes the shortcut of predicting actions from scene and instruction alone, so the only route to lowering the action loss runs through z . The expert learns to read touch through z before any joint training loosens the rest of the policy, in the spirit of the staged alignment strategies explored for language–action models ( Wang et al., 2026a ) .

阶段 3：端到端联合训练。

当预测器建立基础并其接口对齐后，除始终处于冻结状态的触觉编码器主干外，其余部分均被解除冻结，并依据标准预训练方法，仅以动作目标为基准，对完整策略进行联合训练。Stage 2 阶段的视觉-语言掩码被移除，从而恢复直接的专家路径，专家再次能够同时看到场景与指令，与 zz 并行运行。来自动作目标的梯度现在通过预测器、两个投影层、动作专家以及视觉-语言主干共同流动，使感知堆栈能够适应专家的实际需求。Stage 1 的对比与重建目标不再实施。预测器仅通过动作梯度保持其基础，第 5.5 节中的扰动探测也证实其保留了该基础，而非重新路由。

With the predictor grounded and its interface aligned, we unfreeze everything except the always-frozen tactile encoder backbone and train the full policy jointly under the standard pre-training recipe, on the action objective alone. The vision–language mask of Stage 2 is removed, so the direct prefix-to-expert path is restored and the expert again sees scene and instruction alongside z . Gradients from the action objective now flow together through the predictor, the two projections, the action expert, and the vision–language backbone, letting the perception stack adapt to what the expert actually needs. The contrastive and reconstruction targets of Stage 1 are no longer applied. The predictor keeps its grounding through the action gradient alone, which the perturbation probe of Section 5.5 confirms it retains rather than reroutes.

4.3 监督任务适配

我们通过基于数百个演示数据的监督微调（SFT）方法，将预训练的 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} - VTLA 检查点适配到每个下游任务中，从该检查点开始温启动，并在较小规模下重复使用预训练方案。根据 3 章节所述，归一化统计值始终根据任务自身数据重新计算，绝不复用预训练结果。相同的预训练方案适用于真实机器人任务与模拟任务套件，每个任务对应一个策略。

We adapt a pretrained 𝒩 0 -VTLA checkpoint to each downstream task by supervised fine-tuning(SFT) on a few hundred demonstrations, warm-starting from that checkpoint and reusing the pre-training recipe at reduced scale. Normalization statistics are always recomputed on the task’s own data and never reused from pre-training, per Section 3 . The same recipe covers both real-robot tasks and the simulated task suite, with one policy per task.

我们将该方法命名为 ALTER，即 Advantage Labeling from Trajectory Events and Relative Progress 的缩写。在固定部署语料库下，ALTER 能够实现基于优势条件的离线强化学习。 (Physical Intelligence et al., 2025a) 无需额外的环境交互，如图中所示 6 . 清晰的演示提供了基于信号与地面的阶段间隔下的密集进度监督，这些阶段的边界通过触觉接触变化、末端执行器运动学、夹持器状态以及视觉事件提示来确定。而不完美的部署轨迹则提供了来自触觉检测到的物体掉落事件及记录的人类干预修正的稀疏前后对比信息。这些密集与稀疏的信号共同训练了特定任务的成对进度模型。随后，我们冻结该模型，并将其应用于所有用于离线策略学习的轨迹。 将每个观测值与场景开始时的估计值进行比较，以判断全局任务阶段的变化；同时将其与后续一个动作块时的估计值进行比较，以判断局部执行变化。在每一个预测阶段内，我们依据估计的局部变化对样本进行排序，将排名较高的样本赋予正标签，将排名较低的样本赋予负标签，并将这些标签附加到用于策略训练的任务提示中。

We call our method ALTER , short for Advantage Labeling from Trajectory Events and Relative Progress. Given a fixed deployment corpus, ALTER performs advantage-conditioned offline RL ( Physical Intelligence et al., 2025a ) without additional environment interaction, as summarized in Figure 6 . Clean demonstrations provide dense progress supervision from signal-grounded stage intervals, whose boundaries are localized using tactile contact changes, end-effector kinematics, gripper state, and visual event cues. Imperfect deployment trajectories instead provide sparse before-and-after preferences from tactile-detected object-drop events and logged human-in-the-loop corrections. These dense and sparse signals jointly train a task-specific pairwise progress model. We then freeze the model and apply it to every trajectory retained for offline policy learning. Comparing each observation with the episode start estimates global task phase, while comparing it with the observation one action-chunk later estimates local execution change. Within each predicted stage, we rank samples by estimated local change, assign positive labels to higher-ranked samples and negative labels to lower-ranked ones, and append the resulting label to the task prompt used for policy training.

After deploying task-adapted policies, we retain clean teleoperation demonstrations, autonomous rollouts, and human-in-the-loop (HIL) rollouts. The HIL corpus also includes staged-recovery episodes that start from selected error states and record human-teleoperated recovery trajectories. Clean demonstrations receive dense stage-progress annotations from a signal-grounded pipeline. From representative demonstrations, Gemini-3.5-Flash ( DeepMind, 2025 ) generates a shared task template comprising the L3 objective, ordered L2 stages, and their L1 steps. A human reviews this task-level template once, after which its vocabulary and ordering remain fixed across demonstrations of that task. For each demonstration, tactile contact changes identify candidate transition times, supplemented by end-effector motion, gripper state, and visual GEBD cues ( Shou et al., 2021 ) . After these candidates are merged and deduplicated, the VLM maps each resulting interval to an entry in the task template. It therefore assigns stage semantics to pre-segmented intervals rather than predicting timestamps from the full video. We do not assign monotone labels to complete autonomous or HIL trajectories because they may regress or retry. Instead, tactile contact loss localizes object-drop events, while HIL logs mark correction intervals. These timestamps define local event comparisons. 接触会改变操控方式，由接触行为决定。四项发现为这一结论提供了证据。

Touch changes manipulation where contact decides it. Four findings collect the evidence.

触摸将插入操作从一次性的视觉承诺，转变为基于接触情况的重试循环。

两个最难的真实机器人插入操作展示了该竞赛最清晰的差距。插座插入，即向插座中插入插头的操作，达到了 85%85\% 针对 60%60\% 用于 π0.5\pi_{0.5} ，而板卡插入功能用于安装扩展卡，可实现扩展 25%25\% 在一项任务中 ACT 以及 π0.5\pi_{0.5} 两者均彻底失败 0%0\% . 代表性部署实验揭示了这些性能提升背后的行为差异。一旦视觉对齐表明插入是可行的， π0.5\pi_{0.5} 执行向下运动。当插头或卡片与边缘接触而非插入插槽时，该过程会继续尝试，但最终失败。 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} - VTLA 相反，机器人通过抬起手臂、重新调整位置并再次尝试插入来应对意外的接触。这种模式在模拟环境中重复出现，其中“插入孔”与“插入管”均发挥作用。 95%95\% 以及 99%99\% . 因此，触觉路径将插入动作从单一的视觉规划动作转变为闭环过程，该过程能够检测到的阻塞尝试并从中恢复。

The two hardest real-robot insertions show the clearest margins of the campaign. Socket Plugging, inserting a plug into an outlet, reaches 85 % against 60 % for π 0.5 , and Board Insertion, seating an expansion card, reaches 25 % on a task where ACT and π 0.5 both fail outright at 0 % . Representative rollouts reveal the behavioral difference behind these gains. Once visual alignment suggests that insertion is possible, π 0.5 commits to the downward motion. When the plug or card meets the rim instead of entering the slot, it continues the attempt and fails. 𝒩 0 -VTLA instead responds to the unexpected contact by lifting the arm, realigning, and attempting the insertion again. The pattern repeats in simulation, where Insert Hole and Insert Tube reach 95 % and 99 % . The tactile pathway therefore changes insertion from a single visually planned motion into a closed-loop process that can detect a blocked attempt and recover from it.

通过调整开口大小，触摸操作能够实现对夹持力的精准控制。

grip力而非接触位置决定了另一类任务。在真实机器人“瓶子站立”任务中，被操控的对象是一个空心的、具有柔性的塑料瓶。π0.5\pi_{0.5} 使夹持器在瓶口周围夹持过深，以足够牢固的方式捏住瓶子，从而将整个瓶子提起，而非使其留在支撑面上。𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} - VTLA 则对夹持器的开口进行微小的连续调整，保持足够的接触力以控制瓶子，同时不会过度夹持导致瓶子倾倒，从而确保瓶子保持直立状态。尽管触觉传感器并非直接测量力值，但其变形信号与接触力密切相关，为调节 grip 提供了实用的反馈信号。这种行为与更广泛的研究结果一致。 在仿真中，举杯动作达到 30%30\% 水平，而真实机器人基线则保持在 0%0\% 水平；提物罐和抓取芯片动作则达到 88%88\% 水平。

Grip force rather than placement decides a second family of tasks. In the real-robot Bottle Standing task, the manipulated object is an empty, compliant plastic bottle. π 0.5 closes the gripper too far around the bottle mouth, pinching it firmly enough to lift the entire bottle instead of leaving it on the supporting surface. 𝒩 0 -VTLA instead makes small, continuous adjustments to the gripper aperture, maintaining sufficient contact to control the bottle without gripping hard enough to lift it, and consequently stabilizes the bottle upright. Although the tactile sensor does not measure force directly, its deformation signal is strongly coupled to contact force and provides a practical feedback signal for regulating grip. This behavior is consistent with the broader results. Bottle Standing reaches 30 % where both real-robot baselines remain at 0 % , while Lift Can and Grasp Chip reach 88 % in simulation.

Vision–language–action 策略。

现代策略类 vision–language–action（VLA）基于 ACT、(Zhao et al., 2023) 与扩散策略 (Chi et al., 2023) 所建立的动作块与生成式视觉运动架构。ACT 能够预测时间上连贯的 action chunks，而扩散策略模型则通过迭代去噪技术生成多模态动作序列。RT- 1 (Brohan et al., 2023b) 提出了用于大规模现实环境控制的变换器策略，随后 RT- 2 (Brohan et al., 2023a) 将互联网预训练的视觉-语言表示与机器人控制相结合，而 RT-X (Open X-Embodiment Collaboration et al., 2024) 与 Octo (Octo Model Team et al., 2024) 则使通用策略能够在不同机器人数据集上得到应用。后续 VLA 系统在两个主要方面实现了多样化：其动作生成机制，以及其扩展、迁移与部署策略。 OpenVLA (Kim et al., 2024) 提供了一个开放式的自回归主干模型，而 π0\pi_{0}、(Black et al., 2025) 和 π0.5\pi_{0.5}、(Physical Intelligence et al., 2025b) 则通过流匹配机制生成连续式的 action chunks。自回归动作模型 tokens 利用语言模型接口，并能够与预训练主干模型自然融合；而连续生成头模型则直接在连续空间内处理低级指令，能够捕捉多种不同且有效的动作序列。除了动作生成功能外，UniVLA (Bu et al., 2025) 和 Qwen- VLA、(Wang et al., 2026a) 还能实现任务、环境及本体层面的跨域迁移。StarVLA (StarVLA Community, 2026) 注重模块化特性，MiMo-Embodied (Hao et al., 2025) 致力于实现跨本体规模的扩展，而 Pragmatic VLA、(Wu et al., 2026c) 则专注于实际应用部署。 最新的基础级系统进一步提升了数据与模型规模：小米-机器人- 1 (Xiaomi Robotics Team et al., 2026) 在更多数据上进行预训练 100,000100{,}000 现实世界轨迹的时长，其 U0 变体 (Li et al., 2026b) 将动作合成与世界基础模型 CronusVLA 相结合 (Li et al., 2026a) 整合多帧上下文，InternVLA-A1。 5 (Ma et al., 2026) 将理解、潜在预见与行动统一起来，以及 GR- 3 (Cheang et al., 2025) , GR00T N1 (Bjorck et al., 2025) ，以及 SmolVLA (Shukor et al., 2025) 将通用型策略转化为经济实惠且高效的策略。如 VLABench 这样的基准测试套件 (Zhang et al., 2025a) 及 LIBERO-Plus (Fei et al., 2026) 追踪这一进展，在受控扰动下探索长期视野推理能力与鲁棒性。在这些系统中，泛化能力通过扩展异构预训练混合模型，或通过学习可于适应过程中复用的任务与本体抽象机制来实现。 这些策略共同实现了语义基础、跨机器人数据缩放以及生成式连续控制功能。然而，它们的观测空间仍主要围绕摄像头、语言及机器人状态展开，导致接触与滑动现象只能间接被感知。 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} - VTLA 保留语言依赖的流匹配机制 π0.5\pi_{0.5} 作为骨干结构，但增加了专用的高分辨率触觉路径。

Modern vision–language–action (VLA) policies build on action-chunk and generative visuomotor architectures established by ACT ( Zhao et al., 2023 ) and Diffusion Policy ( Chi et al., 2023 ) . ACT predicts temporally coherent action chunks, whereas Diffusion Policy models multimodal action sequences through iterative denoising. RT-1 ( Brohan et al., 2023b ) established transformer policies for real-world control at scale, and RT-2 ( Brohan et al., 2023a ) subsequently connected internet-pretrained vision–language representations to robot control, while RT-X ( Open X-Embodiment Collaboration et al., 2024 ) and Octo ( Octo Model Team et al., 2024 ) scaled generalist policies across heterogeneous robot datasets. Subsequent VLA systems have diversified along two broad axes: their action-generation mechanisms and their strategies for scaling, transfer, and deployment. OpenVLA ( Kim et al., 2024 ) provides an open autoregressive backbone, while π 0 ( Black et al., 2025 ) and π 0.5 ( Physical Intelligence et al., 2025b ) generate continuous action chunks through flow matching. Autoregressive action tokens reuse the language-model interface and scale naturally with pretrained backbones, whereas continuous generative heads model low-level commands directly in continuous space and can capture multiple distinct, valid action sequences. Beyond action generation, UniVLA ( Bu et al., 2025 ) and Qwen-VLA ( Wang et al., 2026a ) extend transfer across tasks, environments, and embodiments. StarVLA ( StarVLA Community, 2026 ) emphasizes modularity, MiMo-Embodied ( Hao et al., 2025 ) targets cross-embodiment scaling, and Pragmatic VLA ( Wu et al., 2026c ) focuses on practical deployment. Recent foundation-scale systems push data and model size further: Xiaomi-Robotics-1 ( Xiaomi Robotics Team et al., 2026 ) pretrains on more than 100,000 hours of real-world trajectories, its U0 variant ( Li et al., 2026b ) couples action synthesis with a world foundation model, CronusVLA ( Li et al., 2026a ) aggregates multi-frame context, InternVLA-A1.5 ( Ma et al., 2026 ) unifies understanding, latent foresight, and action, and GR-3 ( Cheang et al., 2025 ) , GR00T N1 ( Bjorck et al., 2025 ) , and SmolVLA ( Shukor et al., 2025 ) span large generalist to affordable efficient policies. Benchmark suites such as VLABench ( Zhang et al., 2025a ) and LIBERO-Plus ( Fei et al., 2026 ) track this progress, probing long-horizon reasoning and robustness under controlled perturbations. Across these systems, generalization is pursued either by expanding heterogeneous pretraining mixtures or by learning task and embodiment abstractions that can be reused during adaptation. Together, these lines combine semantic grounding, cross-robot data scaling, and generative continuous control. Their observation spaces, however, remain centered on cameras, language, and robot state, leaving contact and slip only indirectly observable. 𝒩 0 -VTLA retains the language-conditioned, flow-matching π 0.5 backbone but adds a dedicated high-resolution tactile pathway.

VTLA 在设计空间中占据了一个相对轻量的位置。 与其训练针对触觉的编码器或解码完整感官轨迹，不如重新利用冻结后的 DINOv2 特征 (Oquab et al., 2024)，保留已测量的触觉信息，并利用视觉与语言技术来预测其在动作时间范围内的整体变化。由此产生的紧凑式潜在状态可直接由动作专家处理。这种潜在-目标模型与 I-JEPA (Assran et al., 2023)、V-JEPA (Bardes et al., 2024)、V-JEPA 2 (Assran et al., 2025)、LeJEPA (Balestriero and LeCun, 2025) 以及 LeWorldModel (Maes et al., 2026) 相关联。其独特之处在于目标设定、定位方式、训练方法以及共享语言约束下的规模控制，均体现在 VLA 中。

Work toward tactile-conditioned vision–language–action control combines transferable tactile representations and scalable data collection with policies that either react to measured touch or predict how contact will evolve. Touch and Go ( Yang et al., 2022 ) and Visuo-Tactile Transformers ( Chen et al., 2022 ) learn joint visual–tactile features. VITaL ( George et al., 2025 ) , Sparsh ( Higuera et al., 2024 ) , AnyTouch ( Feng et al., 2025 ) , and UniTouch ( Yang et al., 2024 ) emphasize transfer across tasks or sensors, and FTP-1 ( Yuan et al., 2026 ) scales cross-sensor transfer to a generalist tactile policy pretrained on thousands of hours of tactile data. FreeTacMan ( Wu et al., 2026a ) , exUMI ( Xu et al., 2025 ) , ViTaMIn ( Liu et al., 2025 ) , Touch in the Wild ( Zhu et al., 2025b ) , and UniVTAC ( Chen et al., 2026a ) use robot-free collection or simulation to reduce contact-rich data costs. Reactive Diffusion Policy ( Xue et al., 2025 ) uses touch for fast feedback, 3D-ViTac ( Huang et al., 2024 ) performs spatial fusion, TouchGuide ( Zhang et al., 2026b ) provides inference-time steering, and Multi-Modal Policy Consensus ( Chen et al., 2026b ) fuses controllers, and T-Rex ( Niu et al., 2026 ) pairs a variable-rate architecture with high-frequency touch for tactile-reactive dexterous control. TLA ( Hao et al., 2026 ) , Tactile-VLA ( Huang et al., 2025 ) , VLA-Touch ( Bi et al., 2026 ) , and OmniVTLA ( Cheng et al., 2026 ) further integrate observed touch into language-conditioned control, while Neural Feels ( Suresh et al., 2024 ) targets fine-grained state estimation. Seeing Touch from Motion ( Xu et al., 2026 ) reads fine-grained contact states from tactile motion correlation, fusing touch and vision in a modality-aware mixture-of-transformers policy. Force-aware variants condition on measured or estimated force: ForceVLA ( Yu et al., 2025 ) adds a force-aware mixture-of-experts, and TA-VLA ( Zhang et al., 2025b ) a torque-aware interface for contact-rich control. Together, they establish the value of current touch for closed-loop correction and grounding. Predictive methods complement this reactive signal by estimating contact before it unfolds: Imagine2Touch ( Ayad et al., 2024 ) predicts local tactile readings ahead of contact, and TTP ( Zhang et al., 2026a ) transfers a future-tactile expert to robot policies. HapticVLA ( Gubernatorov et al., 2026 ) likewise estimates rather than measures touch, though its target is the current signal: a tactile token inferred from vision and robot state replaces the sensor at deployment. Visuo-Tactile World Models ( Higuera et al., 2026 ) , OmniVTA ( Zheng et al., 2026 ) , Tactile-WAM ( Wu et al., 2026b ) , Dream-Tac ( Lou et al., 2026 ) , VT-WAM ( Tian et al., 2026 ) , TacForeSight ( Zang et al., 2026 ) , and ContactWorld ( Zhang et al., 2026c ) instead forecast richer dynamics, sensory futures, and actions. A parallel predictive line forecasts future observations rather than latents: video-generation policies such as UniPi ( Du et al., 2023 ) , GR-1 ( Wu et al., 2024 ) , and GR-2 ( Cheang et al., 2024 ) synthesize pixel futures to guide action, and Unified World Models ( Zhu et al., 2025a ) couple video and action diffusion, and World Guidance ( Su et al., 2026 ) models the world in a learned condition space rather than in pixels to guide action generation. Against this backdrop, 𝒩 0 -VTLA occupies a lighter point in the design space. Rather than training a tactile-specific encoder or decoding a full sensory trajectory, it repurposes frozen DINOv2 features ( Oquab et al., 2024 ) , retains measured touch, and uses vision and language to predict its net change over the action horizon. The resulting compact latent conditions the action expert directly. This latent-target formulation is related to I-JEPA ( Assran et al., 2023 ) , V-JEPA ( Bardes et al., 2024 ) , V-JEPA 2 ( Assran et al., 2025 ) , LeJEPA ( Balestriero and LeCun, 2025 ) , and LeWorldModel ( Maes et al., 2026 ) . Its distinction lies in the target, placement, training recipe, and scale within a shared language-conditioned VLA.

离线强化学习与基于部署数据的 VLA 策略优化。

部署过程会暴露出静态演示中不存在的分布变化与长尾故障，而人类干预则能提供修正性数据。RECAP (Physical Intelligence et al., 2025a) 通过基于优势条件的策略提取，从演示、自主经验及干预数据中学习。SOP (Pan et al., 2026) 则能够跨机器人集群进行数据收集与学习。LWD (Wang et al., 2026b) 采用基于价值的更新机制来处理基于流的虚拟场景。VLA -RL (Lu et al., 2025) 采用基于片段过程奖励的轨迹级强化学习。一条互补机制可将稀疏结果转化为密集的过程监督信息。视觉-语言模型可充当上下文内价值估计器 (Ma et al., 2025)，而 Robo-Dopamine (Tan et al., 2026) 则学习与步骤相关的多视角奖励。SARM (Chen et al., 2026c) 对阶段及阶段内进展进行建模，以用于过滤与重新加权时长不固定的演示。ARM (Mao et al., 2026) 则对相对进展、退化或停滞进行标签化。 学习到的进展或价值随后在 GR-RL (Li et al., 2025) 中用于筛选演示数据，在 ProgVLA (Kim et al., 2026) 中用于调整权重 flow matching，在 ProgressVLA (Yan et al., 2026) 中用于指导行为，并在 Robo-ValueRL (Xia et al., 2026) 中用于支持离线到在线适应。与我们的设置最为接近的 χ0\chi_{0} 和 (Yu et al., 2026) 能够根据配对观测数据预测与阶段相关的相对进展，而 RECAP (Physical Intelligence et al., 2025a) 则通过二值化优势来约束 VLA 的训练过程。χ0\chi_{0} 通过均匀分配阶段并使用经过的时间来确定其进展目标。ALTER 则通过演示时长来调整阶段，并在触觉检测到的物体掉落事件以及记录的 HIL 修正信息周围添加局部比较监督，以辅助基于优势的离线策略学习。

Deployment exposes distribution shifts and long-tail failures absent from static demonstrations, while human interventions provide corrective data. RECAP ( Physical Intelligence et al., 2025a ) learns from demonstrations, autonomous experience, and interventions through advantage-conditioned policy extraction. SOP ( Pan et al., 2026 ) scales collection and learning across robot fleets. LWD ( Wang et al., 2026b ) uses value-guided updates for flow-based VLAs. VLA-RL ( Lu et al., 2025 ) performs trajectory-level RL with segment-derived process rewards. A complementary line converts sparse outcomes into dense process supervision. Vision–language models can serve as in-context value estimators ( Ma et al., 2025 ) , while Robo-Dopamine ( Tan et al., 2026 ) learns step-aware, multi-view rewards. SARM ( Chen et al., 2026c ) models stages and within-stage progress for filtering and reweighting variable-duration demonstrations. ARM ( Mao et al., 2026 ) labels relative progression, regression, or stagnation. Learned progress or value then filters demonstrations in GR-RL ( Li et al., 2025 ) , weights flow matching in ProgVLA ( Kim et al., 2026 ) , guides actions in ProgressVLA ( Yan et al., 2026 ) , and supports offline-to-online adaptation in Robo-ValueRL ( Xia et al., 2026 ) . Closest to our setting, χ 0 ( Yu et al., 2026 ) predicts stage-aware relative progress from paired observations, while RECAP ( Physical Intelligence et al., 2025a ) conditions VLA training on binarized advantages. χ 0 derives its progress targets by spacing stages uniformly and using elapsed time. ALTER instead weights stages by demonstrated duration and adds local comparison supervision around tactile-detected object-drop events and logged HIL corrections for advantage-conditioned offline policy learning.

8 结论

我们提出了 𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} - VTLA 这一 vision–tactile–language–action 基础模型，该模型能够预测结果，而非直接应对结果。在感知方面，潜隐触觉 tokens 能够预测接触现象。一个可训练的投影机制构成的冻结视觉骨干，将我们自研的视觉-触觉传感器所生成的夹持器接触差异图像转化为 tokens；而一个轻量化的预测器则将这些 tokens 与视觉-语言上下文结合，生成潜隐 zz，从而直接调节流匹配动作专家的行为，无需涉及视觉-语言处理步骤，使得触摸被视作预测目标，而非额外的观测上下文。为了优化策略，ALTER 将部署语料库中的学习过程转化为基于优势条件的离线强化学习。 其成对进展模型能够估算全局任务阶段与局部执行变化，这些结果被转化为离线策略学习所需的阶段相对优势条件。通过三阶段流程，该新初始化的路径能够在不破坏预训练的 π0.5\pi_{0.5} 主干网络的情况下实现在线运行。Stage 1 阶段通过未来触觉目标来强化预测器；Stage 2 阶段冻结触觉感知模块，并屏蔽视觉-语言路径，以使生成的潜在表示与动作专家对齐；Stage 3 阶段则对完整策略进行联合训练。该流程基于规范化的跨本体动作空间以及 NeoData 大规模精心策划的多平台语料库。表征层面证据已为设计提供了支持。在 Stage 1 之后，潜在表示 tokens 能够以 92.3%92.3\% 最高- 1 精度检索对应的未来触觉目标，其精度远高于 3.2%3.2\% 概率水平。 闭环结果也指向了同一方向。𝒩0\mathcal{N}_{0} - VTLA在模拟测试集上取得了最高的平均得分，比最弱的基线水平高出超过十九分；在九项NeoReal真实机器人任务中则保持不败。

We presented 𝒩 0 -VTLA , a vision–tactile–language–action foundation model that predicts outcomes instead of reacting to them. For perception, latent tactile tokens predict contact. A frozen visual backbone with a trainable projection turns the gripper’s contact-difference images from our self-developed visuo-tactile sensor into tokens, and a lightweight predictor compresses these tokens, together with the vision–language context, into a latent z that conditions a flow-matching action expert directly, never entering the vision–language prefix, so that touch is treated as a prediction target rather than as additional observation context. For policy improvement, ALTER formulates learning from deployment corpus as advantage-conditioned offline RL. Its pairwise progress model estimates global task phase and local execution change, which are converted into stage-relative advantage conditions for offline policy learning. A three-stage recipe brings this newly initialized pathway online without destabilizing the pretrained π 0.5 backbone. Stage 1 grounds the predictor against a future-tactile target, Stage 2 freezes the tactile perception stack and masks the vision–language pathway to align the resulting latents with the action expert, and Stage 3 trains the full policy jointly. The recipe rests on a canonical cross-embodiment action space and NeoData, a large-scale curated multi-platform corpus. Representation-level evidence already supports the design. After Stage 1, the latent tokens retrieve their matching future-tactile target at 92.3 % top-1 accuracy, far above the 3.2 % chance level. The closed-loop results point the same way. 𝒩 0 -VTLA attains the highest average on the simulation suite by a margin of more than nineteen points over the strongest baseline, and goes unbeaten across the nine NeoReal real-robot tasks.

未来研究方向。

根据本报告可直接得出的两个研究方向：首先，此处定义的自由潜在模型与监督变体，属于触觉表征学习更广泛设计空间中的两个方向；我们认为，将预测潜在模型框架应用于通过块时接触变化的总量紧凑估计来约束动作生成，而非依赖原始感官数据 tokens，是超越本报告所采用的特定预测器架构值得探索的方向。其次，ALTER 模型可扩展至更广泛的任务类型，以研究其在不同操控场景下的有效性。

Two directions follow directly from this report. First, the free-latent and supervised variants defined here are two points in a broader design space for tactile representation learning, and we see the predictive-latent framing, conditioning action generation on a compact estimate of the net contact change over the chunk horizon rather than on raw sensory tokens, as a direction worth pursuing beyond the specific predictor architecture used in this report. Second, ALTER can be extended to a broader range of tasks to study its effectiveness across different manipulation settings.

完整的动作块执行。

在服务时刻，动作专家会发出一个涵盖第 H=50H=50 步全训练时域的 action chunk 信号，该信号由第 2.1 节中设定的一个前缀编码及一次去噪处理构成，每次策略请求对应十次欧拉步。部署协议要求控制器在请求下一个步骤之前，必须执行返回数据块中的每一步。这并非默认便捷设置。如果只执行数据块中被截断的部分——即少于完整的 5050 步数——再进行重新预测，则机械臂可能处于目标位置但夹持器未闭合的状态，因为夹持器闭合指令集中在数据块的末尾，而早期的重规划会忽略这些指令。这种行为属于执行调度机制的特性，而非底层模型的特征；在完整数据块执行下，夹持器能够正确闭合。

At serve time the action expert emits an action chunk spanning the full training horizon of H = 50 steps set in Section 2.1 , from one prefix encoding and one denoising solve of ten Euler steps per policy request. The deployment contract requires the controller to execute every step of a returned chunk before requesting the next one. This is not a convenience default. Executing only a truncated prefix of each chunk, fewer than the full 50 steps, before re-predicting can leave the arm at the target position with the gripper never closed, because the gripper-closing commands are concentrated in the tail of the chunk that early re-planning discards. This behavior is a property of the execution schedule, not of the underlying model, which closes the gripper correctly under full-chunk execution.

触觉基准值重置。

第部分的触觉路径 2.2 以每集接触差异基线为基准，而非以原始触觉帧为基准。在服务时刻，在集开始时发出的重置操作会清除之前存储的基线值。重置后接收到的第一个观测值被作为所有活跃触觉视图的新零接触参考值，因此该时刻的触觉差异恰好为零，与第节中收集时刻所建立的惯例一致。 3 在该序列中，每次后续的观察值都会与同一存储的基准值进行差分计算，直至下一次重置。如果某个视角的触觉数据流不可用，控制器将退而使用该视角作为自身的基准值，即视为无接触状态，而非拒绝该请求。

The tactile pathway of Section 2.2 conditions on a per-episode contact-difference baseline rather than on raw tactile frames. At serve time, a reset issued at the start of an episode clears any previously stored baseline. The first observation received after a reset is captured as the new zero-contact reference for every active tactile view, so the tactile difference at that instant is exactly zero, matching the convention established at collection time in Section 3 . Every subsequent observation in the episode is differenced against that same stored baseline until the next reset. If a given view’s tactile stream is unavailable, the controller falls back to treating that view as its own baseline, that is, no contact, rather than failing the request.

服务界面。

经过训练的策略通过基于消息的请求/响应接口进行服务。重置消息会清除存储的触觉基准值，以便开始新的过程。预测请求包含当前的观测数据，包括相机图像、活跃的触觉视图、机器人状态以及可选的语言指令，该接口则返回预测的 action chunk 以及基本的时间信息。我们在这种通用层面描述该接口，省略了传输层面和部署脚本的细节。

The trained policy is served behind a message-based request/response interface. A reset message clears the stored tactile baseline for a new episode. A predict request carries the current observation, comprising camera images, active tactile views, robot state, and an optional language instruction, and the interface returns the predicted action chunk together with basic timing information. We describe the interface at this level of generality and omit transport-level and deployment-script details.
