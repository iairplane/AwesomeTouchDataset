摘要

具备力感知的操作要求机器人结合任务情境与触觉反馈，判断合适的交互作用力。我们提出 Tacinf，这是一套低成本的模块化基础设施，使策略能够根据任务语言、视觉与触觉，同时预测机器人动作和指尖力。任务指令也可以指定期望的施力程度。Tacinf 集成仿真数据合成和触觉评测基准（Tabero）、视觉—触觉—语言—动作策略学习（vision-tactile-language-action，VTLA）、使用与部署端相同夹爪的手持采集（Tacumi），以及按任务对齐的仿真到真机评测（sim-to-real，Taco）。这些组件把多源数据采集、基础模型训练与硬件部署连成一套流程。我们验证了仿真数据、基准、控制器及 Tacumi 等各个组件，并将开源整套系统。 仅用触觉仿真数据微调的基础模型可以零样本（zero-shot）迁移至不同的真机任务。在八种策略变体中，仿真与真机的平均任务成功率高度相关（Pearson r＝0.998，平均最大排序违例 MMRV＝0.010）。对报告测力结果的四种策略而言，在仿真和真机的全部三项任务中，要求用力时的平均夹持力都高于要求轻柔操作时。当物体位置与具体实例均随机化时，结合遥操作、仿真与 Tacumi 数据，可将真机任务平均成功率从仅用遥操作数据的 20% 提升到 60%。

Force-aware manipulation requires robots to infer appropriate interaction forces from task context and tactile feedback. We present Tacinf, a low-cost, modular infrastructure that enables policies to predict robot actions and fingertip forces from task language, vision, and touch. Task instructions can optionally specify the desired force level. Tacinf integrates simulation data synthesis and tactile benchmarking (Tabero), vision-tactile-language-action (VTLA) policy learning, handheld data collection using the same gripper as robot deployment (Tacumi), and task-aligned sim-to-real evaluation (Taco). Together, these components form an integrated workflow connecting data collection from multiple sources, foundation-model training, and hardware deployment. We validate each component, including the simulation data, benchmark, controller, and Tacumi, and are releasing the full stack as open source. A foundation model fine-tuned solely on tactile simulation data transfers zero-shot to different real-robot tasks. Across eight policy variants, mean task success rates are strongly correlated between simulation and the real robot (Pearson r=0.998, mean maximum rank violation MMRV=0.010). For the four policies with reported force measurements, mean grip force is higher under firm instructions than under gentle instructions on all three tasks in both domains. When both object positions and instances are randomized, a mixture of teleoperation, simulation, and Tacumi data raises mean real-robot task success from 20% with teleoperation alone to 60%.

1 引言

机器人操作基础模型已迅速发展。针对力感知任务，这些模型需从指令、场景及触觉反馈中推断并调节物理交互，同时从图像和任务描述中选择动作。力需求可能是明确的，例如 act 中的“轻柔”或“坚定”指令；也可能是隐性的，如打开容器或擦拭表面等任务要求。无论哪种情况，策略都必须从这些指令、场景和触觉反馈中推断出适当的接触行为。触觉数据对训练此类策略至关重要，但硬件与传感器设计的差异使得有效数据集的收集成本极高。在执行过程中，系统必须利用实时反馈来调整其行为并施加适当的力。表现不佳的策略可能会损坏物体或机器人本身，因此亟需低成本评估平台以支持策略测试与优化。

Foundation models for robot manipulation have advanced rapidly. Force-aware tasks require these models to infer and regulate physical interaction as well as select motions from images and task language. Force requirements may be explicit, as in instructions to act “gently” or “firmly”, or implicit in tasks such as opening a container or wiping a surface. In both cases, the policy must infer appropriate contact behavior from the instruction, scene, and tactile feedback. Tactile data are essential for training such policies, but differences in hardware and sensor design make effective datasets costly to collect. During execution, the system must use real-time feedback to adjust its behavior and apply appropriate forces. Poorly performing policies can damage objects or the robot itself, creating a need for low-cost evaluation platforms that support policy testing and refinement.

我们引入了 Tacinf（图 1），这是一个全栈开源基础设施，用于触觉与力感知操作。Tacinf-Tabero 通过触觉观测与力监督生成模拟数据，为评估 vision-tactile-language-action（VTLA）策略提供基准测试。Tacinf-VTLA 提供模块化触觉接口，可用于实现不同策略。Tacinf-Tacumi 提供用于机器人部署的硬件，以及通过遥操作和手持演示收集触觉观测数据与力目标的能力。Tacinf-Taco 支持基于任务导向的 sim-to-real 评估与多源数据训练。这些组件共同支持数据收集、基于模拟的评估、sim-to-real 迁移以及低成本硬件、计算资源与数据成本的策略部署。Tacumi 套件成本低于 2,500，而最低计算需求仅需一台 RTX 4090 设备。

We introduce Tacinf (Fig. 1 ), a full-stack open-source infrastructure for tactile and force-aware manipulation. Tacinf-Tabero generates simulated data with tactile observations and force supervision and provides a benchmark for evaluating vision-tactile-language-action (VTLA) policies. Tacinf-VTLA offers modular tactile interfaces for implementing different policies. Tacinf-Tacumi provides hardware for robot deployment and for collecting tactile observations and force targets through teleoperation and handheld demonstrations. Tacinf-Taco supports task-aligned sim-to-real evaluation and training on data from multiple sources. Together, these components support data collection, simulation-based evaluation, sim-to-real transfer, and policy deployment at low hardware, compute, and data costs. The Tacumi kit costs under $2,500, and the minimum compute requirement is a single RTX 4090.

我们的实验验证了系统各组件的有效性，并得出了三个主要结论。首先，仅基于仿真数据进行优化的触觉基础模型可以部署使用。 zero-shot 在真实的机器人上，至少达到成功率 98% 在类似 LIBERO 的任务中，根据我们的评估指标来看，预测力与遥操作参考值非常接近。其次，在八项 Tacinf 测试中均表现良好。 VTLA 策略变体下，仿真通过成功率与皮尔逊相关系数，能够精准保留真实机器人的排名特性 r=0.998r=0.998 平均最大秩违反（MMRV） =0.010=0.010 . 所报道的四种策略中，测量的握力差异反映了指令的力度差异，且不同对象与场景下的绝对力值各不相同。第三，遥操作、Tacumi以及仿真数据均做出了互补性的贡献。 我们展示了每个源数据的实用性，包括在没有机器人参与的情况下收集的数据示例，并在低数据收集成本的前提下，将针对随机位置的非分布目标的成功率从 20% 提升至 60%。

Our experiments validate the system components and yield three main findings. First, a tactile foundation model fine-tuned solely on simulation data can be deployed zero-shot on the real robot, achieving success rates of at least 98% on LIBERO-like tasks, with predicted forces close to the teleoperation references under our evaluation metrics. Second, across eight Tacinf-VTLA policy variants, simulation closely preserves the real-robot ranking by success rate, Pearson r = 0.998 , mean maximum rank violation (MMRV) = 0.010 . Measured grip force distinguishes firm from gentle instructions for the four policies reported, with absolute forces varying across objects and domains. Third, teleoperation, Tacumi, and simulation data make complementary contributions. We demonstrate the utility of each source, including demonstrations collected without a robot, and increase success from 20% to 60% on out-of-distribution objects at randomized positions with a low data-collection cost.

2 相关研究

2.1 仿真平台与合成数据

机器人仿真为策略学习与评估提供了可重复的环境。早期的基础设施如 robosuite、LIBERO 和 CALVIN 建立了可复用的操作任务及基于语言的评估协议 Zhu et al. (2020); Liu et al. (2023); Mees et al. (2022)。近期的新平台通过 RoboCasa 中的家庭场景多样性、ManiSkill3 中的 GPU 并行仿真 Isaac Lab，以及 RoboVerse 中的统一资产与接口，进一步扩展了这些能力 Nasiriany et al. (2024); Tao et al. (2025); NVIDIA et al. (2025b); Geng et al. (2025)。MuBlE 将 MuJoCo 动态与 Blender 渲染相结合，适用于需要视觉与物理推理的任务 Nazarczuk et al. (2025)。在数据生成层面，RoboTwin 和 RoboTwin 2.0 结合任务构建与演示生成技术以实现双手操作，而 SkillMimicGen 则通过技能级别生成的方式复用演示数据 Mu et al. (2025); Chen et al. (2025); Garrett et al. (2025)。GraspVLA 进一步展示了大规模合成动作数据在策略预训练中的应用 Deng et al. (2025)。 这些方法促使我们重新利用现有的轨迹，但仅通过轨迹重放并不能确保接触力或触觉观测信息能够在不同模拟器之间传递。

Robot simulation provides reproducible environments for policy learning and evaluation. Early infrastructures such as robosuite, LIBERO, and CALVIN established reusable manipulation tasks and language-conditioned evaluation protocols Zhu et al. (2020) ; Liu et al. (2023) ; Mees et al. (2022) . Recent platforms extend these capabilities through household scene diversity in RoboCasa, GPU-parallel simulation in ManiSkill3 and Isaac Lab, and unified assets and interfaces in RoboVerse Nasiriany et al. (2024) ; Tao et al. (2025) ; NVIDIA et al. (2025b) ; Geng et al. (2025) . MuBlE couples MuJoCo dynamics with Blender rendering for tasks requiring both visual and physical reasoning Nazarczuk et al. (2025) . At the data-generation level, RoboTwin and RoboTwin 2.0 combine task construction and demonstration generation for bimanual manipulation, while SkillMimicGen reuses demonstrations through skill-level generation Mu et al. (2025) ; Chen et al. (2025) ; Garrett et al. (2025) . GraspVLA further demonstrates the use of large-scale synthetic action data for policy pretraining Deng et al. (2025) . These approaches motivate our reuse of existing trajectories, but trajectory replay alone does not establish that contact forces or tactile observations transfer across simulators.

一种互补方向用于重建真实环境，以进行基于模拟的策略评估。SIMPLER 研究视觉与控制方面的差异，并评估模拟性能是否能够预测真实机器人的行为；PolaRiS 则将神经场景重建与模拟数据联合训练相结合 Li et al. (2025b); Jain et al. (2026)。SimFoundry 能够自动化场景重建，并生成与任务相关的场景变体，而 SceneRig 则能从单张图像中重建适用于模拟的 3 D 场景 Ranawaka et al. (2026); Luo et al. (2026)。Seed3D 2.0 则生成用于模拟的 3 D 资产 Gu et al. (2026)。Ctrl-World 则采用基于行为的生成世界模型来进行策略评估与优化 Guo et al. (2026)。这些研究为真实场景到模拟场景的重建、数据融合以及跨领域策略排序提供了先例。Tacinf-Taco 则将这一方向应用于触觉观测与力条件评估。

A complementary direction reconstructs real environments for simulation-based policy evaluation. SIMPLER studies visual and control gaps and evaluates whether simulated performance predicts real-robot behavior; PolaRiS combines neural scene reconstruction with simulation-data co-training Li et al. (2025b) ; Jain et al. (2026) . SimFoundry automates scene reconstruction and generates task-relevant scene variations, while SceneRig reconstructs simulation-ready 3D scenes from single images Ranawaka et al. (2026) ; Luo et al. (2026) . Seed3D 2.0 generates 3D assets for simulation Gu et al. (2026) . Ctrl-World instead uses an action-conditioned generative world model for policy evaluation and improvement Guo et al. (2026) . These works establish precedents for real-to-sim reconstruction, data mixing, and cross-domain policy ranking. Tacinf-Taco adapts this direction to tactile observations and force-conditioned evaluation.

触觉模拟数据集与基准测试也正在逐步发展。VTDexManip 支持视觉-触觉预训练与灵巧操作，SafeDiff 则利用 SafeDoorManip50k 评估基于力量的交互。 UniVTAC 整合多传感器触觉合成、表征学习与操作基准测试 Liu et al. (2025b); Wei et al. (2025); Chen et al. (2026) . SoftVTBench 将视觉触觉观测数据与独立变形真实值进行配对，以评估可变形物体交互质量 Jing et al. (2026) . OpenViTac 在成对模拟与真实环境场景中对视觉-触觉策略的基准测试及相关报告 sim-to-real 不同策略家族间的协同一致 Wu et al. (2026) Tacinf-Tabero 专注于将现有的操作轨迹转化为同步的视觉-触觉-语言监督机制，并评估与任务完成相关的指令依赖交互力。

Tactile simulation datasets and benchmarks are also emerging. VTDexManip supports visuotactile pretraining and dexterous manipulation, SafeDiff evaluates force-aware interaction using SafeDoorManip50k, and UniVTAC unifies multi-sensor tactile synthesis, representation learning, and manipulation benchmarking Liu et al. (2025b) ; Wei et al. (2025) ; Chen et al. (2026) . SoftVTBench pairs visuotactile observations with independent deformation ground truth for evaluating deformable-object interaction quality Jing et al. (2026) . OpenViTac benchmarks visuo-tactile policies in paired simulation and real-world settings and reports sim-to-real agreement across policy families Wu et al. (2026) . Tacinf-Tabero focuses on converting existing manipulation trajectories into synchronized vision-tactile-language supervision and on evaluating instruction-dependent interaction forces alongside task completion.

2.2 触觉仿真方法

基于视觉的触觉模拟必须考虑接触几何结构、传感器变形以及图像生成过程。TACTO将物理引擎产生的接触信息与高效的光学渲染技术Wang et al. (2022)相结合。Taxim通过基于示例的光学映射技术，并利用弹性变形与叠加原理来建模标记运动；FOTS则提供快速的光学及标记运动近似方法，用于触觉-运动学习Si and Yuan (2022); Zhao et al. (2024)。这些方法能够构建高效的传感器观测模型，不过其校准假设与近似方法需与完整的弹性体动力学模型区分开来。TacEx将软体接触模拟与Taxim及FOTS技术整合在一起，形成Isaac Sim。TacSL则提供支持策略学习的GPU并行视觉触觉库，其光学渲染与标记场提取均使用相同的模拟变形机制，从而确保两种观测数据的一致性Nguyen et al. (2024); Akinola et al. (2025)。

Vision-based tactile simulation must account for contact geometry, sensor deformation, and image formation. TACTO couples physics-engine contact information with efficient optical rendering Wang et al. (2022) . Taxim calibrates an example-based optical mapping and models marker motion using elastic deformation and superposition; FOTS provides fast optical and marker-motion approximations for tactile-motor learning Si and Yuan (2022) ; Zhao et al. (2024) . These methods offer efficient sensor-observation models, although their calibration assumptions and approximations must be distinguished from a complete elastomer dynamics model. TacEx integrates soft-body contact simulation with Taxim and FOTS in Isaac Sim. TacSL provides a GPU-parallel visuotactile library with support for policy learning. Its optical rendering and marker-field extraction use the same simulated deformation, keeping the two observation streams consistent Nguyen et al. (2024) ; Akinola et al. (2025) .

基于物理的方法更侧重明确的接触力学机制。DiffTactile通过可微传感器与物体模拟，实现系统识别与轨迹优化；而Taccel则将接触与刚体动力学整合到基于GPU并行技术的触觉模拟平台Si et al. (2024); Li et al. (2025c)中。HydroShear则对路径依赖的剪切累积及粘滑转变进行建模 Dang et al. (2026)。学习观测传递技术提供了一种互补方案：SimShear从模拟观测与剪切条件中生成考虑剪切效应的触觉图像；Cross-Sensor Touch Generation则能在不同传感器设计之间转换触觉观测数据 Freud et al. (2025); Rodriguez et al. (2025)。诸如AnyRotate、UniTac2Pose和VT-Refine等应用表明，模拟触觉技术可支持手持操作、姿态估计及策略优化 Yang et al. (2025); Wu et al. (2025b); Huang et al. (2025b)。 这些研究共同揭示了接触精度、校准成本与仿真效率之间的权衡关系，并建立了触觉机制 sim-to-real 将转移作为重要的评估目标 Yang et al. (2025); Freud et al. (2025) . 遵循 TacEx 所展示的模块化集成模式，Tacinf 使用 Taxim 进行光学观测，使用 FOTS 处理标记运动。我们的贡献在于将 Taxim 与 FOTS 进行校准整合，形成可扩展的数据与评估流程。我们对其进行了评估 sim-to-real 跨策略变体之间语言相关力水平的传递。与从单一模拟变形产生两种流数据的模拟器（如 TacSL）相比，这种模块化方法以牺牲每样本变形一致性为代价，换取了在大规模范围内重放开源轨迹所需的处理效率。 因此，我们采用标记位移作为相对剪切和方向的提示，并直接从物理引擎接触反应中推导出监督与控制的扭矩。

Physics-based approaches pursue more explicit contact mechanics. DiffTactile combines differentiable sensor and object simulation for system identification and trajectory optimization, while Taccel integrates contact and rigid-body dynamics in a GPU-parallel tactile simulation platform Si et al. (2024) ; Li et al. (2025c) . HydroShear additionally models path-dependent shear accumulation and stick-slip transitions Dang et al. (2026) . Learned observation transfer offers a complementary route: SimShear generates shear-aware tactile images from simulated observations and shear conditioning, and Cross-Sensor Touch Generation translates tactile observations between sensor designs Freud et al. (2025) ; Rodriguez et al. (2025) . Applications such as AnyRotate, UniTac2Pose, and VT-Refine demonstrate how simulated touch can support in-hand manipulation, pose estimation, and policy refinement Yang et al. (2025) ; Wu et al. (2025b) ; Huang et al. (2025b) . Together, these studies illustrate a trade-off between contact fidelity, calibration effort, and simulation throughput, and they establish tactile sim-to-real transfer as an important evaluation target Yang et al. (2025) ; Freud et al. (2025) . Following the modular integration exemplified by TacEx, Tacinf uses Taxim for optical observations and FOTS for marker motion. Our contribution is the calibrated integration of Taxim and FOTS into a scalable data and evaluation pipeline. We evaluate the sim-to-real transfer of language-conditioned force levels across policy variants. 3.1 Tacinf-Tabero

Tacinf-Tabero 是 Tacinf 的仿真基准组件。它提供可重复的语言相关触觉操作任务、可扩展的多模态数据生成机制，以及用于评估物理交互质量的基于处理过程的指标。

Tacinf-Tabero is the simulation benchmark component of Tacinf. It offers reproducible language-conditioned tactile manipulation tasks, scalable multimodal data generation, and process-aware metrics for evaluating physical interaction quality.

3.1.1 数据复用

开源操作数据集是宝贵的社区资源。我们开发了一种可复用的工作流程，用于将 MuJoCo 及其他平台上的数据集传输到基于 Isaac Sim 的 Tacinf-Tabero 基准测试上。该工作流程通过高质量的渲染提升了视觉逼真度，并通过触觉观测数据丰富了现有演示内容，从而减少了从零开始重建每个数据集的需求。

Open-source manipulation datasets are valuable community resources. We develop a reusable workflow for transferring datasets from MuJoCo and other platforms to the Isaac Sim-based Tacinf-Tabero benchmark. This workflow improves visual fidelity through high-quality rendering and augments existing demonstrations with tactile observations, reducing the need to reconstruct each dataset from scratch.

我们首先在 Isaac Lab 和 NVIDIA et al. (2025b) 环境中重建任务环境，以匹配原始领域，包括资产、初始物体与机器人姿态以及成功逻辑。目前，该流程可通过 SimFoundry Ranawaka et al. (2026) 或大型语言模型实现部分自动化。随后，我们在重建的场景中重放原始轨迹，从而获得符合人类行为轨迹的多模态数据。将这些轨迹应用于触觉操作设置时面临两个挑战：首先，末端执行器不同——我们的机器人使用集成视觉触觉传感器的夹持器，其几何结构与原始版本存在差异；其次，底层物理行为也有所不同：某些原始数据集依赖操作空间控制 Khatib (1987) 机制，而该机制对物理参数极为敏感。由真实机器人遥操作或 UMI 设备收集的数据集也带来了仿真与真实之间的差距。 因此，我们进行两项调整：( 1 ) 通过调整机械臂基座姿态来使末端执行器工具中心点（TCP）处于正确位置；以及 ( 2 ) 在轨迹重放过程中使用高增益比例微分（PD）关节控制器，以最小化累积跟踪误差。

We first reconstruct task environments in Isaac Lab NVIDIA et al. (2025b) to match the source domains, including assets, initial object and robot poses, and success logic. This workflow can currently be partially automated with SimFoundry Ranawaka et al. (2026) or large language models. We then replay the source trajectories in the reconstructed scenes to obtain multimodal data that follow the demonstrated human trajectories. Transferring these trajectories to tactile manipulation settings presents two challenges. First, the end-effector differs: our robot uses a gripper integrated with visuo-tactile sensors whose geometry differs from the original. Second, the underlying physical behavior differs: some source datasets rely on Operational Space Control (OSC) Khatib (1987) , which is highly sensitive to physical parameters. Datasets collected by real-robot teleoperation or UMI devices also introduce simulation-to-real gaps. We therefore make two adjustments: (1) align the end-effector tool center point (TCP) by adjusting the robot-arm base pose; and (2) use a high-gain proportional-derivative (PD) joint controller during trajectory replay to minimize cumulative tracking error.

我们使用触觉夹持器对轨迹重放的成功率进行了评估，并得到了相当高的成功案例比例。我们将这一比例称为数据保留率。

We evaluate the success rate of trajectory replay with the tactile gripper and obtain an acceptable proportion of successful episodes. We refer to this proportion as the data retention rate.

3.1.3 语言条件力值数据生成

我们专注于那些仅靠任务完成无法充分描述所需交互方式的任务，包括明确的力指令以及接触敏感的物体处理。我们将这些任务分为两类：( 1 ) 语言驱动的力级任务；以及( 2 ) 对可变形物体或具有关节部分的物体的操控。Tabero主要用于验证第一类任务，而我们在实际场景中直接验证第二类任务的相应行为。

We focus on tasks in which the desired interaction cannot be specified adequately by task completion alone, including explicit force instructions and contact-sensitive object handling. We distinguish two categories: (1) language-conditioned force-level tasks; and (2) manipulation of deformable objects or objects with articulated parts. Tabero is primarily used to validate the first category, while we validate the corresponding behavior for the second category directly in real-world scenarios.

3.2.1 指尖力分解与双接触扭矩映射

(2026) . Our force decomposition also draws on the force-constrained formulation used in gentle-manipulation policy learning Zhou et al. (2026) . In our implementation, a two-finger parallel gripper has a single opening and closing degree of freedom, so the planar contact at its fingertips decomposes naturally into two parts: the components squeezing each other along the closing axis form the grip force , while the remaining components constitute the applied interaction on the object, represented as an estimated six-dimensional wrench at the TCP under a two-contact approximation (3D force plus a 3D moment vector whose grasp-axis component is neglected). During grasping, the normal force that compresses the sensor pads against the object surface dominates; when the gripper transports, wipes, or twists the object, shear forces and moments also influence the object motion.

我们开发了 Tacinf-Tacumi 手持式多模态界面，用于收集具有接触行为的操作演示。遵循 UMI 与 Chi et al. (2024) 所提出的无机器人教学范式，该系统结合触觉感知与实时主动力反馈技术，使操作者在直接操作物体时能够感知并调节抓取负载。它同步捕捉末端执行器运动、夹持器开口及接触状态信息，为需要精细力调节的任务提供多模态演示。

We develop Tacinf-Tacumi, a handheld multimodal interface for collecting demonstrations of contact-rich manipulation. Following the robot-free teaching paradigm of UMI Chi et al. (2024) , the system combines tactile sensing with real-time active force feedback, so operators can perceive and regulate grasping load while directly manipulating objects. It synchronously captures end-effector motion, gripper aperture, and contact observations to provide multimodal demonstrations for tasks requiring fine force regulation.

3.3.1 精细力量调节

该手持式界面利用位置指令和扭矩反馈建立双向交互循环，遵循 Lawrence (1993) 这种双侧接口的位置-力架构。在指令通道中，触发旋转被映射为目标夹持器开孔尺寸，并转化为 DAMIAO 电机的运动指令。实际开孔尺寸则通过传输关系从电机位置反馈中获取。目标开孔尺寸与实际开孔尺寸分别代表了操作员的意图和夹持器的执行状态。

The handheld interface uses position commands and torque feedback to establish a bidirectional interaction loop, following the position-force architecture of bilateral interfaces Lawrence (1993) . In the command channel, trigger rotation is mapped to a target gripper aperture and converted into a motion command for the DAMIAO motor. The actual aperture is obtained from motor position feedback through the transmission relationship. The target and actual apertures represent operator intent and gripper execution state, respectively.

The Xense sensors provide contact-force estimates using the manufacturer’s calibration, supporting contact observations, real-robot force statistics, and the grip-force feedback controller in Section 3.2 . This design combines active feedback for the operator with contact sensing for policy learning and robot control while preserving the distinct physical meanings of the two signals.

3.3.2 数据收集

该系统分别获取夹持器的六自由度刚体姿态与单自由度开孔参数，以表示末端执行器的运动及抓取状态。跟踪设计需考虑轨迹可靠性、系统集成复杂度以及不同采集环境中的部署开销。

The system separately acquires the gripper’s six-degree-of-freedom rigid-body pose and single-degree-of-freedom aperture to represent end-effector motion and grasp state. The tracking design considers trajectory reliability, system integration complexity, and deployment overhead across collection environments.

现有的手持式采集系统采用不同的姿态获取策略。UMI 通过手腕与摄像头的观测数据以及惯性测量数据来估计运动轨迹，但其原始论文指出，该方法依赖于足够丰富的环境纹理，在纹理缺失的场景中，如墙壁为纯白色的空间中，存在局限性 Chi et al. (2024)。TacUMI 将 VIVE Tracker 集成到手持设备中，作为利用专用跟踪设备获取操作轨迹的示例 Cheng et al. (2026a)。对于基于 Lighthouse 的实现方式，如 VIVE Tracker 3.0，外部基站的安装与空间配置要求在不同采集点之间移动时进行额外的准备工作 HTC VIVE (n.d.)。 ViTaMIn-B 则采用 Meta Quest 控制器，其有效演示比例高于同时定位与映射（SLAM）方法所使用的比例 UMI 在重量分配任务上，为手持式收集设备中的消费者虚拟现实跟踪技术提供实证支持 Li et al. (2025a) .

Existing handheld collection systems employ different pose-acquisition strategies. UMI estimates trajectories from wrist-camera observations and inertial measurements, but its original paper acknowledges a dependence on sufficient environmental texture, with limitations in texture-deficient settings such as rooms with plain white walls Chi et al. (2024) . TacUMI integrates a VIVE Tracker into the handheld device, providing an example of acquiring manipulation trajectories with a dedicated tracking device Cheng et al. (2026a) . 3.3.3 低成本与执行器一致性

通过硬件设计、校准或适配措施，之前的接口能够解决采集与部署之间的差异 Yu et al. (2025a); Xu et al. (2025a); Zhang et al. (2025a); Wi et al. (2026)。Tacinf-Tacumi 使用相同的低成本夹持器电机来进行手持采集与机器人执行，从而复用该夹持器驱动设计及其控制与状态接口。在部署过程中，机器人通过相应的电动夹持器进行操作，而触发器仅在演示采集阶段作为人机交互界面。这种设计避免了为部署阶段选择不同的专用夹持器电机，从而减少了硬件选择、控制接口适配及系统集成方面的工作。

Prior interfaces address collection-to-deployment differences through hardware design, calibration, or adaptation Yu et al. (2025a) ; Xu et al. (2025a) ; Zhang et al. (2025a) ; Wi et al. (2026) . Tacinf-Tacumi uses the same low-cost gripper motor for handheld collection and robot execution, reusing the gripper actuation design and its command and state interfaces. During deployment, the robot operates the corresponding motorized gripper, while the trigger serves only as the human interface during demonstration collection. This design avoids selecting a different, dedicated gripper motor for deployment, reducing hardware selection, control-interface adaptation, and system integration effort.

复用执行设计有助于减少执行器更换所带来的控制与校准差异。其成本优势源于低成本电机的选择以及收集与执行过程中的设计复用。尽管采用共享执行设计，但在收集与部署过程中仍可能出现接触响应差异。传动几何结构、指尖几何结构、传感器安装及控制参数都需要匹配或校准。在此范围内，该设计减少了部署所需的额外硬件与工程要求，同时为演示数据与机器人执行接口之间的一致性提供了基础。

Reusing the actuation design helps reduce control and calibration differences introduced by actuator replacement. Its cost advantage derives from the low-cost motor selection and design reuse across collection and execution. With a shared actuation design, contact responses can still differ between collection and deployment. Transmission geometry, fingertip geometry, sensor mounting, and control parameters require matching or calibration. Within this scope, the design reduces additional hardware and engineering requirements for deployment while providing a basis for consistency between the demonstration data and the robot execution interface.

Each extension retains the common time step, normalization, force supervision, and evaluation interfaces.

(2025) 这样的触觉 sim-to-real 研究，Taco 采用两种实用标准。首先，视觉与物理分离原则：仅使用高斯或神经外观模型进行渲染，而绝不用于接触检测；明确的碰撞几何模型则负责处理接触、支撑和阴影接收。其次，与策略相关的一致性：参数经过调整以匹配策略所使用的数据结构、分布和视觉观测结果。精确匹配物理参数往往不切实际，因为这需要广泛的测量和校准工作，且部分参数受到商业限制。我们通过检查接触起始点、接触区域持续性、力大小、标记响应以及动作重放来评估与策略相关的一致性。

Following real-to-sim evaluation approaches that align policy-relevant observations and control Li et al. (2025b) ; Jain et al. (2026) , and tactile sim-to-real studies such as AnyRotate and SimShear Yang et al. (2025) ; Freud et al. (2025) , Taco uses two practical criteria. First, visual-physical separation : Gaussian or neural appearance models are used only for rendering and never for contact; explicit collision geometry handles contact, support, and shadow reception. Second, policy-relevant consistency : parameters are adjusted to match the data structures, distributions, and visual observations used by the policy. Exact matching of physical parameters is often impractical because it requires extensive measurement and calibration, and some parameters are commercially restricted. We assess policy-relevant consistency through checks of contact onset, contact-patch persistence, force scale, marker response, and action replay.

3.4.1 任务相关场景重建

该模块将真实工作空间与交互对象转换为 Isaac Sim 格式，从而让渲染与交互使用相同的相机外参及操作区域。工作空间通过 3 D 高斯分布进行可视化呈现，而对象则作为独立的纹理化网格进入物理层。两者均遵循任务坐标系，并遵守视觉与物理分离原则。

This module transfers the real workspace and interactive objects into Isaac Sim, so that rendering and interaction use the same camera extrinsics and operation region. The workspace is represented visually by 3D Gaussians, while objects enter the physical layer as independent textured meshes. Both are aligned to the task coordinate system and obey the visual-physical separation principle.

任务导向型工作空间重建。我们通过 3 D 高斯渲染贴图（3 DGS）和 Kerbl et al. (2023) 将工作空间外观进行表示，并将其转换为可加载至 Isaac Sim 系统的 NuRec 体积模型。机器人本体、白色碰撞台、摄像头及操作区域保持不变；高斯资产通过单一配准矩阵 𝐓reg\mathbf{T}_{\mathrm{reg}} 与任务框架对齐。我们使用力星科技提供的 Odin1 空间摄像头来采集重建数据，因为除了可直接使用的视频流外，其内置的激光雷达里程计还能提供更精确的相机外参参考。该空间摄像头持续记录覆盖可到达空间以及第三人称与腕式摄像头的视野范围内的轨迹，同时记录校准图像、姿态以及 COLMAP 内参/外参 Schonberger and Frahm (2016) 。 该重建结果以标准的 3 DGS 点集形式输出，采用 Z 轴向上、以米为单位的坐标系统。捕获范围集中在策略所使用的视角中：即第三人称相机视锥，以及腕式相机在手臂工作空间内扫描的视锥。无需对整个房间进行均匀覆盖。

Task-Aligned Workspace Reconstruction. We represent workspace appearance with 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) Kerbl et al. (2023) and convert it into an Isaac Sim-loadable NuRec Volume. The task-side robot, white collision table, cameras, and operation region remain unchanged; the Gaussian asset is aligned to the task frame using a single registration matrix 𝐓 reg . We use the Odin1 spatial camera from Liuxing Technology for reconstruction data capture, because in addition to a directly usable video stream, its built-in LiDAR odometry provides a more accurate reference for camera extrinsics. A spatial camera continuously records trajectories around the operation region that cover the reachable space and fields of view of the third-person and wrist cameras, while logging calibrated images, poses, and COLMAP intrinsics/extrinsics Schonberger and Frahm (2016) . The reconstruction is exported as a standard 3DGS point set in a Z-up, meter-based convention. Capture is concentrated in the views used by the policy: the third-person camera frustum and the frusta swept by the wrist camera throughout the arm workspace. Uniform coverage of the room is unnecessary.

3 DGS 后处理。在扫描过程中，机械臂存在于场景中，因此保留其高斯分布会导致在统一机器人描述格式（URDF）定义的模拟机器人中出现重复几何结构。在桌面平面约束下，我们采用随机样本共识（RANSAC）平面估计 Fischler and Bolles (1981)，进行带有桌面保护候选点选择，实施连通组件聚类与形态学过滤，并通过边界框邻域、最近邻生长及基础列约束进行多次清理操作，同时保护如缠绕在臂上的电缆等精细结构。为了确保渲染兼容性，我们移除远场异常值，将高阶球谐函数压缩为仅包含直流分量，并确保声学反射阶数与镜面张量维度一致，必要时对缺失维度进行零填充。 最后，通用场景描述（USD）格式中的场景采用外部变换来消除任务固定的外部框架，仅在与机器人基座相关的内部变换中保留 𝐓reg\mathbf{T}_{\mathrm{reg}}。

3DGS Post-Processing. During scanning, the robot arm is present in the scene, so retaining its Gaussians would create double geometry with the simulated robot defined in the Unified Robot Description Format (URDF). Under a tabletop-plane constraint, we perform random sample consensus (RANSAC) plane estimation Fischler and Bolles (1981) , candidate-point selection with tabletop protection, connected-component clustering and morphological filtering, and multiple cleanup passes based on bounding-box neighborhoods, nearest-neighbor growth, and base-column constraints, while protecting fine structures such as cables wrapped around the arm. For rendering compatibility, we remove far-field outliers, contract higher-order spherical harmonics to DC-only, and ensure that the declared SH order matches the specular tensor dimensions, zero-padding missing dimensions when necessary. Finally, the scene in Universal Scene Description (USD) format uses an outer transform to cancel the task’s fixed external frame and retains only 𝐓 reg in the inner transform relative to the robot base.

物体重建。交互式物体被单独重建并作为独立的 USD 资源导入；它们并非从工作空间的高斯分布中提取出来。对于每个真实物体，我们拍摄一张图像，并使用 Seed3D 2.0 Gu et al. (2026) 生成具有纹理的 3 D 模型，该模型以 USD 格式导出。显式网格遵循 Isaac 的光栅化路径；当高斯外观与物体网格重叠时，物体资源负责物理交互，而高斯层仅提供未建模的背景。导出的 USD 中不存在碰撞几何结构。因此，我们通过 USD 覆盖层添加刚体属性，并通过凸集分解生成碰撞网格，而无需修改生成的资源本身。视觉网格仅用于渲染，碰撞网格仅用于碰撞检测；物体物理特性由覆盖层提供。 由于 Seed3D 的缩放比例与初始姿态可能与真实物体不同，我们将该资产导入到 Isaac Sim 中，重新播放录制的真实演示视频，并沿着各个轴逐步调整物体的缩放比例与初始位置，直至抓取与放置轨迹一致。校准的目的是在模拟重放与真实机器人之间，不仅提升轨迹一致性与成功率，还超越单纯的视觉相似性。在校准过程中，任务演示与机器人轨迹保持不变。在评估之前，我们会通过任务初始化和相机观测来验证重建场景的准确性。

Object Reconstruction. Interactive objects are reconstructed separately and imported as independent USD assets; they are not extracted from the workspace Gaussians. For each real object, we capture one image and use Seed3D 2.0 Gu et al. (2026) to generate a textured 3D model exported as USD. The explicit mesh follows Isaac’s rasterization path; when Gaussian appearance and an object mesh overlap, the object asset handles physical interaction while the Gaussian layer supplies only unmodeled background. The exported USD has no collision geometry. We therefore add rigid-body properties through a USD overlay and generate collision meshes with convex decomposition without modifying the generated asset itself. The visual mesh is used only for rendering and the collision mesh only for contact; object physics is supplied by the overlay. Because Seed3D scale and initial pose can differ from the real object, we import the asset into Isaac Sim, replay the recorded real demonstrations, and iteratively adjust object scale and initial position along each axis until grasp and placement trajectories align. Calibration aims to improve agreement in trajectories and success rates between simulated replay and the real robot, beyond visual similarity alone. Task demonstrations and robot trajectories remain fixed during calibration. Before evaluation, we check the reconstructed scene with task initialization and camera observations.

3.4.2 本体 现实到仿真

仅场景对齐不足以实现策略迁移：模拟的本体必须呈现与真实系统相同的动作与观察语义。为了确保框架的通用性，我们并不重建机器人臂的完整高斯外观，而是仅复现其动力学与运动学系统。此外，我们还明确模拟了共享的 Tacumi 夹持器，包括其夹持器控制与触觉感知机制。

Scene alignment alone is insufficient for policy transfer: the simulated embodiment must expose the same action and observation semantics as the real system. To ensure the generality of our framework, we do not reconstruct the robot arm’s complete Gaussian appearance; instead, we reproduce only its dynamics and kinematics system. In addition, we explicitly simulate the shared Tacumi gripper, including its gripper control and tactile sensing.

操作器系统。我们在 Isaac Lab 中构建 XArm 动力学，并调整其 PD 参数以匹配真实机器人的控制行为。手眼校准提供了相对于机器人基座的三人称相机姿态。共享的 Tacumi 夹持器与传感器组件通过其机械设计决定了腕部相机的相对姿态，因此该姿态无需通过外部传感器模型进行估计。最后，我们调整了共享触觉夹持器的被动关节模式、PD 参数以及上述引入的力-位置混合控制器。

Manipulator System. We build the XArm dynamics in Isaac Lab and tune its PD parameters to match the real robot’s control behavior. Hand-eye calibration provides the third-person camera pose relative to the robot base. The shared Tacumi gripper and sensor assembly determines the wrist camera’s relative pose through its mechanical design, so this pose does not require estimation from an external sensor model. Finally, we tune the shared tactile gripper’s passive-joint mode, PD parameters, and the force-position hybrid controller introduced above.

触觉观测建模。我们的触觉 Real2Sim 路径将 Taxim 光学模型 Si and Yuan (2022) 与 FOTS 标记运动模型 Zhao et al. (2024) 结合在共享的 Isaac Lab 传感器接口中。模拟器首先从垫子下方的深度图像中生成接触表征。Taxim 将局部表面几何结构映射为触觉 RGB 观测信号，而 FOTS 则通过结合接触变形、接触质心变化以及传感器与物体的相对偏航角，产生传感器图像坐标下的标记位移场。这两种模型构成互补的前向模型：它们能够再现输入到策略中的信号，而无需精确复制商业传感器内部的光学结构或弹性体力学特性。 基于 Taxim 和 FOTS 的校准方法，我们利用少量真实的触觉观测数据对上述模型进行校准，并通过 Isaac Sim 中的参数设置，在分辨率、通道语义、预处理及时间更新规则方面使模拟数据与真实数据保持一致。我们共同调整观测映射与接触几何结构。具体而言，触觉垫碰撞区域的法线厚度被视为一个全局变量，通过调整该参数来减少离散时间内的穿透现象，同时保持稳定的接触区域及两种触觉模型的统一激活状态。此校准旨在优化与策略相关的接触响应，超越视觉网格对齐的范畴。 附录 D 详细描述了深度与压痕映射、光学与标记接口以及校准范围；完整的模拟器配置与校准后的资产一同发布，并通过运行清单进行锁定。由于商业限制，我们无法获得 Xense 的完整光学与机械规格。因此，我们仅在观察层面调整摄像机的显示视图与操作深度范围，并不声称存在严格的结构外部配准。

Tactile Observation Modeling. Our tactile Real2Sim path combines the Taxim optical model Si and Yuan (2022) and the FOTS marker-motion model Zhao et al. (2024) in a shared Isaac Lab sensor interface. The simulator first derives a contact representation from the depth image beneath the pad. Taxim maps local surface geometry to a tactile RGB observation, while FOTS combines contact deformation with contact-centroid changes and relative sensor-object yaw to produce a marker displacement field in sensor image coordinates. The two models are complementary forward models: they reproduce the signals exposed to the policy without requiring an exact replica of the commercial sensor’s internal optics or elastomer mechanics. Following the calibration methods of Taxim and FOTS, we calibrate the above models using a small set of real tactile observations and, through parameter settings in Isaac Sim, align the simulated and real data streams in resolution, channel semantics, preprocessing, and temporal-update conventions. We jointly adjust the observation mapping and the contact geometry. In particular, the normal thickness of the tactile-pad collision volume is treated as an asset-level variable and tuned to reduce discrete-time interpenetration while preserving a stable contact patch and consistent activation of both tactile models. This calibration targets contact responses relevant to the policy, extending beyond visual mesh alignment. Appendix D details the depth-to-indentation mapping, optical and marker interfaces, and calibration scope; the complete simulator configuration is released with the calibrated asset and pinned by the run manifest. Due to commercial restrictions, we cannot obtain the complete optical and mechanical specifications of Xense. We therefore only tune the cameras’ rendered view and operating depth range at the observation level, and do not claim strict structural extrinsic registration.

3.4.3 数据增强与联合训练后处理

Taco 通过结合三种数据来源进行协同训练：遥操作数据、Tacumi 数据以及仿真数据。其目标是在保持各数据源之间语言、接触及动作语义一致性的同时，提升策略在实际机器人任务中的泛化能力。

Taco combines three data sources for co-training: teleoperation data, Tacumi data, and simulation data. The goal is to improve the policy’s generalization on real-robot tasks while preserving consistent language, contact, and action semantics across sources.

真实的种子与模拟的视觉增强效果。真实的机器人轨迹提供了原始任务指令、动作以及可用观测数据，并固定了场景与传感器校准参数。相对固定的物体位置有助于模拟的对齐，而 UMI 数据则提供了补充的空间变化。基于对齐后的资产，模拟分支会改变物体外观、实例及背景视觉元素。基于 Tacinf-Tabero 技术，我们在这些变化及受控力条件下重新执行任务，从而生成同步的视觉观测数据、触觉图像、标记场、接触力及动作目标。背景外观的变化不会影响碰撞模型；要替换交互对象的几何形状，则需要相应的碰撞资产以及新的接触模拟。 重新播放不同的场景会生成新的触觉信号与力值标签，以反映更新后的物理交互情况。重新播放完成后，通过接触信号检测与任务完成检测，可过滤掉那些未产生接触或未能完成任务的数据。

Real seeds and simulated visual augmentation. Real-robot trajectories supply the original task instructions, motions, and available observations, and anchor the scene and sensor calibration. Relatively fixed object positions facilitate simulation alignment, while UMI data supply complementary spatial variation. Starting from the aligned assets, the simulation branch varies object appearance or instances and background visual elements. Building on Tacinf-Tabero, we replay the task under these variations and controlled force regimes, producing synchronized visual observations, tactile images, marker fields, contact forces, and action targets. Background appearance changes leave the collision model unchanged; replacing an interactive object’s geometry requires a corresponding collision asset and a new contact rollout. Replaying the varied scene generates new tactile signals and force labels that reflect the updated physical interactions. After replay, a contact-signal check and a task-completion check filter out data that produced no contact or failed the task.

采用 UMI 空间增强技术。Tacumi 通过人类示范行为扩展空间覆盖范围。物体被放置在不同的位置和方向，并以不同的路径被接近。共享夹持器同时记录接触和运动的变化，从而为模拟的视觉与力级变化添加物理空间差异。物体姿态的每种变化都被记录为一种新的示范行为，并伴随相应的动作和接触观测数据。被追踪的 UMI 姿态与传感器观测数据通过共同任务与观测-动作接口在混合之前被表达出来。

Spatial augmentation with UMI. Tacumi extends spatial coverage with human demonstrations. Objects are placed at different positions and orientations and approached along different paths. The shared gripper records the resulting changes in contact and motion together, adding physical spatial variation to the simulated visual and force-level variations. Each variation in object pose is recorded as a new demonstration with corresponding actions and contact observations. Tracked UMI poses and sensor observations are expressed through the common task and observation-action interfaces before mixing.

联合 VTLA 后训练。真实、模拟及 UMI 轨迹被同步、归一化，并分混合批次采样，以便在 3.2 节中结合动作与指尖力监督，共同对单个预训练的 Tacinf- VTLA 策略进行后训练。源数据采样比例及任务/力量级覆盖度属于明确的训练参数。后训练后的策略通过相同的触觉夹持器与混合控制器进行部署。

Joint VTLA post-training. The real, simulated, and UMI trajectories are synchronized, normalized, and sampled into mixed batches to jointly post-train a single pretrained Tacinf-VTLA policy with the action and fingertip-force supervision in Section 3.2 . The source sampling ratio and task/force-level coverage are explicit training settings. The post-trained policy is deployed with the same tactile gripper and hybrid controller.

数据协同效应。在三种数据来源中，仿真数据成本最低，UMI数据次之，而遥操作数据成本最高。仿真数据能够实现视觉元素与物体的泛化，UMI数据则提供抓取方式及物体姿态的变化，遥操作数据则提供精确的示例。第4.8节中的联合训练研究评估了这些互补贡献。三源混合方法在分布外任务上显著优于仅使用遥操作数据的训练。缺乏UMI数据会导致位置泛化受限，缺乏仿真数据则会导致物体泛化受限。遥操作为其他数据来源提供精确的参考示范，而仅使用UMI数据的训练在评估环境中表现不佳。综合这些结果表明，结合三种数据来源具有显著优势。

Data synergy. Among the three data sources, simulation data are the cheapest, UMI data come next, and teleoperation data are the most expensive. Simulation data provide visual-element and object generalization, UMI data provide grasp-approach and object-pose variation, and teleoperation data provide precise examples. The co-training study in Section 4.8 evaluates these complementary contributions. The three-source mixture substantially outperforms teleoperation-only training on out-of-distribution (OOD) tasks. Position generalization is limited without UMI data, and object generalization is limited without simulation data. Teleoperation provides precise reference demonstrations for the other sources, while UMI-only training performs poorly in the evaluated setting. Together, these results illustrate the benefits of combining the three sources.

4 评估

第 4.1 至 4.3 部分在 Tabero 上对基础模型的九种触觉输入变体进行了评估；第 4.4 至 4.6 部分对使用 Taco 数据重新训练的八种模型进行了评估；第 4.7 部分在独立的控制器数据上对一种模型进行了评估；而第 4.8 部分则对使用不同数据混合方式重新训练的六种模型进行了评估。硬件包括一个配备基于 Xense 技术的触觉爪具及其用于数据收集的对应设备 UMI 的 XArm7 机械臂，以及用于 UMI 姿态跟踪的 Meta Quest 3 设备。计算设备包括四台 H20 和两台 A6000 GPU；仅一台 RTX 4090 工作站即可满足完整系统的运行需求。

Sections 4.1 – 4.3 evaluate nine tactile-input variants of the foundation model on Tabero; Sections 4.4 – 4.6 evaluate eight models retrained with Taco data; Section 4.7 evaluates one model on separate controller data; and Section 4.8 evaluates six models retrained with different data mixtures. The hardware consists of one XArm7 arm equipped with an Xense-based tactile gripper and its UMI counterpart for data collection, together with a Meta Quest 3 for UMI pose tracking. Compute devices include four H20 and two A6000 GPUs; a single RTX 4090 workstation is the minimum compute requirement for reproducing the complete system.

评估方案。将两种力量水平视为独立的任务要求，Tabero包含18基础任务，而Taco则包含六项基础任务、六项位置随机化任务以及六项同时涉及位置与物体随机化的任务；另外三项操作任务用于验证控制器性能。任务类别包括语言条件下的力量跟随与特殊物体操作。每个Tabero和Taco基础任务均包含30演示；混合数据与独立任务实验的数据量则分别规定。在仿真中，每个模型需进行每任务50次试验的评估。在真实机器人上，每个模型在力量跟随任务中需进行20次试验，在特殊任务中需进行10次试验，评估标准参见附录B.2。4.1至4.3章节则采用方程（2）中的单指最小力作为握力参数。 第 4.4 至 4.8 节中，由于接触过程中单个指尖的读数并不准确，因此采用左右指尖力大小之和作为近似值；详见附录 C.4。当试验时长超过最大周期长度时，试验即终止。同一任务中坚定且温和的操作条件具有相同的初始物体与机器人状态。真实机器人策略以 20 Hz 速率运行，并通过时间聚合异步执行动作，推理延迟约为 150 – 200 ms；触觉闭环控制器则以相同的 20 Hz 速率运行。

Evaluation protocol. Counting the two force levels as distinct task requirements, Tabero contains 18 base tasks and Taco contains six base tasks, six position-randomized tasks, and six tasks with both position and object randomization; three additional manipulation tasks are used to validate the controller. The task categories are language-conditioned force following and special-object manipulation. Each base Tabero and Taco task has 30 demonstrations; data quantities for mixed-data and standalone task experiments are specified separately. In simulation, each model is evaluated for 50 trials per task. On the real robot, each model is evaluated for 20 trials on force-following tasks and 10 trials on special tasks, using the criteria in Appendix B.2 . Sections 4.1 – 4.3 use the per-finger minimum in Eq. ( 2 ) as grip force. Sections 4.4 – 4.8 use the sum of left and right fingertip force magnitudes as an approximation because individual fingertip readings are inaccurate during contact; see Appendix C.4 . A trial is terminated when it exceeds the maximum episode length. Firm and gentle conditions of the same task share identical initial object and robot states. The real-robot policy runs at 20 Hz and executes actions asynchronously through temporal ensembling, with an inference latency of roughly 150–200 ms; the tactile closed-loop controller runs at the same 20 Hz rate.

4.8 数据协同

我们评估第 3.4.3 节中数据增强与联合训练后处理流程是否能够结合具有不同成本和规模的数据源的互补优势。遥操作数据涵盖固定位置和物体。UMI 数据则引入物体位置和路径的变化，但物体本身保持不变。仿真数据则在任务位置处引入物体实例或外观的变化。因此，增加单一数据源的规模会提升其现有覆盖范围内的样本密度；但它不会直接增加该源中不存在的位置或物体状态。遥操作数据的收集时间比 UMI 数据更长，而仿真数据的成本最低。 每个数据单元包含针对六项 Taco 基础任务中的每一项的 30 示例数据，且每种策略均直接在不同数量数据单元的混合样本上进行训练，这些样本使用相同的坐标系统、观测数据、动作、时间步长及归一化接口。在对象随机化测试中，物理对象与模拟训练对象存在明显差异，如图 14 所示；附录 B.3 详细说明了测试设置。表 6 报告了在位置随机化及位置+对象随机化条件下，针对六项原始任务进行的六种训练混合样本（仅真实样本、仅 umi 样本、真实+umi (1 : 1)、模拟+umi (5 : 1)、真实+模拟 (1 : 5) 以及真实+模拟+umi (1 : 5 : 1)）。 所有结果均表现为稳定的/温和的成功率，以及策略在真实机器人场景中保持时所预测的平均握力。

关键发现。

UMI 仅在评估中的低数据环境下无法实现训练成功，而 0% 在随机化环境下在固定条件下则表现良好。UMI 通过与遥操作或模拟轨迹结合使用，能够发挥其作为混合数据训练中空间变化来源的作用。在位置随机化条件下，所有多源混合方案均优于仅使用真实数据的基线方案。平均成功率最高的是真实数据+ umi，其数据与测试场景最为匹配，其次是真实数据+模拟数据+ umi。向真实数据中添加 UMI 使固定条件下的成功率从 30.0% 提升至 83.3%，使温和成功率从 33.3% 提升至 70.0%。 在位置与物体随机化条件下，排序发生了变化：真实数据 + umi 以及仅真实数据均出现显著下降，表明分布偏移使得异常任务变得困难；而 sim+ umi 则达到了 40.0% 的平均成功率，真实+模拟 30.0% 以及真实+模拟+ umi 和 60.0% 也是如此。三源混合方式在两种情况下均使平均成功率比仅真实数据提高了 38.3 和 40.0 个百分点，是唯一一种在两种情况下平均增益均超过 30 点的多源混合方式。这些结果反映了各源之间的互补性：UMI 演示提供了物体位置和接近方式的多样性，模拟提供了物体外观和背景的多样性，而真实轨迹则确立了传感器与执行器的统计特性。 数据量同样重要，但实际与仿真实验的设置与结果表明，能力提升并非仅依赖于更多数据；数据本身的特性与多样性更为重要。

UMI-only training fails in the evaluated low-data setting, with 0% success under the firm condition in both randomization settings. UMI demonstrations contribute when combined with teleoperation or simulation trajectories, supporting their role as a source of spatial variation in mixed-data training. Under position randomization, every multi-source mixture improved on the real-only baseline. Mean success was highest for real+umi, whose data most closely match the test scenarios, followed by real+sim+umi. Adding UMI to real data raised firm success from 30.0% to 83.3% and gentle success from 33.3% to 70.0%. Under position+object randomization the ordering changed: real+umi and real-only both showed substantial drops, indicating that the distribution shift makes out-of-distribution tasks difficult, whereas sim+umi reached 40.0% mean success, real+sim 30.0%, and real+sim+umi 60.0%. The three-source mixture improved mean success over real-only by 38.3 and 40.0 percentage points in the two settings, the only multi-source mix with a mean gain above 30 points in both settings. These results reflect the complementary coverage of the sources: UMI demonstrations supply object-position and approach variation, simulation supplies object-appearance and background variation, and real trajectories anchor the sensor and actuator statistics. Data volume also matters, but the setup and results of real+sim show that the capability gain does not rely solely on more data; the characteristics and diversity of the data themselves matter more.

Every mixture with recorded force statistics predicts a higher mean force under firm than under gentle instructions in both settings. We attribute the larger separation mainly to increased data volume. In particular, adding substantial simulation data brings the force predictions closer to the original teleoperation labels.

这三个数据来源构成了一个数据金字塔结构，能够平衡数据效率与数据收集成本。它们的组合使得在随机位置及分布外物体条件下的真实机器人任务中，从 20.0% 到 60.0% 的测试任务成功率得到提升。在数据量较少的情况下，仅使用 UMI 数据进行训练会失败，而 UMI 数据与其他来源结合使用则更为有效。在无需遥操作数据的随机条件下， sim+ umi 混合数据也表现出良好的表现。这些结果表明，三个数据源相互补充，通过有限数据的协同训练能够提升任务性能。

The three data sources form a data pyramid that balances data efficiency and collection effort. Their combination increases mean success on held-out real-robot tasks with randomized positions and out-of-distribution objects from 20.0% to 60.0%. In this low-data setting, UMI-only training fails, while UMI data are more effective when combined with other sources. The sim+umi mixture also performs well under randomized conditions without teleoperation data. These results demonstrate complementary contributions from the three sources and improved task performance through co-training with limited data.

5 结论

我们提出了 Tacinf，这是一个用于力感知操作中的触觉基础模型的模块化基础设施。其策略接口能够根据任务语言、视觉信息及触觉反馈来预测动作及指尖力目标，对于需要明确力调节的任务，还支持可选的语言条件化力水平设置。我们的实验表明，力目标监督、高级触觉观测以及执行过程中的反馈各自发挥着不同的作用。在评估的仿真环境中，力监督机制与力目标接口是主要性能提升的来源，而某些触觉表征则进一步提升了任务表现。真实机器人控制器实验表明，反馈机制有助于力追踪与任务执行。那些仅基于仿真数据调整并直接应用于真实机器人的策略，无需进一步策略优化。 在八种不同的策略组合与六种任务条件下（三种任务，每种任务分别采用强力与柔和力水平），模拟实验以高成功率准确还原了真实机器人的表现，所报告的力值指标差异极小。在遥操作、模拟实验及 UMI 数据上的协同训练能够平衡数据效率与收集工作量。从 20.0% 到 60.0%，基于三种数据源的混合训练在随机位置及分布异常物体条件下的真实机器人任务平均成功率有所提升；这种提升并非仅源于数据量的增加。在数据量较少的情况下，仅使用 UMI 数据的训练无法取得良好效果，而 UMI 数据与其他数据源结合使用则更为有效。在无需遥操作数据的随机条件下， sim+ umi 混合训练也表现出良好的性能。

We present Tacinf, a modular infrastructure for tactile foundation models in force-aware manipulation. Its policy interface predicts actions and fingertip-force targets from task language, vision, and touch, with optional language-conditioned force levels for tasks that require explicit force regulation. Our ablations distinguish the roles of force-target supervision, high-level tactile observations, and feedback during execution. In the evaluated simulation setting, force supervision and the force-target interface account for the main performance gains, while some tactile representations further improve task performance. Real-robot controller experiments demonstrate how feedback contributes to force tracking and task execution. Policies adapted solely on simulation data transfer to the real robot without further policy fine-tuning. Across eight paired policy variants and six task conditions (three tasks, each with firm and gentle force levels), simulation closely preserves the real-robot ranking by success rate, with a small gap in the reported force metrics. Co-training on teleoperation, simulation, and UMI data balances data efficiency and collection effort. The three-source mixture increases mean success on held-out real-robot tasks with randomized positions and out-of-distribution objects from 20.0% to 60.0%; this gain cannot be attributed solely to increased data volume. In this low-data setting, UMI-only training fails, but UMI data are more effective when combined with other sources. The sim+umi mixture also performs well under randomized conditions without teleoperation data.

局限性。资源限制限制了本研究的数据集规模与任务范围，且未对大规模训练进行评估。未来的研究将扩展数据层级结构，并将系统应用于更多类型的机器人本体及双手操作场景。

Limitations. Resource constraints limit the scale of the datasets and tasks in this study, and we do not evaluate large-scale training. Future work will expand the data pyramid and extend the system to additional robot embodiments and bimanual manipulation.

一种常见的主机时间基准能够确保时间戳的可比性，但无法单独实现物理信号的同步采集。传感器采集、传输及缓冲过程可能会引入基于模态的延迟；之前的手持式系统明确区分了时间戳对齐与各模态延迟补偿 Li et al. (2025a)。因此，此处所述的统一时钟约定并不意味着存在可测量的同步误差界限。
