ForceWAM：分开学习交互的未来走向与局部反应
利用任务示教和接触探索数据
实现接触密集型操作

摘要

专家示教展示了如何完成接触密集型任务，但对动作改变时交互力会怎样变化覆盖有限。自由探索不设具体任务目标，却能记录更多接触变化。ForceWAM 是一个力感知的世界–动作模型，分别学习符合任务目标的交互结果，以及局部动作如何改变接触。世界模型从成功示教学会联合预测未来视频和六轴力与力矩轨迹，以描述任务中的运动和接触怎样发展。另一个逆动力学模型（IDM）同时用任务示教和自由探索训练，根据当前观测及记录到的未来视频与力、力矩，学习产生这些结果所对应的机器人动作。 这样，自由探索数据中的力响应就能服务于有目标的操作。部署时，两次世界模型更新之间，逆动力学模型沿用此前预测的未来状态，并依据新到的力与力矩反馈调整动作。在真机的指定力滑动、有扰动和无扰动的翻盒、套环任务中，ForceWAM 的平均成功率达到 8585 至 95%95\% ，高于世界模型与反应式基线。加入无特定任务的自由探索后，成功率提高 1515 至 4545 个百分点，说明这类数据有助于学习如何执行接触密集型动作。项目页面和代码见 https://force-wam.github.io/ 。

Expert demonstrations show how to complete contact-rich tasks, but provide limited coverage of how interaction forces respond to different actions. Task-agnostic play provides broader experience of contact dynamics without specifying task goals. To fully leverage these two types of data, we introduce ForceWAM, a force world action model that decouples knowledge of task-compatible interaction outcomes from knowledge of how local actions change contact. A world model learns from successful demonstrations to jointly predict future video and six-axis force/torque trajectories. Together, these futures describe the expected evolution of motion and contact during the task. A separate inverse dynamics model (IDM) learns from both task and play, mapping current observations and recorded visual-wrench futures to their associated robot actions. Play data thus provide knowledge of force responses for goal-directed execution. At deployment, the IDM reactively translates generated futures into actions, reusing them with fresh wrench feedback between world-model updates. Across real-robot force-regulated sliding, box flipping with and without disturbances, and ring placement, ForceWAM achieves 85 - 95 % mean success, surpassing world-modeling and reactive baselines. Our experiments further show that adding task-agnostic play data improves success by 15 - 45 percentage points, demonstrating its value for learning contact-rich execution. Project page and code are available at https://force-wam.github.io/ .

1 引言

机器人操控通常被描述为预测达到期望视觉状态的动作。然而，涉及接触的任务的确定不仅取决于机器人的移动位置，还取决于交互力在过程中的变化情况。两种视觉上相似的配置可能对应于不足接触、损伤力或卡滞现象。因此，传统力控制方法将交互反馈置于执行的核心位置 (Hogan, 1985) 而近期学习系统表明，力与触感能提升具有接触性的操控能力 (Liu et al., 2025; Xue et al., 2025; Yu et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2026; Wang et al., 2026; Zhuo et al., 2026) 我们将这种学习问题称为以力为中心的操控：策略不仅需感知力，还需学习调节该力所需的物理规律。近期的方法则侧重于学习自适应顺应性或混合力-位置控制机制。 (Hou et al., 2025; Fang et al., 2026; Li et al., 2026c) 我们旨在从数据中直接学习以力为中心的操控任务。 然而，主要障碍在于数据中的这些规律出现位置与策略学习这些规律的方式之间存在不匹配。

Robotic manipulation is often cast as predicting actions that reach a desired visual state. Yet contact-rich tasks are decided not only by where the robot moves, but by how interaction forces evolve along the way. Two visually similar configurations can correspond to insufficient contact, damaging force, or a jam. Classical force control therefore places interaction feedback at the center of execution ( Hogan, 1985 ) , while recent learned systems show that force and touch improve contact-rich manipulation ( Liu et al., 2025 ; Xue et al., 2025 ; Yu et al., 2025 ; Chen et al., 2026 ; Wang et al., 2026 ; Zhuo et al., 2026 ) . We call this learning problem force-centric manipulation : a policy must not merely sense force, but learn the physical regularities needed to regulate it. Recent methods learn adaptive compliance or hybrid force-position control ( Hou et al., 2025 ; Fang et al., 2026 ; Li et al., 2026c ) . We aim to learn the full force-centric manipulation task directly from data. However, the main obstacle is a mismatch between where these regularities appear in data and how policies learn them.

我们称之为任务数据的专家演示展示了任务执行的成功情况。例如，在大约 1010\, N 时刻进行的推箱演示，无需展示机器人施加更大力量、后退或遇到干扰时力的变化过程。相比之下，与任务无关的游戏数据则记录了探索性的按压、滑动及释放行为，提供了更多的视觉与力变化组合以及相关的机器人动作。虽然这些游戏过渡过程未必能证明对特定任务指令的成功执行，但它们仍为逆动力学学习提供了记录的未来动作对。端到端的力感知学习系统 (Yu et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2026) 在任务监督映射中仍面临两个问题：哪种过渡方式推动任务进展，以及哪种动作实现了该过渡。我们的问题是：我们能否更有效地利用这两种数据来源，以学习它们各自所蕴含的独特知识？

Expert demonstrations, which we call task data , show successful task execution. A box-pushing demonstration at roughly 10 N, for example, need not show how force changes when the robot presses farther, backs away, or encounters a disturbance. By contrast, task-agnostic play data record exploratory pressing, sliding, and release, providing additional visual-wrench transitions and associated robot actions. Play transitions need not demonstrate successful behavior for a given task instruction, but they still provide recorded future-action pairs for inverse-dynamics learning. End-to-end force-aware learners ( Yu et al., 2025 ; Chen et al., 2026 ) still couple two questions in one task-supervised mapping: what transition advances the task, and which action realizes that transition. Our question is: can we use these two data sources more effectively to learn the distinct knowledge each provides?

基于逆动力学的视频规划为从交互结果中学习动作提供了基础：录制的过渡过程用于监督逆模型，而生成的未来状态则指导实际执行 (Du et al., 2023; Li et al., 2026b)。在涉及大量接触的操作中，视觉化的未来状态可能无法明确指定接触力的变化规律。关注力和触觉的模型表明，预测接触信号并将其用于反应式控制具有显著价值 (He et al., 2026; Ma et al., 2026)。我们研究“视觉力”未来状态作为从任务与游戏中学习执行方式的条件，将动作与其产生的运动及接触变化联系起来。

Video planning with inverse dynamics provides a foundation for learning actions from interaction outcomes: recorded transitions supervise the inverse model, while generated futures guide execution ( Du et al., 2023 ; Li et al., 2026b ) . For contact-rich manipulation, a visual future can leave the desired evolution of contact forces unspecified. Force- and tactile-aware models have demonstrated the value of predicting contact signals and using them for reactive control ( He et al., 2026 ; Ma et al., 2026 ) . We study visual-wrench futures as the condition for learning execution from task and play, associating actions with both the motion and the contact evolution they produce.

We introduce ForceWAM (Figure 1 ), which uses visual-wrench futures to decouple knowledge of task-compatible interaction outcomes from knowledge of how local actions change contact. Successful task demonstrations train the force world model (ForceWM) to jointly generate future video and six-axis wrench trajectories. The inverse dynamics model (IDM) learns from task and play segments to predict the robot actions that realize a visual-wrench future. During training, it conditions on the current observation and the future recorded in each segment. Any segment that records actions and their resulting motion and contact outcomes can supervise this action prediction, even if it does not complete a task. At deployment, ForceWM-generated futures replace the recorded future conditions used in IDM training, specifying the interaction that execution should realize. The IDM reuses each generated reference within an action chunk, reactively updating its predictions with newly measured wrench feedback.

2 相关研究

力感知操控。

力和触觉反馈提供的接触信息，仅凭视觉难以推断出来 (Lee et al., 2019; Suresh et al., 2024; Ai et al., 2024) . ForceMimic 将力-运动演示捕获与混合模仿相结合，而 ForceVLA 则将力整合进来 vision-language-action 策略与 ForceVLA2 将这一思路进一步拓展至混合力-位置控制领域 (Liu et al., 2025; Yu et al., 2025; Li et al., 2026c) . 除了将力作为观测变量外，反应式策略利用新测量的接触信号在执行过程中更新动作。 (Calandra et al., 2018) . RDP 结合了慢速潜在策略与快速触觉执行器，而 ImplicitRDP 则通过因果注意力在单一扩散网络中实现块内力反馈 (Xue et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2026) . FA-RDP 根据操作阶段调整推理频率，而 LIFT 则结合反应式力注入与在线纠正监督机制 (Zhuo et al., 2026; Wang et al., 2026) . ForceWAM 将与任务兼容的交互结果相关知识，与局部动作如何改变接触状态的相关知识分离开来：ForceWM 描述了成功交互应有的外观与感觉，而逆动力学模型（IDM）则将这一预期转化为机器人动作，并通过块内扭矩反馈进行响应式更新。IDM 以当前观测结果及视觉-扭矩预期为条件，从而能够从任务与游戏状态的转换中学习。

Force and tactile feedback provide contact information that is difficult to infer from vision alone ( Lee et al., 2019 ; Suresh et al., 2024 ; Ai et al., 2024 ) . ForceMimic couples force-motion demonstration capture with hybrid imitation, while ForceVLA integrates force into vision-language-action policies and ForceVLA2 extends this line to hybrid force-position control ( Liu et al., 2025 ; Yu et al., 2025 ; Li et al., 2026c ) . Beyond incorporating force as an observation, reactive policies use newly measured contact signals to update actions during execution ( Calandra et al., 2018 ) . RDP combines a slow latent policy with a fast tactile executor, whereas ImplicitRDP realizes within-chunk force feedback through causal attention in a single diffusion network ( Xue et al., 2025 ; Chen et al., 2026 ) . FA-RDP adapts inference frequency to manipulation phase, and LIFT combines reactive force injection with online corrective supervision ( Zhuo et al., 2026 ; Wang et al., 2026 ) . ForceWAM decouples knowledge of task-compatible interaction outcomes from knowledge of how local actions change contact: ForceWM models what a successful interaction should look and feel like, while an inverse dynamics model (IDM) turns that future into robot actions and reactively updates them with within-chunk wrench feedback. The IDM is conditioned on the current observation and visual-wrench future, which lets it learn from both task and play transitions.

世界行为模型。

世界行为模型将未来的观测预测与机器人行为生成联系起来 (Bi et al., 2026a; Bi et al., 2026b; Cai et al., 2026) 如 LingBot-VA、Cosmos Policy、DreamZero、FACT 和 GE-所所示 Act 2.0 (Li et al., 2026a; Kim et al., 2026; Ye et al., 2026; Peng et al., 2026; AgiBot Research Team et al., 2026) . 一种相关路径将视频规划与动作推理分离开来 (Xu et al., 2025) UniPi 使用单独训练的 IDM 来转换生成的视觉规划，而 VERA 则开发了一种基于雅可比矩阵的 IDM，该 IDM能够通过自我对战独立学习。 (Du et al., 2023; Li et al., 2026b) . 基于接触感知的模型将预测扩展至力与触觉动态，以用于规划与反应式控制 (Higuera et al., 2026; Zheng et al., 2026; Zhou et al., 2026; Madan et al., 2026; NeoteAI Team and Fudan TEAI Team, 2026) 其中，FAWAM 共同预测动作与扭矩，并基于人类干预及策略调整来训练残差修正项。 (He et al., 2026) . TacPAC 利用实时触觉观测与缓存的触觉动作预测来修正现有行为 action chunk ，通过针对已展示动作的监督校正器进行优化 (Ma et al., 2026) . ForceWAM 将与学习任务相关的结果与学习局部动作-接触响应分离开来，同时保留一种基于力的反应式推理过程。成功的演示表明，该模型能够训练出视觉-扭矩未来生成器；而任务与游戏窗口则直接通过记录下的未来状态及相关动作来训练 IDM。在部署过程中，IDM 将预测的视觉-扭矩未来作为任务条件参考，并在缓存的未来窗口内利用新测量的扭矩反馈来动态修正每个动作。因此，这两个模块在所学内容上被解耦，但在执行过程中通过力反馈实现连接，而非仅将视觉未来作为静态规划目标。这种设计使得与任务无关的接触转换成为执行任务条件下的视觉-扭矩未来任务的直接监督来源。

World action models connect future observation prediction with robot action generation ( Bi et al., 2026a ; Bi et al., 2026b ; Cai et al., 2026 ) , as exemplified by LingBot-VA, Cosmos Policy, DreamZero, FACT, and GE-Act 2.0 ( Li et al., 2026a ; Kim et al., 2026 ; Ye et al., 2026 ; Peng et al., 2026 ; AgiBot Research Team et al., 2026 ) . A related route separates video planning from action inference ( Xu et al., 2025 ) : UniPi translates generated visual plans with a separately trained IDM, and VERA develops a Jacobian-based IDM that can learn independently from self-play ( Du et al., 2023 ; Li et al., 2026b ) . Contact-aware models extend prediction to force and tactile dynamics for planning and reactive control ( Higuera et al., 2026 ; Zheng et al., 2026 ; Zhou et al., 2026 ; Madan et al., 2026 ; NeoteAI Team and Fudan TEAI Team, 2026 ) . Among these, FAWAM jointly predicts actions and wrenches and trains residual corrections from human interventions and policy rollouts ( He et al., 2026 ) . TacPAC uses live tactile observations and cached tactile-action predictions to correct an existing action chunk, with its corrector supervised against demonstrated actions ( Ma et al., 2026 ) . ForceWAM separates learning task-compatible outcomes from learning local action-contact responses while retaining a force-aware reactive inference process. Successful demonstrations train a visual-wrench future generator, while task and play windows directly train an IDM on their recorded futures and associated actions. At deployment, the IDM uses the predicted visual-wrench future as a task-conditioned reference and reactively corrects each action with newly measured wrench feedback within the cached future window. Thus, the two modules are decoupled in what they learn but connected through force feedback during execution, rather than using a visual future only as a static planning target. This makes task-agnostic contact transitions a direct source of supervision for executing task-conditioned visual-wrench futures.

4 实验

我们围绕四个问题展开评估。（Q1）完整系统是否提升了具有真实机器人接触交互的操控能力？（Q2）当 IDM 能够执行更多与任务无关的操作时，其执行方式如何变化？（Q3）ForceWM 在闭环执行过程中预测了怎样的视觉与接触变化？以及（Q4）视觉与扭矩的未来状态如何影响执行效果？

We organize the evaluation around four questions. (Q1) Does the complete system improve real-robot contact-rich manipulation? (Q2) How does IDM execution change as it gains access to more task-agnostic play? (Q3) What visual and contact evolution does ForceWM predict during closed-loop execution? 4.1 实验设置

任务。

我们在四种高接触条件下对三种真实机器人任务进行了评估。滑动式操作涉及将末端执行器沿力计中心线移动，同时目标为 1010\, N 的正常力。成功需满足全程完成以及满足 55 - 1515\, N 接触力标准。箱体翻转则需持续接触及可控的推力以将箱体推过边缘，而其干扰变体（Flip-D）则在接触过程中引入外部推力。河内环放置任务则是将环固定在 1010\, mm 杆上，接触力决定环是正确就位还是卡住。所有四种条件均具有高接触性，并采用六轴扳手反馈机制。它们在不同于要求的接触类型上有所区别——包括持续的力维持、持续推力、排斥外部干扰以及精确就位接触。

We evaluate three real-robot task families under four contact-rich conditions. Sliding drags the end effector along the centerline of a force scale while targeting a normal force of 10 N. Success requires full-path completion and satisfaction of the 5 - 15 N contact-force criterion. Box flipping requires sustained contact and a controlled pushing force to carry the box over its edge, and its disturbed variant (Flip-D) adds an external push during contact. Hanoi ring placement seats a held ring onto a 10 mm pole, where contact force decides whether the ring seats or jams. All four conditions are contact-rich and use six-axis wrench feedback. They differ in the kind of contact they demand—continuous force maintenance, sustained pushing, rejection of an external disturbance, and fine seating contact.

4.3 Q2：添加的任务无关游戏是否能提升执行效率？

我们使用所有任务演示数据，对五台 IDM 进行训练，并让 00、5050、100100、150150 或 200200 在每个任务家族中执行若干游戏episode，同时独立生成中间子集（图 4）。ForceWM 参数及优化器更新次数在不同变体中保持固定。

We train five IDMs with all task demonstrations and 0 , 50 , 100 , 150 , or 200 play episodes per task family, drawing the intermediate subsets independently (Figure 4 ). ForceWM and the number of optimizer updates are fixed across variants.

力调节式滑动行为表现出最大的绝对增益，从 40%40\% 到 85%85\% 能够在 55 - 1515\, N 接触力标准下成功完成路径。该任务反复将路径上的运动与力调节相结合。成功的演示无需说明当接触载荷偏离正常状态时如何 act。按压、滑动和释放等动作为学习此类响应提供了额外的动作力转换。IDM 的未来状态将这些动作与其接触结果关联起来。这一显著改进与这种监督机制对持续力调节的重要性相一致。

Force-regulated sliding shows the largest absolute gain, from 40 % to 85 % success in completing the path under the 5 - 15 N contact-force criterion. This task repeatedly couples motion along a path with force regulation. Successful demonstrations need not cover how to act when contact loads depart from nominal conditions. Pressing, sliding, and release in play provide additional action-wrench transitions for learning such responses. The IDM’s future condition associates these actions with their contact outcomes. The large improvement is consistent with the value of this supervision for sustained force regulation.

在箱子翻转过程中，通过游戏操作可将成功率从 75%75\% 提升至 95%95\%，且不会引发干扰；而在外部推挤作用下，则从 65%65\% 提升至 85%85\%。在此情况下，通过多次构建与接触释放的练习，有助于 IDM 在箱子旋转过程中调整推挤动作。河内环的放置方式也从 75%75\% 提升到了 90%90\%：局部接触经验同样有助于在就座过程中选择正确动作，而经过训练的任务相关行为则保留了所需的环-杆几何特征。这些结果表明，游戏带来的优势适用于不同的接触需求，包括持续的力量调节、旋转及放置等过程。在 ForceWM 保持固定的情况下，这些改进反映了从记录下的游戏行为及动作中获得的执行学习能力的提升，而无需额外的任务训练。

For box flipping, play raises success from 75 % to 95 % without disturbances and from 65 % to 85 % with external pushes. Here, additional experience of building and releasing contact can help the IDM adapt pushing actions as the box rotates. Hanoi ring placement improves from 75 % to 90 % : local contact experience can likewise help select actions during seating, while the task-trained future retains the required ring-pole geometry. These results suggest that the benefit of play extends across different contact demands, from continuous force regulation to rotation and placement. With ForceWM held fixed, these gains reflect improved execution learning from recorded play futures and actions, without additional task demonstrations.

相比之下，被评估的端到端模型——ImplicitRDP和Cosmos Policy——并未从额外的游戏数据中获益：它们的共享策略直接从游戏行为中学习，即使这些行为并不推动任务进展。额外的游戏行为可能会改变它们的动作分布，使其偏离成功的任务行为，包括当Cosmos Policy预测未来情况时也是如此。通过将与任务兼容结果的认知与局部动作-接触响应的认知分离开来，ForceWAM可以在IDM中从游戏中学习，而无需对非任务行为进行ForceWM训练。

By contrast, the evaluated end-to-end models, ImplicitRDP and Cosmos Policy, do not benefit from added play data: their shared policies learn directly from play actions even when those actions need not advance the task. The added play can therefore shift their action distributions away from successful task behavior, including when Cosmos Policy also predicts futures. By decoupling knowledge of task-compatible outcomes from knowledge of local action-contact responses, ForceWAM can learn from play in the IDM without training ForceWM on off-task behavior.

5 结论

我们提出了 ForceWAM 模型，该模型将与任务相关的交互结果知识与局部动作如何改变接触状态的知识分离开来。任务演示用于训练视觉-扭矩未来模型，而任务与游戏数据则用于训练逆动力学模型，后者可将生成的未来状态转化为带有力反馈的动作。真实机器人实验表明，该模型的任务成功率高于现有基线模型，且游戏训练带来的效果也较为稳定。这表明，利用任务与游戏数据来学习互补知识，为实现可扩展且稳定的富含接触的操控技术提供了可行的路径。

We presented ForceWAM, which decouples knowledge of task-compatible interaction outcomes from knowledge of how local actions change contact. Task demonstrations train a visual-wrench future model, while task and play data train an inverse dynamics model that translates generated futures into actions with force feedback. Real-robot experiments show higher task success than the evaluated baselines and consistent gains from play. This suggests that using task and play data to learn complementary knowledge offers a promising path to scalable, stable contact-rich manipulation.

A fixed adapter flattens the current 6 -D wrench or the future 16 × 6 trajectory and periodically repeats it across the corresponding video-latent-shaped slot. 附录 C 机器人动作表示

每个机器人动作包含一个笛卡尔 TCP姿态目标和一个夹持器指令。在训练数据中，该目标由预测窗口开始时的 TCP姿态和夹持器状态表示。姿态以预测窗口起始时的基础 TCP姿态为基准进行表达。旋转通过滚转、俯仰和偏航来表示。在部署阶段，预测的相对姿态会与缓存的基础 TCP姿态结合，进而转换为机器人控制器可接受的绝对姿态-夹持器指令。

Each robot action comprises a Cartesian TCP-pose target and a gripper command. In the training data, it is represented by the TCP pose and gripper state at the next recorded timestep. Poses are expressed relative to the base TCP pose at the start of the prediction window. Rotation is represented by roll, pitch, and yaw. At deployment, the predicted relative pose is composed with the cached base TCP pose and converted to the absolute pose-gripper command accepted by the robot controller.

该实现从 ImplicitRDP (Chen et al., 2026) 中继承了一个辅助预测目标。除了可执行的相对姿态-夹持器动作外，它还预测出由力产生的虚拟姿态以及标量刚度值。该辅助模块与可执行模块共同进行训练，但在推理后会被丢弃：机器人仅接收解码后的 TCP 姿态和夹持器宽度。该辅助目标能够规范从 IDM 多模态输入中学习的表示方式。经过相对姿态转换后，完整的扩散目标具有 14 维度及 18 步长的预测时间范围。只有与当前慢窗对齐的前缀才具有执行资格。

The implementation inherits an auxiliary prediction target from ImplicitRDP ( Chen et al., 2026 ) . In addition to the executable relative pose-gripper action, it predicts a force-derived virtual pose and a scalar stiffness. This auxiliary block is trained jointly with the executable block, but is discarded after inference: the robot receives only the decoded TCP pose and gripper width. The auxiliary target regularizes representations learned from the IDM’s multimodal inputs. After relative-pose conversion, the full diffusion target has 14 dimensions and an 18-step prediction horizon. Only the prefix aligned with the current slow window is eligible for execution.
