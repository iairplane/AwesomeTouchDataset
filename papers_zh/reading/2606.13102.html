FTP-1 面向富接触操作的跨传感器通用触觉基础策略

摘要

通用的视觉机器人策略已经取得进展，现有触觉策略的适用范围仍多限于固定的本体和传感器配置。不同硬件的触觉信号差异较大，因此很难跨传感器泛化。我们提出 FTP-1。据我们所知，这是首个用大规模异构触觉操作数据预训练，并可通过微调迁移到多种传感器与本体的通用触觉基础策略。FTP-1 支持图像式、阵列式和状态式等多种触觉输入。针对不同输入使用相应的编码器，将它们映射成统一的、考虑本体形态的潜在 tokens，再由共享的触觉 Transformer 专家联合建模。 预训练数据包含 ∼\sim 3,000 小时触觉操作，汇集自 26 个数据源，覆盖 21 种传感器，包含人和机器人的示教。由此，FTP-1 学到可迁移至预训练阶段未见传感器的触觉技能。下游微调实验跨越 5 种硬件配置。FTP-1 在已见传感器配置上将富接触操作成功率提高 +17.2%。同时，该策略成功迁移到两种未见的触觉传感器配置，成功率提高 +31%。FTP-1 为触觉操作建立了首个统一的基础基线，为后续触觉策略提供共享的模型起点。全部模型检查点、数据集和训练代码将在未来发布。

Despite the success of vision-based generalist robotic policies, existing tactile-based policies remain tied to fixed embodiments and sensor setups. This is because tactile signals are highly heterogeneous across hardware, making cross-sensor generalization difficult. We present FTP-1, to the best of our knowledge, the first generalist foundation tactile policy pretrained on large-scale heterogeneous tactile manipulation data for finetuning transfer across diverse sensors and embodiments. FTP-1 supports varied tactile inputs, including image-, array-, and state-based signals, by using heterogeneous encoders to project them into unified morphology-aware latent tokens that are jointly modeled by a shared tactile Transformer expert. Pretrained on ∼ 3,000 hours of tactile manipulation data aggregated from 26 data sources, spanning human and robot demonstrations across 21 sensors, FTP-1 learns tactile skills that transfer beyond the sensors seen during pretraining. Across downstream finetuning experiments spanning 5 hardware configurations, FTP-1 improves contact-rich manipulation on seen sensor setups by +17.2% and, surprisingly, transfers to two previously unseen tactile-sensor setups, achieving a +31% gain in success rate. FTP-1 establishes the first unified foundation baseline for tactile manipulation, providing future tactile policies with a shared model-level starting point. All model checkpoints, datasets, and training code will be released in the future.

1 引言

通用机器人策略 [7, 6] 希望让机器人学习超出按任务单独训练的范式 [92, 14]，转向可以高效适配不同本体、任务与环境的大规模预训练策略。在大规模异构数据上扩大数据量、算力和模型容量后，近期基于视觉的通用策略，如 π0.5\pi_{0.5} [34]，已表明预训练可以为下游操作任务提供有效的初始化。

Generalist robotic policies [ 7 , 6 ] seek to move robot learning beyond task-specific training [ 92 , 14 ] toward large-scale pretrained policy models that can be efficiently adapted to diverse embodiments, tasks, and environments. By scaling data, computation, and model capacity over large-scale heterogeneous datasets, recent vision-based generalist policies, such as π 0.5 [ 34 ] , have shown that pretraining provides powerful initialization for downstream manipulation tasks.

触觉策略学习对这一通用范式的探索仍然有限。触觉在接触密集的精细操作中有关键作用，这使问题格外重要。近期的预训练触觉策略 [12, 45, 95] 开始扩大用于预训练的触觉数据，却依然依赖少量传感器配置、观测格式和机器人本体。限制专用设计的是不同设备的信号差异：传感器的模态、分辨率、形态与接触响应各不相同。本文研究触觉操作 [31, 26] 能否采用通用基础策略的范式 [37]：一个触觉策略能否吸收不同来源的触觉经验，并迁移到预训练时没有见过的传感器与本体？

However, this generalist paradigm remains largely underexplored in tactile-based policy learning. This is a critical gap, as tactile sensing plays an essential role in contact-rich, fine-grained manipulation. Recent pretrained tactile policies [ 12 , 45 , 95 ] have begun to scale tactile data for policy pretraining, but remain tied to limited sensor configurations, observation formats, and robot embodiments, rather than a generalist policy. This sensor-specific design is fundamentally constrained by the heterogeneity of tactile signals across hardware, where sensors differ in modality, resolution, morphology, and contact response. In this work, we ask whether tactile manipulation [ 31 , 26 ] can be learned under a generalist foundation policy paradigm [ 37 ] : can a single tactile policy absorb heterogeneous tactile experience and transfer to sensors and embodiments beyond those seen during pretraining?

我们提出 FTP-1。据我们所知，它是首个使用大规模异构触觉操作数据预训练、可经微调迁移到不同传感器和本体的通用触觉基础策略（图 1）。这里的通用触觉基础策略是一个统一的动作生成模型，在多种传感器和本体上预训练，共用组件可适配新的硬件和任务。FTP-1 支持图像、阵列和状态三类触觉输入。为应对不同触觉输入的差异，我们设计了形态感知触觉 Token 空间（MTTS）作为跨传感器的统一接口。MTTS 用一组固定的功能区域 tokens 表示触觉输入，并加入跨设备共享的功能区域嵌入。不同形式的触觉再经各自的编码器投射到这个共同空间。 在 MTTS 之上，FTP-1 使用多专家基础策略架构 [7, 34]，由一个共享的触觉 Transformer 专家 [64] 联合建模各个触觉 tokens，从中学习跨传感器可复用的触觉表征和操作技能。

We present FTP-1, to the best of our knowledge, the first generalist F oundation T actile P olicy pretrained on large-scale heterogeneous tactile manipulation data for finetuning transfer across diverse sensors and embodiments (Fig. 1 ). Here, a generalist tactile foundation policy is a single action-generating model pretrained across heterogeneous sensors and embodiments, with shared components that can be adapted to new hardware and tasks. FTP-1 supports image-, array-, and state-based tactile inputs. To address tactile heterogeneity, we introduce Morphology-Aware Tactile Token Space (MTTS) as a unified interface across sensors. MTTS represents tactile inputs with a fixed set of functional-area tokens, each augmented by a shared function-area embedding. Heterogeneous tactile encoders are then used to project different tactile inputs into this common space. On top of MTTS, FTP-1 adopts a multi-expert foundation-policy architecture [ 7 , 34 ] , where a shared tactile Transformer expert [ 64 ] jointly models tactile tokens to learn reusable tactile representations and manipulation skills across sensors.

我们还构建了 FTP-1-Dataset。这套大规模异构触觉操作数据集汇总了 26 个来源，包含约 3,000 小时的人类和机器人示教，覆盖 21 种触觉传感器。全部数据都通过 MTTS 接口标准化。使用这些数据预训练，让 FTP-1 获得能够迁移到不同传感器和本体的触觉操作技能。

We further curate FTP-1-Dataset, a new large-scale heterogeneous tactile manipulation dataset aggregated from 26 data sources. It contains approximately 3,000 hours of human and robot demonstrations across 21 tactile sensors, with all data standardized through our unified MTTS tactile interface. Pretraining on this dataset enables FTP-1 to acquire transferable tactile manipulation skills across sensors and embodiments.

2.1 形态感知式触觉 Token 空间（MTTS）

Tactile sensors vary in modality, resolution, and morphology, making cross-sensor representation sharing challenging. FTP-1 addresses this challenge with a Morphology-Aware Tactile Token Space (MTTS), augmented by shared functional-area embeddings. MTTS organizes tactile signals into 24 functional areas [ 51 ] , each represented by one token; the full definition is shown in Fig. 3 . For in-hand tactile signals, we use slots 0-14 to represent different hand functional regions. For force/torque signals from wrists and fingers, we use slots 15-20. For parallel grippers, the two gripper-side sensors are mapped to the thumb-tip slot (slot 0) and index-fingertip slot (slot 1), reflecting their two-finger grasping function. Slots 21-23 are reserved for future use.

根据此定义，特定传感器的触觉信号按功能区域进行分组，并编码为统一的、具备本体感知功能的触觉 tokens。为了明确每个 token 的功能区域，我们在将 tokens 输入后续架构之前，添加了一种可学习的功能区域嵌入向量，该向量在所有传感器中共享。我们为左手/右手使用不同的功能区域嵌入向量，以便区分它们的触觉 tokens。

Under this definition, sensor-specific tactile signals are grouped by functional area and encoded into unified morphology-aware tactile tokens. To indicate the functional area of each token, we add a learnable functional-area embedding, shared across all sensors, before feeding the tokens into the subsequent architecture. We use separate functional-area embeddings for the left/right hands to distinguish their tactile tokens.

2.2 用于 MTTS 分词的非均匀触觉编码器

尽管 MTTS 提供了统一的 token 接口，但不同传感器的触觉输入在形态与方式上仍存在差异。因此，我们采用异构触觉编码器 [66] 将各传感器特有的输入转化为 MTTS 可识别的token。对于每个传感器，我们首先依据 MTTS 功能区域对其信号进行分组，然后将其分为三种观测类型之一：图像、数组或状态。

Although MTTS provides a unified token interface, tactile inputs from different sensors still vary in shape and modality. We therefore use heterogeneous tactile encoders [ 66 ] to tokenize sensor-specific inputs into MTTS. For each sensor, we first group its signals according to MTTS functional areas, then categorize each group into one of three observation types: image, array, or state.

对于图像型输入（例如） GelSight [85] ), 我们使用一种轻量级的传感器专用方案 ViT [17] 随后进行共享预训练 T3 Transformer 触觉编码器 [91] 在多个传感器之间，并使用最终的 [CLS] token 作为触觉 token . 对于阵列型输入（例如，接触式） [65] ), 我们使用一个 CNN [68] 捕捉空间触觉结构，并将每个功能区域压缩为单一单元 token 对于状态型输入（例如力-扭矩），我们对原始状态进行傅里叶编码 [32] 并以轻量级方式处理它 MLP 对于具有多个功能区域且观测形状相同的传感器，我们将这些区域的对应编码器进行共享，从而减少传感器特定的参数，并促使共同的触觉动态能够一致地被建模。详细内容请参见附录。 B.2 .

For image-type inputs (e.g., GelSight [ 85 ] ), we use a lightweight sensor-specific ViT [ 17 ] followed by a shared pretrained T3 Transformer tactile encoder [ 91 ] across sensors, and use the final [CLS] token as the tactile token. For array-type inputs (e.g., Contactile [ 65 ] ), we use a CNN [ 68 ] to capture spatial tactile structure and compress each functional area into one token. For state-type inputs (e.g., force-torque), we Fourier-encode the raw state [ 32 ] and process it with a lightweight MLP. For sensors with multiple functional areas of the same observation shape, we share the corresponding encoder across these areas to reduce sensor-specific parameters and encourage common tactile dynamics to be modeled consistently. Details are provided in App. B.2 .

Finally, FTP-1 adopts a multi-expert architecture [ 7 ] to fuse tactile tokens with vision-language perception for action generation, as shown in Fig. 2 . Built on π 0.5 [ 34 ] , FTP-1 processes image observations and language instructions with a pretrained vision-language Transformer expert [ 3 ] , whose outputs are attended by a flow-matching Transformer action expert [ 7 ] . We further fuse proprioceptive state through adaptive RMSNorm [ 34 ] : an MLP encodes the Fourier features of proprioception, which are normalized and concatenated with flow-matching timestep features to condition the attention blocks. Details are provided in App. B.3 .

与之前的触觉增强方案不同 VLA 通过轻量级适配器将触觉输入注入视觉-语言专家中的模型 [90, 12, 31, 40] , FTP- 1 使用独立的触觉专家来提取触觉信息 tokens . 该设计（ 1 ) 支持在微调过程中对未见传感器上的预训练共享触觉专家进行复用（第节） 4.1 ), 促进可迁移的触觉操作技能，并且 ( 2 ) 不会干扰预训练的视觉语言知识。它还（ 3 ) 提升了触觉处理效率与高接触性操作性能，这与我们与基于适配器的方法（如 Tactile-）的对比结果一致。 VLA [31] （节） 3 及第6节 4.1 ). 在融合过程中，来自动作专家的查询会结合触觉专家的键与值以及视觉-语言上下文。 视觉-语言与触觉专家各自参与其对应的 token 流；动作专家 tokens 则参与所有三个流。这种块级注意力掩码使得触觉信息能够直接影响动作生成，同时保留视觉-语言与触觉处理的独立性。其连接机制详见附录 B.4。触觉专家是一个 300 M-参数变换器 [64]。更复杂的模态融合方案，例如基于 MoE 的融合方式 [20, 40, 59]，在我们的实验中并未带来明显的性能提升，因此我们采用了最简单的多专家设计。这些选择共同构成了一种简单的模块化架构，有助于学习可迁移的跨传感器与本体间的触觉操作技能。

Unlike prior tactile-augmented VLA models that inject tactile inputs into the vision-language expert via lightweight adapters [ 90 , 12 , 31 , 40 ] , FTP-1 uses an independent tactile expert for extracted tactile tokens. This design (1) supports reuse pretrained shared tactile expert on unseen sensors during finetuning (Section 4.1 ), promotes transferable tactile manipulation skills, and (2) avoids disturbing pretrained vision-language knowledge. It also (3) improves tactile processing efficiency and contact-rich manipulation performance, as shown by our comparison with adapter-based methods such as Tactile-VLA [ 31 ] (Sec. 3 and Sec. 4.1 ). For fusion, queries from the action expert attend to keys and values from the tactile expert together with the vision-language context. The vision-language and tactile experts each attend within their own token stream; action tokens attend to all three streams. This block attention mask lets tactile information condition action generation directly while preserving separate vision-language and tactile processing. The connectivity is detailed in App. B.4 . The tactile expert is a 300M-parameter Transformer [ 64 ] . More complex modality fusion designs e.g. MoE-based fusion [ 20 , 40 , 59 ] , did not yield consistent gains in our experiment, so we adopt the simplest multi-expert design. Together, these choices provide a simple modular architecture for learning transferable tactile manipulation skills across sensors and embodiments.

3 微调实验

在本节中，我们探讨核心问题：FTP- 1预训练是否通过学习触觉传感器感知的有效表征，从而提升下游触觉策略微调效果？为回答此问题，我们将FTP- 1预训练的检查点分发至3独立机构，这些机构在模拟与真实机器人环境中针对不同的本体及任务集进行了评估。训练细节详见App. D。接下来，我们将描述评估设置及结果。

In this section, we address the central question: Does FTP-1 pretraining improve downstream tactile policy finetuning by learning effective representations for tactile sensor perception? To answer this, we distribute FTP-1 pretrained checkpoint to 3 independent institutions, each evaluates on a distinct embodiment and task suite across simulation and real-robot settings. Training details are provided in App. D . We next describe the evaluation setup and results.

4 可迁移触觉操作能力实验

既有研究表明，拿目标域的传感器做触觉预训练能改善下游策略 [91, 12, 95]。但大规模异构预训练能否迁移到未见过的传感器，尚没有明确结论。这里，我们考察在异构触觉操作数据上预训练的动作生成策略，能否通过微调实现这样的迁移。

While prior work shows that in-domain tactile-sensor pretraining improves downstream policy performance [ 91 , 12 , 95 ] , whether large-scale heterogeneous pretraining enables transfer to unseen sensors remains unclear. We study finetuning transfer of an action-generating policy pretrained on heterogeneous tactile manipulation data.

这里的「未见传感器」指 FTP-1 预训练中没有采用过的具体触觉硬件。Xense 和 Contactile 属于预训练已覆盖的图像与阵列模态，但硬件本身没有见过。下游微调时，FTP-1 使用目标传感器采集的示教训练新初始化的专用编码器，同时复用预训练的触觉专家、功能区域嵌入，以及图像型输入使用的共享 T3 Transformer 主干 [91]。视觉语言专家和动作专家也从 FTP-1 的检查点初始化。各方法使用相同的目标任务示教，微调协议见附录 D。我们先评估微调后的表现，再以消融检验增益是否来自预训练习得的触觉知识。

Here, an unseen sensor is specific tactile hardware absent from FTP-1 pretraining. Xense and Contactile are new hardware within the image- and array-based modality families covered by pretraining. During downstream finetuning, FTP-1 trains a newly initialized sensor-specific encoder on demonstrations collected with the target sensor, while reusing the pretrained tactile expert and functional-area embeddings, as well as the shared T3 Transformer trunk for image-type inputs [ 91 ] . The vision-language and action experts are also initialized from the FTP-1 checkpoint. All methods use the same target-task demonstration sets and the finetuning protocol in App. D . We first evaluate downstream finetuning performance, then conduct ablations to verify whether the gains come from pretrained tactile knowledge.

4.2 改进源自预训练触觉知识

上一节表明，FTP-1 为触觉策略学习提供了有效初始化。即使用未见过的触觉传感器，它也能改善下游微调后的表现。现在要检验的是，这些增益究竟是否源于预训练习得的触觉知识。我们比较两种可能的解释。

The previous section shows that FTP-1 provides a strong initialization for tactile policy learning and improves downstream finetuning performance, even for unseen tactile sensors. However, it remains unclear whether these gains truly come from pretrained tactile knowledge. We therefore examine two possible explanations:

  • •

    假设 1（数据分布）。增益来自 FTP-1 的预训练数据与下游任务的分布更接近，因而比较容易微调。

    Hypothesis 1 (Data Distribution): The gains arise because the FTP-1 pretraining data is closer to the downstream task distribution, making finetuning easier.

  • •

    假设 2（可迁移知识）。FTP-1 学到了可迁移的触觉操作知识，给下游触觉策略微调提供了更好的起点。

    Hypothesis 2 (Transferable Knowledge): FTP-1 learns transferable tactile manipulation knowledge, providing better initialization for downstream tactile policy finetuning.

为验证这两种解释，我们沿用 FTP-1 的预训练设置，包括相同的数据和优化配置，去掉触觉输入和触觉相关架构，得到无触觉预训练检查点（NTP）。微调时，重新加回与 FTP-1 相同的触觉架构，将得到的模型称为 NTP-1。按上一节的微调设置，我们评估 NTP-1 在 UniVTAC [10]（已见传感器）和 FlexivXense（未见传感器）的表现。

To verify this, we pretrain a No-Tactile-Pretraining checkpoint (NTP) using the same setup as FTP-1, including the same pretraining data and optimization settings, but excluding tactile inputs and tactile-related architecture. During finetuning, we add the same tactile-related architecture as FTP-1 during finetuning and refer to the resulting model as NTP-1. We evaluate NTP-1 on UniVTAC [ 10 ] (seen sensor) and FlexivXense (unseen sensor), using the same finetuning setup as previous section.

结果见图 7，详见附录 E.6。所有模型的架构完全一致，只有预训练检查点不同。在 UniVTAC 上，NTP-1 好于 FTP-π0.5\pi_{0.5}，这表明 FTP-1-Dataset 的数据分布可能更接近 UniVTAC。不过 NTP-1 仍低于 FTP-1，说明 FTP-1 的预训练为触觉分支提供了有效初始化。在 FlexivXense 上，FTP-1 相对 NTP-1 提升 +37.5%，说明含触觉的预训练对该场景的迁移很重要。触觉分支没有预训练的 NTP-1 对触觉变化更敏感，在插入的关键阶段也容易发出不稳定的动作。 综合来看，结果支持假设 2（可迁移知识）。FTP-1 的触觉分支从预训练中学到了可迁移到下游接触密集型任务的通用触觉操作知识。

Together, these results support Hypothesis 2 (Transferable Knowledge): the tactile branch of FTP-1 benefits from pretraining, which encodes general tactile manipulation knowledge that can be transferred to downstream contact-rich tasks.

5 相关研究

在附录 A 与表格 5 – 6 中，提供了关于传感器覆盖、动作生成及迁移评估的详细综述与比较。最新的通用机器人策略 [37, 7, 34, 6, 48] 在任务、本体及环境之间展现出强大的迁移能力，但大多忽略了触觉感知。现有的触觉策略与 Vision-Tactile-Language-Action（VTLA）方法 [26, 31, 12, 86, 40] 证明了触觉反馈在涉及接触操作的操控中的重要性，但这些方法通常依赖于特定的传感器、任务或本体。触觉表征与策略预训练方法 [27, 21, 23, 91, 95, 70] 能够提升触觉感知或下游控制性能，但未能实现跨不同触觉硬件的端到端、不依赖传感器的通用触觉策略。

A detailed review and a comparison of sensor coverage, action generation, and transfer evaluation are provided in App. A and Tabs. 5 – 6 . Recent generalist robot policies [ 37 , 7 , 34 , 6 , 48 ] show strong transfer across tasks, embodiments, and environments, but largely omit tactile sensing. Existing tactile policy and Vision-Tactile-Language-Action (VTLA) methods [ 26 , 31 , 12 , 86 , 40 ] demonstrate the value of tactile feedback for contact-rich manipulation, but are typically tied to specific sensors, tasks, or embodiments. Tactile representation and policy pretraining methods [ 27 , 21 , 23 , 91 , 95 , 70 ] improve tactile perception or downstream control, but do not establish an end-to-end sensor-agnostic generalist tactile policy across heterogeneous tactile hardware.

FTP- 1 通过将通用策略范式扩展到触觉操作，从而填补了这一空白。与将触觉反馈视为策略的特定传感器附加组件不同，FTP- 1 研究的是是否可以在大规模层面对触觉操作技能进行预训练，并使其能够在不同异构传感器与本体之间迁移应用。这使得 FTP- 1 成为触觉操作的基础策略基准，其评估涵盖了可见与不可见的触觉传感器配置情况。

FTP-1 addresses this gap by extending the generalist policy paradigm to tactile manipulation. Rather than treating tactile feedback as a sensor-specific add-on for policies, FTP-1 studies whether tactile manipulation skills can be pretrained at scale and transferred across heterogeneous sensors and embodiments. This positions FTP-1 as a foundation-policy baseline for tactile manipulation, with evaluation covering both seen and unseen tactile-sensor setups.

6 结论

我们提出了 FTP- 1，这是一种通用基础触觉策略，经过预训练后可用于对多种传感器及本体进行微调。通过基于从 26 来源收集并涵盖 21 传感器的大型异构触觉操作数据集进行预训练，FTP- 1 能够学习可迁移的触觉操作技能。在 5 硬件环境下的实验表明，FTP- 1 能够提升对已感知及未感知传感器的微调性能。这为触觉策略学习提供了统一的模型层面起点。

We present FTP-1, a generalist foundation tactile policy pretrained for finetuning transfer across diverse sensors and embodiments. By pretraining on a large-scale heterogeneous tactile manipulation dataset aggregated from 26 sources and covering 21 sensors, FTP-1 learns transferable tactile manipulation skills. Experiments on 5 hardware setups show that FTP-1 improves finetuning performance on both seen and unseen sensors. This provides a shared model-level starting point for tactile policy learning.

限制与未来研究方向。作为通用触觉策略学习的初步探索，FTP- 1主要聚焦于通用触觉感知，尚未涉及基于触觉或力的伺服控制与操控。将我们的异构编码框架扩展至未来的触觉预测 [95, 51]以及基于预测的低级控制，是一个具有前景的方向 [76, 87, 96]。另一个限制是，我们的预训练数据集的规模与多样性仍有限。构建更大的聚合数据集 [52]以及进一步扩大预训练规模，仍需未来研究完成。

Limitations and Future Directions. As a preliminary exploration of generalist tactile policy learning, FTP-1 mainly focuses on general tactile perception and does not yet address tactile- or force-based servoing and control. Extending our heterogeneous encoding framework to future tactile prediction [ 95 , 51 ] and prediction-based low-level control is a promising direction [ 76 , 87 , 96 ] . Another limitation is that the scale and diversity of our pretraining dataset remain limited. Building larger aggregated datasets [ 52 ] and further scaling up pretraining are left for future work.

A. 1 机器人操作通用策略学习

机器人学习领域的最新进展已从特定任务策略 [14, 92] 转向通用策略范式。通用策略 [37, 52, 2, 34, 6, 54, 48, 47, 66, 61, 60] 通过大规模机器人数据集 [52, 36, 71] 进行训练，能够快速适应新环境、新本体及新任务。除了机器人演示之外，人类运动 [94, 78, 83, 9, 82, 39, 22, 88]、语义潜在信息 tokens [50, 18, 8]、视频生成 [79, 84, 4, 29] 以及视觉-语言问答 [34, 42, 41] 等异构学习信号也被纳入训练框架中，以提升模型的泛化能力。然而，现有的通用策略大多未包含触觉输入，而触觉输入对于涉及接触操作的操作至关重要。

Recent progress in robot learning has shifted from task-specific policies [ 14 , 92 ] toward the generalist policy paradigm. Generalist policies [ 37 , 52 , 2 , 34 , 6 , 54 , 48 , 47 , 66 , 61 , 60 ] are trained on large-scale robotic datasets [ 52 , 36 , 71 ] and can rapidly adapt to new environments, embodiments, and tasks. Beyond robot demonstrations, heterogeneous learning signals such as human motion [ 94 , 78 , 83 , 9 , 82 , 39 , 22 , 88 ] , semantic latent tokens [ 50 , 18 , 8 ] , video generation [ 79 , 84 , 4 , 29 ] , and vision-language answering [ 34 , 42 , 41 ] have also been incorporated into training frameworks to improve generalization. However, existing generalist policies largely omit tactile inputs, which are crucial for contact-rich manipulation.

A. 2 高接触操作策略学习

通用触觉策略的缺失，主要源于触觉硬件的多样性 [13]。不同平台上的触觉感知方式各不相同，因此跨传感器泛化变得困难 [27, 67, 35]。然而，最新的传感器特定视觉-触觉策略学习研究 [26, 30, 51, 77, 32, 81, 20, 24, 63, 16, 43, 38, 74, 28] 表明，触觉信号能显著提升富含接触的操作效果。与此同时，Vision-Tactile-Language-Action ( VTLA ) 模型 [31, 5, 86, 89, 80, 40] 通过引入触觉输入，对预训练的基于视觉的基础策略进行增强 [7, 72]。相比之下，FTP- 1 则是一种原生基于触觉的基础模型 [62]，而非特定任务的策略或视觉-策略扩展形式。

The absence of generalist tactile policies is largely due to the heterogeneity of tactile hardware [ 13 ] . Across platforms, tactile observations vary in modality, making cross-sensor generalization challenging [ 27 , 67 , 35 ] . Nevertheless, recent sensor-specific visuo-tactile policy learning works [ 26 , 30 , 51 , 77 , 32 , 81 , 20 , 24 , 63 , 16 , 43 , 38 , 74 , 28 ] show that tactile signals substantially improve contact-rich manipulation. In parallel, Vision-Tactile-Language-Action (VTLA) models [ 31 , 5 , 86 , 89 , 80 , 40 ] augment pretrained vision-based foundation policies with tactile inputs [ 7 , 72 ] . In contrast, FTP-1 is a natively tactile foundation model [ 62 ] , rather than a task-specific policy or a vision-policy extension.

A. 3 大规模触觉表征预训练

近期研究探讨了触觉表征预训练技术，以提升下游任务中的触觉感知能力。其中一条研究路线 [45, 46, 11, 97, 10, 91]，包括 Sparsh、[27]、AnyTouch、[21] 以及 ViTaL [23]，通过从视觉-触觉对数据中学习触觉编码器，进而用于下游策略微调。然而，这些表征并未针对机器人操作进行端到端优化。另一条研究路线则直接在动作标签化的触觉操作数据上预训练触觉策略，包括 VTAO-BiManip [58]、FreeTacMan [70]、OmniVTA [95] 和 TAMEn [69]，但受限于传感器特定的设置以及数据集的多样性和规模限制。而 FTP- 1 则提供了一种与传感器无关的通用基础策略，该策略通过大规模预训练，能够适应多种不同的触觉机器人环境。

Recent works have explored tactile representation pretraining to improve tactile perception for downstream tasks. One line of work [ 45 , 46 , 11 , 97 , 10 , 91 ] , including Sparsh [ 27 ] , AnyTouch [ 21 ] , and ViTaL [ 23 ] , learns tactile encoders from vision-tactile pairs for downstream policy finetuning. However, these representations are not optimized end-to-end for robotic manipulation. Another line directly pretrains tactile policies on action-labeled tactile manipulation data, including VTAO-BiManip [ 58 ] , FreeTacMan [ 70 ] , OmniVTA [ 95 ] , and TAMEn [ 69 ] , but remains limited by sensor-specific setups and dataset diversity and scale. FTP-1 instead offers a sensor-agnostic generalist foundation policy with large-scale pretraining for diverse tactile robot setups.

This comparison separates the breadth of a data collection or representation framework from the sensor and embodiment transfer actually evaluated at the policy level.

B. 1 统一动作空间（UAS）

不同本体形式采用不同的机器人控制动作空间。例如，一个机器人可在关节空间内控制单臂，而另一个机器人则需要双手末端执行器姿态控制。为了应对这种多样性，FTP- 1 采用了统一动作空间（UAS）机制，将所有机器人指令表示为固定长度的稀疏向量 [48]。在某一控制步骤中，预测动作为 𝐚=[𝐚L,𝐚R,𝐚e​g​o,𝐚s​u​p]∈ℝD\mathbf{a}=[\mathbf{a}^{L},\mathbf{a}^{R},\mathbf{a}^{ego},\mathbf{a}^{sup}]\in\mathbb{R}^{D}，其中 𝐚L\mathbf{a}^{L} 和 𝐚R\mathbf{a}^{R} 分别表示左臂和右臂的控制信号，𝐚e​g​o\mathbf{a}^{ego} 表示头部姿态控制信号，而 𝐚s​u​p\mathbf{a}^{sup} 则用于额外控制功能，如运动或腰部运动。

Different embodiments use different action spaces for robotic control. For example, one robot may control a single arm in joint space, while another may require bimanual end-effector pose control. To handle this heterogeneity, FTP-1 adopts a Unified Action Space (UAS) that represents all robot commands as fixed-length sparse vectors [ 48 ] . For one control step, the predicted action is 𝐚 = [ 𝐚 L , 𝐚 R , 𝐚 e ​ g ​ o , 𝐚 s ​ u ​ p ] ∈ ℝ D where 𝐚 L and 𝐚 R denote the left- and right-arm control signals, 𝐚 e ​ g ​ o denotes the head-pose control signal, and 𝐚 s ​ u ​ p provides supplementary slots for additional controls such as locomotion or waist motion.

对于每个臂段 b∈{L,R}b\in\{L,R\}，其动作被定义为 𝐚b=[𝐭wb,𝐫wb,𝐪armb,𝐪handb]\mathbf{a}^{b}=[\mathbf{t}_{w}^{b},\mathbf{r}_{w}^{b},\mathbf{q}_{\mathrm{arm}}^{b},\mathbf{q}_{\mathrm{hand}}^{b}]；其中 𝐭wb∈ℝ3\mathbf{t}_{w}^{b}\in\mathbb{R}^{3} 表示手腕平移，𝐫wb∈ℝ6\mathbf{r}_{w}^{b}\in\mathbb{R}^{6} 表示 6 D 型手腕旋转形式，[15] 表示臂关节，𝐪armb∈ℝ7\mathbf{q}_{\mathrm{arm}}^{b}\in\mathbb{R}^{7} 表示臂部关节控制槽，而 𝐪handb∈ℝ32\mathbf{q}_{\mathrm{hand}}^{b}\in\mathbb{R}^{32} 表示规范手关节控制槽。

For each arm b ∈ { L , R } , the arm action is defined as 𝐚 b = [ 𝐭 w b , 𝐫 w b , 𝐪 arm b , 𝐪 hand b ] , where 𝐭 w b ∈ ℝ 3 is the wrist translation, 𝐫 w b ∈ ℝ 6 is the 6D wrist rotation representation [ 15 ] , 𝐪 arm b ∈ ℝ 7 denotes arm joints, and 𝐪 hand b ∈ ℝ 32 denotes canonical hand joint control slots.

B. 2 异构触觉编码器细节

在此我们介绍了各类触觉输入对应的触觉编码器的详细架构。

Here we provide the detailed architecture of tactile encoders for each type of tactile input.

图像型输入。对于具有 (H,W,C)(H,W,C) 形状的输入，我们遵循 T3 -编码器至 [91] 的流程：每个触觉图像被调整为 224×224224\times 224 尺寸，由特定传感器的 Transformer 编码器进行编码（深度= 3，宽度= 768，头数= 12），随后被输入到共享的 Transformer 模块中（深度= 9，宽度= 768，头数= 12）以进行统一令牌化处理。该共享模块以预训练的 T3 权重 [91] 作为初始化，最终的 [CLS] token 被用作触觉表示。

Image-type inputs. For inputs of image shape ( H , W , C ) , we follow T3-Encoder [ 91 ] : each tactile image is resized to 224 × 224 , encoded by a sensor-specific Transformer encoder (depth=3, width=768, head=12), and then passed to a shared Transformer module (depth=9, width=768, head=12) for unified tokenization. The shared module is initialized from pretrained T3 weights [ 91 ] , and the final [CLS] token is used as the tactile representation.

阵列型输入。对于形状为 (H,W,D)(H,W,D) 的输入，其中 H,WH,W 表示阵列的分辨率，DD 表示每个单元的信号维度，我们会在信号维度上应用傅里叶编码 [32]，将其与原始输入进行拼接，然后通过 3 -层 CNN [68] 处理该结果，再经过 2 -层的 ReLU MLP 处理，从而得到触觉 token。

Array-type inputs. For inputs of shape ( H , W , D ) , where H , W is resolution of the array and D is the dimension of signal for each unit, we apply Fourier encoding [ 32 ] on the signal dimension, concatenate it with the original input, and process the result with a 3-layer CNN [ 68 ] followed by a 2-layer ReLU MLP to obtain the tactile token.

状态型输入。对于形状为 (D,)(D,) 的输入，我们应用 [32] 的傅里叶编码，将其与原始状态进行拼接，并通过 3 -层 ReLU MLP 处理来生成触觉 token。

State-type inputs. For inputs of shape ( D , ) , we apply Fourier encoding [ 32 ] , concatenate it with the original state, and use a 3-layer ReLU MLP to obtain the tactile token.

所有触觉 tokens 数据随后通过 LayerNorm [1] 进行归一化处理，并加入共享的函数区域嵌入，最后通过 2 -层 GELU MLP 进行投影，以匹配触觉专家输入维度。

All tactile tokens are then normalized by LayerNorm [ 1 ] , augmented with shared function-area embeddings, and projected by a 2-layer GELU MLP to match the tactile expert input dimension.

B. 5 其他细节

在状态与动作归一化方面，我们采用了 z 分数归一化方法 [7]，与基于分位数的归一化方法 [34] 相比，在实验中能更好地提升动作生成质量，尤其适用于涉及多触点操作的精细动作。触觉专家模型是一个具有 1024 宽度、18 深度、MLP 维度以及 4096、8 注意力头的 Transformer，其头部维度为 256。我们使用 AdamW [49] 对模型进行训练。在之前的实验中，我们还尝试了 Muon [44] 优化器。尽管我们发现 Muon 优化器显著提升了收敛速度与离线动作均方误差，但降低了真实机器人场景下的泛化能力与鲁棒性，因此最终模型仍采用 AdamW。

For state and action normalization, we adopt z-score normalization [ 7 ] , which yielded better action generation quality than quantile-based normalization [ 34 ] in our experiments, especially for fine-grained action in contact-rich manipulation. The tactile expert is a Transformer with width 1024, depth 18, MLP dimension 4096, 8 attention heads, and head dimension 256. We train the model with AdamW [ 49 ] . We also try Muon [ 44 ] optimizer in our earlier experiments. Although we find that Muon optimizer improves convergence speed and offline action MSE significantly, it reduces generalization and robustness in real-robot rollouts, so we use AdamW in the final model.
